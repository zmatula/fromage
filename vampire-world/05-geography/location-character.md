# Location Character

Making places feel real with sensory details, local history, and personality.

---

## The Scar

### What It Looks Like

**From a Distance**
- A wound in the landscape, miles across
- Darker than it should be—absorbs light
- Shapes at the edges that don't quite resolve
- Sometimes shimmers, like heat haze but wrong
- Birds don't fly over it

**The Edge**
- Ground becomes glassy, fused
- Temperature drops suddenly
- Air tastes metallic
- Sound carries strangely—whispers from far away
- Nothing grows within 100 yards of the edge

**Inside** (from those who've entered and returned)
- Ruins of Neth'Korath, preserved
- Buildings intact but... wrong
- Angles don't match up
- Shadows that move
- Things watching

### What It Sounds Like

**The Silence**
- Not quiet—silent
- Sound just stops
- Your own breathing sounds alien
- Footsteps echo strangely
- Some people hear voices

**The Whispers**
- From nowhere
- In no language
- But almost words
- The longer you stay, the clearer they get
- That's when you should leave

### What It Smells Like

**At the Edge**
- Ozone
- Burnt copper
- Something sweet underneath
- Like blood that's been heated

**Inside**
- Nothing
- Your sense of smell stops working
- Comes back wrong afterward
- Some people never get it back right

### What Lives There

**Nothing**
- No animals
- No insects
- No plants
- Nothing that belongs to this world

**But**
- Things have been seen moving
- Shapes that shouldn't be
- Expeditions have lost people
- Not to attacks—they're just gone

### Local Beliefs

**The Border Villages Say**
- The dead of Neth'Korath still walk inside
- The Ascension worked—but not how they wanted
- The thing they tried to become is still in there
- If you go too deep, it wears you home

**Travelers Are Warned**
- Don't look at the center
- Don't follow the voices
- Don't touch anything that's too clean
- Don't stay past sunset

### What's Actually There

**The Capital Ruins**
- Neth'Korath's structures, mostly intact
- The Apex Spire base—spire itself gone
- Archives, treasuries, laboratories
- Everything anyone wants

**Why No One Gets It**
- Distance to center: about three miles
- People start... changing after one mile
- Thoughts get confused
- Priorities shift
- They forget why they came
- They forget to leave

## Vel'Thoras: Where Seven Wakes

### The Exterior

**Location**: Hidden valley in Valdrian border region

**Approach**
- Valley seems empty at first
- Then you notice the cliff face is too regular
- Then you see the doors—massive, sealed
- Vampire script above them, partially eroded
- Signs of previous expeditions (camps, tools, bones)

### Level 1-3: The Outer Complex

**What Expeditions Have Seen**
- Long corridors, vampire architecture
- Some areas collapsed
- Some areas looted (picked clean over centuries)
- Some areas still sealed (can't open them)
- Evidence of post-collapse violence (bodies, damage)

**The Smell**
- Dust and old stone
- Faint chemical undertones
- Deeper in: copper (old blood)
- Deeper still: something else

### Level 4-6: The Research Complex

**Less Explored**
- Active systems still running
- Lights in some corridors
- Temperature controlled
- Dangerous—traps, defenses

**The Laboratories**
- Vessel creation equipment
- Medical instruments
- Containment chambers (most empty, some not)
- Records (partially destroyed)

### Level 7: Where Seven Wakes

**The Stasis Chamber**
- His chamber and others
- Emergency systems still active
- Failing after 1,200 years
- The hum of machinery
- The smell of old blood and preservatives

**Seven's First Sight**
- The lid opening
- Dim blue light
- Dust motes in the air
- Silence except for the machines
- No one there

### What It Feels Like to Wake There

**Physical**
- Cold
- Stiff
- Hungry (so hungry)
- Confused

**Sensory**
- The hum is the only sound
- The light is wrong
- Everything smells like death
- No heartbeats anywhere

**Emotional**
- Where is Velanna?
- How long has it been?
- What happened?
- Am I alone?

## Major Cities

### Valdris (Capital of Valdrian Empire)

**The Layout**
- River city, bridges everywhere
- Old town on island (government)
- New town sprawls both banks
- Walls within walls (defensive layers)

**What You Notice First**
- The smell of the river (not good)
- The noise—constant, everywhere
- The people—pushing, rushing
- The guards—watching everyone

**Districts**

**The Crown Isle** (government)
- Imperial palace
- Noble estates
- Church of Divine Light cathedral
- Where power lives

**The Docks** (commerce)
- Warehouses, merchants, ships
- Money being made
- Also: crime, smuggling, artifacts

**Scholar's Row** (University)
- University of Brightholme
- Libraries, academies
- Also: Order of Dawn headquarters

**The Ash** (poor quarter)
- Named for the purge-era burnings
- Still poor
- Still dangerous
- Where you go if you don't want to be found

**The Danger**
- Succession crisis has made everything tense
- Noble factions have armed men
- Church is watching scholars
- Wrong word in wrong place can kill you

### Iron Crown (Northern Capital)

**The Layout**
- Mountain fortress expanded into city
- Built upward more than outward
- Levels carved into rock
- The palace is at the top

**What You Notice First**
- The cold (always cold)
- The wind (never stops)
- The stone (everything is stone)
- The discipline (everyone has a purpose)

**Characteristics**

**Everything Is Military**
- Even civilians move with order
- Streets are wide (for troops)
- Buildings are defensible
- Everyone can fight

**Everyone Is Watching**
- Northern intelligence is good
- Strangers are noticed
- Papers are checked
- Don't be suspicious

**It Works**
- Clean streets
- No visible poverty
- Efficient services
- But also: no freedom

**The Danger**
- Aldric XII is preparing for war
- Troops are everywhere
- Saying the wrong thing is treason
- Foreigners are potential spies

### Freeport (Largest Free City)

**The Layout**
- No walls (free city = no military)
- Grew organically, no plan
- Neighborhoods distinct and chaotic
- The harbor is the center

**What You Notice First**
- The chaos (no one in charge)
- The freedom (do what you want)
- The danger (no guards)
- The opportunities

**Characteristics**

**Everything Is For Sale**
- Artifacts, information, people
- If it exists, someone's selling it
- Prices fluctuate constantly
- Buyer beware

**Everyone Is Equal**
- No nobility recognized
- No Church authority
- Your money is your status
- Your contracts are your law

**It Doesn't Really Work**
- Banking houses have real power
- Crime is everywhere
- Poor get exploited
- "Freedom" means free to suffer

**The Danger**
- Coup aftermath means banks are fighting
- Stormwind's agents are here
- Consortium's agents are here
- Being in the wrong faction's territory is fatal

## Distinctive Locations

### The Prophet's Folly (Broken Lands)

**What It Is**
- The Ascending Spire where Prophet Vaelen's cult died
- Still standing, still avoided
- 3,000 people died here in one night
- Something happened that no one understands

**What It Looks Like**
- Vampire spire, intact
- Bodies... should have decomposed
- They haven't
- They're still there, frozen in mid-ritual

**What People Say**
- Don't go there
- Don't touch anything
- Don't look at the bodies
- Especially don't look at the Prophet
- He's still standing at the center
- Some people say he moves

### The Listening Wall (Ironpeak)

**What It Is**
- A section of ancient vampire fortification
- Built into the mountain
- Can't be broken down or tunneled through
- And it... listens

**What Happens**
- Stand near the wall
- Speak
- It remembers
- Speak again years later
- It answers with what you said before
- Or what others said
- It has 1,200 years of conversations

**Why It Matters**
- Evidence. Proof. Blackmail.
- Dwarves control access
- You want to know what someone said 500 years ago?
- The wall knows

### Thornhaven (Silverwood Border)

**What It Is**
- Last trading post before elven territory
- Run by half-elves (rare)
- Only place most people can meet elves
- Very controlled

**What It's Like**
- Clean, quiet, careful
- Everything has a price
- Elves don't come here
- But elven goods do
- Information is the real commodity

**Why It Matters**
- Want to get message to Silverwood? Here.
- Want elven artifacts? Here.
- Want to know what elves are doing? Here (sort of).

## How to Use Locations

### Sensory Details Matter

Don't say "they entered the city."
Say:
> "The river stench hit them first, then the noise—a thousand voices, carts on stone, somewhere a child screaming. Guards at the gate already watching. The city was awake and hungry."

### Locations Have Personality

- Valdris: ambitious, fading, dangerous
- Iron Crown: disciplined, cold, watchful
- Freeport: chaotic, free, treacherous
- The Scar: wrong, patient, hungry

### Locations Shape Scenes

The same conversation is different in:
- Valdris: watched by Church
- Iron Crown: watched by Crown
- Freeport: watched by everyone

**Use that.**

*A place is not a location—it's a character. It has history, personality, mood. It shapes what happens there. The Scar doesn't just contain the protagonist's past—it wants him. Vel'Thoras doesn't just hide secrets—it's dying and so are they. Valdris doesn't just have politics—it smells like ambition and fear. Write places like people. Give them what they want. Make them act on it.*
