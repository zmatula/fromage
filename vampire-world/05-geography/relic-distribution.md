# Relic Distribution

Where the significant vampire artifacts are located, who controls them, and their story relevance.

---

## Major Artifacts by Location

### Valdrian Empire

**Crown of Seeing**
- **Type**: Divination device
- **Location**: Imperial vaults, Valdris Prime
- **Holder**: Emperor Cassius VIII
- **Discovery**: Year 312
- **Capabilities**: Grants visions (unreliable, draining)
- **Current Use**: Strategic intelligence (driving Cassius mad?)
- **Story Relevance**: May give emperor visions of protagonist

**Imperial Collection** (~200 items)
- **Location**: Valdris Prime vaults
- **Holder**: Imperial government
- **Status**: Largest known collection
- **Notable Items**: Various tools, weapons, devices
- **Access**: Scholars under supervision
- **Story Relevance**: Could help protagonist understand himself

---

### Northern Dominion

**Vel'Krath Weapons Cache**
- **Type**: Military equipment
- **Location**: Distributed to units
- **Holder**: Northern military
- **Discovery**: Year 745
- **Capabilities**: Enhanced weapons, defenses
- **Current Use**: Standard military integration
- **Story Relevance**: Best artifact-equipped army

**Living Artifact**
- **Type**: Unknown (autonomous intelligence?)
- **Location**: Veil Keeper custody (secret)
- **Holder**: Veil Keepers (Northern wants back)
- **Discovery**: Year 1118
- **Capabilities**: Shows awareness, demands blood
- **Current Use**: Sealed, studied
- **Story Relevance**: Connection to protagonist? Key information?

---

### Ironpeak Confederation

**Forge of Souls**
- **Type**: Creation device
- **Location**: Deep hold (secret)
- **Holder**: Dwarves
- **Discovery**: Year 358
- **Capabilities**: Can create artifact-like items
- **Current Use**: Never used (officially)
- **Story Relevance**: Could repair/modify protagonist?

**Deep Hold Collections**
- **Type**: Various
- **Location**: Sealed chambers
- **Holder**: Individual holds
- **Status**: Secret; contents unknown
- **Story Relevance**: Dwarves know more than they say

---

### Maritime Republic

**Market Artifacts**
- **Type**: Various
- **Location**: Goldport and Port Sovereign
- **Holder**: Private collectors, merchants
- **Status**: Largest trade in artifacts
- **Notable**: Best expertise, worst ethics
- **Story Relevance**: Information and tools for sale

**University Collections**
- **Type**: Research items
- **Location**: Scholar's Haven
- **Holder**: Inheritor-influenced academics
- **Status**: Study focus
- **Story Relevance**: Inheritor resources

---

### Sanctified States

**Officially**: No vampire artifacts held
**Reality**: Order of Dawn uses "purified" artifacts

**Order of Dawn Arsenal**
- **Type**: Military equipment
- **Location**: Dawnhaven
- **Holder**: Order of Dawn
- **Status**: Secret; officially "purified"
- **Capabilities**: Anti-vampire weapons
- **Story Relevance**: Used to hunt protagonist

**Sealed Sites**
- **Type**: Unknown
- **Location**: Church-controlled ruins
- **Holder**: Church
- **Status**: Sealed, "destroyed"
- **Story Relevance**: What are they hiding?

---

### Silverwood Domain

**Unknown**
- Elves certainly have artifacts
- Probably most significant after Sel'Naroth
- Complete vampire-era archives
- Nothing shared with outside world
- Story Relevance: Ultimate information source

---

### Broken Lands

**Goblin Holdings**
- **Type**: Various (blood magic sources?)
- **Location**: Secret sites
- **Holder**: Goblin shamans
- **Status**: Hidden from humans
- **Story Relevance**: Blood magic connection

## Strategic Artifact Distribution

### Power Balance
- Valdrian: Most numerous, best catalogued
- Northern: Best integrated militarily
- Maritime: Best studied academically
- Ironpeak: Most secret, most significant?
- Sanctified: Least (officially)
- Silverwood: Unknown (probably most)

### Conflicts Over Artifacts
- Valdrian-Northern: Border ruin access
- Everyone-Veil Keepers: Living Artifact
- Inheritors-Everyone: Acquisition attempts
- Order of Dawn-Inheritors: Seizure and destruction

## Story-Critical Artifacts

### For Understanding Protagonist
- **Vel'Thoras Archives**: His creation records

- **Forge of Souls**: Repair/modification

- **Vel'Krath Weapons**: Defense

### For Threatening Protagonist
- **Crown of Seeing**: Might detect him

### For Story Climax
- **Gate Fragment**: Dimensional significance?
- **The Scar Contents**: If anything survives
- **Sel'Naroth Archives**: Ultimate truth
