# Sel'Naroth

The Twilight Covenant's sanctuary—possibly intact, possibly inhabited, hidden in elven territory.

---

## Overview

**Type**: Refuge/sanctuary complex
**Location**: Silverwood Domain (specific location unknown)
**Covenant**: Twilight (Covenant of the Silver Twilight)
**Built**: Year -300 to -100
**Original Purpose**: Survival refuge, knowledge preservation, population sanctuary
**Current Status**: Unknown—elves control all access

---

## History

### Vampire Era

**Year -300**: Construction began after Twilight recognized dangers
**Year -200**: Acceleration as collapse risks grew
**Year -100**: Fully operational sanctuary
**Year 0**: Sealed during collapse; High Seer Selene led survivors inside

### Post-Collapse

**Year 934**: Expedition found entrance (defenses activated)
**Year 1175**: Inheritors located probable entrance (expedition wiped out)
**Year 1050**: Elves close borders (possibly related)
**Year 1200**: Entrance locations known; no access possible
