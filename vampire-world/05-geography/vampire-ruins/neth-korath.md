# Neth'Korath

The Mind Covenant's capital—destroyed in the Ascension Experiment. Now The Scar.

---

## Overview

**Type**: Capital city and research hub
**Location**: The Scar (central-north continent)
**Covenant**: Mind (Covenant of the Eternal Mind)
**Built**: Year -810 to -500
**Original Purpose**: Covenant capital, Bloodweb nexus, dimensional research
**Current Status**: Completely destroyed; uninhabitable wasteland

---

## History

### Vampire Era

**Year -810**: Founded after Covenant formation
**Year -600**: Bloodweb nexus installed
**Year -200**: Dimensional research accelerated
**Year -54**: First successful dimensional breach
**Year 0**: Ascension Experiment destroyed city

### The Ascension Experiment

**14th Deepwinter, Year 0**
- Attempt to create dimensional anchor
- Breach resonated with Bloodweb
- Inverted the entire network
- ~100,000 vampires died instantly
- City destroyed in seconds
- Created The Scar

### Post-Collapse

**Year 0 onward**: Completely avoided
**Year 1178**: Dimensional readings detected
**Year 1200**: Readings increasing
