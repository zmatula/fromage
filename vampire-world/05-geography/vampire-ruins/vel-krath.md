# Vel'Krath

The Throne Covenant's military headquarters—source of the Northern Dominion's artifact arsenal and the mysterious Living Artifact.

---

## Overview

**Type**: Military command complex
**Location**: Northern Dominion highlands
**Covenant**: Throne (Covenant of the Crimson Throne)
**Built**: Year -800 to -500
**Original Purpose**: Military command, weapons development, training
**Current Status**: Upper levels exploited; deep complex sealed

---

## History

### Vampire Era

**Year -800**: Construction began after Covenant formation
**Year -500**: Peak military operations
**Year -100**: Internal security increased (coup fears)
**Year 0**: General Aethra attempted evacuation; failed

### Post-Collapse

**Year 745**: Deep expedition found weapons cache
**Year 934**: Twilight Border expedition originated here
**Year 1118**: Living Artifact recovered
**Year 1200**: Northern military uses facility regularly

## The Living Artifact

### Discovery (Year 1118)
- Found in deep arsenal level
- Activated when touched
- Showed signs of awareness
- Demanded blood
- Seized by Veil Keepers

### What It Is
- Unknown—several theories
- Trapped vampire intelligence?
- Autonomous system?
- Something like protagonist?
- Communications device?

### Current Status
- In Veil Keeper custody
- Location secret
- King Aldric wants it back
- Could provide key information
