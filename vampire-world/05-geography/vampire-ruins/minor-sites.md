# Minor Vampire Sites

A registry of the 15+ known minor vampire ruins scattered across the continent.

---

## Classification

**Major Sites**: Capital cities, primary facilities (4 total)
**Minor Sites**: Secondary facilities, outposts, specialized installations
**Discovery Status**: Known, partially explored, sealed, or rumored

---

### The Southern Catacombs
**Location**: Southern Valdrian province
**Type**: Administrative/residential complex
**Status**: Partially explored
**Notable**: Year 812 stasis vampire discovered and destroyed here
**Artifacts**: Several recovered, most low-value
**Danger Level**: Moderate (some active traps)

### Goldport Undercity
**Location**: Beneath Goldport
**Type**: Trade and storage facility
**Status**: Largely explored
**Notable**: Foundation of Goldport's artifact market
**Artifacts**: Mostly stripped
**Danger Level**: Low

### Eastern Archive
**Location**: Near Oldtown
**Type**: Library/records facility
**Status**: Partially explored
**Notable**: Source of many translated texts
**Artifacts**: Documents primarily
**Danger Level**: Moderate

### River Junction Complex
**Location**: Valdris-Goldwater confluence
**Type**: Transportation hub
**Status**: Flooded; partial exploration
**Notable**: Underwater sections inaccessible
**Artifacts**: Some recovered from accessible areas
**Danger Level**: High (flooding, structural)

### Valdrian Border Posts (multiple)
