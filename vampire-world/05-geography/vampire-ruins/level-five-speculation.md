# Vel'Thoras Level Five: Speculation

The secret level that might not exist—and what might be down there if it does.

---

## Evidence for Existence

### Architectural Analysis

**The Space Problem**:
Vel'Thoras was built into a mountain. Based on the known levels (Surface through Level Four), the facility uses only about 60% of the available volume. The remaining 40% is either solid rock or undocumented space.

**The Power Readings**:
Inheritor instruments detect power consumption patterns below Level Four. Not much—equivalent to basic life support—but consistent. Something is drawing power down there.

**The Ventilation**:
Air flow analysis shows circulation patterns that extend below Level Four. The facility was designed to move air to and from somewhere deeper.

### Documentary Evidence

**The Reference**:
One damaged record from Level One mentions "sublevel research, restricted." No other reference exists. Either it was a mistake, or everything else was deliberately destroyed.

**The Discrepancy**:
Facility construction records show more materials used than the known levels would require. Either there was massive waste (unlikely for Mind covenant), or they built something that isn't on the maps.

**Year 252 Map**:
In Year 252, someone found a "map to Vel'Thoras sublevel" that sparked a treasure rush. The map was never recovered, but multiple expeditions tried to find what it showed. None succeeded. Either the map was fake, or Level Five is real and well-hidden.

### Protocol Nine

**The Evacuation Route**:
Dorian val'Kess evacuated Vessel Eleven to a "secondary facility" per Protocol Nine. The obvious secondary facility would be deeper in Vel'Thoras—Level Five.

**The Logic**:
If you're evacuating during catastrophe, you go down (protected) not out (exposed). Down from Level Four means Level Five.

## Theories About Purpose

### Theory 1: True Vessel Laboratory

**The Idea**: Levels One through Four were the public (or semi-public) facility. Level Five was where the real work happened.

**Supporting Evidence**:
- Vessels 1-6 and 8-10 failed. Where are their bodies? Not on Level Four.
- Velanna's notes reference "comparative analysis." Comparing Seven to what?
- The Vessels were supposedly unique—but what if they weren't the only experiments?

**What Would Be There**:
- Failed Vessel remains
- Earlier prototypes
- The true purpose of the Vessel project
- What Velanna was really trying to create

### Theory 2: Eleven's Destination

**The Idea**: Dorian took Eleven to Level Five during Protocol Nine. Eleven is still there.

**Supporting Evidence**:
- Protocol Nine specified a "secondary facility"
- Eleven was the only other successful Vessel
- Velanna wouldn't put both successes in the same place

**What Would Be There**:
- Eleven in stasis (or awake)
- Eleven's modified laboratory
- Whatever Eleven has been doing for 1,200 years
- A being who's had centuries to plan

### Theory 3: Mordecai's Contingency

**The Idea**: Mordecai didn't put all his backups in one place. The Mind Codex is one contingency. Level Five is another.

**Supporting Evidence**:
- Mordecai was paranoid and thorough
- He expected the Collapse might kill him
- The Codex is a consciousness backup—but consciousness needs a body
- Level Five might contain prepared vessels (bodies) for transfer

**What Would Be There**:
- Clone bodies or constructed vessels
- Transfer equipment
- Mordecai's real research (more complete than the Codex)
- Maybe another version of Mordecai

### Theory 4: The Actual Experiment

**The Idea**: The Vessels weren't the experiment. They were the control group. Level Five contains the actual subjects.

**Supporting Evidence**:
- Seven and Eleven were made Bloodweb-independent—why?
- The simplest answer: to compare them to subjects who weren't
- What if Velanna was testing something that required a baseline?

**What Would Be There**:
- Subjects who remained Bloodweb-dependent
- Data on what Bloodweb dependency actually does long-term
- The answer to what Velanna was really researching
- Possibly: subjects who evolved in stasis over 1,200 years

### Theory 5: The Forbidden Archive

**The Idea**: Level Five is storage, not research. It contains everything Mind covenant didn't want found—even by other Mind members.

**Supporting Evidence**:
- Mind was obsessed with knowledge
- But some knowledge was suppressed even within the covenant
- They needed somewhere to put dangerous secrets
- Level Five is perfect: deep, hidden, protected

**What Would Be There**:
- Research too dangerous to publish
- Knowledge that could destroy vampire civilization
- The truth about the Collapse (maybe they knew it was coming)
- Weapons, techniques, or creatures too terrible to use

## Access Questions

### How to Reach It

**From Level Four**:
There must be an entrance from Level Four—the stasis level. But no entrance is visible on known maps. Either it's:
- Hidden (secret door, illusion)
- Sealed (collapsed passage, ward barrier)
- Unconventional (teleport pad, transformation required)

**Protocol Nine Route**:
Dorian used Protocol Nine to reach Level Five. That route might still be accessible—if anyone can find it. The route would be hidden, protected, and probably keyed to specific people (Velanna, her senior staff, Vessels).

**Seven's Access**:
As Velanna's creation, Seven might have automatic access. His blood, his presence, his identity might open doors that have been sealed for 1,200 years. He might not even know he's a key.

### What Blocks It

**Wards**:
Mind covenant wards were sophisticated. Level Five would have the best. Detecting intruders, killing intruders, trapping intruders, misleading intruders—probably all at once.

**Physical Barriers**:
Collapse damage might have blocked the passage. Or it might have been deliberately collapsed to prevent access. Breaking through risks destroying whatever's inside.

**The Guardian**:
What if something guards Level Five? A construct, a bound spirit, a failed Vessel kept alive as security. Something that's been waiting 1,200 years for intruders.

## Who's Looking

### The Inheritors

**Knowledge**: They have the best Vel'Thoras data and suspect Level Five exists.
**Goal**: Find it before anyone else. Secure whatever's there.
**Problem**: They can't get past Level Three door. Even if they could, they might not have Level Five access.

### Valdrian Imperial Expedition

**Knowledge**: They've funded archaeological surveys and know the facility is deeper than mapped.
**Goal**: National prestige. Demonstrate imperial capability.
**Problem**: They're thinking like archaeologists, not Mind covenant security designers.

### Northern Kingdom

**Knowledge**: Minimal—they just want to control whatever's there.
**Goal**: Prevent Valdris from getting it.
**Problem**: They don't understand what they're looking for.

### Seven

**Knowledge**: Fragments. Velanna's memories include references he doesn't fully understand.
**Goal**: Initially none—he doesn't know Level Five exists. Then: find answers.
**Advantage**: He's probably a key. He was created there. The facility might recognize him.

## What Finding It Changes

### If It's Empty

Level Five might be a myth. The evidence is circumstantial. If someone finally reaches it and finds nothing:
- The theories collapse
- Eleven's location remains unknown
- The mysteries stay mysterious
- But: why would Mind covenant build empty space?

### If Eleven Is There

Finding Eleven changes everything:
- Seven isn't unique
- Eleven has had 1,200 years to evolve
- There are now two Vessels with potentially different goals
- The balance of power shifts toward whoever controls both

### If Mordecai's Contingency Is There

Finding another version of Mordecai:
- Raises questions about identity (which is the real one?)
- Provides access to complete Mind covenant knowledge
- Creates a being with the power and motivation to rebuild vampire civilization
- Makes the Mind Codex less valuable—or more dangerous

### If the Forbidden Archive Exists

Finding suppressed knowledge:
- Could explain the Collapse
- Could provide weapons against The Scar
- Could contain things that should never be known
- Would make whoever finds it the most powerful faction in the world

## Plot Implications

### The Expedition Race

Multiple factions will try to reach Level Five once Seven's awakening makes it relevant. The race to get there—and control what's found—drives mid-story conflict.

### Seven's Choice

If Seven can access Level Five and others can't, he becomes the gatekeeper. Does he:
- Share what he finds?
- Hide it?
- Trade access for safety?
- Destroy it to prevent misuse?

### The Revelation

Whatever is in Level Five will reveal something fundamental about:
- The Vessel project's real purpose
- What Velanna knew about the Collapse
- Why Seven was made the way he was
- What vampire civilization was really trying to achieve

This revelation will reframe everything that came before.

## Connection to Seven's Awakening

### Why Now

Seven's stasis is failing because Level Four life support is failing. But Level Five might have independent systems. If Level Five is intact:
- Its existence might be why Level Four survived this long
- Reaching it might stabilize Seven
- Or it might wake up whatever else is down there

### The Sequence

1. Seven wakes on Level Four
2. He finds evidence of Level Five
3. He has to decide: explore or escape
4. Exploring means answers but danger
5. Escaping means safety but ignorance

### Velanna's Design

What if Velanna planned this? What if Seven waking was always supposed to lead to Level Five? What if he's meant to find whatever's there—because only he can use it, understand it, or stop it?

Velanna didn't just create Seven. She created a sequence. Level Five might be the final step.
