# Vel'Thoras

The Mind Covenant's premier research complex—and the location of the protagonist's stasis chamber. Where everything begins.

---

## Overview

**Type**: Major research facility / Covenant headquarters
**Location**: Eastern Valdrian territory, Thornwood Hills, 50 miles from The Scar
**Covenant**: Mind (Covenant of the Eternal Mind)
**Built**: Year -600 to -400
**Original Purpose**: Advanced research, consciousness studies, Sanguine Vessels Initiative
**Current Status**: Outer complex explored; lower levels sealed; inner sanctum unknown
**Strategic Value**: Extreme—multiple factions converging

---

## History

### Construction Era (Year -600 to -400)

Vel'Thoras began as Mordecai's personal research retreat. He chose the location for three reasons: proximity to Neth'Korath (for Bloodweb access), isolation from other covenants (for secrecy), and the specific dimensional properties of the Thornwood Hills (something in the earth made certain experiments easier).

**Year -600**: Mordecai purchased the land from the Crimson Throne—a political favor that cost him considerably.

**Year -580**: Surface construction completed. Research began in temporary facilities.

**Year -540**: First underground level excavated. Mordecai began relocating sensitive projects from Neth'Korath.

**Year -470**: Deep excavation reached current depth. Core facilities activated.

**Year -400**: Vel'Thoras declared Mind Covenant's primary research headquarters.

The facility was never finished. Mordecai kept expanding, adding levels, laboratories, chambers for projects he wouldn't name. The final blueprints—if they exist—show a complex far larger than anyone realizes.

### Golden Age Operations (Year -400 to -100)

For three centuries, Vel'Thoras was the center of vampire intellectual achievement. Projects included:

**Consciousness Transfer Research**: Attempting to move vampire minds into new bodies, objects, or the Bloodweb itself. Partial successes. Many failures. Many dead subjects.

**Dimensional Studies**: Probing the barriers between worlds. Mapping what existed beyond. Bringing things through. Losing people into the gaps.

**Blood Magic Enhancement**: Refining vampire abilities beyond natural limits. The research that led to the Sanguine Vessels.

**The Memory Archives**: Recording and storing the experiences of dying vampires. Preserving knowledge that would otherwise be lost. Creating the foundation for the Blood Archives artifact.

**Year -312**: Mordecai created the Mind Codex here—encoding his entire consciousness into an artifact. He didn't tell anyone why. He didn't tell anyone he'd done it.

**Year -200**: Velanna joined as Lead Researcher. She would eventually run the Sanguine Vessels Initiative.

### The Final Century (Year -100 to 0)

As tensions between covenants increased, Vel'Thoras became more secretive.

**Year -68**: After the Council's suspension, Mordecai closed Vel'Thoras to all non-Mind visitors.

**Year -50**: Sanguine Vessels Initiative officially began. Twelve subjects created in Vessel Lab 7.

**Year -12**: Vessel Seven created. Showed unexpected traits—empathy, emotional complexity, attachment.

**Year -7**: Vessel Seven placed in long-term stasis. Official reason: "behavioral irregularities requiring extended observation." Real reason: Velanna wasn't sure what she'd made.

**Year -3**: Vessel Eleven created—the "improved" version. Colder. More controlled. More vampire.

**Year 0, Hour 0**: The Collapse. Dimensional breach at Neth'Korath killed 99% of vampires instantly. Vel'Thoras was close enough to feel the wave but far enough to survive. Automated systems sealed the facility. Stasis chambers continued operating. Everyone awake died.

### Post-Collapse (Year 0 to Present)

**Year 0-50**: The facility sat untouched. Locals avoided the "cursed place." Stories of lights in the hills, sounds from underground.

**Year 312**: First attempted exploration. A group of treasure hunters found the surface ruins. They broke through to the Upper Complex and found... nothing valuable. Traps killed two. They left.

**Year 405**: Major expedition from early Valdrian kingdom. They mapped the Upper Complex, recovered some artifacts, lost four people to security systems. Report noted "deeper levels inaccessible—sealed and defended."

**Year 720**: Scholar expedition attempted to breach Middle Complex. Reached Level 4 before automated defenses activated. All killed. Their notes survived and revealed the facility's scope.

**Year 878**: First successful penetration of Middle Complex. Joint expedition—Valdrian Academy, Church observers, Imperial guards. They recovered significant research notes, several artifacts, and evidence of the Sanguine Vessels Initiative. This was the first time anyone knew the Vessels existed.

**Year 1047**: Inheritors infiltrate an official expedition. They reach Level 5 before being stopped. They find evidence of stasis chambers. Subjects still active.

**Year 1112**: Deep expedition reaches Level 6—close to the stasis bay. They encounter defenses they cannot pass. They report "power signatures consistent with active life support." The race to breach begins.

**Year 1180**: Inheritor covert team gets further than anyone—reaches Level 7's outer corridors. They recover awakening protocols before being driven back. They know exactly where Seven is. They know how to wake him.

**Year 1198**: Valdrian Empire announces "Imperial Archaeological Initiative"—a cover for their planned breach attempt.

**Year 1200**: Multiple factions preparing simultaneous operations.

## Layout

### Surface Level (Destroyed)

What remains of Vel'Thoras above ground isn't much. The Collapse's shockwave flattened most structures. Weather and scavengers finished the rest.

**The Foundation Ring**: Stone foundations showing the original buildings' footprints. Administration, housing, a great hall. All gone.

**The Entrance Plaza**: Where visitors arrived. The main stairs down are blocked by rubble—cleared three times, collapsed three times.

**The East Tower**: Partial walls still stand, maybe 30 feet high. Local legend says it's haunted. Local legend is probably right.

**The Garden**: Mordecai maintained gardens for research subjects—plants from across the vampire world. Now a toxic wilderness. Things grow there that don't grow anywhere else.

### Upper Complex (Levels 1-3) — Explored

The first three levels have been mapped, looted, and picked over for 800 years. Little of value remains.

**Level 1: Administration**

Offices, record halls, meeting chambers. Everything paper is gone—rotted, burned, or removed. Stone tablets remain, covered in languages no one can read (Mind Covenant cipher, never cracked).

Key rooms:
- **Entry Hall**: Grand chamber, vaulted ceiling mostly intact. Where expeditions camp.
- **Records Office**: Empty shelves. Something important was removed—or destroyed.
- **Security Station**: Deactivated guardians—studied, stripped, sold. The control systems are dead.

**Level 2: General Laboratories**

Common research spaces. Benches, equipment mounts, storage. Most equipment was taken long ago.

Key rooms:
- **Lab Complex Alpha**: Twelve interconnected labs. Whatever was researched here, someone cleaned it out thoroughly.
- **Materials Storage**: Some containers remain, sealed by magic that's degraded but not gone. Opening them is risky.
- **Staff Quarters**: Small chambers where researchers lived. Personal items left behind—the Collapse was sudden.

**Level 3: Library Wing**

Mind Covenant libraries were legendary. This was a minor branch.

Key rooms:
- **Reading Halls**: Empty shelves, damaged murals showing scenes of vampire scholars.
- **Scroll Repository**: Some documents remain—minor research, accounting, correspondence. Valuable to historians but not to treasure hunters.
- **Restricted Section**: Door sealed, still holding. Nobody's breached it in 1200 years. Whatever's inside, it was worth protecting.

### Middle Complex (Levels 4-6) — Partially Explored

This is where the danger begins. Security systems remain active. Power still flows. The facility is protecting something.

**Level 4: Specialized Research**

The serious work happened here.

Key rooms:
- **Consciousness Lab**: Where transfer experiments occurred. Containment circles still active—something might be trapped.
- **Dimensional Study**: Equipment for probing barriers between worlds. Heavily damaged, possibly self-destructed.
- **Biological Research**: The Sanguine Vessels' early work. Animal subjects. Trial runs. Tanks of things that might still be alive.

Security: Automated guardians—mechanical constructs following ancient orders. Ward systems—magical barriers that burn, trap, or redirect. Both functional, both deadly.

**Level 5: High Security**

Only three expeditions have reached this level.

Key rooms:
- **Project Vault**: Sealed doors requiring codes no one knows. The Inheritors think awakening protocols are stored here.
- **Secure Laboratory**: Where Velanna worked on the Vessels. Still sealed.
- **Power Junction**: The facility's nervous system. Control this and you control everything below.

Security: Guardian types not seen on upper levels—faster, smarter, harder to kill. Ward patterns that adapt—they learn from intrusion attempts.

**Level 6: Pre-Sanctum**

One expedition reached this level (Year 1112). They couldn't go further.

Key rooms:
- **Guard Station**: Final security checkpoint. Multiple guardian types—including some that look disturbingly human.
- **Preparation Chamber**: Where subjects were readied for stasis. Equipment, medical supplies, holding cells.
- **The Gate**: Entrance to Level 7. Sealed. The Year 1112 expedition couldn't open it. The Year 1180 Inheritors barely could.

Security: Everything active. Everything deadly. Something down here knows intruders are coming and doesn't want them getting through.

### Lower Complex (Level 7) — Sealed

The Inheritors reached the outer corridors in Year 1180. They didn't reach the heart.

**Vessel Lab 7**

Where Seven sleeps.

- Twelve stasis chambers originally
- Seven's chamber: Position 7, still functional, barely
- Empty chambers: 1-6, 8-12 (subjects removed or died)
- Research stations: Notes, equipment, Velanna's private records
- The timer: Counting down to automatic awakening

**Central Stasis Bay**

Larger chamber, purpose unknown.

- Multiple stasis units, different from Vessel chambers
- Contents unknown
- Power signatures suggest some are active
- What else did they put to sleep?

**Velanna's Sanctum**

Her personal laboratory.

- Where she worked alone
- Where she recorded her real notes
- Where she kept her secrets
- Still sealed, even to Level 7 access

### Deep Core (Level 8+) — Unknown

The blueprints show eight levels. The reality may be different.

- Power signatures from below Level 7
- Dimensional readings similar to The Scar
- Something is down there
- Mordecai built it
- He didn't tell anyone what it was

## Security Systems

### Automated Guardians

**Type A: Sentinel**
- Humanoid constructs, 7 feet tall
- Armed with blade appendages
- Follow patrol patterns, respond to intrusion
- Present on all levels, increasing numbers below
- Can be destroyed with sufficient force

**Type B: Seeker**
- Smaller, faster, spider-like
- Hunt in packs, overwhelm targets
- First deployed on Level 4
- Difficult to destroy—they just keep coming

**Type C: Warden**
- Larger, heavily armored, single units
- Guard specific locations
- Extremely dangerous—Year 720 expedition lost everyone to one
- Present on Levels 5-7

**Type D: Mimic**
- Appear human
- Reported on Level 6 only
- The Year 1112 expedition thought they'd found survivors
- They were wrong

### Ward Systems

**Barrier Wards**: Block passages. Some visible, some not. Walking into one unaware can mean death.

**Trap Wards**: Triggered by intrusion. Fire, acid, force, dimensional displacement. Some can be disarmed. Some can't.

**Alarm Wards**: Alert other systems to intrusion. The facility knows when you arrive.

**Confusion Wards**: Affect minds—disorientation, hallucination, suggestion. The Year 1180 team reported one member turned on the others before being restrained.

### Power Systems

Vel'Thoras runs on blood.

Not metaphorically. The power system uses stored blood to generate energy. Massive tanks in the Deep Core have been slowly depleting for 1200 years. They're almost empty.

When the power fails:
- Guardians stop
- Wards fall
- Stasis chambers shut down
- Seven dies

The facility has maybe 2-5 years of power remaining. Maybe less.

### The Intelligence

Some researchers believe Vel'Thoras is controlled by a central intelligence—either a trapped consciousness or a sophisticated control system that mimics one. Evidence:
- Security responses seem coordinated, not just reactive
- The facility "learns" from intrusion attempts
- Some expedition survivors report feeling watched—personally watched, by something that knew them

If there's an intelligence, it's been alone for 1200 years. That much isolation changes things.

- Functional artifacts (behind security)

### Suspected (Lower Complex)

- Velanna's private research

- Answers about Seven's true purpose

- Dimensional research materials

## The Factions Converging

### Valdrian Empire

**Official Stance**: "Archaeological preservation of pre-Collapse heritage"
**Actual Goal**: Claim artifacts for Imperial treasury, establish control over strategic site
**Status**: Preparing major expedition for Year 1200
**Resources**: Military escort (200 soldiers), Imperial scholars, Church observers
**Leader**: Lord Curator Brennan Ashworth—Imperial antiquarian, ambitious, underestimates the danger
**Timeline**: Planning spring expedition
**Will They Wake Seven?**: Not intentionally—but they might trigger awakening by disrupting systems

### Inheritors of Blood

**Official Stance**: "Preservation of vampire knowledge"
**Actual Goal**: Wake Vessel Seven, study him, use him
**Status**: Ready to move—they have awakening protocols
**Resources**: Small team (15), all experts, all fanatics
**Leader**: Vera Nighthollow—leads Inheritor operations, has been planning this for decades
**Timeline**: Ready now—waiting for the right moment
**Will They Wake Seven?**: Yes—that's the entire point

### Veil Keepers

**Official Stance**: "Truth must be known"
**Actual Goal**: Prevent others from controlling what's found—or bury it so deep no one can use it
**Status**: Have infiltrated Valdrian expedition
**Resources**: Three agents in the Imperial team, plus independent team on standby
**Leader**: Unknown handler, agent-in-place is Scholar Mira Venn
**Timeline**: Moving when the Empire moves
**Will They Wake Seven?**: Depends what they find—they might decide he's too dangerous to live

### Order of Dawn

**Official Stance**: "Destroy all vampire remnants"
**Actual Goal**: Kill anything that wakes up, destroy the facility
**Status**: Have strike team in position
**Resources**: Twenty zealots with consecrated weapons and explosives
**Leader**: Commander Aldric Sunfire—believes he's on a holy mission
**Timeline**: Waiting for someone else to breach the defenses, then moving
**Will They Wake Seven?**: Only to kill him

### Northern Dominion

**Official Stance**: "This site belongs to all humanity"
**Actual Goal**: Deny Valdris the prize, maybe grab it themselves
**Status**: Monitoring, prepared to act
**Resources**: Military company nearby, covert team in the hills
**Leader**: Spymaster Lord Kell Ironhand
**Timeline**: If Valdris gets something big, the North will move
**Will They Wake Seven?**: Probably not—but they'll want to claim him if someone else does

### Unknown Party

Someone else is watching. Signs in the Thornwood Hills—camps, tracks, observation points. Not matching any known faction.

Who?
- Elves? (Why?)
- Independent treasure hunters? (How do they know?)
- Someone with inside information? (From where?)
- The Church, operating outside its stated mission?

## Seven's Awakening

### The Trigger Options

**Automatic Timer**: Set for 1200 years. The timer is completing. Seven could wake on his own, any day now.

**Deliberate Revival**: The Inheritors have awakening protocols. They can bring him up whenever they choose.

**System Failure**: Power is failing. If stasis shuts down improperly, Seven wakes—damaged, disoriented, possibly dying.

**Intrusion Disruption**: If someone reaches Level 7 and damages systems, Seven might wake as emergency protocol.

### What He Wakes To

**Immediate Environment**:
- Dark (lights failing)
- Cold (climate control gone)
- Silent (no one has spoken here in 1200 years)
- Alarms (intrusion detected, systems responding)

**Physical State**:
- Stasis sickness (disorientation, nausea, weakness)
- Memory fragmentation (1207 years compressed into gaps)
- Hunger (he'll need blood, soon)
- Damage (stasis systems are failing—he may wake hurt)

**Threats**:
- Guardians (following ancient protocols to contain him)
- Intruders (multiple groups converging)
- The facility itself (systems failing, structure unstable)
- His own confusion (he won't know who he is, where he is, or why)

### Escape Options

**Main Exit**: Through six levels of guardians, wards, and possibly hostile humans.

**Emergency Exit**: Supposed to exist—direct route to surface. Location unknown. May be collapsed.

**Vertical Shaft**: Utility passage through all levels. Dangerous, requires climbing, possibly blocked.

**Wait for Intruders**: Let them reach him, take their exit route. Risky—they may not be friendly.

**Find Another Way**: Vel'Thoras is bigger than anyone knows. Maybe there are passages in the Deep Core. Maybe Velanna left him an escape route. Maybe.

## The Questions

### Why 1200 Years?

The stasis timer was set for exactly 1200 years. Why?

- Arbitrary number?
- Calculated for vampire civilization to be forgotten?
- Waiting for specific conditions?
- Waiting for something to happen at The Scar?
- Connected to whatever Mordecai built in the Deep Core?

### What's in the Deep Core?

Mordecai excavated below Level 7. He didn't tell anyone why. The dimensional readings suggest something similar to The Scar—but controlled. Did he build a breach? A gateway? A trap?

### Why Was Seven Special?

He was put in long-term stasis for "behavioral irregularities"—empathy, emotion, attachment. But those are human traits, not vampire traits. Was that a mistake? Or was it the point?

### What Did Velanna Know?

She kept private records. She kept secrets. She created Seven and then put him to sleep for 1200 years. She created Eleven as the "improved" version. Was Seven the mistake? Or was Eleven?

### Is Someone Helping Seven?

The facility's security seems... incomplete. Gaps in the defenses. Passages that shouldn't be open. Almost like someone wants him to escape.

Who?

The intelligence controlling the facility?
Velanna's posthumous instructions?
Another survivor?

Or is it a trap?

## Sensory Experience

### Approaching the Site

The Thornwood Hills feel wrong. Trees grow twisted here. Animals avoid the center. Local farmers won't tend fields within five miles of the ruins. They say the ground hums at night. They're not wrong.

The surface ruins appear between the trees—broken walls, empty foundations, the skeleton of the East Tower. Scavengers picked it clean centuries ago. But something remains. You feel watched before you see the entrance.

### Upper Complex

Dust. Everything is covered in dust. Footprints from previous expeditions. Scrape marks where artifacts were dragged out. The air is stale—nothing has circulated in decades. Sounds echo wrong, like the space is larger than you can see. Cold, even in summer. Getting colder as you descend.

The guardians you see here are dead—stripped, studied, hollow. But you find one that isn't. Its eyes follow you. You're not sure if it's still active or just... watching.

### Middle Complex

The air changes. Cleaner. Circulated. The facility is still alive down here. Lights flicker—not dead, just inconsistent. You hear humming from the walls—power systems, still working. The guardians here are intact. Waiting.

The wards are invisible until you're almost in them. The expedition ahead of you lost someone here—his scorch mark is still on the floor. You step around it.

### Lower Complex

You won't reach here. Almost no one does. But if you did—

Power signatures everywhere. Something is breathing down here. Not literally—maybe—but the facility pulses like a heartbeat. The corridors are narrower, darker, more personal. This is Velanna's domain. Her work. Her secrets.

And somewhere, in the deepest chamber, someone is sleeping.

He's been sleeping for 1200 years.

He's about to wake up.
