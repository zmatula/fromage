# Vel'Thoras

The Mind Covenant's premier research complex—and the location of the protagonist's stasis chamber.

---

## Overview

**Type**: Major research facility
**Location**: Eastern Valdrian territory, 50 miles from The Scar
**Covenant**: Mind (Covenant of the Eternal Mind)
**Built**: Year -600 to -400
**Original Purpose**: Advanced research, Sanguine Vessels Initiative
**Current Status**: Outer complex explored; inner sanctum sealed

---

## History

### Vampire Era

**Year -600**: Construction began
**Year -400**: Primary research hub established
**Year -50**: Sanguine Vessels Initiative started here
**Year -12**: Protagonist created in Vessel Lab 7
**Year -7**: Protagonist placed in stasis
**Year 0**: Facility sealed during collapse; automatic systems maintained stasis

### Post-Collapse

**Year 878**: Outer complex discovered
**Year 1112**: Deep expedition reached near inner sanctum
**Year 1172**: Inner sanctum located but not breached
**Year 1200**: Multiple factions planning breach attempts

## The Stasis Chamber

### Location
- Level 7, Lower Complex
- Lab designation: Vessel Lab 7
- Protected by multiple security systems
- Backup power still functioning

### The Protagonist's Status
- In stasis for 1,200 years
- Systems approaching maintenance limits
- Automatic timer nearing completion
- Could wake at any time
- Or be woken by intervention

- Other Vessel chambers (empty)
