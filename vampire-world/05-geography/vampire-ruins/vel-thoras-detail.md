# Vel'Thoras: Facility Architecture

The layout of the Mind covenant research facility where Vessel Seven sleeps—level by level, danger by danger.

---

## Overview

Vel'Thoras was Mind covenant's secondary research facility, located 400 miles from Neth'Korath. It specialized in consciousness research, including the Sanguine Vessels project.

The facility was built into a mountain, with multiple levels descending into the earth. The upper levels housed administration and general research. The deep levels held sensitive projects—including Seven's stasis chamber.

---

## Why Vel'Thoras Survived

### The Paradox

Vel'Thoras sits approximately 50 miles from The Scar's edge—well within Zone 4, where dimensional instability should cause equipment failure and "radiation-like sickness." Yet Seven's stasis chamber has functioned for 1,200 years. This demands explanation.

### The Answer: Mordecai's Foresight

Vel'Thoras was built to survive.

**What Mind Covenant Knew**: Between Year -61 and -32, Mind researchers detected dimensional pollution at critical levels. They knew something catastrophic was coming—they just couldn't prevent it. Vel'Thoras was specifically designed as a contingency facility.

**The Shielding**:
- **Depth**: 500+ feet underground; the mountain provides mass shielding against dimensional effects
- **Wards**: Dimensional stabilization wards designed by Mordecai himself, the foremost mind of the covenant
- **Independence**: Self-contained power systems that don't rely on the Bloodweb
- **Active Protection**: The Mind Codex (Mordecai's consciousness) may be actively maintaining the facility's defenses

### Why It's Failing Now

Even Mordecai's preparations have limits. After 1,200 years:
- Power reserves are finally depleting
- Wards are degrading faster than they can self-repair
- The Scar's expansion is increasing ambient dimensional stress
- Level Four's stasis systems are failing

Seven is awakening because the facility's protections have finally exceeded their design tolerances. This isn't activation—it's collapse.

### The Implication

If Vel'Thoras was designed to survive the Collapse, Mordecai knew what was coming. The facility wasn't just research—it was a lifeboat. What else did he prepare for? And why did he want the Vessels to survive when everything else burned?

## Surface Level: The Facade

**Original Function**: Reception, security screening, visitor housing
**Current Status**: Collapsed ruins, partially excavated

**What Explorers Find**:
- Broken reception hall with Mind covenant symbols
- Security checkpoint (inactive but recognizable)
- Collapsed residential quarters
- Entry to Level One (partially blocked)

**Dangers**:
- Structural instability (cave-ins)
- Wild animals nesting in ruins
- Occasional scavenger presence
- Ward remnants (minimal)

**Notable Features**:
- The "welcome chamber" still has intact frescoes showing Mind's achievements
- A directory of facility levels (damaged but partially readable)
- Visitor logs from Year -5 to Year 0 (researchers who never left)

## Level One: Administration

**Original Function**: Records, offices, general laboratories
**Current Status**: Damaged, partially explored

**Layout**:
- Central corridor running 200 meters
- Branching office wings (east and west)
- Record archive (north)
- General laboratory complex (south)
- Stairs to Level Two (three access points)

**What's Been Found**:
- Administrative records (Year -50 to 0)
- Research schedules (reveal project names but not details)
- Personal effects of researchers
- Empty supply rooms

**What Hasn't Been Found**:
- Any mention of "Sanguine Vessels" (that name wasn't used above Level Three)
- Key cards for deeper levels
- Velanna's personal office (it's on Level Three)

**Dangers**:
- Collapsing floors
- Ward remnants (detect intruders, trigger alarms that no longer work)
- Locked doors that resist opening
- Poison gas traps (degraded but occasionally functional)

**Exploration History**:
- Year 312: First official expedition reached this level
- Year 720: Expedition killed by ward activation (7 dead)
- Year 1145: Inheritors mapped most of this level

## Level Two: General Research

**Original Function**: Standard laboratories, storage, staff quarters
**Current Status**: Intact but dangerous

**Layout**:
- Laboratory ring (12 major labs around perimeter)
- Central storage hub
- Staff living quarters (could house 200)
- Medical bay
- Stairs to Levels One and Three

**What's There**:
- Research equipment (most non-functional)
- Experiment records (various projects)
- Personal journals of researchers
- Blood storage (degraded, some crystallized)

**Key Rooms**:

**Laboratory Seven** (Consciousness Transfer Research):
Contains notes on "project transition"—early work that led to the Vessels. Not the actual Vessel research (that's deeper) but the theoretical foundation.

**The Medical Bay**:
Still has equipment. Some ambient-powered devices still work—but the healing artifacts here might be drawing power from The Scar. Using them could be dangerous.

**Staff Quarters, Room 17**:
Dorian val'Kess's quarters. His personal effects might reveal his role in Protocol Nine and where he took Eleven.

**Dangers**:
- Active wards (this level was protected)
- Automated defense systems (some still function)
- Biological hazards (old experiments that didn't die)
- Psychological effects (some rooms cause fear or disorientation)

**Exploration History**:
- Year 1189: Inheritors reached this level
- Year 1195: Two Inheritors killed by ward activation
- Year 1198: Partial mapping completed

## Level Three: Sensitive Research

**Original Function**: Classified projects, senior researcher offices
**Current Status**: Sealed, unexplored

**Layout** (Theoretical—Based on Records):
- Central security checkpoint
- Senior researcher offices (including Velanna's)
- Classified laboratories
- Access to Level Four (heavily secured)
- Alternative exit (Protocol Nine route?)

**What's Expected**:
- Velanna's personal office and notes
- Detailed Vessel project documentation
- The "rejected Vessels" (1-6, 8-10)—or their remains
- Security controls for lower levels

**Velanna's Office**:
This is where the real records are. Her official notes are sanitized; her personal files would show:
- Why Seven was different
- What Eleven was for
- The actual purpose of the Vessel project
- What she knew about the Collapse

**The Secondary Exit**:
Protocol Nine referenced a "secondary facility." There should be an evacuation route from this level—the path Dorian took with Eleven.

**Dangers**:
- Unknown—no one has reached this level
- Expected: Maximum security wards, lethal traps, possibly active guardians
- The door to this level has resisted all opening attempts

**Why It's Sealed**:
The Level Three door isn't just locked—it's warded specifically against opening. Someone sealed it deliberately. From inside or outside?

## Level Four: Stasis Complex

**Original Function**: Sanguine Vessels stasis bay, critical life support
**Current Status**: Functional (Seven is still alive)

**Layout** (Theoretical):
- Stasis chamber ring (designed for 12 Vessels)
- Life support control center
- Monitoring station
- Emergency power systems
- No exit to Level Five (Level Four is the deepest—officially)

**The Stasis Chambers**:
Seven is in one of twelve chambers. The others contain:
- Empty chambers (Vessels 1-6, 8-10 died in testing)
- Chamber Eleven (empty—Eleven was evacuated)
- Seven's chamber (occupied)

**Life Support Status**:
After 1,200 years, the systems must be failing:
- Power reserves depleted
- Blood supply exhausted (Seven hasn't fed in 1,200 years)
- Stasis field degrading

Seven is awakening because the stasis is failing, not because someone activated him.

**The Monitoring Station**:
Designed to watch all twelve Vessels. Contains:
- Individual status readouts
- Alert systems
- Override controls
- Records of what happened to each Vessel

**Dangers**:
- Unknown—depends on what security remains
- Seven himself (he'll wake hungry)
- Possible: Other things kept in stasis that shouldn't wake up

## Level Five: The Secret Level

**Original Function**: Unknown
**Current Status**: Unknown
**Existence**: Unconfirmed

**Evidence It Exists**:
- Architectural analysis suggests space below Level Four
- Power consumption records show usage below the stasis level
- One reference in damaged records: "sublevel research, restricted"
- The Protocol Nine "secondary facility" might be here

**Theories**:

**Eleven's Destination**:
Dorian took Eleven down, not out. Level Five could be where Eleven has been for 1,200 years.

**True Vessel Purpose**:
Level Four created Vessels. Level Five might house what Vessels were created for—the actual experiment they were subjects of.

**Mordecai's Contingency**:
Mind Codex is one backup. Level Five might be another—a complete research archive or even another consciousness vessel.

**The Failure**:
Whatever went wrong with Vessels 1-6 and 8-10 happened here. Their bodies might still be here.

## The Protocol Nine Route

### Reconstruction

Based on records and architecture:

**Starting Point**: Level Three, Velanna's office area
**Exit**: Unknown (possibly Level Five, possibly external)

**Probable Route**:
1. Level Three secondary corridor (hidden from main traffic)
2. Emergency stairwell (separate from main stairs)
3. Through or past Level Four (Eleven wouldn't go through stasis bay)
4. To Level Five or external exit

**Key Question**: Did Dorian take Eleven down to Level Five, or out to an external evacuation point?

If down: Eleven is still in Vel'Thoras, below Seven.
If out: Eleven could be anywhere.

## When Seven Awakens

### The Immediate Situation

Seven wakes in Level Four with:
- No memory of where he is
- Extreme hunger (1,200 years without feeding)
- Degraded stasis making him weak
- No way out (unless doors are open from above)

### What He Finds

**In his chamber**: Status readouts showing 1,200 years elapsed
**In adjacent chambers**: Empty pods, dead Vessels
**In monitoring station**: Records of what happened to everyone else

### His Options

**Wait**: Someone is coming (the expedition). But he's starving.
**Go up**: Try to reach the surface. But he doesn't know what's above.
**Go down**: If Level Five exists, it might have answers—or food.

### What Expeditions Find

If they reach Level Four before he wakes:
- They find him in stasis
- They can study him, contain him, or try to communicate
- They control the situation

If they reach Level Four after he wakes:
- They find an empty chamber or a hungry vampire
- He might attack, flee, or try to communicate
- He controls the situation
