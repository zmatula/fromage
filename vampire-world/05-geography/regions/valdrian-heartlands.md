# Valdrian Heartlands

The fertile eastern plains and river valleys that form the core of the Valdrian Empire.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Eastern continent
**Size**: ~300,000 square miles
**Climate**: Temperate four seasons
**Controlling Power**: Valdrian Empire
**Population**: ~3.5 million

---

## Geography

### Terrain
- River valleys and plains
- Rolling hills
- Managed forests
- Rich farmland

### Major Features
- **Valdris River**: 800-mile lifeline
- **Goldwater tributary**: Trade route south
- **Valdrian Woods**: Ancient managed forest
- **Eastern Hills**: Low, cultivated

### Climate
- Warm summers, cold winters
- Regular rainfall
- Long growing season (6-7 months)
- Occasional droughts

---

## Major Settlements

**Valdris Prime** (capital, ~300,000)
- Imperial seat
- Largest artifact collection
- University center
- Trade hub

**Goldport** (~150,000)
- Primary artifact market
- River-sea junction
- Wealthy merchants
- Inheritor presence

**Oldtown** (~70,000)
- Ancient settlement
- University city
- Historical archives
- Scholar concentration
