# Northern Highlands

The harsh mountains and forests of the Northern Dominion—martial lands rich in minerals and vampire military ruins.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Northern continent
**Size**: ~350,000 square miles
**Climate**: Continental, harsh winters
**Controlling Power**: Northern Dominion
**Population**: ~3 million

---

## Geography

### Terrain
- Mountain valleys
- Dense coniferous forests
- Fast rivers
- Limited farmland

### Major Features
- **Northern Spine**: Mountain border with Valdris
- **Northern Pines**: Vast timber forest
- **Northern Rush River**: Timber transport
- **Vel'Krath plateau**: Former vampire military hub

### Climate
- Long harsh winters
- Short intense summers
- Heavy snowfall
- 4-5 month growing season

---

## Major Settlements

**Ironhold** (capital, ~200,000)
- Fortress-city
- Military headquarters
- War Council seat
- Mountain protected

**Vel'Krath Reach** (~80,000)
- Built on vampire ruins
- Military academy
- Artifact research
- Expedition base

**Northgate** (~120,000)
- Trade with dwarves
- Mining center
- Merchant guilds
- Mixed population
