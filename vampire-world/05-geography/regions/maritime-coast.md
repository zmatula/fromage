# Maritime Coast

The southern coast and island archipelago—commercial heart, cosmopolitan culture, Inheritor stronghold.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Southern coast and islands
**Size**: ~200,000 square miles (including sea)
**Climate**: Mediterranean
**Controlling Power**: Maritime Republic
**Population**: ~3 million

---

## Geography

### Terrain
- Coastal plains
- Island chains
- Natural harbors
- Limited hinterland

### Major Features
- **Sunken Coast**: Former plain, now islands
- **Sovereign Strait**: Trade chokepoint
- **Coastal Range**: Low protecting mountains
- **Island Jewel**: Luxury resort island

### Climate
- Hot dry summers
- Mild wet winters
- Seasonal rainfall
- Year-round growing

---

## Major Settlements

**Port Sovereign** (capital, ~350,000)
- Greatest port
- Trade Council seat
- Most diverse city
- Artifact markets

**Scholar's Haven** (~120,000)
- University center
- Research facilities
- Inheritor base
- Free inquiry

**Merchant's Rest** (~100,000)
- Banking center
- Wealthy families
- Finance hub
- Insurance guilds
