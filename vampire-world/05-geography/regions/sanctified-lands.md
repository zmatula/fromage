# Sanctified Lands

The warm central plateau ruled by the theocratic Sanctified States—most populous, most hostile to vampires.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Central-south continent
**Size**: ~500,000 square miles
**Climate**: Warm, mild
**Controlling Power**: Sanctified States
**Population**: ~5 million

---

## Geography

### Terrain
- Rolling plateau
- River valleys
- Extensive farmland
- Few mountains

### Major Features
- **Central Plateau**: Breadbasket
- **Pilgrim's Way**: Sacred road network
- **Sun Valley**: Religious heartland
- **Southern Hills**: Border region

### Climate
- Mild winters
- Warm summers
- Adequate rainfall
- 8-9 month growing season

---

## Major Settlements

**Solarium** (capital, ~400,000)
- Church headquarters
- Holiest city
- Theocrat's seat
- Pilgrimage center

**Dawnhaven** (~180,000)
- Order of Dawn headquarters
- Fortress-monasteries
- Military training
- Inquisition base

**Grainfield** (~100,000)
- Agricultural hub
- Food distribution
- Storage centers
- Trade point

---

## Vampire Era

### Former Use
- Peripheral territory
- Food production
- Servant populations
- Minor installations

### Ruins Present
- Fewer than other regions
- Minor sites only
- Some sealed by Church
- "Purified" or destroyed

### Current State
- Church controls all access
- Most ruins declared cursed
- Order of Dawn guards sites
- Scholarship suppressed

---

## Strategic Value

- Largest food production
- Largest population
- Religious authority
- Order of Dawn base
- Central location

---

## Story Relevance

- Most dangerous for protagonist
- Order of Dawn hunts from here
- Must avoid or cross disguised
- Religious crisis potential
- Persecution increasing
