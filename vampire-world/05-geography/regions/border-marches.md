# The Border Marches

Contested zones between major powers—flashpoints for conflict and hidden opportunities.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Various borders
**Size**: ~100,000 square miles combined
**Climate**: Various
**Controlling Power**: Disputed
**Population**: ~1 million

---

## Major Border Zones

### Valdrian-Northern March
- Mountain passes
- Former vampire territories
- Ruin access disputes
- Heavily militarized
- War flashpoint

### Silverwood Borders
- Forest edges
- Treaty lines from Twilight War
- Human resentment
- Elven enforcement
- Logging pressure

### Goblin Frontier
- Multiple powers border
- Raiding zones
- No clear lines
- Constant skirmishing
- Buffer territories

### Maritime Hinterland
- Between Maritime and Sanctified
- Trade route control
- Religious vs commercial
- Refugee flows
- Tension point

---

## Characteristics

### Settlements
- Fortress towns
- Merchant posts
- Mixed populations
- Frontier culture
- Rough justice

### Law
- Overlapping claims
- Military rule common
- Smuggling prevalent
- Refugees concentrate
- Opportunity for outlaws

### Economy
- Trade (legal and illegal)
- Military supply
- Ruin exploration
- Scavenging
- Mercenary work
