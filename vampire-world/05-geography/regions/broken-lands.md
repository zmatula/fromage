# The Broken Lands

Marginal territories of goblin tribes—scattered wastelands hiding underground networks and blood magic secrets.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Scattered marginal areas
**Size**: ~150,000 square miles (nominal)
**Climate**: Various (mostly harsh)
**Controlling Power**: Broken Lands Confederation
**Population**: ~800,000

---

## Geography

### Terrain
- Badlands and wastes
- Swamps and marshes
- Caves and tunnels
- Marginal surface land

### Major Features
- **The Wastes**: Badland territories
- **Deep Warrens**: Underground networks
- **Raid Routes**: Secret paths to human lands
- **Sacred Sites**: Shaman locations

### Climate
- Varies by location
- Generally harsh
- Poor agriculture
- Underground more stable

---

## Major Settlements

**None permanent**
- Mobile camps
- Underground warrens
- Hidden from humans
- Constantly moving

---

## Vampire Era

### Former Use
- Mining operations
- Labor camps
- Experimental subjects
- Disposal areas

### Ruins Present
- Many minor sites
- Some unknown to others
- Underground connections
- Blood magic sources

### Current State
- Goblins control access
- Some sites secret
- Blood magic tradition preserved
- Strategic value hidden

---

## Strategic Value

- Hidden ruins
- Blood magic knowledge
- Underground networks
- Desperation creates options
- No rules

---

## Story Relevance

- Blood magic connection
- Possible desperate allies
- Dangerous but understanding
- Underground movement
- Revenge vs cooperation
