# Ironpeak Mountains

The central mountain range of dwarven holds—neutral power, deep secrets, and ancient vampire connections.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Central continent
**Size**: ~100,000 square miles (surface; underground unknown)
**Climate**: Alpine
**Controlling Power**: Ironpeak Confederation
**Population**: ~1.5 million

---

## Geography

### Terrain
- High mountain peaks
- Deep valleys
- Underground holds
- Limited surface agriculture

### Major Features
- **Ironpeak** (highest): 16,000 feet
- **Iron Gate Pass**: Main crossing
- **Deep Holds**: Underground cities
- **Ironflow source**: Major river origin

### Climate
- Harsh at elevation
- Milder in valleys
- Heavy precipitation
- Short growing season

---

## Major Settlements

**Ironpeak Hold** (administrative, ~150,000)
- Confederation center
- Diplomatic point
- Foreign access
- Trade gateway

**Deepforge** (~120,000)
- Industrial heart
- Weapons production
- Mining center
- Technical expertise

**Goldvault** (~100,000)
- Banking center
- Treasure storage
- Financial control
- Record keeping
