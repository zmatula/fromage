# The Scar

The 50-mile radius wasteland where the Ascension Experiment breached dimensions—permanently uninhabitable and dimensionally unstable.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Central-north (former Neth'Korath location)
**Size**: ~8,000 square miles
**Climate**: None (uninhabitable)
**Controlling Power**: None
**Population**: 0

---

## Geography

### Terrain
- Blasted wasteland
- Glass-like surface in center
- Dead zones radiating out
- Reality distortions

### Major Features
- **Ground Zero**: Where experiment occurred
- **Glass Plain**: Fused earth from heat
- **Dead Zone**: Nothing grows, 30-mile radius
- **Distortion Ring**: Dimensional instability, outer edge

### Climate
- Unpredictable
- Sudden temperature shifts
- Strange weather
- Radiation-like effects

---

## Current State

### What Happened
- Site of Ascension Experiment
- Dimensional breach opened
- Bloodweb inversion started here
- ~100,000 vampires died instantly

### Present Condition
- Nothing lives
- Dimensional readings increasing since Year 1178
- Occasional visual distortions
- Strange sounds reported
- Avoided by all

### What's There
- Former Neth'Korath capital ruins (destroyed)
- Unknown deep facilities
- Dimensional weak points
- The breach (sealed? dormant?)

---

## Danger Level

**Extreme**
- Immediate death in center
- Sickness in inner zones
- Disorientation in outer zones
- Reality may be unstable
- Something may be stirring

## Strategic Value
