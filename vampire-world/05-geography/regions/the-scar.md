# The Scar

The 50-mile radius wasteland where the Ascension Experiment breached dimensions—permanently uninhabitable, dimensionally unstable, and possibly waking up.

---

## Overview

**Location**: Central-north continent, former site of Neth'Korath
**Size**: ~8,000 square miles (50-mile radius from Ground Zero)
**Climate**: Non-existent (environmental laws don't apply consistently)
**Controlling Power**: None—unclaimed, unclaimable
**Population**: 0 (officially); unknown (actually)

---

## Geography

### Terrain Zones

**Zone 1: Ground Zero** (0-5 miles)
- Instant death to any living creature
- Air itself is wrong—doesn't fill lungs properly
- Glass-like surface fused at temperatures that shouldn't exist
- Visible reality distortions: objects appear in multiple places simultaneously
- The ruins of Neth'Korath's central spire—still standing, somehow
- No one has reached it since Year 0 and returned

**Zone 2: The Glass Plain** (5-15 miles)
- Fused earth, crystallized in patterns that suggest screaming faces
- Temperature fluctuates 100 degrees within minutes
- Sound travels wrong—echoes arrive before words
- Some structures still standing, filled with something that moves
- Death within hours without protection
- Three expeditions have reached this zone; one returned

**Zone 3: The Dead Zone** (15-30 miles)
- Nothing grows, nothing lives, nothing decays
- Bodies from Year 0 still lie where they fell—preserved perfectly
- Radiation-like sickness within days
- Compass needles spin or point toward center
- Equipment fails unpredictably
- Most expeditions stop here

**Zone 4: The Distortion Ring** (30-50 miles)
- Outer edge, "safest" area
- Dimensional instability causes reality slips
- Travelers sometimes see Year 0 events replaying
- Weather comes from nowhere
- Wildlife behaves strangely—predators won't enter, prey walks in
- Settlements exist just outside, full of strange people

## The Phenomena

### Documented Effects

**The Whispers**
First reported: Year 89
Every expedition reports hearing whispers in languages they don't know but almost understand. The whispers increase closer to the center. Scholar Merik Tann (Year 445) transcribed some phonetically—they matched no known language, but three Inheritor linguists who studied his notes went mad within a year.

**The Figures**
First reported: Year 234
Shapes seen in the Glass Plain. Humanoid but wrong. They don't move when watched directly but are always closer when you look away. Expedition Commander Voss Krell (Year 1145) swore one spoke to him. He refused to say what it said. He killed himself six days after returning.

**The Replay**
First reported: Year 312
Sometimes the Collapse happens again, visually. Observers in the Distortion Ring see the sky tear open, see the light, see the vampires fall. It lasts seconds or hours. It's always silent. Scholar Denna Marsh theorized it's not a replay but a loop—the Collapse is still happening, always happening, time folded back on itself.

**The Pull**
First reported: Year 567
Some people feel compelled to walk toward the center. Not many. Not predictably. But every expedition loses someone to it. They just start walking. They don't respond. They don't stop. They don't come back.

**The Growth**
First reported: Year 1178
The Scar is getting larger. Slowly—a few hundred yards per year—but measurably. The Distortion Ring is expanding. Effects that were at the 40-mile mark are now at 45. Something is happening.

## History

### Day Zero

At the moment of the Ascension Experiment, Neth'Korath contained approximately 100,000 vampires—the highest concentration in the world. They were there to witness Mordecai's triumph.

They died in 0.3 seconds.

The dimensional breach didn't just kill them—it unmade them. The Bloodweb that connected all vampires carried the destruction outward like fire through dry grass. But the epicenter was here. Whatever happened, it burned a hole in reality itself.

### The Early Expeditions (Year 10-100)

**Year 23: The Valdris Survey**
First official expedition. Twenty soldiers, three scholars. Purpose: assess damage, map territory, determine if salvage possible.
Result: Four returned. They reported the Glass Plain, the preserved bodies, the whispers.
Outcome: Emperor Marcus II declared The Scar forbidden territory.

**Year 67: The Church Purification**
High Priest Aldric led 200 faithful to "cleanse the wound in God's creation."
Result: None returned. Observers on the edge reported seeing them walk into the Distortion Ring. They didn't stop. They didn't turn back.
Outcome: Church declared The Scar "beyond purification" and discouraged further attempts.

**Year 89: The Inheritor Probe**
First secret society expedition. Five members, best equipment available.
Result: Two returned, one sane. Brought back samples of glass, soil, and a small object they refused to discuss.
Outcome: Inheritors began long-term monitoring. Whatever they found, they didn't share.

### The Middle Period (Year 100-800)

Expeditions became less frequent as the immediate post-Collapse chaos settled. The Scar was avoided, feared, and slowly forgotten by common people. But scholars and secret societies never stopped watching.

**Year 234: The Veil Keeper Mission**
Purpose: Unknown. They sent seven members.
Result: Three returned. They brought back extensive observations about the Figures and began their long project of documenting Scar phenomena.
Outcome: The Veil Keepers established permanent observation posts on the Distortion Ring's edge.

**Year 445: The Tann Expedition**
Scholar Merik Tann, obsessed with the whispers, mounted a private expedition with twelve volunteers.
Result: Tann returned alone. He'd gotten within 15 miles of center—further than anyone since Year 0. His transcriptions of the whispers caused three Inheritor scholars to lose their minds.
Outcome: Tann's notes were seized by the Church and never returned. Tann himself disappeared in Year 447.

**Year 567: The Northern Reconnaissance**
Northern Kingdom sent military scouts to assess The Scar as potential defensive position.
Result: Lost first expedition entirely. Second expedition reported the Pull for the first time—two scouts walked toward center and didn't stop.
Outcome: Northern Kingdom abandoned military interest. The Scar was useless for defense because it killed everyone.

### The Modern Period (Year 800-1200)

**Year 812: Correlation Noted**
The same year as the Sel'Naroth discovery, instruments at Scar observation posts detected unusual readings. No one made the connection publicly. Some did privately.

**Year 1047: The Academy Study**
Valdrian Academy of Natural Philosophy sent a carefully planned, well-equipped expedition to gather samples and take measurements.
Result: Expedition reached Zone 3, gathered samples, took readings, returned with minimal casualties (three dead, two mad).
Outcome: First systematic data about The Scar. Showed that dimensional instability was real, measurable, and not decreasing.

**Year 1145: The Krell Expedition**
Commander Voss Krell, veteran explorer, led the deepest successful penetration—reached the outer edge of Zone 2.
Result: Saw the Figures. One spoke to him. He returned, refused to discuss it, killed himself six days later.
Outcome: His expedition notes were purchased by the Inheritors. Content unknown.

**Year 1178: Growth Discovered**
Veil Keeper observation posts noted that their instruments were measuring phenomena further from center than before. The Scar was expanding.
Outcome: Information shared with Valdrian Academy. Kept from public. Monitoring intensified.

**Year 1195: The Incident**
Something emerged from the Distortion Ring near the village of Keth's Hollow.
Result: Village abandoned. Thirty-seven people missing. No bodies found.
Outcome: Covered up as "plague evacuation." But the witnesses talked. Rumors spread.

## The Theories

### Official Position (Church of Divine Light)

The Scar is divine punishment for vampire hubris. It will heal when God wills it. Studying it is blasphemy. The whispers are demons. The Figures are damned souls. The expansion is a warning to the sinful.

**Problems**: Doesn't explain the measurable, predictable nature of many phenomena. Doesn't explain why "divine punishment" is getting worse 1200 years later.

### Academic Theory (Valdrian Academy)

The Ascension Experiment tore the barrier between dimensions. The Scar is a wound that hasn't healed. The phenomena are dimensional bleed-through. The whispers and Figures are entities from elsewhere, pressing against weak points.

**Problems**: If it's just a wound, why is it growing? Wounds heal or stabilize. This is spreading.

### Inheritor Hypothesis (Secret)

The experiment didn't fail. It succeeded in breaching dimensions. But something came through. It's been gestating in The Scar for 1200 years. The growth isn't spreading damage—it's the entity's expansion. The whispers aren't residual energy—they're communication attempts.

**Problems**: If true, what do you do about it? What's the entity? What does it want?

### Veil Keeper Analysis (Secret)

The Collapse wasn't caused by dimensional breach—it was caused by Bloodweb inversion. But the dimensional breach is real and separate. Two catastrophes happened simultaneously. The Scar is what's left of both. The inversion killed the vampires. The breach created... something else.

**Problems**: Implies the Collapse wasn't an accident but a cover for something worse.

### The Forbidden Theory

Spoken only in whispers: The Collapse was intentional. Someone wanted the dimensional breach. Someone wanted the vampires dead. Someone is feeding The Scar, helping it grow. Someone is waiting for something to come through.

Who?

No one says. No one knows. But The Scar wasn't supposed to still be growing after 1200 years. Something is wrong.

## What's Really There

### The Ruins of Neth'Korath

The vampire capital wasn't completely destroyed. The central spire still stands—visible from Zone 3 on clear days. It shouldn't be standing. Nothing should have survived Ground Zero. But it's there.

What's inside? No one has reached it since Year 0. But the Figures seem to come from that direction.

### The Bodies

They don't decay. They don't rot. They lie where they fell 1200 years ago, perfectly preserved. What does that mean? The scholars say it's dimensional stasis—time doesn't work properly in The Scar. But some of the bodies have moved between expeditions. Not much. Not obviously. But they're in different positions than they were.

### The Object

The Inheritors brought something back in Year 89. They've never said what it was. But they've never stopped monitoring The Scar. They've never stopped sending expeditions. They're looking for something. Or looking for more of something.

### The Breach

Is it closed? Dormant? Active? No one knows. Ground Zero is unreachable. Whatever's there is still there. Maybe it's sealed. Maybe it's waiting. Maybe it's already open and has been for 1200 years, just too small to notice.

Until now.

## Current Situation (Year 1200)

### The Expansion

The Scar grew more in Year 1199 than in any previous year. The rate is accelerating. If it continues, Keth's Hollow—the abandoned village—will be inside the Distortion Ring within five years. Other settlements within twenty.

### The Readings

Inheritor instruments show dimensional instability at highest recorded levels. Valdrian Academy confirms. Something is happening. Something is changing.

### The Incident

Thirty-seven people vanished from Keth's Hollow. No bodies. No blood. No signs of struggle. They just... weren't there anymore. The official story is plague, but the Church inspector who investigated the scene burned all his notes and retired to a monastery. He won't discuss what he saw.

### The Preparations

Multiple factions are preparing Scar expeditions for Year 1200:

**Valdrian Imperial Expedition**: Official, well-funded, army escort. Goal: reach Ground Zero, assess threat, find solution.

**Inheritor Deep Team**: Secret, experts only. Goal: reach the central spire, retrieve "key artifacts," understand what's awakening.

**Order of Dawn Strike Force**: Secret, fanatics. Goal: reach center, detonate consecrated explosives, destroy whatever's there.

**Veil Keeper Observation Team**: Semi-secret, documentation focus. Goal: record everything, prevent others from covering up what they find.

**Northern Kingdom Scout Team**: Military, deniable. Goal: assess if The Scar can be weaponized.

### The Timing

Everyone is moving at once. Why? Because of the readings. Because of the growth. Because of Keth's Hollow. Because something is happening and no one knows what.

And because of Vel'Thoras.

The Inheritors believe that when Vessel Seven awakens—when any Vessel awakens—it will trigger a response in The Scar. The Vessels were created with Bloodweb energy. The Scar was created by Bloodweb destruction. They're connected.

If Seven wakes, The Scar might wake too.

## The Observers

### The Settlers

Just outside the Distortion Ring, small communities have formed. People who want to be left alone. Smugglers. Cultists. Researchers. The mad and the obsessed. They live close to something that should be avoided, and they've been changed by it.

**Keth's Crossing**: Trading post, information hub. Run by a woman named Vera Ashblood who's been there forty years and isn't aging.

**The Watcher Camp**: Veil Keeper observation post. Officially abandoned, actually staffed. They've been documenting everything since Year 234.

**The Penitent Circle**: Religious community that believes praying at The Scar's edge will heal it. They've lost members to the Pull. They keep praying.

**The Scavengers**: Groups that go into Zone 4 looking for artifacts, bodies, anything valuable. High mortality rate. High profits if you survive.

### The Watchers

Every faction has eyes on The Scar. It's too dangerous to ignore and too important to forget. The dimensional breach that killed the vampires might be the key to understanding—or preventing—future catastrophes.

Or causing them.

---

## Connection to Seven's Awakening

### The Resonance Theory

The Inheritors believe the Sanguine Vessels were created using energy drawn from dimensional sources—the same sources that were breached in the Collapse. If Seven awakens, that energy becomes active again. The Scar might respond.

### The Timeline Problem

Seven's stasis was set for 1200 years. The Scar is growing fastest now, at 1200 years. Coincidence? Or was something supposed to happen when that timer ran out?

### The Worst Case

If Seven awakens and The Scar responds—if the dimensional breach opens fully—what comes through? The whispers have been trying to communicate for 1200 years. The Figures have been watching. Something is on the other side.

And it's been waiting.

## Sensory Experience

### Approaching the Distortion Ring

The world feels wrong before you see anything change. The light is too sharp or too flat. Your shadow doesn't move right. Animals go quiet, then loud, then quiet. You smell ozone and rot and something sweet underneath. Your skin itches like something is crawling under it.

Then you see the shimmer at the boundary—the air bending like heat haze. You hear the whispers start, just at the edge of comprehension. You feel the Pull, just a little, just wondering what's in there.

Most people stop. Most people turn back.

Some people keep walking.

### Inside Zone 4

Time slips. You lose minutes, gain hours. The sun is in the wrong place. Your instruments give readings that contradict each other. You hear people talking who aren't there—sometimes yourself, sometimes people you know, sometimes no one you recognize.

The bodies start appearing around mile 35. Vampires, frozen where they fell, perfect after 1200 years. They look like they're sleeping. They look like they might wake up.

### The Glass Plain

You won't reach it. Almost no one does. But if you did—

The ground reflects the sky but wrong. The patterns look like faces, bodies, scenes from the Collapse frozen in crystal. The air tastes like copper and burning. The Figures are there, at the edge of vision, always watching, always closer when you look away.

The whispers are clear here. You can almost understand them. You want to understand them.

That's when most people die.
