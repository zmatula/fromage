# World Map Description

The continental layout, climate zones, and geographical features that shape politics, travel, and the distribution of vampire remnants.

---

## Continental Overview

### The Known World

**Name**: Commonly called "the Realm" or "the Known Lands"
**Size**: Approximately 2,000 miles east-west, 1,500 miles north-south
**Shape**: Roughly rectangular main continent with island chains
**Population**: ~45 million sentient beings

### Major Geographical Divisions

1. **The Northern Highlands**: Mountains and cold forests (Northern Dominion)
2. **The Eastern Heartlands**: Plains and river valleys (Valdrian Empire)
3. **The Central Plateau**: Rolling hills and farmland (Sanctified States)
4. **The Western Forests**: Ancient woodlands (Silverwood Domain)
5. **The Southern Coast**: Mediterranean climate, islands (Maritime Republic)
6. **The Central Mountains**: Ironpeak range (Ironpeak Confederation)
7. **The Broken Wastes**: Marginal lands, badlands (Broken Lands)

---

## Climate Zones

### Cold North
- **Location**: Northern Dominion
- **Climate**: Continental, harsh winters, short summers
- **Precipitation**: Moderate, heavy snow
- **Growing Season**: 4-5 months

### Temperate East
- **Location**: Valdrian Empire
- **Climate**: Temperate four seasons
- **Precipitation**: Regular rainfall
- **Growing Season**: 6-7 months

### Warm Central
- **Location**: Sanctified States
- **Climate**: Mild winters, warm summers
- **Precipitation**: Adequate
- **Growing Season**: 8-9 months

### Mediterranean South
- **Location**: Maritime Republic
- **Climate**: Hot dry summers, mild wet winters
- **Precipitation**: Seasonal
- **Growing Season**: Year-round possible

### Highland Mountains
- **Location**: Ironpeak range
- **Climate**: Alpine, varied by elevation
- **Precipitation**: High (snow and rain)
- **Growing Season**: Limited

### Temperate West
- **Location**: Silverwood Domain
- **Climate**: Mild, high moisture
- **Precipitation**: Frequent rain
- **Growing Season**: 7-8 months

## Major Geographical Features

### Mountain Ranges

**The Ironpeak Range** (Central)
- Divides continent roughly in half
- Highest peaks: 15,000+ feet
- Contains all dwarven holds
- Key passes control trade
- Rich in minerals

**The Northern Spine** (North)
- Forms Valdrian-Northern border
- Moderate height: 8,000-10,000 feet
- Several contested passes
- Former vampire border fortresses

**The Coastal Range** (South)
- Protects Maritime hinterland
- Low: 4,000-6,000 feet
- Easy passes
- Contains minor ruins

### Rivers

**The Valdris** (East)
- Largest river, 800 miles
- Flows through Valdrian heartland
- Major trade artery
- Capital built on it

**The Ironflow** (Central)
- Flows from Ironpeak mountains
- 500 miles
- Industrial importance
- Dwarven trade route

**The Goldwater** (Southeast)
- Connects Valdris to coast
- 400 miles
- Trade with Maritime

**The Northern Rush** (North)
- Fast, dangerous
- 600 miles
- Timber transport
- Seasonal navigation

**The Silver Stream** (West)
- Marks Silverwood border
- 300 miles
- Uncrossable without elven permission

### Forests

**The Silverwood** (West)
- Ancient, dense, vast
- Estimated 50,000 square miles
- Elven territory
- Impenetrable to outsiders

**The Northern Pines** (North)
- Coniferous forest
- Major timber source
- Contains several ruins
- Frontier feel

**The Valdrian Woods** (East)
- Managed forests
- Oak and beech
- Some ancient sections
- Minor ruins

### Notable Terrain

**The Scar** (Central-North)
- Site of Ascension Experiment
- 50-mile radius wasteland
- Dimensional instability
- Nothing grows
- Avoided by all

**The Broken Wastes** (Various)
- Badlands, swamps, caves
- Goblin territories
- Marginal land
- Underground networks

**The Sunken Coast** (South)
- Former coastal plain
- Subsided during collapse
- Island archipelago now
- Maritime territory

## Political Boundaries

### Empire Territories

**Valdrian Empire** (East)
- Eastern plains and river valleys
- ~400,000 square miles
- Best farmland
- Most vampire ruins (accessible)

**Northern Dominion** (North)
- Northern highlands and forests
- ~350,000 square miles
- Rich in minerals
- Vel'Krath ruins

**Sanctified States** (Central-South)
- Central plateau
- ~500,000 square miles
- Largest population
- Few major ruins

**Maritime Republic** (Coast/Islands)
- Southern coast and islands
- ~200,000 square miles (including sea)
- Trade routes
- Coastal ruins

**Ironpeak Confederation** (Mountains)
- Central mountain range
- ~100,000 square miles
- Underground extent unknown
- Deep ruins

**Silverwood Domain** (West)
- Western forests
- ~50,000 square miles (claimed)
- True extent unknown
- Sel'Naroth sanctuary

**Broken Lands** (Marginal)
- Scattered territories
- ~150,000 square miles (nominally)
- Mostly underground
- Many hidden ruins

### Contested Zones

**Valdrian-Northern Border**
- Mountain passes
- Former vampire territories
- Ruin access disputes
- Militarized

**Silverwood Borders**
- Exact boundaries unclear
- Twilight War treaty lines
- Human resentment
- Elven enforcement

**Goblin Frontier**
- Multiple powers border
- Raiding zones
- No clear lines
- Ongoing conflict

## Racial Distribution

### Humans
- Dominant everywhere except Silverwood and Ironpeak
- 90%+ in Sanctified States
- 85% in Valdrian Empire
- 75% in Northern Dominion
- 60% in Maritime Republic

### Dwarves
- 95% in Ironpeak Confederation
- 15% in Northern Dominion
- 5% in other powers (merchants, craftsmen)

### Elves
- 100% in Silverwood Domain
- Rare elsewhere
- Some half-elves in Valdris
- Rumors of hidden communities

### Goblins
- 100% in Broken Lands
- None officially elsewhere
- Underground presence unknown

### Mixed Populations
- Port Sovereign most diverse
- Border towns varied
- Interior cities human-dominated

## Vampire-Era Geography

### Former Vampire Territories

**Throne Covenant** (North)
- Northern Dominion area
- Military installations
- Vel'Krath capital
- Surface structures demolished

**Mind Covenant** (East)
- Valdrian Empire area
- Research facilities
- Neth'Korath capital (destroyed)
- Vel'Thoras research complex

**Twilight Covenant** (West)
- Silverwood area
- Preserved sites
- Sel'Naroth sanctuary
- Underground refuges

### The Collapse Impact

**The Scar**: Dimensional breach, permanently uninhabitable
**Coastline**: Subsided 50-100 feet in south
**Mountains**: Some peaks collapsed
**Rivers**: Several changed course

### What Remains

**Above Ground**: Almost nothing intact
- Demolished by former servants
- Weathered over 1,200 years
- Foundations sometimes visible
- Occasionally mistaken for natural

**Below Ground**: Much intact
- Protected from weather
- Hidden from destroyers
- Still dangerous
- Increasingly discovered

## Strategic Locations

### Choke Points

**Iron Gate Pass**: Controls east-west trade

**Silverwood Crossing**: Only elven trade point (closed)

### Defensive Positions

**Ironhold**: Mountain fortress, nearly impregnable
**Valdris Prime**: River defenses
**Dawnhaven**: Order of Dawn fortress
**Silverwood**: Forest itself is defense

### Economic Centers

**Port Sovereign**: Trade hub
**Goldport**: Artifact market
**Ironpeak**: Manufacturing
**Grainfield**: Agriculture

## The Protagonist's Geography

### Starting Location

**Vel'Thoras**: Deep underground, Valdrian territory
- Near Valdrian-Northern border
- 50 miles from The Scar
- Research facility with stasis chambers
- Multiple factions approaching

### Initial Options

**East**: Into Valdrian heartland (most people)
**North**: Into Northern Dominion (martial)
**South**: Toward Maritime (cosmopolitan)
**West**: Toward Silverwood (closed)
**Mountains**: Into Ironpeak (neutral)

### Travel Considerations

- Must avoid Order of Dawn monasteries
- Veil Keeper networks in all cities
- Inheritors concentrated in Maritime
- Sanctified States most dangerous
- Silverwood closed but might open for him
- Ironpeak might shelter him
