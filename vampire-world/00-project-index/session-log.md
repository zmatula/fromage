### Decisions Made

- **Year System**: Year 0 = collapse, negative years = pre-collapse, positive = post-collapse
- **Power Scaling**: Protagonist is impressive but not peak vampire capability
- **Mystery Structure**: All five mysteries interconnect and support multiple interpretations
- **Theme Integration**: Themes explored through consequence, not preaching

### Key Connections Established

- Protagonist creation tied to collapse trigger
- Race origins question creates political stakes
- Historical unreliability creates conflict opportunities
- Power costs manifest at every level (personal to civilizational)
