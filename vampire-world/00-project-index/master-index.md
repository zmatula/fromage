## Quick Reference

**Core Premise**: Ancient vampire civilization collapsed 1,200 years ago. Present day is medieval tech level. Protagonist is an experimental vampire relic with enhanced but limited magic.

**Key Themes**: Price of power, unreliability of history, cycles of rise and fall, burden of legacy, knowledge vs wisdom.

**Style**: GRRM - moral complexity, political realism, historical ambiguity, consequences matter, no plot armor.
