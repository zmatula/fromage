# Master Audit Summary - GRRM-Style Revision Plan

## Overview

This document consolidates findings from the 8-dimension audit into prioritized implementation phases. Total findings: ~30 critical breaks, ~40 missing connections, ~50 enrichment opportunities.

---

## Priority Tiers

### Tier 1: Critical (Breaks Believability)
Issues that shatter suspension of disbelief and must be fixed before any narrative work.

### Tier 2: High (Missing Core Systems)
Connections that should exist but don't—economic pressures, cultural contamination, information flow.

### Tier 3: Medium (Depth Enhancement)
Enrichment opportunities that add texture and make the world feel lived-in.

### Tier 4: Polish (Atmospheric Details)
Fine-grained additions that create authenticity without affecting core systems.

## Cross-Cutting Concerns

### Consequence Chains
Every fix should ripple outward. When adding river tolls, show:
- Who controls them
- Who resents paying
- What happens if blocked
- How this creates political leverage

### Information Lag
Every new system should show timing:
- How long to spread
- What distortion occurs
- Who knows first vs last

### Economic Pressure
Every cultural/political element should trace to economics:
- What does it cost?
- Who pays?
- What leverage does it create?

## Success Criteria

The revision succeeds when:
- Every critical break is resolved
- Economic pressure is visible in every political decision
- Cultural contamination appears in character speech
- Information flows realistically with lag and distortion
- Secrets have visible costs to maintain
- Geography constrains all ambitions
- Consequences ripple through multiple dimensions

The revision fails when:
- Fixes exist in isolation
- Systems don't connect to each other
- Economic pressures are mentioned but not felt
- Characters sound interchangeable
- Information moves at plot-convenient speed
