# Fourth-Pass Audit Process Plan

## Overview

This audit performs final verification after three revision cycles. Focus areas:
1. Complete cascade verification (all numbers, names, references)
2. Narrative flow coherence (can story actually be told?)
3. Character voice distinctiveness (do characters sound different?)
4. Timeline feasibility (does everything fit in Year 1200?)
5. Remaining polish (minor inconsistencies, gaps, opportunities)

---

## Audit Focus Areas

### 1. Complete Cascade Verification
**File**: `audit-pass4-01-cascade-complete.md`

---

### 2. Narrative Flow Coherence
**File**: `audit-pass4-02-narrative-flow.md`

**Purpose**: Verify story can actually be told with documented constraints.

**Check**:
- Can protagonist physically complete journey given constraints?
- Do faction responses create pursuable story beats?
- Do character arcs intersect at documented points?
- Does information flow allow characters to make documented decisions?
- Do economic pressures create documented political actions?
- Does timeline allow all events before Year 1200 ends?

---

### 3. Character Voice Distinctiveness
**File**: `audit-pass4-03-character-voice.md`

**Purpose**: Verify characters sound distinctly different based on culture.

**Check**:
- Do speech patterns match cultural DNA?
- Are sample lines actually distinctive?
- Do knowledge gaps match character perspectives?
- Do fatal flaws manifest in documented behavior?
- Can you identify character by speech alone?
- Do NPCs have enough voice markers?

---

### 4. Timeline Feasibility
**File**: `audit-pass4-04-timeline-feasibility.md`

**Purpose**: Verify all Year 1200 events can actually occur.

**Check**:
- Succession crisis: Can it resolve in documented timeframe?
- Maritime coup: Is 10-month runway realistic?
- Northern invasion: Can they prepare and attack in year?
- Protagonist journey: Does travel + feeding + pursuit work?
- Silverwood response: Does Week 4-5 action fit story?
- Information spread: Do factions learn at documented rates?

---

### 5. Remaining Polish
**File**: `audit-pass4-05-polish.md`

**Purpose**: Find minor issues and enhancement opportunities.

**Check**:
- Any remaining terminology inconsistencies
- Missing cross-references between related files
- Underdeveloped areas that could use expansion
- Redundant content that could be consolidated
- Formatting and structural consistency
- Documentation gaps for writing reference

---

## Audit Execution Process

### Step 1: Systematic File Review
Read all major files systematically:
- All faction files (empires + secret societies)
- All character files (major + supporting)
- All plot architecture files
- Key cultural and geographic files

### Step 2: Cross-Reference Tracing
For each major element:
- Trace through all mentions
- Verify consistency
- Note discrepancies

### Step 3: Story Walkthrough
Mentally walk through protagonist's journey:
- Week by week
- Check constraints at each point
- Verify faction responses make sense
- Confirm character intersections work

### Step 4: Voice Sampling
For each major character:
- Read all speech samples
- Check cultural markers
- Verify distinctiveness

### Step 5: Issue Documentation
Categorize findings:
- Critical (breaks story)
- High (significant inconsistency)
- Medium (notable gap)
- Low (polish opportunity)
