## Fix 1.1: Knowledge Recovery Timeline

**Source**: audit-02-temporal.md
**File**: `02-timeline/master-timeline.md` or create `02-timeline/knowledge-recovery.md`

**Problem**: Knowledge recovers impossibly fast (Year 60: last reader dies → Year 278: successful translation).

**Implementation**:
Add entries showing gradual recovery:
- Year 60-120: Complete collapse; no systematic preservation
- Year 120-150: Cave monasteries preserve fragments (location later lost)
- Year 150-200: Dwarves reverse-engineer Bloodweb node (technical knowledge)
- Year 200-250: Multiple competing translation attempts, mostly wrong
- Year 250-280: First successful synthesis from multiple sources
- Year 280-350: Scholarly network forms; systematic compilation begins
- Year 350-400: Knowledge becomes teachable again

Add specific "Lost Centuries Mysteries":
- Who were the cave monks? (Debate continues)
- What knowledge did they actually preserve? (Fragments, not systems)
- Why did some schools succeed while others failed? (Method mattered)

## Fix 1.3: Helena Ashford Character Split

**Source**: audit-08-information.md
**File**: `04-factions/secret-societies/true-origins.md`

**Problem**: Helena occupies three roles simultaneously (investigator, descendant, unknowing).

**Implementation**:
Split into two characters:

**Helena Ashford** (keep):
- Veil Keeper archivist
- Descendant of Merik Ashford (murdered Year 789)
- Knows family history includes "suspicious scholar death"
- NOT the primary investigator of origins

**Maren Thorne** (new):
- Senior Veil Keeper investigating organization origins
- No family connection to founding murders
- Discovers evidence pointing to Ashford, Thornwood, Vane families
- Eventually approaches Helena with findings

- Maren investigates → finds Helena's family connection → approaches Helena
- Helena learns truth about ancestor → identity crisis → must choose response

## Fix 1.6: Bloodweb Efficiency Clarification

**Source**: audit-02-temporal.md
**File**: `02-timeline/master-timeline.md` or vampire technology documentation

**Problem**: Efficiency drops from 100% to 15% without matching mortality.

**Implementation**:
Clarify efficiency definition:

```markdown
## Bloodweb Efficiency Explained

Efficiency percentage refers to **network reliability**, not power output:
- 100% efficiency: Network functions continuously; no interruptions
- 70% efficiency: Outages occur weekly; backup systems engaged frequently
- 50% efficiency: Outages occur daily; some nodes permanently offline
- 30% efficiency: Network unstable; cascading failures common
- 15% efficiency: Network barely functional; imminent total collapse

### Why Population Didn't Crash Earlier
- Individual vampires can survive short outages (hours to days)
- Wealthy vampires had personal reserves
- Rural vampires adapted to intermittent access
- True crisis only occurs with total network collapse (Year 0)

### The Collapse Moment
At 15% efficiency, any additional strain triggers cascade:
- Ascension Experiment drew massive power
- Remaining network couldn't compensate
- Total failure within hours
- 94% mortality from simultaneous starvation
```

## Fix 1.7: Living Artifact Seizure History

**Source**: audit-08-information.md
**File**: `08-secrets/shadow-truths.md` or create new section

**Problem**: No explanation of how Veil Keepers seized Northern military asset.

**Implementation**:
Add "Living Artifact History" section:

```markdown
## The Living Artifact: Complete History

### Discovery and Study (Year 1118-1125)
- Northern expedition to Vel'Krath discovers artifact
- Artifact shows awareness, demands blood
- Northern scholars study for 7 years
- Artifact proves dangerous: 3 researchers killed
- King Thorne (Aldric's grandfather) concerned

### Veil Keeper Approach (Year 1125-1135)
- Veil Keepers learn of artifact through academic network
- Determine it must be contained, not studied
- Open diplomatic channel to Northern court
- Offer: "We will contain it safely; you avoid the danger"

### Agreement (Year 1135-1145)
- King Thorne agrees to transfer
- Political calculation: Can't admit what it is, can't control it
- Veil Keepers take custody with agreement to:
  - Keep it sealed and contained
  - Never use it as weapon
  - Return if Northern requests (under certain conditions)
- Agreement kept SECRET from court (only king knew)

### The Knowledge Gap (Year 1145-1192)
- King Thorne dies (Year 1165)
- Successor (Aldric's father) learns of artifact but not full history
- King Aldric XII takes throne (Year 1192)
- Discovers artifact exists, demands return
- **Doesn't know why it was given away**

### Current Conflict (Year 1192-present)
- Aldric demands return based on ownership
- Veil Keepers refuse based on danger
- Neither side discusses the original agreement
- Aldric assumes theft; Veil Keepers assume he knows history
- Conflict escalates through miscommunication
```
