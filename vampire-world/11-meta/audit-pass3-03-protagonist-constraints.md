# Third-Pass Audit: Protagonist Constraints

## Overview
Verifies protagonist's physical constraints work with plot structure.

---

## Constraints That Work

### Feeding Cycle Creates Perfect Story Beats ✓
- 1-2 week feeding need with 28-day degradation curve
- Week 0 escape, Week 1 full capacity, Week 2-3 weakness begins
- Hunger window (Days 8-14) coincides with regional pursuit (Week 2-3)
- Creates moral choice: animals vs humans vs enemies

### Travel Speed Matches Pursuit Dynamics ✓
- ~20-25 mi/day (50% human speed)
- 1-2 week head start before organized hunt
- Can reach ally territory in 3-5 weeks
- Forces convergence—must find shelter by Week 4

### Sunlight Restriction Amplifies Geographic Pressure ✓
- Night travel (20 mi) or slow covered day (30 mi, risky)
- Forces wilderness evasion rather than roads
- Naturally constrains to remote areas where forces are sparse

### Fragmentary Archive Knowledge as Resource ✓
- Random, incomplete vampire script from chaotic awakening
- Makes him valuable without giving too much power
- Unlocks later Archives partially, never total dump

### Escape Sequence Timing vs Faction Awareness ✓
- Week 0-1: Only local threat (Inheritors/Veil Keepers)
- Week 2-3: Regional forces as he reaches mid-journey
- Week 5+: Continental response but should have allies
- Matches awakening-response-timeline perfectly

---

## Constraint Problems

### HIGH: Border Clash During Peak Hunger

**Constraint**: Days 15-21 (50% strength, perception dulled)

**Plot Element**: Intersection Point Event 3 (Border Clash) is mid-story

**Issue**: If Border Clash is Week 3, Seven is near feral state during major combat

**Severity**: HIGH

**Fix Options**:
- Move Border Clash to Week 2 (Days 14-20, above 50%)
- Have Seven feed immediately before encounter
- Have Seven captured before clash (not direct combatant)
- Reframe as political/intelligence rather than combat

---

### MEDIUM: Wildlife Feeding Logistics

**Constraint**: Animal blood 50% effective, requires hunting

**Issue**: Hunting takes hours, creates evidence (carcass, blood trails), pursuing forces may find

**Severity**: MEDIUM

**Fix**: Ensure Week 1-2 has natural shelter/cave locations for rest and hunt; introduce as desperation story beats

---

### MEDIUM: Arrival at Distant Allies

**Constraint**: 140-175 miles in 35 days at 20-25 mi/day

**Issue**: Cannot reach major power centers—first allies must be intermediate contacts

**Severity**: MEDIUM (appears already intended)

**Fix**: Confirm early allies are local (Kira, borderlands NPCs), major characters meet Week 2-3+

---

### LOW: Season Not Specified

**Constraint**: Summer = 8 hrs darkness, Winter = 14+ hrs

**Issue**: No explicit season for Year 1200 opening

**Severity**: LOW

**Fix**: Specify season; summer increases pressure, winter is easier

---

### LOW: Physical Vulnerability in Climactic Moments

**Constraint**: Lack of body heat revealed in close contact

**Issue**: Cannot maintain cover with experienced observers

**Severity**: LOW (actually good—reinforces vulnerability)

## Summary

**Verdict**: Protagonist constraints are GRRM-solid. They create cascading pressures that reinforce story beats.

**One Adjustment Needed**: Ensure Event 3 (Border Clash) happens Week 2 (while strong) or Week 5+ (after feeding), not Week 3 (peak weakness).

**The Key Insight**: Hunger and pursuit converge at Week 3, creating natural story pressure. The constraints work because they create ticking clocks, not because they restrict options.
