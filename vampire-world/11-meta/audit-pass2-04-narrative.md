# Second-Pass Audit: Narrative Coherence

## Overview
Ensures story threads work together without contradiction.

---

## Story Elements That Work

### Character Independence & Isolation
- Each POV has compelling personal arc independent of protagonist
- Marcus's faith crisis, Kira's guilt, Elara's daughter search, Gritha's vengeance question, Thandril's obedience dilemma
- Protagonist raises stakes but doesn't create their stories ✓

### Information Asymmetry Framework
- 2-6 week communication lag established ✓
- Faction capabilities documented (Inheritors have crystals, most don't) ✓
- Explains escape windows through staggered learning ✓

### Faction Motivations & Responses
- Veil Keepers: kill on sight (knowledge destroyers) ✓
- Inheritors: protect/study (knowledge seekers) ✓
- Order of Dawn: hunt (theological threat) ✓
- Northern: capture (weapon potential) ✓
- Responses consistent with documented values ✓

### Cultural Collisions
- Protagonist's superiority creates believable friction ✓
- Marcus's faith challenged by living contradiction ✓
- Gritha's rage vs evidence of proto-justice ✓
- Feel organic, not imposed ✓

### Character Fatal Flaws
- Protagonist's need to belong = vulnerable to manipulation ✓
- Marcus's need for authority = crisis when authority wrong ✓
- Kira's paralysis between loyalties = narrative tension ✓
- Flaws feel like real contradictions ✓

## Narrative Problems

**Location**: protagonist.md (needs blood mechanics) + story-arcs.md (assumes travel)

**Issue**: Can "go weeks without feeding (weakened)" but mechanics undefined:
- Who does he feed on during travel?
- How does this constrain routes?
- Does feeding reveal presence?
- Can he sustain combat-strength while traveling?

**Fix**: Document feeding pattern and travel constraints

### HIGH: Silverwood 150-Year Timeline Inconsistency

**Location**: silverwood-domain.md + thandril-shadowleaf.md

**Issue**: Silverwood closed Year 1050 "detecting awakening," Thandril sent Year 1050, but awakening is Year 1200 - 150 years later

Why would elves know exact date 150 years in advance?

**Severity**: HIGH

- Clarify what in 1050 indicated 1200 awakening, OR

## Silverwood Mystery Coherence Test

**What Silverwood Should Know**:
- Vampires used stasis ✓
- Immortals sleep centuries ✓
- Vel'Thoras is major facility ✓
- They can read vampire script ✓

**What Triggers Year 1050**:
- No clear answer documented
- "Protection during dangerous times" (vague)
- Theories: dimensional activity, foreknowledge

**Verdict**: PARTIAL COHERENCE
- Closure makes sense as concept
- 150-year timeframe doesn't clearly connect to protagonist
- Recommendation: Make explicitly permanent mystery

## Summary

**Strong**: Thematic coherence, character independence, information asymmetry

**Critical Gaps**:
1. Protagonist mobility (feeding, sunlight, disguise)
2. Faction learning timeline
3. Silverwood 150-year timing
4. Escape mechanics

These are implementation details the writing needs - not world-breaking but require documentation for internal consistency.
