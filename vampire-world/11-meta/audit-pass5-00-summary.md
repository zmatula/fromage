# Fifth-Pass Audit Summary: Storyline Consistency

## Overall Assessment

The corpus has strong conceptual foundations but significant structural issues that prevent storylines from functioning as a coherent narrative. The primary problems are **geographic/temporal impossibilities**, **arcs operating in isolation**, and **parallel events mistaken for causal chains**.

---

## 🔴 CRITICAL STORY BREAKS

### Geographic/Timeline Impossibilities
1. **Seven cannot reach Scholar's Haven by Week 6** - Travel at night speed requires 40-50 days from Vel'Thoras, only 21-28 days available
2. **Border Clash location impossible** - Seven cannot reach Valdrian-Northern border by Week 2 at night travel speeds
3. **Event 3 before Event 2** - Border Clash (Week 2) occurs BEFORE Crown's Vision (Week 3), breaking causation
4. **Weeks 4-5 narrative void** - Complete gap between Thandril intervention and Blood Archives

### Economic Contradictions
5. **Order budget collapse** - 950K budget vs 2.25M campaign cost means financial crisis by Week 8
6. **Sanctified funding impossible** - Can't fund both persecution AND Order mobilization (1.5M deficit)
7. **Northern invasion suicidal** - 4-month grain reserves with no mechanism to resupply

### Data Inconsistencies
8. **Aldric XII age mismatch** - Registry says 34, detailed file says 48
9. **Solarius VII contradiction** - Registry: "fundamentalist" vs File: "paralyzed moderate"
10. **Sanguine Vessel registry conflicts** - Different people assigned to same Vessel numbers
11. **Seven's memory contradiction** - Both "missing memories" and "knows everyone who died"

---

## 🟡 MAJOR MISSING CONNECTIONS

### Storyline Isolation
- **Faction arcs operate independently** - When Order mobilizes, persecution should weaken; when Northern invades, Order coverage should collapse
- **Dwarven faction absent** - Complete absence from all 12-week arcs
- **Maritime chaos consequences** - Should break artifact verification continent-wide

### Incomplete Character Arcs
- **7 NPCs have no arc structure**: Cassius VIII, Solarius VII, Duke Varen, Duchess Margret, High Thane Borin, Aldric XII, Commander Vara
- **Sister Elena disconnected** - Key potential ally with no intersection points
- **Aurelius has no faith crisis** - Functions as obstacle, not character

### Missing Causation Mechanisms
- **Events 2-3-4 are parallel, not sequential** - Three simultaneous consequences of Event 1, not a chain
- **No HOW explanations** - How does a vision in Valdris cause a border clash?

### Unresolved Setups
- Year 812 vampire testimony (no payoff)
- Crown of Seeing madness history (no payoff)
- Thandril feeding intervention Day 21-22 (no consequences shown)
- Blood Anvil + vampire blood combination (setup without payoff)

## 🔵 STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS

### What Works
- Seven's arc has strong conceptual bones (survival → understanding → agency)
- Faction motivations are clear and grounded
- Thematic foundations are solid (identity, corruption, consequences)
- Events 5-6-7 have decent causal logic

### What's Broken
- **Geography breaks the story** - Distances don't permit the timeline
- **Events 1-4 lack causation** - Parallel plots mistaken for chain
- **NPCs are functions, not characters** - They have tragedies but not arcs
- **Factions don't pressure each other** - Economic constraints should cascade

### Core Problem
The world was built system-by-system (economics, factions, magic) but the **systems don't connect to the story events**. The economic pressures documented in faction files should create visible narrative consequences, but events happen in their own timeline disconnected from these pressures.
