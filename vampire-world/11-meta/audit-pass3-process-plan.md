# Third-Pass Audit Process Plan

## Overview

This audit verifies the second-pass implementation and performs deeper integration analysis now that major systems are complete. Focus areas:
1. Numeric cascade verification (do new numbers work through all references?)
2. New NPC integration (do 10 new characters fit existing faction dynamics?)
3. Protagonist constraint coherence (do feeding/travel/disguise work with plot?)
4. Information system consistency (do communication speeds match faction actions?)
5. Cross-system integration (do economics, politics, and narrative align?)

---

## Audit Focus Areas

### 1. Numeric Cascade Audit
**File**: `audit-pass3-01-numeric-cascade.md`

**Purpose**: Verify second-pass numbers cascade correctly through all references.

**Check**:
- Does 200k Valdris toll appear in all relevant calculations?
- Does 9M Northern grain need reflect in war planning, trade dependencies?
- Does 600k import cost match Northern treasury and budget docs?
- Do Sanctified economics (1.5M revenue) align with military and Church funding?
- Does dwarven food dependency (80%) create documented strategic vulnerability?
- Do military compositions match battle scenarios and campaign planning?

---

### 2. NPC Integration Audit
**File**: `audit-pass3-02-npc-integration.md`

**Purpose**: Verify 10 new NPCs integrate with existing faction dynamics.

**Check**:
- Do NPC economic positions match faction economic profiles?
- Do NPC relationships reference existing characters correctly?
- Do NPC knowledge gaps align with faction intelligence capabilities?
- Do speech patterns match cultural DNA documented for their factions?
- Do NPC motivations create documented conflicts with other NPCs?
- Are succession candidates' positions reflected in political crisis docs?

---

### 3. Protagonist Constraint Coherence
**File**: `audit-pass3-03-protagonist-constraints.md`

**Purpose**: Verify protagonist's physical constraints work with plot structure.

**Check**:
- Do feeding requirements create story beats at appropriate intervals?
- Does travel speed (half human) match faction pursuit timelines?
- Do disguise limitations create tension in documented encounter scenarios?
- Does escape sequence provide Seven with resources matching later scenes?
- Do awakening response timelines give Seven documented escape windows?
- Can protagonist physically reach locations in story arcs given constraints?

---

### 4. Information System Coherence
**File**: `audit-pass3-04-information-coherence.md`

**Purpose**: Verify communication infrastructure aligns with faction actions.

**Check**:
- Do faction learning times match communication speeds documented?
- Do propaganda campaigns use methods matching faction capabilities?
- Do information broker locations align with trade route documentation?
- Do message costs create realistic budget constraints?
- Do knowledge gaps match what factions could actually learn?
- Does information distortion match documented communication reliability?

---

### 5. Cross-System Integration Audit
**File**: `audit-pass3-05-cross-system.md`

**Purpose**: Verify economics, politics, information, and narrative work together.

**Check**:
- Do economic pressures drive documented political decisions?
- Do information asymmetries create documented conflicts?
- Do geographic constraints match travel times and military campaigns?
- Do character arcs reflect their economic and political stakes?
- Do faction responses to protagonist match their documented capabilities?
- Does timeline allow for documented events given travel/communication speeds?

## Audit Execution Process

### Step 1: Implementation Inventory
Create list of all files modified in second-pass implementation:
- 13 modified files
- 12 new files
- Focus on integration points between them

### Step 2: Systematic Cross-Reference
For each audit dimension:
- Compare new content against existing documentation
- Trace numbers through calculation chains
- Verify character references are bidirectional
- Check timeline feasibility

### Step 3: Issue Documentation
For each finding:
- **Location**: Specific file and line
- **Type**: Inconsistency, gap, integration failure
- **Severity**: Critical, high, medium, low
- **Suggested fix**: Brief recommendation

### Step 4: Prioritization
Rank findings by:
- Impact on story coherence
- Cascading effects on other systems
- Implementation complexity
