# Artifact Histories

Major artifacts with origins, journeys, contested ownership, and what they actually do versus what people think.

---

## The Crown of Seeing

### Origin

**Created**: Year -380
**Creator**: Covenant of the Eternal Mind, Archon Vaelith's predecessor
**Purpose**: Remote viewing of distant events

**What It Was For**
- Spy on other covenants
- Monitor distant territories
- Observe dimensional experiments
- See through the eyes of anyone touched by Bloodweb

### What It Actually Does

**Real Capabilities**
- Shows visions of distant places (up to 100 miles)
- Can see the past of a place (up to one day prior)
- Requires blood to activate (wearer's blood)
- Shows truth, but user chooses where to look

**Limitations**
- Can't see through strong wards
- Drains the wearer (extended use is dangerous)
- Shows what IS, not what WILL BE
- Sometimes shows things you shouldn't see

### What People Think It Does

**Common Beliefs**
- Shows the future (false)
- Shows anyone anywhere (false)
- Grants mind-reading (false)
- Makes you omniscient (very false)

**Why the Confusion**
- Few have used it
- Descriptions got exaggerated
- People want it to be more than it is

### Journey

**Year -380 to 0**: In Mind Covenant treasury. Used rarely.

**Year 0**: Lost in collapse. Mind capital destroyed.

**Year 312**: Found by Valdrian expedition in ruins.

**Year 312-400**: Valdrian royal family. Caused paranoia in three kings. One went mad.

**Year 400-620**: Stolen by Inheritors. Hidden in vault.

**Year 620**: Inheritor schism. Crown taken by splinter faction.

**Year 620-850**: Used by splinter faction to gain power. They were destroyed by Order of Dawn.

**Year 850**: Order of Dawn has it. Supposedly destroyed.

**Year 850-1100**: Actually in Church archive. Used by High Confessors.

**Year 1100-1180**: Stolen again. By Veil Keepers.

**Year 1180-present**: Unknown. Veil Keepers claim they don't have it. They're lying.

### Who Wants It

**Northern Kingdom**: Aldric XII would pay anything
**Inheritors**: Want it back
**Order of Dawn**: Want to destroy it (or use it)
**Maritime Banks**: Would buy it to sell it
**Veil Keepers**: Have it, won't admit it

### Current Location

**Actually**: Veil Keepers' most secure vault, Free Cities
**What They'll Say**: "We don't have it"
**Why They're Lying**: It's the most valuable intelligence tool in existence

## The Forge of Souls

### Origin

**Created**: Year -650
**Creator**: Covenant of the Crimson Throne
**Purpose**: Create vampire-loyal soldiers

**What It Was For**
- "Forge" servant warriors with absolute loyalty
- Modify their minds during creation
- Make them fearless, obedient, strong
- Build the perfect army

### What It Actually Does

**Real Capabilities**
- Can modify a person during creation of weapons/armor
- Binds them to the item being forged
- Makes them loyal to whoever controls the forge
- Also makes them... less themselves

**The Cost**
- Subject loses memories
- Subject loses personality
- Subject becomes the weapon
- Not death, but not really life

### What People Think It Does

**Common Beliefs**
- Makes perfect weapons (true, but)
- Creates super soldiers (true, but)
- Grants power (true, but)
- No downsides (false)

**Why Dwarves Wanted It**
- Perfect craftsman's tool
- Make legendary weapons
- Bind warriors to kings
- They didn't understand the cost

### Journey

**Year -650 to 0**: In Throne Covenant stronghold. Used extensively.

**Year 0**: Survived collapse in deep vault.

**Year 358**: Dwarven expedition finds it.

**Year 358-500**: Dwarves experiment. Create "forge-bound" warriors. Warriors become less and less themselves. Eventually: can't stop fighting. Kill themselves and others.

**Year 500**: Dwarves seal it in deepest vault. Officially "too dangerous to use."

**Year 500-1000**: Sealed. Occasionally unsealed by ambitious thanes. Always resealed after disasters.

**Year 1000-present**: Supposedly still sealed.

### The Truth

**What Dwarves Don't Admit**
- They've used it more recently than they say
- Some "forge-bound" dwarves still exist
- They're not dead—they're locked in sealed halls
- Still fighting after 500 years
- Can't die

**What It Means**
- The Forge doesn't just modify—it imprisons
- The warriors are still in there
- They've been fighting and dying and being repaired by the Forge
- For centuries
- They want out

### Who Wants It

**Northern Kingdom**: For super soldiers
**Valdris**: For succession wars
**Inheritors**: To study
**Order of Dawn**: To destroy
**Goblins**: They know what it really does. They want to free the prisoners.

### Current Location

**Actually**: Deepest vault of Ironpeak, guarded by forge-bound dwarves who can't leave
**Why It's Guarded by Its Victims**: They can't stop. They were made to guard it. They'll guard it forever.

## The Blood Archives

### Origin

**Created**: Accumulated Year -500 to 0
**Creator**: Multiple Mind Covenant scholars
**Purpose**: Complete record of blood magic research

**What They Contain**
- Servant race creation records
- Goblin modification experiments
- Bloodweb design documents
- Sanguine Vessels research notes
- Things no one should know

### What They Actually Are

**Physical Form**
- Crystal matrices storing information
- Require blood to access (like everything vampire)
- Information appears in the mind
- Can take days to "read" one crystal

**The Contents**
- Technical: how to modify races
- Historical: what they did and why
- Philosophical: vampire debates about ethics (yes, they had them)
- Personal: individual researchers' notes and doubts

### What People Think They Are

**Common Beliefs**
- Just technical records (false—also personal)
- Complete (false—many crystals missing)
- Objective (false—written by vampires)
- Easy to read (false—takes blood and time)

### Journey

**Year 0**: Scattered in collapse. Individual crystals everywhere.

**Year 50-300**: Mostly destroyed or lost.

**Year 612**: Major cache found—30 crystals.

**Year 612-800**: Passed through various hands. Kingdoms, scholars, collectors.

**Year 800**: Inheritors acquire most of the cache. 23 crystals.

**Year 800-1124**: Inheritors study them. Learn disturbing things.

**Year 1124**: Exposure. Some crystals seized by Order.

**Year 1124-present**: Split between Inheritors, Order, private collectors.

### The Important Crystals

**The Goblin Crystal**
- Contains records of goblin creation
- Would prove goblins were made, not born
- Inheritors have it
- Goblins want it desperately

**The Sanguine Crystal**
- Contains Vessels Initiative summary
- Would explain what Seven is
- Order has it
- They haven't been able to read it properly

**The Ethics Crystal**
- Contains vampire debates about whether they'd gone too far
- Would prove vampires weren't just monsters
- Unknown location
- Would change everything

### Who Wants Them

**Inheritors**: To complete their collection
**Order of Dawn**: To destroy them
**Goblins**: To prove what was done to them
**Scholars**: To understand vampire civilization
**Seven**: To understand what he is

### Current Locations

**Inheritor vault**: 18 crystals
**Order archive**: 8 crystals
**Private collectors**: 4 crystals known, probably more
**Unknown**: At least 20 crystals never found

## The Mind Codex

### Origin

**Created**: Year -45
**Creator**: Archon Mordecai himself
**Purpose**: Preserve his mind if Transcendence failed

**What It Was For**
- Complete copy of Mordecai's consciousness
- Backup in case of death
- Could theoretically restore him
- Or just access his knowledge

### What It Actually Does

**Real Capabilities**
- Contains Mordecai's memories, thoughts, personality
- Can "speak" with him if properly accessed
- Requires blood to activate
- Requires mental strength to survive contact

**The Danger**
- Mordecai's consciousness tries to take over
- Extended contact risks personality bleed
- He wants out
- He wants a body

### What People Think It Does

**Common Beliefs**
- Contains knowledge (true)
- Is just a book/record (false—it's a mind)
- Can be safely studied (very false)
- Would cooperate (absolutely false)

### Journey

**Year 0**: In Mordecai's quarters when he died. Vault survived.

**Year 667**: Found by expedition to ruined capital. Barely survived extracting it.

**Year 667-700**: Studied by Free Cities scholars. Three died. Two went mad.

**Year 700**: Sealed "for safety."

**Year 700-900**: Unsealed twice. Both times ended badly.

**Year 900**: Inheritors acquire it. "We can handle it."

**Year 900-1000**: Inheritors study it carefully. Lose four people. Learn a lot.

**Year 1000-present**: In deepest Inheritor vault. Rarely accessed.

### What Mordecai Wants

**His Goals** (still pursuing from inside the Codex)
- A body (anyone's will do)
- To complete Transcendence
- To restore vampire civilization
- He's not evil—he just doesn't care about anything else

**His Strategy**
- Pretends to be helpful
- Offers knowledge
- Builds trust
- Tries to take over when guard is down

### Who Wants It

**No one with sense.**

**Northern Kingdom**: Aldric XII doesn't know what it really is
**Inheritors**: Have it, regret it
**Order of Dawn**: Want to destroy it
**Seven**: Might be the only one who could safely talk to Mordecai (vampire mind)

### Current Location

**Inheritor deepest vault, Free Cities**
**Guarded by**: The Inheritor who lost the most people to it. She checks on it every day.
**Why**: "So the bastard knows we're still watching."

## The Living Artifact

### Origin

**Created**: Year -2
**Creator**: Unknown—different research team from Vessels
**Purpose**: Unknown

**What It Might Be**
- Vessel Eleven (different design)
- A different kind of experiment
- Something worse
- Something better

### What It Is

**Year 1118 Discovery**
- Found in northern ruin complex
- Sealed chamber with active systems
- Something alive inside
- Not vampire, not servant, not anything anyone recognized

**What They Saw**
- Humanoid
- Alive (barely)
- Not quite conscious
- Reacted to blood

**What They Did**
- Sealed the chamber
- Reported it
- Order of Dawn arrived
- Sealed it further
- Classified everything

### What People Know

**Public Knowledge**: Nothing. Event suppressed.

**Scholar Rumors**: Something was found. Something alive. Something hidden.

**Inheritor Intelligence**: Detailed second-hand account. They're desperate to access it.

**Order Records**: Complete file. Classified above High Confessor.

### What It Might Mean

**If It's Vessel Eleven**
- Seven isn't alone
- Different design might mean different abilities
- Could be ally or enemy
- Could be worse than Seven or better

**If It's Something Else**
- Vampire experiments went further than known
- This is what they became
- Or what they were trying to become
- Or what they were trying to fight

### The Location

**Northern Dominion, exact location classified**
- Sealed by Order of Dawn
- Guarded constantly
- Deep underground
- Active vampire systems still running

### Who Wants It

**Everyone who knows about it.**

**Northern Kingdom**: It's in their territory. They want it.
**Order of Dawn**: Have it. Terrified of it.
**Inheritors**: Would do anything for access.
**Seven**: Might be his only family.

### The Questions

- What is it?
- Is it conscious?
- Can it communicate?
- What does it want?
- What was it made for?
- What happens if it wakes up?

## Patterns in Artifact History

### They All Have Bodies

Every major artifact has cost lives:
- Crown of Seeing: dozens
- Forge of Souls: hundreds
- Blood Archives: unknown
- Mind Codex: at least seven
- Living Artifact: unknown

### They All Corrupt

Extended use or contact changes people:
- Crown: paranoia
- Forge: loss of self
- Archives: disturbing knowledge
- Codex: personality bleed
- Living Artifact: unknown

### Everyone Lies About Them

- Order claims to destroy them. Keeps them.
- Inheritors claim to study them. Hoard them.
- Kingdoms claim to protect them. Hide them.
- Everyone wants what they say they don't want.

### They Create Conflict

Every artifact has been fought over. Every artifact will be fought over again.
