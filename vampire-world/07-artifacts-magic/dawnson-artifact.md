# The Dawnson Artifact

A vampire artifact buried in Year 631, still active, still demanding blood—and no one knows where it is.

---

## Overview

**Name**: Unknown (Aldric Dawnson never named it)
**Type**: Blood-powered enhancement device
**Created**: Unknown (pre-Collapse)
**Buried**: Year 631, Northern Kingdom territory
**Status**: Active but hidden

This is the artifact that drove Aldric Dawnson to murder his family and found the Order of Dawn as a cover-up. It's been buried for 569 years. It's still demanding blood. Someone—or something—has been feeding it.

---

## Known Properties

### Original Function

Based on the Dawnson family's use (Year 580-631):
- Extended lifespan
- Enhanced vitality and health
- Increased physical capabilities
- Potential minor regeneration

### The Blood Cost

The artifact demands blood to function. The progression:
- **Years 1-10**: Animal blood sufficient
- **Years 11-30**: Required human blood (criminals, enemies)
- **Years 31-50**: Required innocent blood
- **Year 50+**: Demands increasing; users began considering children

This progression is typical of blood-powered artifacts. They don't corrupt users—they reveal what users will do for power.

### Why It Can't Be Destroyed

Aldric tried to destroy it when he saw what his family was becoming. He failed.

Blood-powered vampire artifacts are nearly indestructible. They were designed to survive:
- Fire
- Physical destruction
- Magical attacks
- Burial

The Forge of Souls might be able to unmake it. Nothing else has worked.

---

## The Burial

### Location

Aldric buried the artifact "in the Northern wilderness" around Year 631. Based on travel times from the Dawnson estate and his recorded absence:
- Northern Kingdom territory
- Within 100 miles of what would become the Northern border
- Mountainous or forested (to hide the burial site)
- Probably marked somehow (he may have planned to retrieve it)

### The Grave

Aldric didn't just drop it in a hole. Based on his background as a minor noble with military training:
- Deep burial (10+ feet)
- Probably in a container (lead box, stone chest)
- Possibly warded (he had access to minor magic)
- Location recorded somewhere (but his notes were destroyed)

### Who Knows

**No one knows the exact location**. Aldric died in Year 687 without telling anyone.

But:
- The Order of Dawn has access to his original documents
- Some documents were sealed, not destroyed
- A thorough search of Order archives might find clues

---

## Evidence of Activity

### The Phenomena

Something in the Northern Kingdom region is demanding blood. Phenomena consistent with a buried blood artifact:

**Livestock Deaths**:
Villages within a certain radius report animals dying drained of blood. Not all animals—specific ones, at specific times. The pattern suggests feeding, not disease.

**Missing Persons**:
Disappearances cluster in certain areas. Some bodies are never found. Others are found drained. This has been attributed to bandits, wolves, or vampire cultists. It's been happening for 569 years.

**The Growth Pattern**:
The affected area has slowly expanded over centuries. In Year 631, it was a single village. Now it's perhaps 50 miles in radius. The artifact's reach is growing as it feeds.

### Order of Dawn Investigations

The Order has investigated these phenomena repeatedly:
- Year 720: First organized investigation; inconclusive
- Year 890: Major expedition; found evidence of blood magic but no source
- Year 1100: Investigation suppressed by senior leadership
- Year 1180: Brother Aldric Valorian begins private investigation

Why would the Order suppress investigation of vampire activity? Because they suspect the source is connected to their founder. They can't let that become known.

---

## Brother Aldric Valorian's Investigation

### The Investigator

Brother Aldric Valorian (yes, named after the founder) is the son of Cardinal Valorian (Sword faction). He became an Inquisitor but found himself horrified by what the Order does. He now secretly warns targets and undermines investigations.

But this investigation is different. He's not trying to protect people from the Order—he's trying to find the truth about the Order itself.

### What He's Found

- Pattern of disappearances centered on specific Northern coordinates
- Order records that mention a "founder's burden" being "laid to rest"
- Sealed documents that reference a "family heirloom" that "must never be recovered"
- His own growing suspicion that Aldric the Lightbringer wasn't a saint

### What He Suspects

Brother Valorian suspects the Order's founder buried something dangerous and the Order has been covering it up for 500 years. He doesn't yet know about the family massacre.

If he finds the artifact, he faces a choice:
- Destroy it (if possible) and bury the secret
- Expose it and destroy the Order's legitimacy
- Use it (the temptation of power)

---

## Who's Looking

### Northern Kingdom

They've noticed the disappearances but haven't connected them to a single source. They attribute it to:
- Remaining vampire corruption
- Criminal activity
- Normal predation

They're not actively searching for an artifact. They don't know one exists.

### Inheritors

The Inheritors know something is there. Their instruments detect blood magic signatures in the region. But:
- The readings are diffuse (buried deep, spread out)
- They can't pinpoint the source
- They've focused on more promising sites (Vel'Thoras, sanctuaries)

If they learned the artifact was related to Order of Dawn's founding, they'd prioritize finding it—leverage over the Order.

### Order of Dawn

The Order leadership knows. They've known for centuries. They've:
- Suppressed investigations
- Sealed documents
- Eliminated members who got too close
- Maintained the cover-up

Why? Because if the truth comes out:
- Their founder was a murderer, not a saint
- They've covered it up for 500 years
- They've allowed the artifact to keep killing
- Their entire legitimacy collapses

## What Happens When Found

### If Destroyed

- The phenomena stop
- The Order's secret might remain hidden
- Or the destruction could reveal what was buried and why

### If Retrieved

- Someone has a powerful blood artifact
- They face the same corruption the Dawnsons did
- The Order's secret is at risk

### If Publicized

- Order of Dawn loses moral authority
- Their founder becomes a villain
- 500 years of cover-up is exposed
- The organization might collapse—or reform

### If Seven Finds It

Vampire blood can power blood artifacts far more efficiently than human blood. If Seven found this artifact:
- It would respond to him immediately
- He could use it without the corruption cycle (maybe)
- He'd learn what it did to the Dawnson family
- He'd understand why humans fear vampire artifacts

## Connections

### To Order of Dawn

The artifact is the Order's founding sin. Everything they do is tainted by:
- The murder it caused
- The cover-up they maintain
- The deaths they've allowed to continue
- The lies they tell about their founder

If the Inquisition hunts Seven as vampire corruption while ignoring the actual vampire artifact under their feet, the hypocrisy is complete.

### To Northern Kingdom

The artifact is in Northern territory. The King doesn't know it exists. If he learned:
- A vampire artifact has been killing his subjects for 569 years
- A religious order has known and done nothing
- The Order was founded on murder

The political implications are enormous.

### To Seven

Seven will be hunted as vampire corruption. He might point out:
- He's not killing anyone
- The buried artifact has killed for 569 years
- The Order protects its founder's artifact while hunting him
- Who's the real corruption?
