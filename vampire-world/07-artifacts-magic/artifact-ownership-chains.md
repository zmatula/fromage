# Artifact Ownership Chains

Five major artifacts traced from creation to Year 1200—every owner, every theft, every death.

---

## Why Ownership Matters

Artifacts aren't just magical items. They're inheritance, legitimacy, and leverage. Knowing who owned an artifact—and who took it from them—reveals:
- Hidden alliances
- Unsettled debts
- Murder motives
- Legitimate claims

Every artifact has a chain. Every chain has blood.

---

## The Crown of Mordecai

### Creation (Year -180)

**Creator**: Archon Vaelith the Seeker (commissioned by Mordecai)
**Purpose**: Focus and amplify mental powers—telepathy, memory manipulation, consciousness projection
**Materials**: Bloodstone core, Starweave circuitry, seven vampire tears (willingly given)

The Crown was Mordecai's primary tool for Mind covenant leadership. It let him speak directly into any vampire's mind, anywhere in the world.

### Vampire Era Owners

**Year -180 to 0**: Mordecai the Ambitious
- Never removed it in public
- Slept with it
- It may have fused with his skull

### The Collapse

The Crown was on Mordecai's head when the Ascension Experiment failed. It should have been destroyed.

It wasn't.

### Post-Collapse Journey

**Year 3**: Found by scavenger Kael Ashwood in Neth'Korath rubble
- Didn't know what it was
- Sold it to a merchant for twelve silver

**Year 3-45**: Merchant Aldric Vane
- Recognized it as vampire work
- Hid it, studied it, went mad
- Died in asylum, possessions auctioned

**Year 45**: Purchased by House Valorian (Valdris)
- Knew what they were buying
- Kept it secret for generations
- Believed it would make them emperors

**Year 234**: Stolen by Valorian servant
- Servant was Veil Keeper agent
- Veil Keepers held it for study
- Three scholars went mad studying it

**Year 312**: Traded to Inheritors for information
- Inheritors couldn't make it work
- Stored in secure vault
- Mostly forgotten

**Year 789**: Brought to Sel'Naroth facility
- Inheritors thought proximity to vampire tech might activate it
- It didn't—but readings changed when near the stasis chambers

**Current Status (Year 1200)**:
- Location: Inheritor vault beneath Valdris capital
- Condition: Dormant but intact
- The Inheritors don't know that Vera Nighthollow wears it during private rituals

### The Secret

The Crown still contains fragments of Mordecai's consciousness. It whispers to anyone who wears it. Vera has been listening.

She's not sure if the whispers are Mordecai, the Crown itself, or her own madness. But they've told her things about Seven that she couldn't know otherwise.

### Who Wants It

**House Valorian**: It was stolen from them. They want it back.

**The Church**: It's vampire corruption. They want it destroyed.

**Prince Ragnar**: He knows it exists. He's been searching for years.

## The Blade of Cassius

### Creation (Year -340)

**Creator**: Dwarven Master Thorin Soulforge
**Purpose**: Vampire-slaying weapon (ironic, given who commissioned it)
**Materials**: Soulforged steel, dragon blood quenching, binding runes from Mordecai

Cassius the Patient had this blade made to kill vampires—specifically, Mordecai, if the coup required it. It's one of the few weapons that can permanently kill a vampire.

### Vampire Era Owners

**Year -340 to 0**: Cassius the Patient (Throne covenant)
- Never used it on Mordecai
- Used it on seventeen other vampires
- Named it "Patience"

### The Collapse

Cassius was fighting during the Collapse—the only Archon who died in combat rather than the inversion. He was fighting his own troops who were trying to flee.

The Blade was pulled from his hand by a dying soldier.

### Post-Collapse Journey

**Year 0-23**: Unknown
- Lost in the chaos
- Someone had it—bodies kept appearing drained of blood

**Year 23**: Found by Northern warlord Erik Bloodaxe
- Recognized as vampire weapon
- Used it to unite the Northern tribes
- Founded what became Northern Kingdom

**Year 23-456**: Northern Royal Line
- Passed from king to king
- Symbol of Northern legitimacy
- "Bloodaxe" became the dynasty name

**Year 456**: Stolen during the Ashford Rebellion
- Rebel Lord Marcus Ashford took it
- Rebellion failed
- Ashford executed, blade "lost"

**Year 456-634**: Church of Divine Light
- They took it from Ashford
- Kept it secret—vampires were gone, why advertise the blade?
- Stored in Cathedral vault

**Year 634**: Given to Aldric the Lightbringer
- Founding gift for the Order of Dawn
- Perfect weapon for vampire hunters
- They renamed it "Dawnbringer"

**Year 634-1123**: Order of Dawn
- Carried by successive Grand Masters
- Used to destroy three vampire artifacts
- Became the Order's primary symbol

**Year 1123**: Lost in the Keth's Hollow incident
- Grand Master Aldric Valorian investigating The Scar
- He entered the Distortion Ring
- He never returned
- Neither did the Blade

**Current Status (Year 1200)**:
- Location: Unknown—somewhere in The Scar
- Condition: Unknown
- The Order desperately wants it back

### The Secret

Grand Master Valorian didn't go into The Scar willingly. He was pulled—by the same force that draws others. The Pull.

The Blade is in the Distortion Ring, and something there wanted it. Something that knew what it was. Something that wanted a weapon that could kill vampires.

### Who Wants It

**Order of Dawn**: It's their symbol. Without it, their legitimacy suffers.

**Northern Kingdom**: It was theirs first. They've never stopped wanting it back.

**The Scar**: Something in there has it. That's what terrifies everyone.

## The Mirror of Selene

### Creation (Year -400)

**Creator**: Selene the Eternal (Twilight covenant)
**Purpose**: Scrying and prophecy—see anywhere, any time, any possibility
**Materials**: Pure silver, vampire blood glass, bound spirits of three oracles

The Mirror was Twilight's greatest intelligence tool. Selene could see what other covenants planned before they planned it.

### Vampire Era Owners

**Year -400 to 0**: Selene the Eternal
- Never let anyone else use it
- Consulted it daily
- It may have shown her the Collapse coming

### The Collapse

Selene disappeared during the Collapse. Not died—disappeared. Her body was never found. Neither was the Mirror.

### Post-Collapse Journey

**Year 0-89**: Unknown
- Selene and Mirror both vanished
- Twilight survivors searched for decades
- Never found either

**Year 89**: Fragments surface
- Inheritors at Sel'Naroth found Mirror shards
- Broken, but each shard still showed visions
- They collected five fragments

**Year 89-234**: Inheritor Study
- Tried to reassemble—failed
- Each fragment showed different things
- Some showed the past, some the future, some lies

**Year 234**: Three fragments stolen
- Thieves unknown—possibly Veil Keepers
- Inheritors kept two
- The other three scattered

**Current Locations (Year 1200)**:

**Fragment 1**: Inheritor vault
- Shows past events (sometimes accurate)

**Fragment 2**: Inheritor vault
- Shows possible futures (always frightening)

**Fragment 3**: Elven Silverwood
- How they got it is unknown
- They won't discuss it

**Fragment 4**: Dwarven Ironpeak
- "Found" in a mine (lies)
- They're using it for something

**Fragment 5**: Unknown
- Last seen in the Maritime Federation
- Lost in House war Year 1089
- Someone has it

### The Secret

The Mirror isn't broken. It shattered itself.

When Selene saw what was coming—the Collapse, the death, everything—she asked the Mirror to show her how to stop it. The Mirror showed her that she couldn't. She asked it to show her how to survive. It showed her.

Then she broke it so no one else could see what she'd seen. Some knowledge is too dangerous.

But the fragments still work. They still show visions. And they're trying to reunite.

### Who Wants It

**The Inheritors**: They want the other fragments. Reunited, it might work fully.

**The Elves**: They have one fragment and won't explain why.

**Selene**: If she's alive—and she might be—she wants to keep it broken.

## The Forge-Key

### Creation (Year Unknown—Pre-Vampire)

**Creator**: Unknown—predates vampire civilization
**Purpose**: Operate the Forge of Souls
**Materials**: Unknown—no one can analyze it

The Forge-Key is the only way to activate the Forge of Souls. Without it, the Forge is just a very large room with strange machinery. The Key is why dwarves control the Forge—they have the Key.

### Pre-Vampire History

The Key existed before vampires. It was made by whoever built the Forge—and no one knows who that was.

Dwarves found both Forge and Key when they first dug into the mountains, long before vampires rose. They've guarded both ever since.

### Vampire Era Owners

**Year -800 to 0**: Dwarven Kings
- Vampires wanted the Key desperately
- Dwarves never gave it up
- This is why vampires never fully conquered dwarves

**Year -200**: Mordecai tried to steal it
- Sent a team of Mind covenant agents
- Team disappeared in the mines
- Dwarves didn't even acknowledge the attempt

**Year -50**: Cassius tried to take it by force
- Sent an army into the mountains
- Army never returned
- The mountains have defenses no one understands

### The Collapse and After

**Year 0**: Dwarves sealed the Forge
- Used the Key one last time
- Sealed the Forge against all access
- Put the Key... somewhere

**Year 0-1200**: Hidden
- Dwarves claim it's in their deepest vault
- Or held by the King himself
- Or in a location only three people know
- They give different answers to different people

**Current Status (Year 1200)**:
- Location: Officially, the Dwarven King's vault
- Actually: Unknown even to most dwarves
- The Forge remains sealed

### The Secret

The Key is not in any vault. It's not with the King.

It's buried with the last Dwarven Smith who used the Forge—Master Aldric Deepstone, who sealed it in Year 0. His tomb is hidden in the deepest mines.

The current Dwarven King doesn't know this. His father didn't tell him. The secret has been lost through succession.

The Key is waiting in a dead dwarf's hands, in a tomb no one can find, for someone who knows where to look.

### Who Wants It

**Everyone**: The Forge can create artifacts. Whoever controls it controls magical supremacy.

**Northern Kingdom**: They've been trying to get Forge access for two centuries.

**The Inheritors**: They know what the Forge can do. They'd trade almost anything for the Key.

**Seven**: He might actually know where it is. Velanna knew—and she might have told him.

## The Blood Codex

### Creation (Year -50)

**Creator**: Mordecai the Ambitious
**Purpose**: Contain his complete consciousness, knowledge, and plans
**Materials**: Human skin pages, vampire blood ink, binding souls of seven scholars

The Blood Codex—often called the Mind Codex—is Mordecai's immortality. His body died in the Collapse, but his mind lives in the book.

### Vampire Era Owner

**Year -50 to 0**: Mordecai
- Created it as backup for the Ascension Experiment
- Encoded everything he knew
- Planned to transfer to a new body if needed

### The Collapse

The Codex was in Mordecai's personal vault when the Collapse happened. The vault survived—it was built to survive anything.

### Post-Collapse Journey

**Year 23**: Found by Valdrian soldiers
- Emperor Marcus I's expansion reached Neth'Korath ruins
- Soldiers found the vault
- Most died opening it (traps)
- Survivors brought the Codex to the Emperor

**Year 23-67**: Imperial Collection
- Scholars couldn't read it—pages moved
- Three scholars went mad trying
- Emperor locked it away

**Year 67**: Stolen by Church
- High Priest Aldric took it for "purification"
- Actually wanted to study it
- He also went mad

**Year 89**: Given to the Inheritors
- Church decided it was too dangerous
- Inheritors promised to contain it
- Church didn't want it anymore

**Year 89-1200**: Inheritor possession
- They've studied it for 1,100 years
- Made some progress reading it
- Lost seven scholars to madness
- Believe they're close to breakthrough

**Current Status (Year 1200)**:
- Location: Inheritor primary vault
- Condition: Active—it moves, whispers, watches
- They believe Seven's blood might be the key to reading it

### The Secret

The Codex isn't a book. It's Mordecai.

When his body died, his consciousness transferred fully into the pages. He's been aware for 1,200 years, watching, waiting.

He knows everything the Inheritors know—they've discussed secrets in front of him for centuries. He knows about Seven. He knows about the Collapse. He knows who he wants to blame.

He's been slowly driving scholars mad to prevent them from learning how to read him. He doesn't want to be read. He wants to be released.

If Seven's blood can activate the Codex, Mordecai will attempt to transfer into Seven's body. The Inheritors think they're studying the Codex. The Codex is studying them.

### Who Wants It

**The Inheritors**: They have it. They want to read it.

**Mordecai**: He wants out. He's been trapped for 1,200 years.

**Seven**: He doesn't know he wants it. But Velanna's memories are pushing him toward it. She wanted something in that book.

## The Ownership Web

### Connections Revealed

Tracking these five artifacts reveals hidden patterns:

**The Valorian Thread**: House Valorian has owned or touched three artifacts (Crown, Blade history, Church Blade custody). They've been building toward something for centuries.

**The Church Involvement**: The Church has held four of five artifacts at some point. They claim to want them destroyed but keep studying them.

**The Inheritor Collection**: They now hold or know about three artifacts. They're the closest thing to a vampire artifact museum.

**The Northern Claim**: Northern Kingdom has legitimate historical claims to two artifacts (Blade, Mirror fragment). They use this to justify expansion.

### Deaths Connected to Artifacts

**The Crown**: At least eleven (three Valorian servants, three Veil scholars, five others)

**The Blade**: Hundreds in wars fought over it

**The Mirror**: Unknown but certainly many

**The Key**: Dozens in failed theft attempts

**The Codex**: Seven confirmed scholars, probably more

Artifacts are measured in blood as much as gold.

## Plot Connections

### When Seven Awakens

**The Crown**: The whispers will intensify. Vera will hear Mordecai screaming about Seven.

**The Blade**: The Order will need it to face Seven. They'll have to enter The Scar.

**The Mirror**: Fragments will show visions of Seven. Different fragments, different visions.

**The Key**: Seven might know its location. Everyone will want to ask him.

**The Codex**: Mordecai will try to reach Seven. Father and son, in a sense—both made by Mind covenant.

### Artifact Reactivation

With vampire blood (Seven's), dormant artifacts might activate:

- The Crown might work fully
- The Mirror might reassemble
- The Codex might open

Seven is the key to the past. These artifacts will make that literal.
