# Relic Registry

Comprehensive list of all significant vampire artifacts with mechanics, history, and plot relevance.

---

## Major Artifacts

### Crown of Seeing

**Classification**: Divination Device (Information System)

**Physical Description**:
- Circlet of dark metal with blood-red gemstones
- Runes inscribed around interior band
- Gems pulse faintly when active
- Uncomfortable to wear (by design?)

**Discovery**:
- Year 312, unknown ruin in Valdrian territory
- Found by early Valdrian explorers
- Immediately claimed by monarchy

**Current Location**: Imperial vaults, Valdris Prime
**Current Holder**: Emperor Cassius VIII

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Wear and concentrate
- **Power Source**: User's blood and life force
- **Effect**: Visions of past, present, or future events
- **Cost**: Severe headaches, nosebleeds, fatigue; extended use causes madness
- **Limitations**: Visions unclear, fragmentary; future is probability only; cannot control what you see

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Command-level strategic intelligence
- Vampire Use: Worn by high-ranking officials for battle coordination
- Why It Exists: Part of military intelligence suite

**Plot Relevance**:
- May give Cassius visions of protagonist awakening
- Driving the emperor toward madness
- Could show protagonist his own past
- Succession fight may center on who gets it

---

### Forge of Souls

**Classification**: Creation Device (Biological/Manufacturing System)

**Physical Description**:
- Massive chamber with central forge apparatus
- Channels for blood and ambient energy
- Binding matrices on walls and floor
- Tools for precision work

**Discovery**:
- Year 358, deep dwarven hold (exact location secret)
- Found by dwarven miners
- Never reported to outside world

**Current Location**: Sealed deep hold chamber
**Current Holder**: Ironpeak Confederation (most secret possession)

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Complex ritual requiring multiple operators
- **Power Source**: Blood and ambient energy
- **Effect**: Can bind spirits to matter, create quasi-living constructs
- **Cost**: Massive ambient energy drain; blood sacrifice required
- **Limitations**: Cannot create true life; constructs have limited intelligence; requires expertise

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Create servants and tools
- Vampire Use: Made guardian constructs, enchanted weapons, modified beings
- Why It Exists: Peak creation technology

**Plot Relevance**:
- Could potentially repair or modify protagonist
- Dwarves will trade nothing for access
- What would convince them?
- Could create army of constructs (if anyone dared)

---

### Living Artifact

**Classification**: Unknown (Unique)

**Physical Description**:
- Crystalline sphere, fist-sized
- Dark red with swirling interior
- Warm to touch
- Pulses rhythmically

**Discovery**:
- Year 1118, Vel'Krath deep complex
- Northern expedition found it
- Veil Keepers took custody

**Current Location**: Veil Keeper secure facility (location secret)
**Current Holder**: Veil Keepers

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Unknown (activates itself)
- **Power Source**: Demands blood (fed by Veil Keepers)
- **Effect**: Shows awareness, responds to questions (sometimes)
- **Cost**: Blood; psychological unease
- **Limitations**: Cannot control; gives cryptic answers; may be manipulating handlers

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Unknown
- Vampire Use: Unknown
- Why It Exists: Theories include: preserved consciousness, artificial intelligence, bound entity, experimental Vessel

**Plot Relevance**:
- Connection to protagonist? (Another Vessel?)
- Information source about vampire era
- Veil Keepers won't share
- Northern Dominion wants it back
- What does it want?

### Blood Archives

**Classification**: Information Storage (Information System)

**Physical Description**:
- Crystallized blood formations in various shapes
- Range from palm-sized to person-sized
- Deep red to black coloration
- Some glow faintly

**Discovery**:
- Year 612, Deep Library (Maritime coast)
- Major discovery of vampire records
- Academic sensation

**Current Location**: Maritime universities, primarily Scholar's Haven
**Current Holder**: Various academic institutions (Inheritor influenced)

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Blood contact with crystal
- **Power Source**: User's blood
- **Effect**: Access recorded information (memories, knowledge, instructions)
- **Cost**: Small blood amount; mental fatigue; risk of information overload
- **Limitations**: Full access requires vampire blood; partial access for others; some corrupted or encrypted

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Long-term knowledge storage
- Vampire Use: Libraries, archives, instruction manuals
- Why It Exists: Vampires valued knowledge preservation

**Plot Relevance**:
- Protagonist could fully access (valuable to Inheritors)
- Information about Sanguine Vessels Initiative
- May contain dangerous knowledge
- Inheritors trading access for cooperation

---

### Mind Codex

**Classification**: Information Storage (Information System)

**Physical Description**:
- Metal box with crystal inlays
- Inscribed with warning runes
- Opens to reveal crystalline matrices
- Cold to touch

**Discovery**:
- Year 667, Valdrian dig site
- Found with safety warnings intact
- Studied cautiously

**Current Location**: Imperial Library, Valdris Prime
**Current Holder**: Valdrian Empire

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Touch crystal matrix and concentrate
- **Power Source**: User's mental energy
- **Effect**: Experience recorded thoughts/memories as if your own
- **Cost**: Mental fatigue; headaches; risk of thought contamination
- **Limitations**: Can overwrite user's memories if not careful; some content corrupted; addictive

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Training, instruction, experience sharing
- Vampire Use: Education, interrogation, entertainment
- Why It Exists: Direct knowledge transfer more efficient

**Plot Relevance**:
- Protagonist might use to recover lost memories
- Risk of implanting false memories (again)
- Contains vampire-perspective experiences
- May show what they were really like

---

### Gate Fragment

**Classification**: Transportation Device (Infrastructure System)

**Physical Description**:
- Arch of dark stone, 8 feet tall
- Half destroyed, cracked
- Runes still faintly visible
- Dimensional residue detectable

**Discovery**:
- Year 534, border ruins
- Recognized as part of larger system
- Carefully secured

**Current Location**: Valdrian Empire secure vault
**Current Holder**: Imperial government

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Cannot be activated (missing paired gate)
- **Power Source**: Would require Bloodweb
- **Effect**: Originally instant transportation between paired gates
- **Cost**: Originally minimal with infrastructure; now would be enormous
- **Limitations**: Cannot function alone; paired gate destroyed; dimensional instability risk

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Transportation network between cities
- Vampire Use: Instant travel for officials, military, commerce
- Why It Exists: Empire needed rapid movement

**Plot Relevance**:
- Shows what infrastructure existed
- Dimensional instability makes it dangerous
- May connect to The Scar somehow
- Could experiments reactivate? Should they?

---

### Healer's Stone

**Classification**: Medical Device (Biological System)

**Physical Description**:
- Oval stone, palm-sized
- Warm, faintly luminous
- Veins of red through white material
- Pleasant to hold

**Discovery**:
- Year 478, Maritime coast ruin
- Most studied artifact
- Highly valued

**Current Location**: Private collection, Goldport
**Current Holder**: Merchant house (rumored Inheritor)

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Hold against wound and concentrate
- **Power Source**: User's blood and ambient energy
- **Effect**: Accelerates healing significantly
- **Cost**: Fatigue, hunger; major wounds drain operator
- **Limitations**: Cannot cure everything; cannot regrow limbs; cannot reverse death

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Medical treatment
- Vampire Use: Healing for valuable servants or injured vampires
- Why It Exists: Vampires valued medical knowledge

**Plot Relevance**:
- Could help protagonist after damage
- May trade hands multiple times
- Inheritors want it for study
- Who will pay most?

### Vel'Krath Weapons Cache

**Classification**: Military Equipment (Military System)

**Physical Description**:
- Hundreds of weapons and armor pieces
- Various types and sizes
- Enhanced but appear normal
- Some have active abilities

**Discovery**:
- Year 745, Vel'Krath upper levels
- Northern explorers
- Immediately integrated into military

**Current Location**: Distributed to Northern military units
**Current Holder**: Northern Dominion military

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Use as normal weapon/armor
- **Power Source**: Self-maintaining enchantments
- **Effect**: Enhanced durability, sharpness; some have active abilities (fire, frost, etc.)
- **Cost**: None for passive effects; active abilities drain user
- **Limitations**: Fixed abilities; cannot be modified; may have unknown functions

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Military equipment for vampire forces
- Vampire Use: Standard issue for Covenant of the Crimson Throne
- Why It Exists: Military power required equipped forces

**Plot Relevance**:
- Makes Northern army most dangerous
- May contain weapons effective against protagonist
- Unexplored deeper levels have more
- What else is in Vel'Krath?

---

### Order of Dawn Arsenal

**Classification**: Anti-Vampire Weapons (Military System)

**Physical Description**:
- Silver-coated weapons
- Inscribed with "holy" symbols
- Actually vampire-made
- Hidden modifications

**Discovery**:
- Various sources, various times
- "Purified" for Church use
- Origins concealed

**Current Location**: Dawnhaven armory
**Current Holder**: Order of Dawn

**Mechanics**:
- **Activation**: Normal weapon use
- **Power Source**: Self-maintaining
- **Effect**: Enhanced against vampire vulnerabilities
- **Cost**: None
- **Limitations**: Specifically designed to hunt vampires; less effective against others

**Historical Context**:
- Original Purpose: Civil war weapons (vampire vs vampire)
- Vampire Use: Covenant conflicts required such weapons
- Why It Exists: Vampires fought each other too

**Plot Relevance**:
- Designed to kill protagonist
- Church hypocrisy (using vampire weapons)
- Could be turned against them
- What if protagonist took one?

## Minor Artifacts

### Scrying Mirrors

**Type**: Divination devices
**Quantity**: Several dozen known
**Locations**: Various (Valdrian, Maritime, private)
**Mechanics**: Visual remote viewing; draining; unreliable
**Plot Use**: Intelligence gathering, protagonist detection

### Preservation Vessels

**Type**: Storage containers
**Quantity**: Hundreds known
**Locations**: Everywhere
**Mechanics**: Keep contents fresh indefinitely; passive
**Plot Use**: Common trade item; some contain unknowns

### Light Crystals

**Type**: Illumination devices
**Quantity**: Thousands known
**Locations**: Everywhere
**Mechanics**: Glow when touched; self-maintaining
**Plot Use**: Common utility; prove vampire tech was mundane too

### Healing Salves

**Type**: Medical supplies
**Quantity**: Limited (consumable)
**Locations**: Various collectors
**Mechanics**: Accelerated healing on application
**Plot Use**: Valuable trade item; limited supply

### Message Crystals

**Type**: Communication devices
**Quantity**: Dozens of pairs
**Locations**: Various rulers and organizations
**Mechanics**: Linked pairs allow short message transmission
**Plot Use**: Secret communication; espionage

### Enhanced Tools

**Type**: Various (craftsman tools, agricultural implements)
**Quantity**: Many
**Locations**: Throughout former vampire territory
**Mechanics**: Work better, last longer
**Plot Use**: Prove vampire era had beneficial technology

### Memory Shards

**Type**: Information storage fragments
**Quantity**: Unknown
**Locations**: Scattered in ruins
**Mechanics**: Brief memories or information when touched
**Plot Use**: Clues, hints, atmosphere

## Artifact Classification System

### By Danger Level

**Safe**:
- Light crystals
- Preservation vessels
- Enhanced tools
- Healing supplies

**Moderate**:
- Scrying devices
- Message crystals
- Memory shards
- Basic weapons

**Dangerous**:
- Mind Codex
- Blood Archives
- Healer's Stone
- Enhanced weapons

**Extreme**:
- Crown of Seeing
- Forge of Souls
- Gate Fragment
- Living Artifact
- War constructs

**Catastrophic**:
- Dimensional equipment
- Anything from The Scar
- Unknown experimental devices

### By Function

**Information**: Crown of Seeing, Blood Archives, Mind Codex, Scrying Mirrors, Message Crystals
**Military**: Vel'Krath Cache, Order Arsenal, War Constructs
**Medical**: Healer's Stone, Healing Salves
**Infrastructure**: Gate Fragment, Light Crystals
**Creation**: Forge of Souls
**Unknown**: Living Artifact
