# Bloodweb Collapse Mechanics

The technical details of how vampire civilization's infrastructure killed its creators.

---

## What the Bloodweb Was

### Basic Function

The Bloodweb was a magical infrastructure network connecting all vampire settlements. Think of it as:
- Power grid (magical energy distribution)
- Communication network (telepathic links)
- Life support system (enhanced blood potency)
- Transportation network (portal gateways)

All in one system. Built over 800 years. Maintained by Mind covenant.

### How It Worked

**Energy Flow**:
1. Bloodweb nodes drew ambient magical energy from the environment
2. Energy was channeled through crystalline conduits to distribution points
3. Individual vampires connected to local nodes through their blood
4. The blood connection allowed bidirectional energy flow

**The Connection**:
Every vampire was "connected" to the Bloodweb through a subtle modification to their blood—essentially a magical frequency that allowed them to receive and transmit energy. This connection was made at birth (for born vampires) or during turning (for converted humans).

**Enhancement**:
The Bloodweb didn't just provide energy—it enhanced vampires. Blood potency increased. Magical abilities strengthened. Lifespan extended. Vampires with strong connections were measurably more powerful than isolated ones.

### The Dependency Problem

**Year -1000 to -500**: Bloodweb expanded; vampires became dependent on enhanced blood
**Year -500 to -200**: Dependency deepened; disconnected vampires suffered
**Year -200 to -100**: 95% of vampires couldn't survive without connection
**Year -100 to 0**: System started failing; vampires died in node failures

The dependency was gradual and invisible. Each generation was slightly more connected than the last. By the Final Century, most vampires had never experienced life without the Bloodweb—they didn't know how dependent they were.

## The Inversion

### What Happened

The Ascension Experiment at Neth'Korath opened dimensional conduits. These conduits resonated with the Bloodweb's frequency—the same frequency that connected vampires to the network.

Instead of channeling energy TO vampires, the resonance reversed the flow. The Bloodweb began pulling energy FROM vampires into the dimensional breaches.

### The Physics

**Normal operation**: Ambient energy → Nodes → Vampires
**After inversion**: Vampires → Nodes → Dimensional breach

The blood connection that let vampires receive energy now forced them to transmit it. The more strongly connected you were, the faster you died.

### The Cascade

1. **First 10 minutes**: Vampires in Neth'Korath died as the breach pulled their life force
2. **First hour**: Death spread through primary nodes to connected cities
3. **First day**: Cascade reached secondary nodes across the continent
4. **First week**: Sundering Wave (physical/magical shockwave) expanded at 100 miles/day
5. **Months 1-6**: Remote vampires died from blood failure as enhanced potency disappeared

The cascade killed approximately 14 million vampires in six months.

---

## Why Disconnection Didn't Save You

### Timing

Disconnection required a complex magical procedure—it couldn't be done in minutes. By the time vampires knew the Bloodweb was killing them, they were already being drained.

### Damage

Even if you disconnected fast enough, you'd already lost energy. Vampires who disconnected early reported:
- Severe weakness (couldn't move for days)
- Blood hunger (needed massive feeding)
- Magical burnout (couldn't use abilities for months)
- Permanent reduction in power

Many who disconnected still died from these effects.

### Infrastructure Loss

The Bloodweb provided enhanced blood potency. Without it, normal blood couldn't sustain vampire physiology. Disconnected vampires faced starvation even if they survived the initial drain.

This is why the collapse killed 99% of vampires despite lasting six months. Even those who survived the initial cascade eventually starved.

## Why Some Survived

### Twilight Sanctuaries

Twilight covenant built isolated systems with:
- Separate power sources (not connected to main Bloodweb)
- Self-contained blood enhancement
- Sealed chambers (blocked Sundering Wave)

The 25,000 vampires in sanctuaries never experienced the inversion.

**Status in Year 1200**: Unknown. Sanctuaries were sealed from outside. Some may have survived; most probably failed from other causes (power depletion, internal conflict, design flaws).

### The Sanguine Vessels

Vessels were designed to be Bloodweb-independent:
- Modified blood that self-enhanced
- Internal energy reserves
- No connection frequency

When the inversion happened, they weren't affected because they weren't connected. This is why Vessel Seven survived.

**Status in Year 1200**: Seven is alive. Eleven may be alive. Others died in experimentation or during the collapse itself (Vessels 8-10 were being "optimized" through Bloodweb connection when it inverted).

### Accidental Survivors

Some vampires survived through luck:
- Located in areas with weak node connections
- Injured in ways that disrupted their blood frequency
- In locations shielded by geography or magic

These were rare—maybe 50-100 worldwide. They would have awakened to a world where they couldn't feed properly and everyone was hunting vampires.

## The Sundering Wave

### What It Was

When Neth'Korath collapsed, the accumulated energy released as a physical/magical shockwave. This wasn't the inversion—it was separate, like the explosion after a fire.

### Effects

**Physical**:
- Destroyed buildings within 50 miles
- Created the Glass Plain (fused earth)
- Killed all living things in radius

**Magical**:
- Disrupted remaining Bloodweb connections
- Opened secondary dimensional tears
- Left permanent instability (The Scar)

### Speed and Range

The wave expanded at approximately 100 miles per day. It took about a month to cover the continent. Remote areas felt it as:
- Sudden Bloodweb disconnection
- Ground tremors
- Strange weather
- Magical interference

The wave didn't kill directly (except at ground zero), but it completed the Bloodweb destruction and caused enormous collateral damage.

## The Dimensional Breach

### What Remains

The dimensional conduits opened during the Ascension Experiment didn't close cleanly. The Scar is the scar tissue—a place where reality didn't heal properly.

### Properties

**Dimensional instability**: The barrier between worlds is thin
**Energy anomalies**: Unpredictable magical effects
**Temporal distortion**: Time doesn't work correctly
**Entity presence**: Things from other dimensions may be there

### The Growth

The Scar has been slowly expanding since Year 1178. This suggests:
- The breach is active, not dormant
- Something is pushing from the other side
- The dimensional barrier is still weakening

If the Scar continues growing, the original collapse effects might return—or something worse might come through.

## Technical Questions

### Could the Bloodweb Be Rebuilt?

In theory, yes. In practice:
- Knowledge of construction is lost
- Materials are rare or unavailable
- It would take centuries
- No one wants vampire infrastructure back

The dwarves have fragments of technical knowledge from the nodes they dismantled. The Inheritors have theoretical understanding. Neither has the resources or desire to rebuild.

### Could the Inversion Happen Again?

The original Bloodweb is destroyed—you can't invert what doesn't exist. But:
- The Scar is dimensionally unstable
- Artifacts still have Bloodweb components
- Someone might try to rebuild
- A new dimensional breach could affect existing magical systems

The danger isn't exactly the same, but similar catastrophes are possible.

### Why Didn't Artifacts Stop Working Immediately?

Many artifacts have internal power reserves or ambient energy collection. They didn't need the Bloodweb for operation—just for enhancement and recharging.

Over 1,200 years, these reserves have depleted. Most artifacts are now:
- Completely dead (majority)
- Operating at reduced capacity
- Functional but degrading
- Self-powered (rare exceptions)

See [Artifact Power Sources](artifact-power-sources.md) for specific items.

## Implications for Year 1200

### The Scar Connection

If Vessel Seven awakens and his blood frequency is detectable, it might resonate with The Scar. His existence proves the technology to create Bloodweb-independent vampires—and that technology originated from the same dimensional research that created The Scar.

Worst case: Seven's awakening triggers a response in The Scar. The dimensional breach opens further. Something comes through.

### Artifact Reactivation

Some artifacts might reactivate in the presence of vampire blood. If Seven handles them, they could:
- Regain lost functions
- Provide information about the collapse
- Become dangerous in unexpected ways
- Draw attention to his location

### Twilight Sanctuaries

If sanctuaries survived, they might still have functioning Bloodweb components—isolated systems that weren't affected by the inversion. This technology would be enormously valuable to:
- Inheritors (studying vampire magic)
- Dwarves (understanding magical engineering)
- Anyone wanting to rebuild vampire power

Finding a functioning sanctuary is a major plot goal for multiple factions.
