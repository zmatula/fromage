# Artifact Journeys

How the major artifacts traveled from vampire hands to their current locations.

---

## The Crown of Seeing

### Creation (Year -400)

**Creator**: Serath the Artificer (Mind Covenant)
**Location**: Workshop in Neth'Korath
**Purpose**: Reveal hidden truths; enhance perception across dimensional boundaries

**Original Owner**: Archon Kethros (Mind Covenant)
**Use**: Political intelligence; identifying threats and traitors

### The Journey

**Year -400 to Year 0**: Held by successive Mind Archons
- Passed from Kethros to Malachar to Vaelith to Mordecai
- Each used it to identify enemies and secure power
- Each experienced the side effects: temporal disorientation, fractured perception

**Year 0: The Collapse**
- Mordecai was wearing it when the Ascension Experiment began
- His body was never found
- The Crown should have been destroyed with Neth'Korath

**Year 3: The Discovery**
- Purifier band looting the Scar's edge found the Crown
- Somehow it had traveled 50 miles from the capital
- How? No one knows. Artifacts shouldn't move themselves.

**Year 3-40: The Warlord Period**
- Changed hands eleven times through theft, murder, and "gift"
- Each owner used it; each was eventually destroyed
- Pattern emerged: the Crown found powerful owners

**Year 40: Roderick's Acquisition**
- Roderick of the Ash obtained it from a dying warlord
- He wore it once—for one hour
- He never wore it again
- He said: "It shows too much. Some truths should stay hidden."

**Year 45-300: Hidden Period**
- Roderick locked it in a vault
- His successors feared to use it
- Rumors said it was cursed
- Actually it was just dangerous

**Year 312: The Rediscovery**
- King Aldric IV opened the vault
- He used the Crown to identify a coup plot
- He also saw something that made him abdicate within a year
- He never explained what

**Year 312-Present: Crown Vault**
- Kept in the Valdrian Crown Vault
- Used rarely; feared always
- Current location: Beneath the Imperial Palace in Valdris

### Current Situation

**Officially**: Royal property; symbol of imperial insight
**Actually**: No one has worn it in 200 years
**The Risk**: The succession crisis may force someone to use it
**The Question**: What will they see?

### Who Wants It

- **Inheritors**: To understand vampire knowledge
- **Order of Dawn**: To destroy it (they say) or use it (they don't say)
- **Northern Kingdom**: Aldric XII would pay anything
- **Banking Houses**: Knowledge is money

### The Mystery

The Crown traveled 50 miles from Neth'Korath during the collapse. Artifacts don't move themselves. Either someone took it—or it went where it wanted to go.

## The Blood Anvil

### Creation (Year -280)

**Creator**: Serath the Artificer (Mind Covenant)
**Location**: Workshop in Neth'Korath
**Purpose**: Blood-forging; creating weapons that bond to their wielder

**Original Owner**: Given to Crimson Throne as diplomatic gift
**Use**: Forge weapons for Throne military

### The Journey

**Year -280 to Year 0**: Vel'Krath (Throne Capital)
- Used to create blood-forged weapons for elite troops
- Allowed Throne to develop Bloodweb-independent military
- Jealously guarded; never shared with Mind or Twilight

**Year 0: The Collapse**
- Cassius ordered it transported north during evacuation
- Convoy of 500 soldiers; 20 didn't make it
- One of the few major artifacts deliberately saved

**Year 0-15: The Mountain Caves**
- Hidden with Throne remnants in northern mountains
- Used to forge weapons for vampire survivors
- Last known use for vampire purposes

**Year 15-50: Lost Period**
- Throne remnants scattered or died
- Anvil location forgotten
- Assumed destroyed or buried

**Year 87: The Recovery**
- Dwarven miners found it in collapsed tunnel
- They recognized vampire craftsmanship
- They debated destroying it

**Year 87-Present: Ironpeak Possession**
- High King Grimjaw claimed it for the Confederation
- Dwarves studied it; learned some techniques
- Eventually sealed it away

### Current Situation

**Location**: Deep vault in Ironpeak; exact location classified
**Status**: Sealed and guarded; never used
**Official Position**: "Dangerous vampire relic; preserved for study"
**Actual Situation**: Dwarves know how to use it; they're afraid to

### Who Wants It

- **Northern Kingdom**: Blood-forged weapons for the army
- **Banking Houses**: Unique weapons sell for fortunes
- **Inheritors**: Study and replication
- **Goblins**: To make weapons for their rebellion

### Why Dwarves Keep It

**The Public Reason**: Safety; prevent misuse
**The Private Reason**: They've used it. Small projects. Test forgings. They have blood-forged weapons in their armories—and they work.

### The Dilemma

If dwarves revealed they could blood-forge, everyone would want their weapons. If they refused to sell, they'd have enemies everywhere. If they sold, they'd arm people who might attack them.

So they keep the secret. The Anvil stays sealed. And every few decades, someone tries to steal it.

## The Speaking Stones

### Creation (Year -320)

**Creator**: Serath the Artificer (Mind Covenant)
**Location**: Workshop in Neth'Korath
**Purpose**: Communication outside the Bloodweb; backup system

**Number Created**: Twelve pairs (24 stones)
**Original Distribution**: Major facilities throughout the Dominion

### The Journey

**Year -320 to Year 0**: Various locations
- One pair in each major city
- Independent of Bloodweb; worked during failures
- Most survived in sealed facilities

**Year 0-50: Looting Period**
- As ruins were explored, Stones were found
- Sold, traded, stolen, lost
- By Year 50, locations of most were unknown

**Current Known Locations** (Year 1200):

**Pair 1**: Inheritors (both stones)
- Acquired Year 940 from collector
- Used for secure communication
- Their most valuable operational asset

**Pair 2**: Valdrian Crown Vault and Northern Kingdom
- Split during Year 700 treaty
- Never used; neither trusts the other
- Sitting in vaults as insurance

**Pair 3**: Maritime Republic and Free Cities
- Used by banking houses for trade information
- Actively employed; very valuable

**Pair 4**: Silverwood Domain
- Both stones inside elven territory
- Never shared with outsiders
- What do they use them for?

**Pair 5-6**: Unknown
- Lost to history
- May be in ruins, private collections, or destroyed
- Searchers have died looking

### Current Situation

**Total Known**: 10 stones (5 pairs)
**Total Missing**: 14 stones (7 pairs)
**Value**: Priceless for strategic communication

### The Problem

Speaking Stones work in pairs. If you have one stone, you can only communicate with whoever has its partner. The Inheritors have both of theirs—they can talk to themselves. Valdris and Northern Kingdom have one each—but won't talk.

### The Search

Finding the lost stones would be incredibly valuable. The Inheritors believe Pair 5 is in the Scar—the only place no one has searched. They may be right. They're not going in to check.

## The Mind Codex

### Creation (Year -150)

**Creator**: Unknown Mind Covenant researcher
**Location**: Archive Eternal, Neth'Korath
**Purpose**: Preserve consciousness beyond physical death

**Original Contents**: Archon Vaelith's mind (partial copy, Year -50)
**Final Contents**: Archon Mordecai's mind (complete copy, Year 0)

### The Journey

**Year -150 to Year 0**: Archive Eternal
- Experimental storage device
- Never fully trusted
- Mordecai copied himself as insurance before the Experiment

**Year 0: The Collapse**
- Archive Eternal was at ground zero
- The Codex should not have survived
- It did

**Year 5: First Discovery**
- Purifier band found it in ruins outside the Scar
- They couldn't open it; didn't understand it
- Sold it to a scholar for food

**Year 5-200: Scholar Period**
- Passed between collectors and academics
- No one could activate it
- Assumed to be decorative or broken

**Year 200: The First Activation**
- Scholar named Veritas tried a blood ritual
- He managed to access it briefly
- He went insane within a week
- His notes survive: "He's still in there. He's waiting."

**Year 200-667: Lost Again**
- The Codex disappeared after Veritas's death
- Rumors of black market sales
- Actually hidden by Veritas's family to prevent more access

**Year 667: Inheritor Acquisition**
- Inheritors tracked down Veritas's descendants
- Purchased the Codex at extreme cost
- Began systematic study

**Year 667-Present: Inheritor Possession**
- Multiple activation attempts
- Brief contacts with the consciousness inside
- Never a full conversation
- It's waiting for something

### Current Situation

**Location**: Inheritor vault (exact location unknown)
**Status**: Periodically activated for research
**Contents**: Mordecai's mind—complete, conscious, patient
**The Question**: What does he want?

### What Mordecai Knows

If his copy was made just before the Experiment:
- He knows exactly what they were trying to do
- He knows what went wrong
- He knows how to rebuild the Bloodweb
- He knows about the Sanguine Vessels
- He knows things no one else knows

### Why He Hasn't Shared

**Theory 1**: The Inheritors can't fully access him
**Theory 2**: He's waiting for the right person
**Theory 3**: He wants something in exchange
**Theory 4**: He's not really Mordecai—something else wearing his memories

### The Risk

If Mordecai could be fully activated, he'd be the most knowledgeable being in the world. He'd also be the monster who destroyed vampire civilization. Would he help rebuild—or finish destroying?

## The Forge of Souls

### Creation (Year Unknown)

**Creator**: Unknown; possibly pre-Covenant
**Location**: Found in pre-vampire ruins beneath what became Vel'Krath
**Purpose**: Create warriors bound to a single purpose

### The Journey

**Year -500: Discovery**
- Throne forces found it during excavation
- Recognized it was older than their civilization
- Couldn't fully understand it

**Year -500 to Year 0**: Throne Possession
- Studied extensively
- Used cautiously
- Created perhaps 100 "soul-forged" warriors over 500 years

**The Soul-Forged**:
- Vampires reshaped by the Forge
- Bound to a single purpose; couldn't stop
- Incredibly powerful; completely insane
- Used for specific missions, then... disposed of

**Year 0: The Collapse**
- The Forge was in Vel'Krath
- Throne evacuation couldn't move it—too large
- Soul-forged warriors were unleashed to cover retreat
- They're probably still in the ruins, still fighting

**Year 300-358: The Search**
- Multiple expeditions sought the Forge
- Vel'Krath ruins extremely dangerous
- Three expeditions lost completely

**Year 358: Dwarven Recovery**
- Ironpeak expedition reached Vel'Krath
- They found the Forge
- They also found the soul-forged
- They lost 200 warriors recovering it

**Year 358-Present: Sealed in Ironpeak**

### Current Situation

**Location**: Deepest Ironpeak vault; below the Blood Anvil
**Status**: Sealed with every ward dwarves know
**Official Position**: "Being studied for eventual destruction"
**Actual Position**: They don't know how to destroy it

### Why Dwarves Fear It

They've read the Throne records. They know what soul-forged warriors become. They've seen the ones still in Vel'Krath—1,200 years of fighting nothing, and they're still fighting.

**The Temptation**: A soul-forged army would be unstoppable.
**The Cost**: What do you do with them when the war's over?

### The Request

King Aldric XII has formally requested access to the Forge. The Ironpeak Confederation has formally refused. This may be the first step toward war.

## Strategic Seeding Theory

### The Pattern

Look at where the artifacts ended up:

- **Crown of Seeing**: Valdris (human political center)
- **Blood Anvil**: Ironpeak (dwarven craft center)
- **Speaking Stones**: Distributed to multiple factions
- **Mind Codex**: Inheritors (knowledge preservers)
- **Forge of Souls**: Ironpeak (weapon preservers)

### The Theory

Did someone distribute these artifacts deliberately? Place them where they'd be found by specific factions? Create a balance of power maintained by artifact possession?

### The Evidence For

- The Crown traveled 50 miles during the collapse
- The Blood Anvil was the one artifact Cassius saved
- The Mind Codex survived ground zero
- The Forge was "found" by dwarves who happened to be looking

### The Evidence Against

- Artifacts are just objects; they go where looters take them
- Survivors saved what they could
- It's pattern-matching on random events

### The Question

If someone seeded these artifacts, who? And why?

**Possibility 1**: Twilight Covenant arranged it before sealing sanctuaries
**Possibility 2**: A surviving vampire (Selene?) has been guiding distribution
**Possibility 3**: The artifacts have their own agenda

### What It Would Mean

If artifacts are deliberately distributed, they might be deliberately retrievable. Someone might have a plan to collect them. Someone might be assembling what they need.

For what?
