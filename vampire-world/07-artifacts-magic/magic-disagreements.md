# Magic System Disagreements

Different cultures have different theories about magic. They argue violently about fundamental questions.

---

## The Core Questions

These are the disagreements that divide scholars, start wars, and get people burned:

1. Where does magic come from?
2. What is blood magic?
3. What happens when you die?
4. What are the other dimensions?
5. Should magic be free or controlled?

---

## Schools of Thought

### The Valdrian Academy (Orthodox Arcanism)

**Central Belief**: Magic is energy manipulation, like heat or light

**Key Tenets**
- Magic exists as ambient force
- Humans (and others) can learn to channel it
- It can be studied, measured, systematized
- It follows rules (even if we don't know them all)

**On Blood Magic**
- Blood is just a particularly powerful channel
- Not inherently evil—just dangerous
- Like fire: useful but can burn you
- Should be studied carefully, used rarely

**On Death**
- Magic and soul are connected but separate
- Soul departs, magic disperses
- Necromancy is real but limited
- Nothing truly survives death

**On Dimensions**
- Other planes exist
- They're not heaven/hell—just... elsewhere
- Can be reached with enough power
- Should probably not be reached

**On Control**
- Magic should be licensed
- Academy-trained mages only
- Prevents disasters
- Prevents abuse

**Who Believes This**
- Valdrian mages
- University scholars
- Pragmatic magic users

**Who They Fight With**
- Church (over blood magic)
- Free Cities (over control)
- Everyone (over everything)

---

### The Church of Divine Light (Sacred Theory)

**Central Belief**: Magic is divine gift OR demonic corruption

**Key Tenets**
- Good magic comes from the Divine Light
- Evil magic comes from darkness (vampires, demons)
- The source determines the morality
- You cannot use evil tools for good ends

**On Blood Magic**
- Inherently demonic
- Blood is life—taking it is taking divinity
- Anyone who uses blood magic is corrupted
- No exceptions
- No matter what they claim

**On Death**
- Soul goes to Light (if worthy)
- Soul goes to darkness (if not)
- Magic use affects judgment
- Blood magic users are damned

**On Dimensions**
- Other planes are demonic realms
- The Scar is gateway to hell
- Dimensional research is forbidden
- We should close breaches, not study them

**On Control**
- Magic should be Church-licensed
- Church mages serve the Light
- Unlicensed mages are suspect
- Vampire-derived magic is heresy

**Who Believes This**
- Church officials
- Sanctified States citizens
- Many common people

**Who They Fight With**
- Everyone else
- Especially academics
- Especially goblins

---

### Free Cities Experimental (Radical Empiricism)

**Central Belief**: Magic is poorly understood—everything is theory

**Key Tenets**
- We don't know where magic comes from
- We don't know its rules
- Everything is hypothesis
- Test everything

**On Blood Magic**
- Effective
- Dangerous
- Should be studied
- "Evil" is a meaningless label

**On Death**
- Unknown
- Maybe soul exists
- Maybe doesn't
- Study it and find out

**On Dimensions**
- Fascinating
- Should be explored
- Carefully
- But explored

**On Control**
- Magic should be free
- Regulation prevents progress
- Mages should self-govern
- The market will sort it out

**Who Believes This**
- Free Cities mages
- Radical scholars
- Inheritors (mostly)

**Who They Fight With**
- Church (over everything)
- Academy (over control)
- Order of Dawn (over existence)

---

### Northern Pragmatism (Military Application)

**Central Belief**: Magic is a weapon—use it effectively

**Key Tenets**
- Don't need to understand to use
- Results matter, theory doesn't
- Train mages like soldiers
- Serve the kingdom

**On Blood Magic**
- Effective
- Should be available to the Crown
- Moral questions are for peacetime
- We're never in peacetime

**On Death**
- Soldiers don't ask
- Mages shouldn't either
- Do your duty
- Leave philosophy to philosophers

**On Dimensions**
- Potential weapons
- Potential threats
- Study for military application
- Or don't—just kill what comes through

**On Control**
- Crown controls everything
- Including mages
- No independent mages
- Magic serves the state

**Who Believes This**
- Northern military
- Crown officials
- Practical people

**Who They Fight With**
- Free Cities (over freedom)
- Valdris (over theory)
- Elves (over everything)

---

### Goblin Blood Tradition (Sanguinary Theory)

**Central Belief**: Blood is everything—blood is memory—blood is power

**Key Tenets**
- Magic is in the blood
- Blood remembers
- Blood connects
- Everything comes from blood

**On Blood Magic**
- Not evil—essential
- The only true magic
- Others are dilutions
- We understand it because we WERE it

**On Death**
- Blood survives
- In children
- In descendants
- Nothing truly dies if blood remains

**On Dimensions**
- Blood can cross them
- Blood connects all planes
- The vampires knew this
- We remember

**On Control**
- Blood cannot be controlled
- Only channeled
- Trying to control it is why vampires fell
- Let it flow

**Who Believes This**
- Goblins
- Some human blood-mages (secretly)
- Those who've studied goblin tradition

**Who They Fight With**
- Everyone
- Especially the Church
- But also the Academy (who call it primitive)

---

### Elven... Something (Unknown)

**Central Belief**: They won't say

**What Outsiders Know**
- Elves have magic
- It's different from everyone else's
- They don't share
- They don't explain

**Theories About Elven Magic**
- Nature-based (what it looks like)
- Time-based (they live so long)
- Dimensional (they seem to know things)
- Blood-based (they were vampire-touched too)
- Something else entirely

**What Elves Say**
- Nothing
- Or cryptic things
- Or things that contradict each other
- On purpose

**The Frustration**
- They clearly have knowledge
- They clearly won't share
- They may have vampire-era secrets
- They definitely have an agenda

## The Heated Arguments

### Where Does Magic Come From?

**Academy**: Ambient energy
**Church**: Divine/demonic
**Free Cities**: We don't know
**North**: Doesn't matter
**Goblins**: Blood
**Elves**: (silence)

**Why It Matters**
- Determines morality of use
- Determines who can use it
- Determines what's possible

**The Fight**
- Church will burn you for wrong answer
- Academy will dismiss you
- Free Cities will debate forever
- North will shrug
- Goblins will laugh

---

### Is Blood Magic Evil?

**Academy**: No—dangerous, not evil
**Church**: Yes—inherently demonic
**Free Cities**: Meaningless question
**North**: Effective is what matters
**Goblins**: Only magic that's real
**Elves**: (they definitely use it; they won't admit it)

**Why It Matters**
- Church will kill blood mages
- Goblins ARE blood mages
- Many artifacts require blood
- Seven requires blood

**The Fight**
- Order of Dawn hunts blood mages
- Scholars study them secretly
- Goblins practice openly in Broken Lands
- Everyone's a hypocrite

---

### Can Vampires Be Understood?

**Academy**: Yes—they were civilization
**Church**: No—they were demons
**Free Cities**: Yes—study everything
**North**: Who cares—use what they left
**Goblins**: Yes—we remember them
**Elves**: (they definitely understand something)

**Why It Matters**
- Determines attitude toward artifacts
- Determines attitude toward Seven
- Determines future of knowledge

**The Fight**
- Church destroys knowledge
- Academy hoards knowledge
- Free Cities publishes knowledge
- North uses knowledge
- Goblins have knowledge no one else does
- Elves have knowledge they won't share

## What Was Lost

### Vampire Era Magic

**What They Could Do**
- Dimensional travel
- Perfect preservation
- Race modification
- Continent-wide networks
- Things we can't replicate

**What We've Recovered**
- Basic blood magic
- Some artifact operation
- Theoretical understanding
- Not the same

**What's Gone Forever**
- Bloodweb (required vampire infrastructure)
- Mass modification (required Bloodweb)
- Dimensional stability (required things we can't rebuild)
- Integration (we use fragments, not systems)

### Post-Collapse Development

**What's New Since Year 0**
- Human systematization
- Church magic theory
- Free Cities experimentation
- Northern military magic
- Goblin tradition formalization

**The Gap**
- We can do things vampires couldn't
- We can't do things vampires could easily
- We understand parts better
- We've lost the whole

## Why Seven Matters

### He Knows Vampire Magic

- Trained by vampires
- Understands their approach
- Can use artifacts properly
- Knows what's possible

### He's Evidence

**For Academy**: Proves vampires were understandable
**For Church**: Proves vampires were abomination
**For Free Cities**: Proves everything should be studied
**For North**: Proves power is available
**For Goblins**: Proves blood is central

**Everyone will interpret him to support their position.**

### He Could Change Everything

- If he shares knowledge: magic advances
- If he's studied: artifacts understood
- If he's killed: knowledge lost again
- If he's controlled: whoever controls him wins
