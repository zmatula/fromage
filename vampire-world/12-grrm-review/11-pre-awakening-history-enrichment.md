# GRRM Review: Enriching Pre-Awakening History

A brutal assessment of what's missing, what's too thin, and how to make 1,200 years of history feel like real history.

---

## The Core Problem

**Your history reads like a timeline, not like history.**

Real history is messy, contradictory, passionate, and human. Your timeline has events but lacks:
- **Lives lived between the dates**
- **Contested interpretations**
- **Failed movements and forgotten heroes**
- **Cultural texture and everyday experience**
- **The sense that people LIVED through this, not just experienced events**

---

## Priority 1: The Vessel Program Needs More Meat

### The Problem

Vessel Seven exists in a vacuum. We know:
- Vessels 1-6 failed
- Vessels 8-10 died in collapse
- Vessel 11 is mysterious
- Vessel 12 was incomplete

**But where are the STORIES?**

### What's Missing

#### The Other Vessels Were People

❌ **Current State**: Numbers in a list, outcomes in parentheses

✅ **What It Should Be**: Each Vessel was a person with a life before conversion:

**Vessel One** - First attempt, Year -37
- Who were they? A volunteer seeking power? A prisoner? Someone's child?
- What went wrong? Did they suffer? Did they beg for death?
- What did Velanna learn? Did she care or just take notes?

**Vessel Four** - The "almost success"
- They survived three months. What was that like?
- Did they think they'd make it?
- Did Seven meet them? Were they warned?
- How did their death affect the researchers?

**Vessel Six** - The one before Seven
- Did Seven watch them die?
- Was Seven told "don't worry, you're different"?
- Does Seven have nightmares about Six's face?

#### The Selection Process

Who were these people BEFORE?
- Were they volunteers? Prisoners? Purchased?
- Did they have families who wondered what happened?
- Were they told what would happen to them?
- Did any resist? Did any break out?

#### The Lab Culture

Vel'Thoras wasn't just Seven's chamber. What was life like?

- Were there researchers who had doubts?
- Did anyone grow attached to Vessels?
- Was there competition between research teams?
- Were there jokes? Rivalries? Affairs?
- Who cleaned up the failures?

### Specific Additions

1. **Pre-conversion memories for Seven** - Fragments that emerge. A face. A name. A place. Give him something to search for about who he was.

2. **Other Vessel journals** - Found in Vel'Thoras. Show what Vessels 4-6 thought was happening to them. Make Seven realize he could have been any of them.

3. **Researcher notes with personality** - Not just data. Velanna's frustrations. Her ambitions. Her moments of "what am I doing?"

## Priority 2: The 1,200 Years Feel Empty

### The Problem

You have a timeline from Year 0 to 1200. But where is the texture? Where are the:

- **Great individuals who shaped eras?**
- **Movements that rose and fell?**
- **Cultural moments everyone remembers?**
- **Disasters that traumatized generations?**
- **Rivalries and hatreds that lasted centuries?**

### What's Specifically Thin

#### The Dark Ages (50-300) Need More Darkness

**Current**: A list of events—plagues, lost knowledge, first translations

**Missing**:
- **False prophets**: People who claimed to know what caused the collapse. Movements that burned themselves out. Cults that sacrificed children to "prevent another collapse."
- **Warlord personalities**: Not just "warlords emerge" but WHO. A charismatic monster. A surprisingly just ruler who was assassinated for it. A woman who united three kingdoms then lost everything to betrayal.
- **The Purges in detail**: Not just "collaborator purges." Who were the collaborators? Some were just servants trying to survive. Some were willing participants. How did children of collaborators live? Were there blood feuds that lasted centuries? Are there families TODAY who hate each other over Year 8 accusations?
- **Lost art and philosophy**: What was being debated? What books were burned? What ideas died? What if there was a great thinker in Year 140 whose work was destroyed by the Church—and some secret copies exist?

#### The Warring Kingdoms (300-700) Need Personality

**Current**: Wars with dates

**Missing**:
- **The great generals and their rivalries**: Give them names. Give them personalities. One who never lost a battle but was terrible at peace. One who won through treachery and lived with it. Two friends on opposite sides.
- **The sieges people still talk about**: Not just "Valdris Expansion Wars." The siege that lasted three years where they ate the dead. The castle that held out until the last child died. The betrayal that opened the gates.
- **The treaties and their failures**: Who negotiated? What were they thinking? Why did they fail? Were there moderates on both sides who almost made peace in Year 450 but were sabotaged by extremists?
- **Common people's experience**: What was it like to be a peasant during the Goblin Wars? A merchant during the Twilight War? A mother during the Sanctified Crusades?

#### The Consolidation (700-1100) Is Suspiciously Stable

**Current**: Four centuries of... not much?

**Missing**:
- **Succession crises**: Who almost became king but didn't? What would have been different if they had?
- **Reformers and reactionaries**: People who tried to change things. People who fought to keep things the same. Debates that divided families.
- **Cultural shifts**: How did people in Year 700 differ from people in Year 1100? What were the generational conflicts? What did parents complain about their children?
- **The quiet tragedies**: A kingdom that slowly declined. A once-great family that fell. A school of thought that everyone forgot.

## Priority 3: Contested History Needs REAL Disagreement

### The Problem

You have "cultural memory matrices" and "different perspectives" but they're too polite. Real historical disagreement is VICIOUS.

### What Real Disagreement Looks Like

#### The Collapse Itself

**Current state**: Different cultures remember it differently

**Needs to be**:

**Human Position (Valdrian Orthodox)**:
- "The vampires were demons who got what they deserved"
- "Any suggestion that they achieved anything good is vampire-apologist heresy"
- "The collapse was divine punishment"
- "Anyone who studies vampire technology is trafficking with evil"

**Human Position (Valdrian Academic)**:
- "They were monsters but they had advanced civilization"
- "We can learn from their technology without worshipping them"
- "The collapse was their hubris, not divine intervention"
- "Suppressing knowledge makes us weaker, not stronger"

**These people should HATE each other.** There should be:
- Academics who were burned for heresy
- Scholars who had to flee countries
- Secret libraries protected by secret societies
- Families divided by what grandpa did during the Purges

#### Specific Events Need Multiple Versions

**Example: The Year 812 Discovery**

The timeline says: "Stasis vampire discovered and destroyed"

But this should be HUGE and CONTESTED:

**Official Version**: "Brave hunters destroyed a monster before it could wake"

**Inheritor Version**: "Idiots destroyed our one chance to understand vampire survival"

**Dawn Order Version**: "Holy warriors prevented a demonic resurrection"

**Scholarly Version**: "The specimen was damaged beyond recovery anyway"

**Conspiracy Version**: "The vampire was TAKEN, not destroyed—someone has it"

**Family Version**: "My great-grandfather was one of the hunters. He died six months later under suspicious circumstances. He knew something."

Who's right? **All of them have SOME evidence.** Characters should argue about this. Present-day politics should involve this. Maybe discovering what REALLY happened in Year 812 is a plot point.

## Priority 4: Missing Human Stories

### The Problem

History is people. Where are yours?

### Specific Additions Needed

#### Named Individuals Who Shaped Eras

Not just "warlords emerge" but:

**Cassandra the Burner** (Year 8-35)
- Led the collaborator purges in the Valdrian region
- Believed she was saving her people from corruption
- By Year 30, she was burning people on rumor
- Assassinated by her own brother who she'd condemned
- Still debated: hero or monster? People name daughters after her. People spit at her statues.

**King Aldric III "the Builder"** (Year 180-245)
- Rebuilt Valdris from warlord territory to kingdom
- Brutal but effective. Killed his brothers.
- His laws still used. His borders still defended.
- Every king since claims his legacy—including the ones he'd have hated.

**The Three Scholars of Ironmount** (Year 278)
- First successful vampire text translation
- One was murdered for it. One recanted. One disappeared.
- Their work survived. Their names are controversial.
- Churches say they're damned. Academics say they're founders.

#### Failed Movements and Forgotten Heroes

**The Dawn Rebellion** (Year 512)
- A general who tried to stop the Goblin Wars
- Believed goblins could be integrated, not exterminated
- Had evidence that goblins were fellow victims of vampires
- Executed for treason. His ideas died with him.
- What if he was RIGHT? What if characters discover his writings?

**The Rationalist Movement** (Year 880-920)
- Scholars who tried to apply vampire-style logic to society
- Were they proto-Inheritors? Dangerous radicals? Visionaries?
- The Church destroyed them completely
- But fragments of their work survive...

## Priority 5: The Seven Years Before Collapse

### The Problem

Vessel Seven was conscious for seven years before stasis. This is MASSIVE unexplored territory.

### What He Experienced (Year -7 to Year 0)

#### His Training

Not just "basic vampire skills" but:
- Who trained him? Did he like them? Did they survive?
- Did he have something like friends among the researchers?
- What was his daily routine?
- Did he ever leave Vel'Thoras?
- Did he see the empire? Meet other vampires?

#### What He Witnessed

- He was alive when Vessel Eight, Nine, and Ten were created
- He may have known them
- He may have watched them prepare for the collapse
- Did he understand what was coming?
- Did anyone tell him?

#### The Final Days

- What was happening at Vel'Thoras before the collapse?
- Were people panicking? In denial? Evacuating?
- Who put him in stasis? Just Velanna?
- What were her last words to him?
- Did she think she'd see him again?

### Specific Scene Potential

**Year -1: The Last Normal Day**
- Seven has a conversation with another researcher
- They discuss something mundane
- He'll remember this conversation forever
- That researcher died the next week

**Day 0, Hour 3: The Emergency**
- Seven is pulled from training
- Everything is happening fast
- Velanna is scared—he's never seen her scared
- She puts him under with incomplete information
- His last sight of the world is her face

These memories—not as full flashbacks but as fragments—give him specific things to mourn, regret, and search for.

## Priority 6: The Immediate Aftermath Needs Gore

### The Problem

"Year 1-50: Chaos and liberation" is too clean.

### What Actually Happened

#### The First Year Was Horror

- Mass starvation (Bloodweb also distributed food)
- Panic, violence, score-settling
- Vampires who survived being hunted and eaten
- Collaborators being tortured for information they didn't have
- Children orphaned, women raped, cities burned
- Diseases from unburied dead

**Specific numbers that hurt:**
- Population before collapse: ~50 million servants
- Population Year 1: ~40 million
- Population Year 5: ~20 million
- Population Year 10: ~10 million

Half the world died in ten years. That's not a transition. That's an apocalypse.

#### The Purges Were Worse Than You've Written

- Not just "collaborator purges" but systematic butchery
- People being accused by their neighbors
- Families denouncing each other to survive
- Knowledge being destroyed because it was "tainted"
- The last speakers of vampire tongue being killed
- Libraries burned because they contained vampire words

**Specific horror story:**
A healer knew vampire medical techniques. She saved hundreds of lives after the collapse. When they discovered how she knew, they burned her. Then their children died of diseases she could have cured.

## Priority 7: The Secret Societies Need Origin Stories

### The Problem

Inheritors, Dawn Order, Veil Keepers appear as established organizations. But how did they start?

### The Inheritors

**Current**: "Form in Year 210 around artifacts"

**Needs**:
- **The founder**: A scholar who survived the purges by hiding
- **The original purpose**: Not just "preserve knowledge" but "save humanity from making the same mistakes"
- **The early years**: Meeting in basements, using code phrases, watching members be arrested
- **The ideological split**: Some wanted to USE the knowledge. Some wanted to preserve it. Some wanted to RESTORE vampire civilization. This split should still exist.
- **The compromises**: Things they've done that haunt them. Assassinations. Sabotage. Betraying their own principles.

### The Order of Dawn

**Current**: "Receives charter in Year 780"

**Needs**:
- **The founder**: A genuine hero who saw real vampires and fought them
- **The early mission**: Legitimate protection of the innocent
- **The corruption**: When did "protect" become "control"? Who made that call? Were they right?
- **The internal conflicts**: Moderates vs extremists. Those who remember the original mission vs those who've only known the organization.

## Priority 8: Specific Enrichment Targets

### Events That Need Full Scenes

| Event | Current | Needs |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Year 812 Discovery | One line | Full chapter of implications. Who found it? Who destroyed it? What did they learn? What did they hide? |
| Great Plague Year 168 | "20% population loss" | What was it like? Who died? How did society change? Are there still mass graves? |
| Year 278 First Translation | One line | Drama of discovery. The three scholars. The persecution. The survival of the work. |
| Year 350 Great Schism | One line | What was the theological dispute? What were the arguments? Why does it still matter? |
| Year 950-1000 Shadow Wars | One line | What were secret societies fighting over? Who died? What was gained/lost? |

### Missing Cultural Details

- **Songs people sing** - Ballads about the collapse. Drinking songs about wars. Lullabies with dark origins.
- **Holidays and remembrances** - Days of mourning. Celebrations of liberation. Regional differences.
- **Common sayings** - "Don't dig too deep" (fear of vampire ruins). "Blood remembers" (goblin saying).
- **Social customs** - How do you show you're not a vampire? What gestures are offensive because vampires used them?

### Missing Economic History

- What's valuable and why?
- How did trade routes develop?
- What economic crises shaped politics?
- How did artifact trade affect everything?

## The Brutal Truth

### What Works

- The collapse itself is well-designed
- The vampire civilization has good bones
- The present-day factions are solid
- The Vessel concept is strong

### What Doesn't Work

- **The timeline reads like a wiki**, not like lived experience
- **There are no individuals** between Year 0 and 1200 that I care about
- **The history is too clean** - not enough failure, contradiction, and messiness
- **Vessel Seven exists in a vacuum** - his seven years awake are almost entirely blank
- **The post-collapse is underpopulated** - both demographically and narratively

### The Core Issue

You've built a world. Now you need to fill it with people.

---

## Revision Priorities

### Must Do (Critical for story to work)

1. **Flesh out Vessel Seven's seven years** - Specific memories, relationships, experiences
2. **Create 3-5 named individuals per era** - People who shaped history, who are still debated
3. **Write the Year 812 discovery in full** - It's a direct precedent for Seven's awakening
4. **Detail the immediate aftermath** - Years 0-10 with real horror and specificity

### Should Do (Significantly improves depth)

5. **Give the other Vessels identity** - Especially 4-6 who Seven may have known
6. **Develop secret society origins** - With founders, splits, and compromises
7. **Add contested history specifics** - Make scholars actually disagree about real events
8. **Create cultural texture** - Songs, sayings, holidays, customs

### Could Do (Enhancement)

9. **Economic history**
10. **Failed movements and forgotten heroes**
11. **More post-collapse named individuals**

## Questions to Answer

### About Vessel Seven

- Does he remember who he was before conversion? (Even fragments?)
- Did he know any other Vessels personally?
- Who was his favorite person at Vel'Thoras?
- What's his last memory before stasis?

### About the Vampire Era

- Were there ANY vampires who had doubts about the system?
- Did any vampires try to warn about the coming collapse?
- What was Velanna's personality beyond "researcher"?
- Did she have family? Friends? Rivals? A lover?

### About the Post-Collapse

- Who's the most famous individual from Year 0-300?
- What's the Year 168 plague called?
- What's the most controversial event of Year 300-700?
- Who founded the Valdrian Empire specifically?

If you can't answer these questions, your history isn't finished.

---

*GRRM didn't just create events—he created people living through events. Westeros feels real because we know who killed whom, who loved whom, who betrayed whom, and why it still matters. Your world needs that same density of human experience.*

**This history should make readers argue about who was right.**
