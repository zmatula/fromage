# TEMPORAL CAUSALITY AUDIT REPORT

## Executive Summary

The vampire-world project demonstrates **strong foundational causality work** with excellent consequence chains documented. However, several critical causality gaps exist, particularly around demographic impacts, knowledge loss patterns, and character-historical connections.

---

## 1. CRITICAL BREAKS - Cause-Effect Chains That Are Broken

### 1.1 The 40 Million Dead - Untraced Demographic Catastrophe

**The Problem**: The project references 40 million deaths from the Collapse, but this massive demographic shift lacks proper forward-rippling.

**Missing Causality**:
- Which regions were depopulated most severely?
- How did labor shortages affect reconstruction?
- What knowledge was lost because practitioners died?
- How did gender/age imbalances affect post-Collapse society?
- Where are the "dead zones" where no one survived to pass on traditions?

**Recommendation**: Add a "Demographic Consequences" section tracking regional death rates, skills lost, marriage pattern disruptions, and land abandonment.

### 1.2 The Knowledge Destruction Gap

**The Problem**: The project states knowledge was deliberately destroyed, but doesn't trace which specific knowledge gaps in Year 1200 result from which specific destruction events.

**Missing Causality Chain**: What SPECIFIC Year 1200 problems exist because of SPECIFIC knowledge lost in Years 1-100?

**Recommendation**: Create explicit chains like:
- "Bloodweb medical applications knowledge → destroyed Year 12 → Year 1200 healing magic is primitive"
- "Dimensional barrier repair techniques → burned Year 35 → Year 1200 no one can fix The Scar"

### 1.3 The Warlord Succession Gap

**The Problem**: The master timeline mentions first warlords (Year 0-1) like Thresh, Mara, General Koss, but their rise/fall doesn't connect to modern noble houses.

**Missing Causality**: Did any warlord dynasties survive? Which Year 1200 noble families descend from which Year 0-50 leaders?

### 1.4 Marcus Ashford's "Plague Nobility" Connection

**The Problem**: Consequence-chains.md states Marcus Ashford's family rose in plague aftermath (Year 168), but Marcus's character file shows no awareness of this history.

**The Break**: Character doesn't reflect established worldbuilding.

**Recommendation**: Add plague nobility background to Marcus's file, show how Order internal politics reflect class tensions.

### 1.5 The Year 1050 Silverwood Closure

**The Problem**: Silverwood closed borders completely in Year 1050, but no one knows why. This should have cascading consequences that aren't explicitly traced.

**Missing Consequences**: What happened to elven trade goods? Which human industries collapsed? Are there half-elves from before 1050 now cut off from heritage?

## 2. MISSED CONNECTIONS - Events That Should Impact Others But Don't

### 2.1 Year 812 Vampire Discovery → Year 1200 Politics

**The Event**: Living vampire discovered in stasis, questioned for six days, destroyed. Records sealed.

**What's Missing**: The political consequences of this cover-up should still be active.

**Proposed Connections**:
- The lord who was "bought off" with lands → his family now controls key territory
- The murdered miner's family became Veil Keepers
- The questioning records in High Priest's vault → current Church faction fights
- The three surviving miners' families have oral traditions

### 2.2 Goblin Wars Genocide → Year 1200 Demographics

**The Event**: Goblin Wars (Year 420-520), described as ethnic cleansing with millions dead.

**What's Missing**: Specific demographic consequences for Year 1200. Goblins still haven't recovered after 680 years.

**Implications Not Traced**: Shamanic knowledge lost with elders, tribal territories lost, gender imbalances, "lost tribes" exterminated entirely.

### 2.3 Great Plague (Year 168) → Medical Magic Taboo

**The Event**: Great Plague kills 20% of population. Medical magic blamed for not preventing it.

**What's Missing**: Specific Year 1200 diseases untreatable that vampires could cure.

### 2.4 Dimensional Pollution (Year -61 to -32) → Year 1178 Readings

**The Event**: Mind Covenant discovered dimensional pollution at critical levels, suppressed it.

**What's Missing**: Explicit connection showing Year 1178 readings are the same phenomenon building for 1,261 years.

### 2.5 Aurelian Code Legal Dominance → Present Political Crises

**The Event**: Valdrian legal system (Year -1175) became continental standard because vampires preserved it.

**What's Missing**: How this legal framework shapes Year 1200 crises. Succession claimants arguing specific provisions.

### 2.6 House Blackwater Origins → Year 1200 Banking Coup

**The Event**: House Blackwater founded Year 620, descended from Sera Blackwater.

**What's Missing**: Who was Sera Blackwater? Why does her lineage matter?

## 5. SUMMARY

The project has excellent consequence chain documentation but needs:

1. **Specific demographic tracing** of the 40 million deaths
2. **Named connections** between historical events and present-day people/places
3. **Character historical grounding** especially for Marcus and Gritha
4. **Artifact provenance completion** for all major items
5. **Explicit causal statements** connecting pre-Collapse problems to Year 1200

The goal: make it impossible to discuss any Year 1200 situation without referencing its historical roots. Currently, about 70% of that web exists. The remaining 30% needs the specific names, dates, and connections that transform worldbuilding from "things happened" to "everything is connected."

**GRRM Test**: *"Pulling any single thread should lead to discovering twenty others."*
