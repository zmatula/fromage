# Humans

The most numerous race, dominant in most territories, and possessing the strongest belief in their own centrality to history.

---

## Overview

**Population**: ~30 million (Year 1200)
**Lifespan**: 60-80 years
**Distribution**: Dominant in four empires; minorities elsewhere
**Character**: Ambitious, adaptable, short-sighted, numerous

---

## Sub-Races and Cultures

### Valdrian (Eastern)
- Conservative, traditional, proud
- Claim descent from pre-vampire royalty
- Strong military and scholarly traditions
- Look down on other humans as "younger"

### Northern (Formerly Throne Territory)
- Martial culture, military aristocracy
- Live on vampire Throne lands
- Fascinated by and afraid of ruins
- "Strength through discipline"

### Sanctified (Central-South)
- Religious, conservative, suspicious
- Church of Divine Light center
- Anti-magic sentiment
- "Divine favor guides us"

### Maritime (Coastal)
- Cosmopolitan, merchant culture
- Most tolerant of other races
- Pragmatic about artifacts
- "Trade over ideology"

### Provincial (Various)
- Rural, traditional, superstitious
- Strong local variations
- Most superstitious about vampires
- "Old ways are safe ways"

## Origin Story

### What Humans Believe
"We were created by the gods in their image. We are the chosen race, meant to rule this world. We had great kingdoms before vampires rose—the Holy Human Empire. Vampires conquered us through treachery and dark magic, not superiority. When the gods struck them down, we reclaimed our rightful place. Divine favor explains our dominance."

### Elements of Truth
- Humans did have kingdoms pre-vampires
- They were the largest servant population
- They did rebuild most successfully post-collapse

### Elements of Myth
- No "Holy Human Empire" existed
- Gods didn't strike down vampires
- Human dominance is demographic and geographic, not divine

### The Shadow Truth
Humans were significantly modified during vampire rule—enhanced for labor, some given magical sensitivity for administrator roles. Current humans are not identical to pre-vampire humans. The "chosen race" narrative compensates for deep insecurity about their servitude.

---

## Relationship with Vampires

### During Vampire Rule
- Largest servant population (40% of subjects)
- Primary labor force
- Administrator class developed
- Some given limited magical training
- Multiple rebellions, all suppressed
- Treated as valuable livestock

### What They Remember
- Enslavement and blood-drinking
- Rebellions and martyrs
- "Optimization" programs (breeding, modification)
- Collaborators who served willingly
- The day they rose up

### Current View
- Vampires were demons/monsters
- Their rule was hell
- Human resistance was heroic
- Artifacts are tools reclaimed from evil
- Any survivor must be destroyed

---

## Collapse Narrative

### The Story Humans Tell
"The gods saw vampire evil and struck them down with holy fire. The righteous were freed. Human heroes led the liberation. We built new kingdoms in a purified world. The Scar is divine judgment. Our civilization is blessed."

### Political Function
- Justifies Church of Divine Light authority
- Supports human racial supremacy
- Discourages artifact study
- Makes questioning dangerous

### Who Benefits
- Church hierarchy
- Human nobility
- Anyone who wants ruins left alone
- Those hiding collaborator ancestry

### Core Values
1. **Divine Favor**: Humans are chosen; success proves it
2. **Hierarchy**: Natural order of rulers and ruled
3. **Tradition**: Old ways are proven ways
4. **Expansion**: Growth is destiny

### Virtues

### Sins

- Magic abuse

## Present-Day Culture

### Government
- Four major forms: Monarchy (Valdrian), Elective Military (Northern), Theocracy (Sanctified), Oligarchy (Maritime)
- Noble class in most kingdoms
- Professional bureaucracy
- Standing armies

### Religion
- Church of Divine Light dominant
- Minor cults and mystery schools
- Ancestor veneration in rural areas
- Anti-magic sentiment (varies)

### Economy
- Agricultural base
- Growing merchant class
- Artifact trade (controversial)
- Dwarven trade dependency

### Social Structure
- Nobility (5%)
- Clergy (3%)
- Merchants/Artisans (15%)
- Peasants/Workers (77%)
- Movement possible but difficult

## Relationship with Ruins and Artifacts

### Official Position
- Ruins are dangerous and cursed
- Artifacts are tools from a dark age
- Study should be controlled
- Some should be destroyed

### Actual Practice
- Ruins are treasure troves
- Artifacts are power
- Royal collections are prestigious
- Scholars study secretly

### Common Beliefs
- Ruins are haunted
- Artifacts can corrupt
- Vampires might return
- Some knowledge is forbidden

- Nobility vs. merchants (rising power)

### Religious Conflicts

- Mystery schools vs. Church
- Magic users vs. anti-magic faction

- Traditionalist vs. modernizer

## Secrets Humans Keep

### From Other Races
- Some noble families have vampire blood
- Collaborator purges killed useful knowledge
- "Divine punishment" story is political
- Church knows more than it admits

### From Each Other
- Noble lineage fraud is common
- Church hierarchy is corrupt
- Artifacts are more useful than admitted
- Human unity is mythology

### From Themselves
- Pre-vampire "golden age" is invented
- Human dominance isn't divine
- Other races have legitimate grievances
- Vampires weren't all evil
