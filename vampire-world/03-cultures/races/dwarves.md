# Dwarves

The practical race that kept the best vampire technical knowledge and built economic power through industrial might and strategic neutrality.

---

## Overview

**Population**: ~4 million (Year 1200)
**Lifespan**: 200-350 years
**Distribution**: Mountain holds, mining colonies, some urban
**Character**: Practical, stubborn, industrious, isolationist

---

## Sub-Races and Cultures

### Ironpeak Dwarves
- Dominant hold and confederation
- Industrial powerhouse
- Control best mines
- "The mountain endures"

### Deep Dwarves
- Ancient holds, deepest underground
- Most traditional and isolated
- Rumored to preserve darkest knowledge
- "Stone remembers"

### Surface Dwarves
- Integrated with human kingdoms
- Craftspeople, merchants, engineers
- Somewhat looked down on
- "Trade builds strength"

### Mining Colonies
- Remote extraction operations
- Rough, practical culture
- Frontier mentality
- "Ore before politics"

## Origin Story

### What Dwarves Believe
"We were born from stone when the mountains formed. The deep earth made us in its image—hard, enduring, patient. Our first holds existed before vampires rose. We built ourselves, taught ourselves, and keep our own counsel. Others come and go; we remain."

### Elements of Truth
- Dwarves had underground civilization pre-vampires
- They did maintain more independence than others
- They kept the most technical knowledge

### Elements of Myth
- They were conquered and served like others
- Some holds made deals with vampires
- "Self-taught" includes vampire-given knowledge

### The Shadow Truth
Dwarves were "optimized" for endurance and spatial reasoning. Their legendary toughness is partly vampire enhancement. They served as engineers and were given technical knowledge others weren't—and they kept it. They were the least mistreated because they were most useful.

---

## Relationship with Vampires

### During Vampire Rule
- 15% of servant population
- Engineers, miners, builders
- Given more autonomy than others
- Worked in underground, harder to control
- Made deals for better treatment
- Respected for skills but still property

### What They Remember
- Being useful enough to be left alone
- The deals they made
- Technical knowledge they were given
- Underground expansion they built
- The advantage of being essential

### Current View
- Vampires were good engineers but poor managers
- Their technology is worth studying
- Don't make enemies of underground things
- Practical lessons over moral judgments

## Collapse Narrative

### The Story Dwarves Tell
"Vampires built too much too fast on bad foundations. Deferred maintenance, overreliance on magic, classic failure modes. When one system broke, everything broke. We saw it coming and prepared. We secured the mines and waited while others fought. Smart."

### Political Function
- Positions dwarves as practical survivors
- Justifies isolationism
- Supports technical authority
- Avoids collaboration judgment

### Who Benefits
- Ironpeak leadership
- Engineering class
- Those who made deals
- Neutral parties in current conflicts

## Cultural Values

### Core Values
1. **Craft**: Quality work is honor
2. **Clan**: Family loyalty above all
3. **Stone**: Endurance and permanence
4. **Trade**: Fair exchange builds wealth
5. **Memory**: Debts and oaths never forgotten

### Virtues
- Reliability
- Craftsmanship
- Honesty (in deals)
- Endurance
- Loyalty

### Sins
- Shoddy work
- Broken oaths
- Wasted resources
- Excessive trust in outsiders
- Forgetting grudges

## Historical Heroes and Villains

### Heroes

**High King Thoram Ironpeak** (Year 3-67)
- United holds post-collapse
- Founded Ironpeak Confederation
- "The Builder"

**Master Smith Dolgrim** (Year 358)
- Found/activated Forge of Souls
- "Father of Blood-Steel"
- Revolutionized dwarven metallurgy

- Mediated peace after Great Northern War
- "The Peacemaker"
- Established dwarven neutrality

### Villains

**Deep King Moradin III** (Year -400 to -200)
- Made extensive deals with vampires
- "The Bargainer"
- Gave them too much

**Merchant Prince Glorin** (Year 500-560)
- Sold weapons to both sides in wars
- "The Profiteer"

## Present-Day Culture

### Government
- Confederation of holds
- High King (elected) leads council
- Each hold has autonomy
- Consensus-based major decisions

### Religion
- Ancestor and craft-god worship
- Rune magic has religious elements
- Deep holds have mysterious practices
- Less organized than humans

### Economy
- Mining and metallurgy
- Weapons manufacturing
- Banking and trade
- Arms deals with all powers

### Social Structure
- Clans (extended families)
- Guilds (crafts)
- Holds (geography)
- Cross-cutting loyalties

## Relationship with Ruins and Artifacts

### Official Position
- Technical study is valuable
- Artifacts are engineering to understand
- Some things should stay buried
- Practical use over theoretical study

### Actual Practice
- Have best vampire technical knowledge
- Forge of Souls is functional vampire artifact
- Deep holds have sealed chambers
- Trade artifacts but keep best

### The Great Secret
The Forge of Souls should probably be destroyed but it's too useful. They've used vampire technology extensively. Some deep holds have things they've never revealed.
