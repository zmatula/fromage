# Vampires (What Remains)

The extinct race that ruled the world, destroyed themselves, and left behind ruins, artifacts, and one experimental survivor who will wake into a world that has forgotten and mythologized them.

---

## Overview

**Population**: ~500 estimated survivors (Year 1200)
**Lifespan**: Immortal (unless killed)
**Distribution**: Stasis, deep hiding, sanctuaries, other dimensions
**Character**: (Remembered as) Powerful, immortal, blood-drinking, alien

---

## What Existed

### Vampire Civilization at Peak
- 15 million population
- Three covenants
- 1,000+ year history
- Advanced magic/technology
- Dominated all other races
- Bloodweb infrastructure

### What Made Them Vampires
- Immortality (no natural death)
- Blood feeding required
- Sunlight vulnerability
- Enhanced strength/senses
- Magical affinity
- Regeneration

### How They Differed from Current Understanding
- They were people, not demons
- Complex society with factions
- Varied morally like any race
- Built a civilization, not just ruled
- Had philosophy, art, love, and fear
- Made mistakes like anyone

---

## What the Protagonist Remembers

### His Direct Experience (Year -7 to 0)
- Laboratory in Vel'Thoras
- Other Vessels (siblings of sorts)
- Researchers who created him
- Training and testing
- Being told he was special
- Being told he was insurance

### What He Was Told
- Vampire civilization was eternal
- The Bloodweb was everything
- Other races were servants (inferior)
- Research would solve all problems
- He would survive what others couldn't
- His memories were given to help him

### What He Knows Is Missing
- He has memories he didn't make
- Some memories are clearly implanted
- His earliest "memories" aren't his
- He doesn't remember his creation clearly
- He doesn't know why he specifically

### What He Doesn't Know
- How bad things really were
- That collapse was imminent
- That he was insurance because they knew
- What happened to others
- What he was really supposed to do
- Why his memories were shaped as they were

---

## Vampire Self-Image

### What Vampires Believed About Themselves
- Most evolved beings
- Meant to transcend all limitations
- Natural rulers of lesser races
- Seekers of truth and power
- Eternal and thus patient
- Superior in all ways

### What Was Actually True
- Very powerful but not omnipotent
- Created dependencies that killed them
- Varied morally like anyone
- Made terrible mistakes
- Fear and hubris drove collapse
- Not as eternal as they believed

### What the Protagonist Will Learn
His implanted memories give him vampire civilization's self-image. Reality will be more complex and disturbing. His people were neither demons nor gods—they were people who had too much power and too little wisdom.

---

## Vampire Cultural Values

### What They Valued
1. **Eternity**: Think in centuries, not years
2. **Power**: Capability proves worth
3. **Knowledge**: Understanding is control
4. **Beauty**: Aesthetic achievement matters
5. **Blood**: Connection, sustenance, identity

### What They Justified
- Servant race subjugation (for their own good)
- Breeding programs (optimization)
- Experimentation on lesser races (necessary progress)
- Bloodweb dependency (inevitable improvement)
- Covenant conflict (natural competition)

### What They Missed
- Sustainability
- Cooperation
- Humility
- Cost/benefit wisdom
- Concern for others

---

## Vampire Divisions

### The Three Covenants (In Vampire View)

**Mind Covenant** (Protagonist's origin):
- "Seekers of transcendence"
- "Leaders of progress"
- "Keepers of knowledge"
- *Also: arrogant, secretive, responsible for collapse*

**Throne Covenant**:
- "Guardians of order"
- "Sword of the Dominion"
- "Maintainers of strength"
- *Also: brutal, paranoid, planning coup*

**Twilight Covenant**:
- "Preservers of the true way"
- "Seers of what must come"
- "Keepers of tradition"
- *Also: passive, superior, abandoned others*

### Internal Conflicts Protagonist May Not Understand
- Covenants were in cold war
- Mind knew risks and proceeded anyway
- Throne was days from military coup
- Twilight let it happen to prove themselves right
