# Elves

The long-lived race that claims ancient wisdom, preserves forbidden knowledge, and maintains dangerous isolation.

---

## Overview

**Population**: ~4 million (Year 1200)
**Lifespan**: 300-600 years
**Distribution**: Silverwood Domain, urban enclaves, scholar communities
**Character**: Long-lived, knowledgeable, arrogant, secretive

---

## Sub-Races and Cultures

### Silverwood Elves
- Dominant elven culture
- Forest isolationists
- Preserve ancient knowledge
- "We endure; others pass"

### Urban Elves
- Live in human cities
- Scholars, advisors, craftspeople
- More integrated but marginalized
- "Knowledge serves survival"

### Wild Elves
- Rejected civilization post-collapse
- Forest tribes
- Hostile to outsiders
- "The old ways are only ways"

### Scholar Elves
- Dedicated to knowledge preservation
- Work across cultures
- Secretive about sources
- "Truth over politics"

---

## Origin Story

### What Elves Believe
"We grew with the forests when the world was young. We are nature's children, connected to the land in ways no other race can be. We existed long before vampires and will exist long after all others fade. Our wisdom comes from centuries of observation, not from books or masters. We are the true inheritors of this world."

### Elements of Truth
- Elves did exist before vampire rule
- They do have longer lives and memories
- They did preserve significant knowledge

### Elements of Myth
- Elves were also modified by vampires
- "Natural wisdom" includes vampire techniques
- They were collaborators as much as victims

### The Shadow Truth
Elves were "optimized" for magical sensitivity and fine crafts. Their long lifespans are partly vampire enhancement. Their "ancient wisdom" includes vampire knowledge they won't admit they have. They were privileged servants, not noble prisoners.

## Relationship with Vampires

### During Vampire Rule
- 20% of servant population
- Craftspeople and magical assistants
- Treated "better" (patronizing benevolence)
- Some given significant magical training
- Closer to vampires than other races
- Deep shame about this

### What They Remember
- Being treated as "almost equals"
- The betrayal of false respect
- Beautiful things they made for masters
- Knowledge they were given
- The privilege they had

### Current View
- Vampires were advanced but flawed
- Not demons—but dangerous people
- Their knowledge is worth preserving
- Their return would be bad
- Humans are worse in some ways

---

## Collapse Narrative

### The Story Elves Tell
"The vampire covenants destroyed themselves through inability to cooperate. We could have helped if they'd asked, but they were too arrogant. We preserved what we could while others burned libraries. We carry the burden of memory that shorter-lived races cannot."

### Political Function
- Positions elves as wise preservers
- Justifies secrecy about knowledge
- Explains why elves know more
- Distances from collaborator guilt

### Who Benefits
- Silverwood leadership
- Scholar class
- Those hiding collaboration extent
- Elves who want to feel superior

## Cultural Values

### Core Values
1. **Patience**: Time solves what haste breaks
2. **Knowledge**: Understanding over ignorance
3. **Nature**: Connection to the living world
4. **Tradition**: Old ways have proven worth
5. **Privacy**: Not everything should be shared

### Virtues
- Wisdom
- Restraint
- Craftsmanship
- Memory
- Discretion

### Sins
- Impulsiveness
- Willful ignorance
- Destruction of knowledge
- Betrayal of secrets
- Forgetting the past

## Present-Day Culture

### Government
- Silverwood: Elder council with High Elder
- Enclaves: Varies; often defer to hosts
- Tribal: Chieftains
- Border closed since Year 1050

### Religion
- Nature worship
- Ancestor veneration
- Forest spirits
- No organized church

### Economy
- Silverwood: Self-sufficient, limited trade
- Urban: Crafts, scholarship, service
- Independent of human economy (mostly)

### Social Structure
- Elders (knowledge = status)
- Warriors
- Craftspeople
- Common elves
- More fluid than humans

---

## Relationship with Ruins and Artifacts

### Official Position
- Ruins should be studied carefully
- Artifacts are dangerous tools
- Knowledge should be preserved
- Not all should be shared

### Actual Practice
- Silverwood has best vampire knowledge
- Secret libraries exist
- Some artifacts functional and used
- Share carefully with scholars

### The Great Secret
Elves can read vampire script. They've never told humans. They know far more than they admit.

## Secrets Elves Keep
