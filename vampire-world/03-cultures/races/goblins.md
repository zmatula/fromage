# Goblins

The marginalized race that suffered most under vampires, preserves dangerous shamanic knowledge, and harbors centuries of justified rage.

---

## Overview

**Population**: ~5 million (Year 1200)
**Lifespan**: 30-50 years
**Distribution**: Wastelands, swamps, urban underclass
**Character**: Resilient, bitter, cunning, underestimated

---

## Sub-Races and Cultures

### Swamp Goblins
- Traditional homeland
- Shamanic traditions strongest
- Most organized
- "The swamp hides, the swamp provides"

### Wasteland Goblins
- Nomadic raiders
- Hardened survivors
- Most aggressive
- "Take what you need"

### Urban Goblins
- Underclass in human cities
- Despised but necessary
- Most assimilated
- "Survive any way you can"

### Mountain Goblins
- Compete with dwarves underground
- Tunnel warfare specialists
- Grudging dwarven respect
- "We were here first"

## Origin Story

### What Goblins Believe
"The spirits made us from swamp and shadow when the world was wet. We were the first people, free and blessed by the spirit world. We had great shamans and sacred places. Then came the blood-drinkers who captured us, bred us like cattle, and cut us open. But we remembered. We kept the old ways. And we will have our reckoning."

### Elements of Truth
- Goblins did have pre-vampire culture
- They were treated worst of all races
- Shamanic traditions are very old

### Elements of Myth
- Not first people
- Swamp homeland was limited
- Traditions include vampire knowledge

### The Shadow Truth
Goblins were the most heavily modified by vampires—used as breeding stock and experimental subjects. Current goblins barely resemble pre-vampire ancestors. Their population was artificially inflated. Their "ancient" shamanic traditions include vampire blood magic given to them deliberately. The deepest wound is also the deepest knowledge.

## Relationship with Vampires

### During Vampire Rule
- 25% of servant population
- Menial labor and experimental subjects
- Bred like livestock
- Used in dimensional experiments
- Treated as barely sentient
- Worst of all races

### What They Remember
- Being bred and culled
- Cut open for experiments
- Children taken
- Starvation and cruelty
- Absolute dehumanization

### Current View
- Vampires were monsters, nothing else
- There was nothing good about them
- Anyone who says otherwise is enemy
- If they return, fight to death

## Collapse Narrative

### The Story Goblins Tell
"We rose up. Our shamans turned vampire magic against them. We poisoned their blood, sabotaged their machines, killed them in their sleep. The fire from the sky was our doing. They fled underground where they still hide, afraid of us. We ended them, and we will end them again."

### Political Function
- Gives goblins agency in their liberation
- Justifies shamanic power
- Creates heroic founding narrative
- Supports continued resistance

### Who Benefits
- Shamanic leadership
- Goblin identity/pride
- Anyone who wants to fight current powers
- Those preserving dangerous knowledge

## Cultural Values

### Core Values
1. **Survival**: Endure above all else
2. **Memory**: Never forget, never forgive
3. **Spirits**: The otherworld gives power
4. **Tribe**: Your people are everything
5. **Revenge**: Debts must be paid

### Virtues
- Resilience
- Cunning
- Loyalty to tribe
- Remembering wrongs
- Spiritual connection

### Sins
- Collaboration
- Forgetting grievances
- Betraying tribe
- Abandoning traditions
- Trusting outsiders

## Relationship with Ruins and Artifacts

### Official Position
- Ruins are vampire evil
- Artifacts are dangerous
- Some knowledge is power
- Never trust surface study

### Actual Practice
- Shamans know vampire techniques
- Blood magic is vampire-derived

### The Great Secret
Goblins know more vampire knowledge than anyone suspects. Their shamanic blood magic is vampire magic. They can read some vampire script. They know where Twilight sanctuaries are.
