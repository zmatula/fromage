# Religious Syncretism

1,200 years of interaction produces religious mixing despite official boundaries.

---

## Church of Divine Light

### Elven Elements (Unacknowledged)
- Solstice festivals (rebranded as saints' days)
- Forest shrine traditions (now "hermit saints")
- Nature imagery in hymns

### Dwarven Elements
- Ancestor commemoration (All Souls observance)
- Craft blessing rituals
- Emphasis on oaths and witness

### Vampire-Era Survivals
- Hierarchical structure mirrors covenant organization
- Lighting rituals echo vampire practices
- "Communion" concept has uncomfortable origins

### Internal Tension
The Scrolls faction knows about these borrowings. The Swords faction refuses to acknowledge them. This is part of why they conflict.

---

## Dwarven Ancestor Worship

### Human Influences
- Saints-like veneration of legendary ancestors
- Pilgrimage to ancestral holds
- Offerings that parallel Church practices

### Elven Influences
- Nature spirits recognized in deep places
- Certain meditation practices

### Why They Don't Admit It
Admitting human influence would undermine dwarven "purity" narrative. But deep hold practices clearly show it.

---

## Elven Nature Worship

### Vampire-Era Additions
- "Spirits" that match dimensional entity descriptions
- Rituals with blood magic elements
- Practices that require ambient energy

### What They Know
The elves know their religion includes vampire elements. They consider it "refined" rather than "borrowed." This is part of their collaborator shame.

---

## Goblin Shamanism

### Vampire Blood Magic (Adapted)
- Techniques are clearly vampire-derived
- But mythology is purely goblin
- Shamans know the truth; tribes don't

### The Tension
Using oppressor's tools against oppressors. Gritha lives this contradiction daily.
