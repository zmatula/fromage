# Cultural Contamination Matrix

1,200 years of contact leaves fingerprints. This documents what each culture absorbed from others.

---

## Human ↔ Elf

### What Humans Absorbed
**Vocabulary**:
- "Arcanum" (elven: arkha-num) - magical study
- "Sylvan" - anything forest-related
- "Twilight" - liminal times/spaces

**Practices**:
- Academic citation methods (Valdrian universities)
- Tea ceremony (noble houses)
- Seasonal festivals (solstice celebrations)

**What They Won't Admit**:
- "Traditional" noble greetings are elven court forms
- Musical scales used in Church hymns are elven

### What Elves Absorbed
**From Urban Living**:
- Clock-based scheduling (vs. natural rhythms)
- Currency economy (vs. gift exchange)
- Written contracts (vs. witnessed oaths)

**What Silverwood Denies**:
- Urban elves have influenced homeland fashions
- Some "ancient" texts are post-Collapse reconstructions

## Human ↔ Dwarf

### What Humans Absorbed
**Trade Vocabulary** (10+ terms in common use):
- "Assay" - quality testing
- "Carat" - purity measure
- "Forge" - any metalworking
- "Ingot" - standard metal unit
- "Alloy" - mixed metals
- "Temper" - heat treatment
- "Smelt" - ore processing
- "Anneal" - stress relief
- "Hallmark" - quality stamp
- "Sterling" - silver standard

**Practices**:
- Guild structure (copied from dwarven holds)
- Contract law (dwarven precision)
- Quality marks on goods

**Architecture**:
- Stone vaulting techniques
- Underground construction
- Bridge engineering

### What Dwarves Absorbed
- Surface timekeeping (vs. deep rhythms)
- Human languages for trade
- Some agricultural knowledge
- Diplomatic protocols

---

## Human ↔ Goblin

### What Border Humans Know
**Vocabulary** (denied but used):
- Warning calls
- Terrain terms for swamp/underground
- Certain curses

**Practices**:
- Checking for raid signs
- Knowing which plants are edible
- Recognizing goblin tribal markers

**Combat**:
- Tunnel fighting techniques
- Ambush recognition
- Night movement

### What Goblins Absorbed
- Human weapon use
- Some agricultural techniques
- Trade goods they value
- Human weaknesses

## Elf ↔ Dwarf

### Historical Contact
During vampire era, both served as specialists. Limited but real contact:

**Shared**:
- Certain technical vocabulary
- Respect for craftsmanship
- Memory of vampire-era cooperation

**Current**:
- Possible secret communication
- Neither trusts humans fully
- Both remember things humans forgot

## Servant Culture Legacy

### Shared Across Races
During vampire era, servant races developed common elements:

**Vocabulary**:
- Handler commands
- Facility terms
- Survival slang

**Gestures**:
- Warning signals
- "Safe" indicators
- Submission displays

**Why It Fragmented**:
- Competition for resources post-Collapse
- Blame for collaboration
- Each race's narrative erased others
- New identities built on difference
