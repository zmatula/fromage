# Contested Histories

Specific events where different factions have incompatible interpretations. These aren't polite academic disagreements—they're vicious, ongoing conflicts that divide families and start wars.

---

## The Nature of Vampire Civilization

### The Central Question

Were vampires evil, or merely dominant?

**This isn't settled. People kill over this.**

---

### The Church Position (Sanctified Orthodox)

**The Doctrine**
- Vampires were literal demons in flesh
- They did not "rule"—they infected
- Their civilization was a corruption of natural order
- The collapse was divine punishment
- Any sympathy for vampires is heresy

**The Evidence They Cite**
- The blood drinking (unnatural)
- The mind control (demonic)
- The breeding programs (abomination)
- The collapse (God's wrath)

**Who Believes This**
- Sanctified States (official doctrine)
- Order of Dawn (founding principle)
- Most common people (raised on it)
- Many Northern and Valdrian clergy

**What Happens to Dissenters**
- Accused of "vampire sympathy"
- Social ostracism
- Loss of employment
- In Sanctified States: imprisonment or execution

---

### The Academic Position (Free Cities Scholarly)

**The Theory**
- Vampires were a civilization—different, not demonic
- They achieved things no one has matched
- They were cruel but also created
- Understanding them serves everyone

**The Evidence They Cite**
- The technology (worked, wasn't magic)
- The scholarship (they studied, not just destroyed)
- The art (they created beauty)
- The internal debates (they disagreed like people)

**Who Believes This**
- Free Cities universities
- Inheritors (secretly)
- Some Northern scholars
- Most serious researchers

**What Happens to Dissenters**
- In academic circles: marginalization
- By Church: accusations of heresy
- Socially: suspicion, isolation

---

### The Goblin Position

**The Truth They Know**
- Vampires made us what we are
- We were their greatest victims
- We lost more than anyone
- You don't understand because it didn't happen to you

**The Evidence They Have**
- Oral histories of transformation
- Genetic memories (real or believed)
- Physical evidence of modification
- The rage that doesn't go away

**What They Want**
- Acknowledgment of what was done
- Not to be treated as monsters for what vampires made them
- The other races to understand

**What They Get**
- Blamed for vampire association
- Killed in wars for "vampire taint"
- Confined to Broken Lands
- Treated as less than people

---

### Why It Matters

**If vampires were demons:**
- Studying them is evil
- Preserving their knowledge is corruption
- Anyone who disagrees is suspect
- Vessel Seven must be destroyed

**If vampires were civilization:**
- Studying them is necessary
- Preserving knowledge is wisdom
- Different views are acceptable
- Vessel Seven might be valuable

**The protagonist's survival may depend on which view prevails.**

## The Collapse: Why It Happened

### The Mind Covenant Theory

**The Claim**
- Mind Covenant caused the collapse through the Ascension Experiment
- They were trying to become gods
- Their hubris killed everyone

**The Evidence**
- Ascension Experiment was Mind project
- Located at Mind Covenant capital
- Mind leadership refused to stop
- All records point to their experiment

**Who Believes This**
- Almost everyone
- This is "official history"
- Even Inheritors accept this

**What It Means**
- Mind Covenant was the villain
- Other covenants were victims
- Intellectual ambition is dangerous

---

### The Throne Conspiracy Theory

**The Claim**
- Throne Covenant sabotaged the Ascension Experiment
- They were planning a coup anyway
- They made the experiment fail worse
- The collapse was partially intentional

**The Evidence**
- Throne was preparing for war
- They had reasons to destabilize Mind
- The timing is suspicious
- Their forces were positioned to survive

**Who Believes This**
- Some scholars
- Some conspiracy theorists
- Those who think collapse was "too convenient"

**The Problem**
- Little hard evidence
- Throne didn't survive either
- Would they destroy themselves to win?

---

### The Twilight Prophecy Theory

**The Claim**
- Twilight Covenant foresaw the collapse
- They could have warned everyone
- They chose to prepare sanctuaries instead
- They let it happen

**The Evidence**
- Twilight had seers and prophets
- They withdrew from politics before collapse
- They built sanctuaries in secret
- Some may have survived

**Who Believes This**
- Those seeking someone to blame besides Mind
- Those who distrust elves (Twilight was elf-aligned)
- Conspiracy theorists

**The Implication**
- If Twilight survives, they have questions to answer
- If they knew and didn't warn, they're guilty
- The survivor sanctuaries may hold the truth

---

### The Servant Sabotage Theory

**The Claim**
- Servant races sabotaged vampire systems
- Accelerated the collapse
- Made it worse than it had to be
- Were not just victims

**The Evidence**
- Known servant resistance (dwarven sabotage Year -20)
- Knowledge of vampire systems (could exploit weaknesses)
- Motivation (freedom)

**Who Believes This**
- Some vampire sympathizers
- Those who want shared blame
- Those who resent "victim narrative"

**The Problem**
- Scale was too large for sabotage alone
- Servants died more than anyone
- This sounds like blaming victims

---

### What's At Stake

Understanding why the collapse happened determines:
- Who is guilty
- Who is innocent
- Whether it could happen again
- What to do with vampire knowledge
- What to do with Vessel Seven

## The Goblin Wars: Necessary or Genocide?

### The Orthodox Position

**The Claim**
- Goblins were vampire-created monsters
- They were aggressive, territorial, hostile
- Containment was necessary for survival
- The war was defensive

**The Evidence**
- Goblin raids on human settlements
- Goblin refusal to negotiate
- Goblin "blood magic" practices
- Physical differences (modified by vampires)

**Who Believes This**
- Official position of all human kingdoms
- Dwarven official position
- Church doctrine
- Most common people

---

### The Revisionist Position

**The Claim**
- Goblins were fellow victims of vampires
- They were reacting to human aggression
- They were fighting for survival, not conquest
- The wars were extermination, not defense

**The Evidence**
- General Caden Stormhold's research (Year 512)
- Goblin oral histories
- Population decline (90% reduction)
- Treatment after "containment"

**Who Believes This**
- Some scholars (quietly)
- Goblins (loudly)
- A few human reformers
- Those who've studied vampire breeding programs

---

### The Goblin Position

**The Truth They Tell**
- We didn't choose to be what we are
- Vampires made us this way
- We tried to survive
- You hunted us for what they did to us

**What They Remember**
- Villages burned
- Children killed
- Holy warriors laughing
- Being called monsters by those who made them

**What They Want**
- Recognition of what was done
- End to containment
- To be treated as people
- Maybe revenge

---

### Why It's Still Fought

**Goblin Nationalism**
- Rising in Broken Lands
- Demanding redress
- Some calling for war
- Some calling for recognition

**Human Fear**
- If goblins were wronged, we're guilty
- If they deserve freedom, what happens?
- If they're angry, are we safe?
- Easier to say they're monsters

**Political Use**
- "Goblin threat" used to justify military spending
- "Goblin sympathy" used to attack political opponents
- No one wants to admit the truth

## The Year 812 Discovery: Destruction or Murder?

### The Order of Dawn Version

**The Claim**
- A demon was found in stasis
- It was destroyed before it could act
- This was holy duty
- Nothing was lost

**The Evidence**
- Church authority over demonic matters
- Standard protocol for vampire remnants
- Divine mandate to destroy evil

**Who Believes This**
- Order of Dawn (official position)
- Sanctified States (official position)
- Those who trust Church authority

---

### The Scholar Version

**The Claim**
- A vampire survivor was executed without trial
- It could have answered questions
- Knowledge was destroyed
- This was murder and vandalism

**The Evidence**
- The vampire cooperated
- It provided information
- It was killed after questioning
- Records were suppressed

**Who Believes This**
- Academics
- Inheritors
- Those who value knowledge
- Those who distrust Church

---

### The Conspiracy Version

**The Claim**
- The vampire wasn't destroyed
- Someone took it
- It's still out there
- The "destruction" was cover story

**The Evidence**
- No body shown
- Records sealed
- Church behavior suspicious
- Why suppress if it was just destroyed?

**Who Believes This**
- Inheritors (some factions)
- Conspiracy theorists
- Those who want to believe

---

### Why It Matters Now

**Precedent for Vessel Seven**
- What happened to Year 812 vampire will happen to him
- Unless something changes
- The debate about 812 is the debate about him

**What Different Outcomes Mean**

*If Year 812 was right:*
- Vessel Seven should be destroyed
- No questions, no study
- Divine mandate

*If Year 812 was wrong:*
- Vessel Seven should be studied
- Questions answered
- Knowledge preserved

## The Elven Isolation: Protection or Hoarding?

### The Elven Position

**The Claim**
- We suffered under vampires like everyone
- We sealed our borders to heal
- We protect dangerous knowledge
- We will share when we're ready

**The Evidence**
- Elven losses under vampire rule
- Sealed vampire ruins in Silverwood
- Stability of elven territories
- "We've survived longer than you"

**Who Believes This**
- Elves (obviously)
- Those who respect elven wisdom
- Those who fear what's in the ruins

---

### The Human Position

**The Claim**
- Elves are hoarding vampire knowledge
- They're hiding something valuable
- They think they're better than us
- They should share

**The Evidence**
- 1,200 years of isolation
- Refusal to allow research
- Economic advantages from controlled trade
- "What are they hiding?"

**Who Believes This**
- Most humans
- Especially Northern Kingdom
- Especially Inheritors
- Anyone denied elven cooperation

---

### The Suspicion

**What Humans Fear**
- Elves have working vampire technology
- Elves are preparing something
- Elves will dominate when ready
- The isolation is preparation, not protection

**What Elves Fear**
- Humans will destroy what they don't understand
- Humans will repeat vampire mistakes
- Humans can't be trusted with power
- Opening up will mean exploitation

---

### The Breaking Point

**Current Situation**
- Northern Kingdom demanding access
- Valdrian Empire pressuring trade
- Inheritors trying to infiltrate
- Elves holding firm

**What Might Happen**
- If elves stay closed: resentment builds
- If elves open: knowledge released, consequences unknown
- If someone forces entry: war
- Vessel Seven might be the key—or the excuse

## How to Use Contested History

### In Character Dialogue

**Example: Two scholars arguing**
> "The vampire was murdered. Eight hundred years of survival, destroyed in an hour."
> "It was a demon. Destroying it was holy duty."
> "You know that's propaganda."
> "You know you're flirting with heresy."

**Example: Human and Goblin**
> "The wars were necessary. Your people were raiding our villages."
> "We were starving. You'd taken our lands."
> "You're monsters."
> "You made us monsters."

### In Plot

**Characters choose sides**
- Based on upbringing
- Based on self-interest
- Based on evidence they've seen

**Discovering truth changes things**
- Finding Corvin's journal changes understanding of Vessels
- Finding Stormhold's research changes understanding of goblins
- Finding Year 812 records changes understanding of Church

### In Theme

**No One Is Right**
- Every position has evidence
- Every position has blind spots
- The truth is complicated
- Choosing is hard

**History Is Political**
- How you remember determines what you do
- Those in power control the narrative
- The marginalized have different stories
- The protagonist will have to navigate this
