# Character Tracker

Tracking what each character knows at each story point and how their knowledge changes.

---

## Purpose

Characters can only know what they could plausibly know. This document tracks knowledge acquisition to prevent characters from knowing things before they should.

---

## Validation Questions

### For Each Scene

1. What does POV character know at this exact moment?
2. How did they learn each piece of information?
3. What do they still not know that might be relevant?
4. What wrong beliefs are they acting on?
5. Will they learn something this scene?

### For Information Revelation

1. Who is learning this?
2. Who already knows?
3. Is this the right time for them to learn?
4. What are consequences of them knowing?
5. Who will they tell?

## Common Knowledge Errors

### Characters Know Too Much
- Information they weren't present for
- Secrets no one told them
- Future events
- Other characters' thoughts

### Characters Know Too Little
- Forgetting what they learned
- Not using available information
- Acting against own knowledge
- Missing obvious conclusions

### Information Spreads Wrong
- Too fast (no travel time)
- Too slow (no network effect)
- To wrong people
- Not to obvious people
