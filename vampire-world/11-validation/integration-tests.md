# Integration Tests

Verifying that all elements of the worldbuilding work together as intended.

---

## Purpose

Individual elements may be internally consistent but fail to integrate properly. These tests verify that the whole is greater than the sum of its parts.

## Recommendations

### Strengths to Maintain

1. **Multi-layered conflicts**: Every confrontation has historical, personal, and political dimensions
2. **Knowledge asymmetry**: What characters don't know drives plot as much as what they do
3. **Thematic consistency**: Core themes appear at every level
4. **Earned revelations**: All secrets have setups and foreshadowing

### Areas for Vigilance

1. **Complexity creep**: Don't add complications that don't serve themes
2. **Pacing balance**: Ensure revelations are spaced for impact
3. **Character voice**: Maintain distinct POV voices throughout
4. **Consequence tracking**: Every action must have tracked effects
