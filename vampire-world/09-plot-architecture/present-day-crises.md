## Overview

Year 1200 is not stable. Four major crises are converging:

1. **Valdrian Succession Crisis** - The empire may shatter
2. **Maritime Republic Coup** - The banks are at war
3. **Northern Expansion** - Aldric XII is making his move
4. **Sanctified Persecution** - The Church is hunting everyone

Without Vessel Seven, these crises would still create war within the year.

With Vessel Seven, they explode immediately.

## The Valdrian Succession Crisis

### The Dying Emperor

**Emperor Cassian IV**
- Age 67, dying of wasting illness
- No direct heir (three sons died young, two daughters in convents)
- Has ruled 40 years
- The empire prospered, but succession was never secured
- He kept waiting for a legitimate male heir
- Time ran out

### The Claimants

**Claimant 1: Duke Aldric Varen (nephew)**
- Cassian's sister's son
- Age 42, capable administrator
- Has strongest blood claim
- Supported by: traditional nobility, Church
- Problem: his wife is Northern (marriage alliance)
- Enemies fear Northern influence through him

**Claimant 2: Duchess Margret Cassian (cousin)**
- Cassian's first cousin
- Age 38, competent and ambitious
- Weaker blood claim but strong political position
- Supported by: reformist nobility, merchants, some military
- Problem: she's a woman
- Valdris has never had a ruling empress

**Claimant 3: Prince-Consort Aldric of Ironpeak (son-in-law)**
- Married Cassian's eldest daughter (now in convent)
- Age 44, former dwarven noble (very unusual)
- Claims he speaks for his wife who speaks for her father
- Supported by: dwarven interests, mountain provinces
- Problem: foreign, weird argument, deeply unpopular

**Claimant 4: The Bastard Cassian (illegitimate son)**
- Cassian's acknowledged bastard by a noble mistress
- Age 30, popular with common people
- No legal claim at all
- Supported by: lower classes, some military (he's a soldier)
- Problem: illegitimacy is absolute bar in Valdrian law
- But laws can change

### The Factions

**The Traditionalists**
- Support Duke Aldric Varen
- Want orderly succession
- Fear chaos more than Northern influence
- Control most rural nobility
- Have Church backing

**The Reformists**
- Support Duchess Margret
- Want to modernize succession laws
- Have urban support
- Control some military
- Have merchant backing

**The Dwarven Bloc**
- Support Prince-Consort
- Want dwarven influence in Valdris
- Control mountain provinces and mining
- Have wealth
- Few actual votes in council

**The Populists**
- Support the Bastard
- Want to break noble control
- Have popular support
- Control some military units
- Have no legitimate path

### The Timeline

**Now (early 1200)**: Cassian has months to live
**Spring 1200**: Expected death
**Summer 1200**: Succession council must meet
**Without resolution**: Civil war by autumn

### What Happens If...

**Aldric Varen wins**: Northern Kingdom gains massive influence. Other powers will try to check this. Possibly war with Maritime Republic.

**Margret wins**: Revolutionary precedent. Other kingdoms will have succession challenges. Church will be furious.

**Prince-Consort wins**: Dwarven influence expands. Humans will be resentful. Probably civil war within a year.

**The Bastard wins**: All law is broken. Anyone can claim anything. Total chaos.

**No one wins**: Civil war. Empire shatters into fragments. Other powers invade.

### Why It Matters for Seven

- Valdris is the largest human power
- Whoever wins will set policy toward vampires
- Aldric Varen: likely to work with Church (destroy Seven)
- Margret: might be pragmatic (study Seven)
- Prince-Consort: dwarven policy (contain Seven)
- The Bastard: unpredictable (anything)

## Valdrian Succession: Economic Factions

### Duke Aldric Varen
- **Backing**: Rural nobility (land-based wealth)
- **Economic Platform**: Preserve traditional land rights, resist merchant rising
- **Financial Support**: ~500,000 crowns from noble houses
- **Trade Policy**: Protectionist; maintain tariffs on foreign goods
- **Who Benefits**: Established aristocracy, traditional military

### Duchess Margret
- **Backing**: Urban merchants and guilds
- **Economic Platform**: Modernize commerce, reduce guild restrictions, open trade
- **Financial Support**: ~400,000 crowns from merchant consortiums
- **Trade Policy**: Free trade; reduce tariffs, welcome foreign investment
- **Who Benefits**: Merchants, craftspeople, Maritime Republic

### Prince-Consort Aldric (Dwarven Heritage)
- **Backing**: Dwarven interests and mountain provinces
- **Economic Platform**: Continental integration, standardize currency
- **Financial Support**: ~600,000 crowns from Ironpeak banking houses
- **Trade Policy**: Unified continental market; dwarven-backed currency standard
- **Who Benefits**: Dwarves, traders, anyone with cross-border business

### The Bastard
- **Backing**: Lower classes and some military
- **Economic Platform**: Wealth redistribution, reduced taxes on poor
- **Financial Support**: ~200,000 crowns (limited; mainly popular donations)
- **Trade Policy**: Unclear; would likely default to advisors
- **Who Benefits**: Common people, commoner-rise soldiers

## The Maritime Republic Coup

### What Happened

**Year 1199**: A coup overthrew the ruling Council of Captains

**Who Did It**: The Three Bank Consortium

**How**: Bought enough captains, bribed enough guards, arrested the rest overnight

**Why**: The old Council was restricting artifact trade, raising taxes on banks, threatening Banking House privileges

### The Old Order

**The Council of Captains**
- Twelve captains, elected by ship-owners
- Balanced trade and military interests
- Had ruled for 300 years
- Were corrupt but predictable

**What They Did**
- Restricted artifact exports (feared destabilization)
- Taxed Banking Houses (needed military funds)
- Maintained naval power (expensive)

### The New Order

**The Three Bank Consortium**
- House Goldveil (oldest, most traditional)
- House Blackwater (Sera Blackwater's descendants)
- House Ironmark (newest, most aggressive)

**What They Want**
- Free artifact trade (profit)
- Lower taxes (profit)
- Reduce navy (save money)
- Control everything (profit)

**Who Leads Them**
- **Director Vera Goldveil**: ancient, cautious, legitimacy-focused
- **Master Erasmus Blackwater**: middle-aged, ruthless, profit-focused
- **Chancellor Kira Ironmark**: young, aggressive, expansion-focused

### The Opposition

**Captain Aldric Stormwind**
- Former Council leader
- Escaped arrest
- Building resistance in the outer islands
- Has loyal ships and crews
- Planning counter-coup

**The Admiral's Faction**
- Navy officers who oppose the banks
- Fear military decline
- Have ships but no money
- Might ally with Stormwind

**The Free Captains**
- Independent traders who fear bank monopoly
- Many ships, no coordination
- Could tip the balance either way

### The Timeline

**Year 1199**: Coup succeeds
**Early 1200**: Consortium consolidating
**Now**: Resistance building
**Spring 1200**: Counter-coup likely
**If it fails**: Bank dictatorship. If it succeeds: civil war.

### What Happens If...

**Consortium holds**: Banks control the sea trade. Artifact trade explodes. Knowledge spreads uncontrollably. Church is furious.

**Counter-coup succeeds**: Naval captains return. Trade restricted. Banks humiliated. Internal purges.

**Stalemate**: Republic fragments. Pirates. Free ports. No central control.

### Why It Matters for Seven

- Maritime Republic controls artifact trade
- They will want to sell access to Seven
- Consortium: highest bidder gets him
- Stormwind: might be more principled—or not
- Either way: Seven is merchandise

## Northern Expansion

### King Aldric XII

**Who He Is**
- Age 42, ruled since Year 1192
- Military genius, political pragmatist
- Believes Northern Kingdom deserves to dominate
- Has been planning expansion for eight years

**What He Wants**
- Valdrian border territories (the Marches)
- Access to Silverwood trade
- Control of artifact sites in the Border Marches
- Eventually: continental hegemony

**Why Now**
- Valdris is in succession crisis (weak)
- Maritime Republic is in coup chaos (distracted)
- Sanctified States are focused on heresy (busy)
- He's ready

### The Military Position

**Northern Forces**
- 30,000 professional soldiers with 50,000 feudal levies available within 30 days (80,000 total rapid deployment)
- Best heavy infantry on continent
- Good cavalry
- Excellent logistics

**Current Deployments**
- Three legions on Valdrian border (ready to move)
- One legion on Silverwood border (watching)
- One legion guarding capital
- Reserves being called up

### The Targets

**Phase 1: The Border Marches**
- Valdrian territory but autonomous
- Valuable agriculture
- Vampire ruins (artifact sites)
- Easy to claim "historical right"

**Phase 2: Valdrian Weakness**
- If succession goes badly, grab more
- Eastern provinces (wealthy)
- Port of Eastwatch (access to sea)
- Maybe even Brightholme

**Phase 3: Silverwood Pressure**
- Force elves to open trade
- Or take border forests
- Access to elven artifacts
- Break the isolation

### The Allies and Enemies

**Potential Allies**
- Sanctified States (share border with Valdris)
- Maritime banks (will fund anyone)
- Dwarven factions (if promised mining rights)

**Definite Enemies**
- Valdris (target)
- Silverwood (target)
- Whoever wins Valdrian succession

### The Timeline

**Now**: Forces positioned
**Spring 1200**: Valdrian emperor dies
**If succession is contested**: Aldric invades the Marches
**Summer 1200**: Possible full war

### What Happens If...

**Expansion succeeds**: Northern dominance. Continental war within 5 years. Everyone else allies against them.

**Expansion fails**: Aldric weakened. Possibly overthrown. Northern isolationism returns.

**Stalemate**: Border war for years. Economy collapses. Everyone suffers.

### Why It Matters for Seven

- Aldric XII knows about the Scar expeditions
- He knows something is being found
- He may know about stasis chambers
- He definitely wants whatever Seven is
- He would use Seven as weapon

## The Sanctified Persecution

### What's Happening

**Year 1195-1200**: The Church is hunting heretics with renewed fervor

**Who's Targeted**:
- Scholars studying vampire texts
- Anyone with suspicious artifacts
- "Vampire sympathizers"
- Magic users without Church license
- "Rationalists"
- Anyone the Church doesn't like

### Who's Leading It

**Grand Master Aurelius Dawnbringer**
- Order of Dawn supreme leader
- Commands the Order's military forces from Dawnhaven
- Absolute believer whose certainty that darkness is rising drives aggressive hunting operations
- Has been preparing for decades

**Archbishop Aldric Stern**
- Highest Church authority in Sanctified States
- Aurelius's political ally
- Provides legal cover for Order actions

**Inquisitor-General Vera Thorne**
- Operational commander
- Runs the actual hunts
- Efficient, thorough, merciless

### The Justification

**The Prophecy**
- Old Twilight prophecy (stolen from elves centuries ago)
- "When the dead sun wakes, blood will flow again"
- Interpreted as vampire return
- Brennan believes it's now

**The Signs**
- Dimensional readings at the Scar (real)
- Increased artifact activity (real)
- Mysterious expeditions (real)
- Vampire cults (maybe real)

### The Methods

**Investigation**
- Informant networks
- Library inspections
- Artifact registration and confiscation
- Interrogation

**Arrest**
- Church authority supersedes secular in "demonic matters"
- Detained without trial
- Families not informed
- Property seized

**Punishment**
- Recantation and penance (lucky)
- Imprisonment (common)
- Execution by fire (heretics)

### The Numbers

**Year 1195-1200**:
- ~500 arrested
- ~100 executed
- ~300 imprisoned
- ~100 "released after recantation"
- Unknown number fled

### The Targets

**Known Targets Currently Hunted**:

**Magister Orin's heir**: Descendant of Three Scholars, has family papers, in hiding

**The Rationalist Circle**: Group of Free Cities scholars, several arrested, others fled

**Brother Aldric Vance**: Church scholar who questioned too much, missing

**"The Archive"**: Rumored complete collection of translated vampire texts, location unknown, many have died supposedly knowing

### The Resistance

**Who Opposes**
- Free Cities (officially neutral but protect scholars)
- Some Valdrian nobles (resent Church authority)
- Inheritors (protecting their people)
- Veil Keepers (exposing the persecution)

**What They Do**
- Hide targets
- Forge documents
- Smuggle people out
- Counter-intelligence

### The Timeline

**Now**: Persecution at height
**Spring 1200**: Major coordinated raid planned
**Summer 1200**: Depends on what they find

### What Happens If...

**Persecution succeeds**: Church dominance. Knowledge suppressed. Inheritors destroyed. Ignorance wins.

**Persecution fails**: Church weakened. Order of Dawn discredited. Aurelius replaced. Knowledge spreads.

**Escalation**: Full Inquisition like Year 1-50. Mass executions. Civil resistance. Possibly war.

### Why It Matters for Seven

- He's the ultimate target
- Aurelius has been preparing for exactly this
- Order will hunt Seven relentlessly
- They'll kill anyone who helps him
- They won't negotiate

## Crisis Economic Analysis

### Valdrian Succession Crisis
**Surface Issue**: No clear heir
**Economic Driver**: Which economic system will rule?
- Duke Aldric: Land aristocracy (protect estates, resist merchant power)
- Duchess Margret: Merchant interests (free trade, guild reform)
- Prince-Consort: Dwarven integration (standardized currency, open borders)
- Bastard: Redistribution (tax the wealthy, expand services)

**Stakes**: Winner determines Valdrian economic policy for generation.

### Northern Expansion
**Surface Issue**: Territorial ambition
**Economic Driver**: Food security crisis
- 40% grain imports from Valdris
- 6-month famine window if embargoed
- Must seize farmland before war, not after

**Stakes**: Northern survival vs Valdrian agricultural heartland.

### Maritime Coup
**Surface Issue**: Banking vs merchant control
**Economic Driver**: Who profits from artifact trade?
- Banks want controlled market (high margins)
- Merchants want open trade (high volume)
- Coup already costing 80,000 crowns/year in lost tariffs

**Stakes**: Control of continental artifact economy.

### Sanctified Persecution
**Surface Issue**: Religious purity
**Economic Driver**: Destroying own economic base
- Scholars fleeing = knowledge loss
- Merchants fleeing = trade loss
- Craftspeople fleeing = production loss
- Agricultural disruption growing

**Stakes**: Sanctified economic self-destruction vs doctrinal purity.

### Veil Keeper vs Inheritor War
**Surface Issue**: Suppress vs preserve knowledge
**Economic Driver**: Control of artifact discovery
- Whoever controls expeditions controls findings
- Whoever controls findings controls power
- ~500,000 crowns/year in artifact value at stake

**Stakes**: Knowledge monopoly = power monopoly.

## How the Crises Intersect

### The Web

**Valdrian Succession + Northern Expansion**
- Aldric XII will exploit succession chaos
- Whichever claimant seems weakest will be attacked
- Some claimants might ally with North for support

**Maritime Coup + Valdrian Succession**
- Banks might fund claimants for trade concessions
- Stormwind might ally with Valdris against banks
- Sea trade disruption affects everyone

**Sanctified Persecution + Everyone**
- Church claims jurisdiction everywhere
- Order operates across borders
- Everyone resents them
- But everyone fears them too

**Northern Expansion + Sanctified States**
- Potential alliance against Valdris
- But Northern Kingdom isn't religious enough
- Tension between military and Church ambitions

### The Powder Keg

Any one of these crises could cause war.

All four together?

Continental conflagration.

### Enter Vessel Seven

Seven doesn't cause the crises.

Seven is the match dropped into the powder.

- Valdris will want him for succession advantage
- Maritime banks will want to sell him
- Northern Kingdom will want him as weapon
- Sanctified will want him destroyed

Everyone will fight over him.

The crises that were going to cause war in a year will cause war in a month.
