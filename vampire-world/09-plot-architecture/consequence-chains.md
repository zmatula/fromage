# Consequence Chains

Tracking cause and effect across storylines—how actions ripple through the story.

---

## Core Principle

Every action has consequences. Every choice matters. No decisions exist in isolation. This is fundamental to GRRM-style storytelling.

---

## Chain 1: The Awakening Cascade

### Trigger
Protagonist awakens in Vel'Thoras

### Immediate Consequences
- Chamber systems fail
- Magical signature detectable
- Expeditions converge
- First encounters occur

### Short-Term Ripples
- Factions confirm Living Artifact reports true
- Hunt escalates across continent
- Protagonist's existence becomes known
- Alliance/enemy formations begin

### Long-Term Effects
- Balance of power shifts toward whoever controls him
- Knowledge race intensifies
- War becomes more likely
- Dimensional threat interest increases

### Cross-Arc Impact
- Awakening → Hunt (begins)
- Awakening → Knowledge (prize appears)
- Awakening → Northern (weapon available)
- Awakening → Succession (wild card enters)

## Character Decision Consequences

### Protagonist Choices

**Trust Vera**
- Access to knowledge but captivity risk
- Inheritor resources available
- May learn truth faster
- May be used and discarded

**Trust Marcus** (if possible)
- Enemy of convenience
- Information about Order
- Constant threat
- Possible redemption story

**Trust Gritha**
- Blood magic assistance
- Goblin alliance
- Used as weapon risk
- Understanding his nature

**Trust No One**
- Survival but isolation
- Slower progress
- Fatal flaw frustrated
- Independence maintained

### Elara Choices

**Use Protagonist**
- Political advantage
- Ethical compromise
- Short-term gain
- Long-term corruption

**Protect Protagonist**
- Ally gained
- Political risk
- Ethical choice
- Uncertain outcome

### Marcus Choices

**Kill Protagonist**
- Mission complete
- Questions silenced
- Possible mistake
- Identity preserved

**Spare Protagonist**
- Mission failed
- Truth possible
- Identity challenged
- Transformation begun

## Consequence Timing

### Immediate (same scene)
- Character reactions
- Physical effects
- Emotional responses
- Tactical adjustments

### Short-Term (soon after)
- News spreads
- Plans adjust
- Alliances shift
- Actions taken

### Long-Term (story-spanning)
- World changes
- Characters transform
- Themes realized
- Resolution shaped

## Unintended Consequences

### Good Intentions → Bad Results

**Elara's reforms** → Destabilize during crisis
**Vera's research** → Reveals dangerous truth
**Marcus's faith** → Blinds to reality
**Protagonist's trust** → Enables manipulation

### Bad Intentions → Good Results

**Gritha's rage** → Reveals historical truth
**Northern aggression** → Unites opposition
**Veil Keeper suppression** → Drives knowledge underground (survives)

## Consequence Chains Matrix

### Action → Consequence → Further Consequence

```
Protagonist awakens
  → Hunt begins
    → Factions conflict
      → War more likely
        → Resources diverted from dimensional threat
          → Threat grows
            → Crisis

Cassius uses Crown
  → Goes mad
    → Succession crisis
      → Empire weakens
        → Northern attacks
          → Continent at war
            → Everything worse

Blood Archives opened
  → Truth revealed
    → Faith shattered
      → Order of Dawn crisis
        → Sanctified States chaos
          → Power vacuum
            → Different threat
```

## Validation

### Every Major Event Must Have

- Clear cause
- Immediate effect
- Ripple effects
- Cross-arc impact
- Character consequences

### No Isolated Events
Everything connects. If it doesn't connect, it doesn't belong.

### No Consequence-Free Actions
Every choice costs. Every decision matters. This is the core of the story.
