# Year 1200 Political Tensions

The marriages, successions, trade wars, and conspiracies about to explode—before Vessel Seven adds vampire chaos to the mix.

---

## The Powder Keg

Year 1200 is not a peaceful time. The surface stability masks:
- Three succession crises
- Two broken betrothals that demand blood
- Trade wars on the verge of becoming real wars
- Religious schisms threatening civil conflict
- Racial tensions at breaking point

Seven isn't awakening into a stable world. He's awakening into a world already on fire.

---

## The Valdrian Succession

### The Current Situation

**Emperor Aldric IV** is dying. Cancer, spreading slowly but unstoppably. His physicians give him two years, maybe less.

He has three children. None of them should rule.

### The Heirs

**Prince Aldric** (27): The official heir
- Cruel, vain, and stupid—the trifecta of bad rulership
- Controlled by his mistress, Lady Sera Blackwood
- Lady Blackwood is Inheritor-connected
- If he inherits, the Inheritors gain imperial power

**Princess Valeria** (24): The competent one
- Brilliant administrator, popular with commoners
- Problem: she's a woman, and Valdrian law bars female succession
- Married to a Northern Kingdom duke—if she ruled, would Valdris become Northern?
- The Northern Kingdom would love that

**Prince Marcus** (19): The wild card
- Genuinely talented but erratic
- Joined the Church young—technically not in succession
- But he never took final vows
- The Church claims him; the throne needs him

### The Factions

**The Traditionalists**: Support Prince Aldric because law is law. Led by Duke Valorian, they control the eastern provinces and the army.

**The Reformists**: Support Princess Valeria—change the law. Led by Merchant-Prince Kael, they control the treasury and ports.

**The Church Faction**: Support Prince Marcus—a holy emperor. Led by Cardinal Aldric Thorne, they control the temples and significant land.

### The Timeline

Emperor Aldric IV dies within two years. When he does:
- Traditionalists will crown Prince Aldric immediately
- Reformists will challenge the succession
- Church will offer "mediation" that somehow results in Marcus
- Civil war within six months

### What Seven Changes

A vampire awakening gives every faction an excuse:

**Traditionalists**: "Divine punishment for our decadence—we need strong leadership."

**Reformists**: "This proves we need new thinking—Valeria understands change."

**Church**: "Only a holy emperor can face this threat—Marcus is chosen."

Seven's awakening accelerates the succession crisis. Instead of two years, they have months.

## The Northern Kingdom Expansion

### The Current Situation

**King Harald III** has a problem: three sons and only two crowns to give them.

The Northern Kingdom inherited Throne covenant territories after the Collapse. It's the largest human kingdom, but it's reaching its limits. The sons need kingdoms. The North needs to expand.

### The Sons

**Prince Erik** (31): Heir to the Northern throne
- Competent but unimaginative
- Will rule well but not expand
- Married to a Northern noble—secure succession

**Prince Bjorn** (28): The conqueror
- Military genius, beloved by soldiers
- Needs a kingdom of his own
- Currently eyeing the Border Marches
- The Valdrian princess's husband

**Prince Ragnar** (23): The scholar
- Interested in vampire history
- Connections to the Academy
- Secretly Inheritor-sympathetic
- Would trade conquest for knowledge

### The Expansion Plans

**Plan A**: Bjorn conquers the Border Marches
- Independent territory, poorly defended
- Would give Bjorn his kingdom
- Triggers war with Valdris (they claim the Marches)

**Plan B**: Valeria inherits Valdris
- She's married to Bjorn
- He becomes Emperor-Consort
- Northern influence over Valdris without war

**Plan C**: Ragnar marries a Valdrian princess
- There isn't one
- But Prince Marcus has a sister who took religious vows
- Vows can be released...

Harald is pursuing all three plans simultaneously.

### The Timeline

Bjorn's army is positioned to invade the Border Marches. They're waiting for:
- Valdrian succession crisis (internal distraction)
- Trade dispute to provide excuse
- Harald's final order

When Aldric IV dies, Bjorn moves.

### What Seven Changes

Northern plans depend on vampire artifacts:

**The Weapons**: Soulforged blades that Bjorn needs for his elite guard. Currently held by the Church.

**The Fortress**: Vel'Thoras sits in Northern-claimed territory. If Seven awakens there, Northern Kingdom claims him.

**The Knowledge**: Ragnar wants vampire research more than territory. Seven is more valuable to him than any conquest.

Seven's awakening splits Northern priorities. Do they conquer, or do they contain the vampire first?

## The Maritime Federation Crisis

### The Current Situation

The Maritime Federation isn't a kingdom—it's a merchant alliance. Seven great trading houses that control the seas and the ports. They elect a Doge every ten years.

The current Doge, **Lorenzo Valdris** (yes, distant Valdrian relation), dies in three months. The election is already ugly.

### The Candidates

**House Aldric**: The bankers
- Control debt across all kingdoms
- Want the Doge seat to forgive... selectively
- Candidate: Sera Aldric, first woman to run

**House Blackwood**: The shippers
- Control the merchant fleet
- Want military expansion to "protect trade routes"
- Candidate: Marcus Blackwood, admiral and war hawk

**House Thorne**: The brokers
- Control trade agreements
- Want stability and status quo
- Candidate: Aldric Thorne, boring but reliable

### The Trade War

The real conflict is over **Northern sea access**.

Northern Kingdom wants direct shipping without Federation fees. Federation survives on those fees. War would hurt both, but neither will back down.

**The Blockade**: Federation ships are "inspecting" Northern vessels. Northern ships are "defending themselves." Three ships have sunk. Deaths are mounting.

**The Alliance Question**: If war comes, who does Valdris support? The Federation shares religion and culture. The North shares a border and a princess.

### The Timeline

Doge Lorenzo dies within three months. The election is contested. During the chaos:
- House Blackwood stages military action against Northern shipping
- Northern Kingdom retaliates against Federation ports
- Valdris must choose sides
- By Year 1201, naval war is likely

### What Seven Changes

Maritime interests in Seven are purely commercial:

**The Artifacts**: Vampire items are worth fortunes. Who controls the vampire controls the market.

**The Routes**: Vampire-era maps show forgotten shipping lanes. Seven might know them.

**The Chaos**: If everyone's fighting over Seven, no one's fighting over trade routes. War might be delayed—or accelerated.

The Federation doesn't care about vampire politics. They care about vampire profits.

## The Marriage Alliances

### The Current Web

Political marriages bind the kingdoms—but several have failed or soured.

#### Successful Marriages

**Princess Valeria (Valdris) & Duke Bjorn (North)**
- Married Year 1189
- No children yet—suspicious
- Either can't have children or won't
- If Valeria can't produce heirs, her succession claim weakens

**Prince Erik (North) & Lady Helena (North)**
- Married Year 1192
- Two children
- Stable, boring, exactly what Harald wanted
- Erik's succession is secure

**Merchant-Prince Kael (Federation) & Lady Sera (Valdris)**
- Married Year 1185
- Three children
- Most successful political marriage of the generation
- Kael's Valdrian connections give him reformation leverage

#### Failed Marriages

**Prince Marcus (Valdris) & Lady Astrid (North)**
- Betrothed Year 1190
- Marcus entered Church instead
- Technically he fled the marriage
- Northern Kingdom considers this an insult
- King Harald demands compensation—hasn't specified what

**Prince Ragnar (North) & Princess Lucia (Federation)**
- Betrothed Year 1195
- Lucia died of fever Year 1197
- Ragnar suspects poison
- He's been investigating—hasn't found proof
- If he finds it, war

#### Broken Betrothals

**Prince Aldric (Valdris) & Lady Sera Valorian**
- Betrothed Year 1188
- Aldric broke it off for his mistress
- Duke Valorian has never forgiven
- He commands the Valdrian army
- When Aldric inherits, expect coup attempt

**Admiral Blackwood (Federation) & Duchess Thorne (Federation)**
- Betrothed Year 1182
- She broke it off—found his letters to another man
- House Blackwood and House Thorne are now enemies
- The Doge election is personal

### The Marriage Market

Several important figures remain unmarried, creating opportunity:

**Prince Aldric (Valdris)**: Has a mistress, needs a wife. Any kingdom who provides one gains influence.

**Prince Ragnar (North)**: Still grieving Lucia, but must marry. The Church has been suggesting the former-Sister Mira.

**Sera Aldric (Federation)**: Running for Doge but unmarried. Marriage to any mainland kingdom would compromise Federation independence.

### What Seven Changes

Seven complicates every marriage calculation:

**Vampire Blood**: If Seven can have children, his bloodline is the most valuable in the world. Every kingdom wants to marry into it.

**Alliance Shifts**: Whoever controls Seven gains power—marriage alliances will shift to accommodate.

**The Old Betrothals**: Vampires arranged marriages among covenants. What if Seven remembers old obligations? What if Velanna was betrothed?

## The Trade Disputes

### Iron and Blood

**The Conflict**: Northern mines produce 60% of continental iron. Federation ships carry 80% of continental trade. Each can strangle the other.

**Current Status**: Northern Kingdom raised iron prices 15%. Federation raised shipping fees 20%. Both are hurt. Neither will stop.

**The Flashpoint**: A Federation ship seized a Northern iron shipment claiming "unpaid fees." Northern soldiers boarded a Federation ship claiming "stolen goods." Both ships sank. Forty dead.

### Grain and Gold

**The Conflict**: Valdris produces most grain. The North needs grain. Valdris charges whatever they want.

**Current Status**: Drought in Year 1199 raised Valdrian prices 40%. The North threatened to "secure grain sources"—meaning conquer Valdrian farmland.

**The Flashpoint**: Valdrian grain merchants were found murdered in Northern territory. Bandits, officially. But the bandits had military training.

### Magic and Markets

**The Conflict**: Dwarves control Bloodstone. Everyone needs Bloodstone. Dwarves charge whatever they want.

**Current Status**: Dwarves raised prices 25% citing "extraction difficulties." Actually, they're testing how much the market will bear.

**The Flashpoint**: Someone tried to raid a dwarven mine. Failed, but three dwarves died. Dwarves are quietly investigating. If they identify the kingdom responsible, trade stops.

### What Seven Changes

Seven's awakening affects every trade calculation:

**Artifact Market**: If artifacts can be reactivated, their value spikes. Everyone wants them. Trade wars over artifacts begin.

**Blood Economy**: If Seven's blood has value (it does), a new commodity enters the market. Who controls supply?

**Vampire Routes**: Seven might know old trade routes, old mine locations, old stockpiles. This knowledge reshapes economics.

## The Coming Conflicts

### Scenario A: Valdrian Civil War

Emperor dies → Prince Aldric crowned → Duke Valorian rebels → Civil war

**Who wins**: Depends on Church position (they control peasant loyalty) and Northern intervention (they want Valeria on the throne)

**Seven's role**: Every faction wants him as legitimacy symbol. "Even the vampire supports us."

### Scenario B: Northern Invasion

Valdrian succession crisis → Bjorn invades Border Marches → Valdris responds → Continental war

**Who wins**: Depends on Federation naval support (they hate the North but need Valdrian trade)

**Seven's role**: Northern Kingdom claims him as found in their territory. Valdris claims him as general human heritage. War over ownership.

### Scenario C: Maritime Collapse

Doge dies → Contested election → Houses go to war → Federation dissolves

**Who wins**: Whoever absorbs the most trade routes and ships

**Seven's role**: Each House wants artifacts for advantage. Seven is leverage in their internal war.

### Scenario D: All of the Above

All three crises hit simultaneously. Continental chaos. This is the most likely outcome.

**Who wins**: Whoever has the most information, the best alliances, and the willingness to sacrifice short-term for long-term.

**Seven's role**: He becomes the central prize. Whoever controls him controls the narrative of the age.

## Who Benefits from Seven's Awakening

### Clear Winners

**The Inheritors**: Their moment has arrived. Centuries of preparation pay off.

**Prince Ragnar (North)**: He wants knowledge more than conquest. Seven is everything he's wanted.

**The Veil Keepers**: Truth is emerging. Their purpose is validated.

### Clear Losers

**Emperor Aldric IV**: Dies knowing his succession is chaos and a vampire makes it worse.

**Prince Aldric (Valdris)**: His weak position becomes untenable.

**Order of Dawn**: Their enemy appears when they're least prepared.

### It Depends

**Northern Kingdom**: Seven could secure their expansion or ruin it.

**Federation**: Seven could be profitable or catastrophic.

**The Church**: Seven could prove their theology or destroy it.

## The Personal Dimension

### The Relationships That Matter

**Duke Valorian & Prince Aldric**: Personal hatred. The Duke will rebel when Aldric inherits. It's not political—it's revenge.

**Prince Ragnar & House Blackwood**: Ragnar believes they killed Lucia. If he proves it, he'll demand blood justice—ancient Northern law allowing personal execution.

**Sera Aldric & Marcus Blackwood**: She broke their betrothal. He's never forgiven. Their competition for Doge is personal destruction disguised as politics.

**Princess Valeria & Prince Aldric**: She's smarter than him and he knows it. He's heir and she knows it. Sibling rivalry with imperial stakes.

### The Secrets

**Prince Marcus** never took final Church vows because he doesn't believe. He's hiding in the Church from his family.

**Princess Valeria** has been quietly funding Reformist militia. If succession fails, she'll fight.

**Prince Ragnar** has been corresponding with the Inheritors. His father doesn't know.

**Admiral Blackwood** is dying of the same cancer as Emperor Aldric. He wants war before he dies.

## Seven's Position

When Seven awakens, he enters:
- Three succession crises
- Two potential wars
- Multiple trade conflicts
- Dozens of personal vendettas
- Every faction wanting to use him

He has no allies, no resources, no understanding of the current world. He's the most valuable person on the continent and the most vulnerable.

His choices in the first weeks will determine:
- Which faction gains him
- Which conflicts accelerate
- Which leaders survive
- What kind of world he lives in

He can't avoid politics. Politics is coming for him whether he wants it or not.
