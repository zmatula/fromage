# Economic Framework

Who has money, how they got it, and what it buys. Wars cost gold. Politics cost more.

---

## The Artifact Economy

### Scale

**Annual Artifact Trade Value**: ~500,000 gold crowns
**For Comparison**:
- Valdrian imperial budget: 2 million crowns
- Northern military budget: 800,000 crowns
- Average merchant's yearly income: 50 crowns

**Artifacts are worth more than kingdoms spend on armies.**

### Types and Values

**Minor Artifacts** (common)
- Decorative, minor function
- Value: 10-100 crowns
- Who buys: collectors, minor nobles, merchants

**Functional Artifacts** (uncommon)
- Actually do something useful
- Value: 100-1,000 crowns
- Who buys: wealthy, scholars, governments

**Major Artifacts** (rare)
- Significant power or knowledge
- Value: 1,000-10,000 crowns
- Who buys: governments, Banking Houses, secret societies

**Legendary Artifacts** (unique)
- Crown of Seeing, Mind Codex, etc.
- Value: Priceless / will start wars
- Who buys: Not for sale (supposedly)

### Who Controls Trade

**Maritime Republic Banking Houses**
- Control ~60% of artifact trade
- Finance expeditions, take percentage
- Have warehouses, appraisers, security
- Set market prices

**Free Cities Dealers**
- Control ~25% of trade
- Less formal, more illegal
- Will sell anything to anyone
- No questions asked

**Government Seizures**
- Control ~10%
- Confiscated or found on own territory
- Usually kept, sometimes sold secretly

**Black Market**
- ~5% by value (but growing)
- Stolen goods, forbidden items
- Very dangerous, very profitable

## Continental Debt Matrix

### Who Owes What to Dwarves

| Power | Principal | Interest | Purpose | Leverage |
|-------|-----------|----------|---------|----------|
| Valdrian Empire | 500,000 | 5% | War debts, infrastructure | High - succession crisis |
| Northern Dominion | 300,000 | 6% | Weapons, fortifications | Medium - good payer |
| Maritime Republic | 200,000 | 4% | Shipbuilding, banking | Low - trade partners |
| Sanctified States | 150,000 | 5% | Order of Dawn equipment | Medium - reliable |

**Total owed to Ironpeak**: 1,150,000 crowns
**Annual interest to dwarves**: ~60,000 crowns

### The Nuclear Option
If dwarves called all debts simultaneously:
- Every major power's treasury emptied
- Military funding collapses
- Trade halts for lack of credit
- Civil unrest follows

This is why everyone maintains dwarven neutrality.

### Current Dwarven Strategy
- Never call debts (destroys customers)
- Use debt for policy influence
- Extend credit to favored factions
- Deny credit to disfavored ones

House Goldvault extended credit to Prince Aldric's faction. House Ironmark backed Princess Sophia. The succession crisis is partly a proxy war between dwarven banking houses.

## The Artifact Expedition Economy

### How It Works

**Stage 1: Financing**
- Someone finds a site
- Goes to Banking House
- House finances expedition
- Takes 50-70% of finds

**Stage 2: Expedition**
- Team assembled
- Equipment purchased
- Site explored
- Artifacts recovered

**Stage 3: Distribution**
- House takes cut
- Team gets remainder
- Artifacts appraised
- Sold to buyers

**Stage 4: Sale**
- Some to collectors
- Some to governments
- Some to secret societies
- Some disappear

### Costs

**Small Expedition** (5-10 people, minor site)
- Cost: 500-1,000 crowns
- Expected return: 1,000-5,000 crowns
- Risk: moderate

**Major Expedition** (20-50 people, significant site)
- Cost: 5,000-10,000 crowns
- Expected return: 20,000-100,000 crowns
- Risk: high (many don't return)

**Legendary Expedition** (deep site, real danger)
- Cost: 50,000+ crowns
- Expected return: Unknown
- Risk: most people die

### Who Gets Rich

**The Houses**: Always
**Successful Expedition Leaders**: Sometimes
**Team Members**: Rarely
**Scholarship**: Never

## What Money Buys

### Political Influence

**Valdrian Council Vote**: 5,000-20,000 crowns
**Northern Official Bribe**: 1,000-5,000 crowns
**Church "Donation"**: 500-10,000 crowns
**Assassination**: 100-5,000 crowns (depending on target)

### Military Power

**Mercenary Company** (500 men, one year): 50,000 crowns
**War Galley**: 10,000 crowns

#### Military Cost Structure

The headline "500,000 crowns for 10,000 men" applies to **active campaigning**. Actual costs vary:

- **Peacetime Garrison**: 50 crowns/soldier/year (basic wages, food, maintenance)
- **Active Campaigning**: 150 crowns/soldier/year (full wages, supplies, replacement equipment)
- **Elite/Artifact Units**: 300 crowns/soldier/year (specialized equipment, hazard pay)

Most large armies consist of **feudal levies** (no direct cost beyond calling them up) plus a **professional core** (paid year-round). This is why Valdrian Empire can maintain 60,000 soldiers on a 2 million crown budget—40,000 are feudal levies, 20,000 professional (1M crowns).

**War Campaign**: 1-2 million crowns (why wars bankrupt kingdoms)

### Information

**Rumor**: 1-10 crowns
**Specific Information**: 10-100 crowns
**Secret**: 100-1,000 crowns
**Classified Government Information**: 1,000-10,000 crowns
**Location of Major Artifact**: Priceless

## Economic Crises

### The Valdrian Succession

**Economic Impact**
- Factions spending reserves on bribes, mercenaries
- Uncertainty stopping investment
- Banking Houses calling in loans
- Treasury depleted

**Who Benefits**
- Banking Houses (lending at high interest)
- Northern Kingdom (buying influence cheap)
- Mercenaries (lots of work)

### The Maritime Coup

**Economic Impact**
- Trade disrupted
- Insurance rates up
- Some routes closed
- Artifact prices volatile

**Who Benefits**
- Banking Houses (they caused it)
- Pirates (chaos)
- Northern Kingdom (disrupted rival)

### The Sanctified Persecution

**Economic Impact**
- Scholars fleeing with knowledge
- Research stopped
- Some artifacts destroyed
- Markets nervous

**Who Benefits**
- Black market (forbidden goods valuable)
- Free Cities (refugees bring knowledge)
- No one, really
