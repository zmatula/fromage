# Premise Constraints - Non-Negotiables

These are the foundational rules that cannot be changed. All worldbuilding must work within these constraints.

---

## Timeline Constraints

### The Collapse
- **When**: Exactly 1,200 years before present day (Year 0)
- **What**: Complete collapse of vampire civilization
- **Result**: Near-total destruction of vampire knowledge, technology, and population

### Present Day
- **Tech Level**: Medieval (Game of Thrones equivalent)
- **Magic**: Exists but is limited, costly, and practical
- **Vampire Awareness**: Current world largely unaware of vampire history
- **Ruins**: Lost vampire cities exist underground, mostly unknown

---

## Race Constraints

### Playable Races
1. **Humans** - Multiple sub-races/cultures
2. **Elves** - Multiple sub-races/cultures
3. **Dwarves** - Multiple sub-races/cultures
4. **Goblins** - Multiple sub-races/cultures
5. **Vampires** - Extinct/nearly extinct (protagonist exception)

### Race Requirements
- Each race must have distinct sub-races with cultural variations
- Each race must have their own interpretation of history
- Each race must have internal conflicts and factions
- Relationships between races must be complex, not binary

---

## Protagonist Constraints

### Nature
- **What He Is**: Experimental vampire relic/creation
- **Origin**: Created during vampire civilization (pre-collapse)
- **Survival**: Somehow survived the 1,200-year gap

### Abilities
- **Magic**: Enhanced compared to present-day practitioners
- **Limitations**: Still limited - NOT overpowered
- **Cost**: Magic must have meaningful costs
- **Growth**: Can develop but within defined limits

### Knowledge
- **Memory**: Fragmented and/or partially false
- **History**: Knows some vampire history but not complete picture
- **Self-Knowledge**: Doesn't fully understand what he is or why he was created

### Story Role
- **Discovery**: Must uncover truths about himself and vampire history
- **Unique**: One of a kind or among very few survivors
- **Catalyst**: His existence should drive plot forward

---

## Magic System Constraints

### General Rules
- Magic is **limited** - not all-powerful
- Magic is **costly** - physical, mental, or material cost
- Magic is **practical** - used for specific purposes, not whimsy
- Magic has **rules** - consistent internal logic

### Vampire Magic vs Current Magic
- Vampire magic was more advanced but still limited
- Current practitioners have lost most vampire techniques
- Some "forbidden" magic is actually vampire methods
- Protagonist bridges the gap but imperfectly

### What Magic Cannot Do
- Resurrection of the dead (true resurrection)
- Time travel or reversal
- Mind reading without cost/limitation
- Infinite power or energy
- Easy solutions to complex problems

---

## World Structure Constraints

### Political
- Multiple empires/kingdoms (6-10 major powers)
- Complex alliances and rivalries
- No clear "good" or "evil" nations
- Internal factions within each power

### Secret Organizations
- 4-8 secret societies with different goals
- Some seek vampire knowledge
- Some seek to destroy/hide it
- Conflicting agendas create tension

### Geography
- Multiple continents/major regions
- Vampire ruins distributed strategically
- Travel takes meaningful time
- Resources create conflict

---

## Narrative Constraints

### GRRM Style Requirements
- **No pure heroes or villains** - everyone is complex
- **Actions have consequences** - choices matter and echo
- **Anyone can die** - no plot armor
- **Multiple POVs** - 5-8 major viewpoint characters
- **Political intrigue** - as important as action
- **Historical ambiguity** - no single "true" version

### Structural Requirements
- Multiple interwoven storylines
- Character arcs with transformation
- Cause-and-effect chains
- Earned payoffs (properly foreshadowed)

### What to Avoid
- Deus ex machina solutions
- Chosen one tropes (protagonist is unique but not prophesied)
- Clear good vs evil
- Magic solving all problems
- Anachronistic attitudes/technology

---

## Information Architecture Constraints

### Historical Knowledge
- Different races remember history differently
- Scholarly consensus differs from popular belief
- Shadow truth differs from both
- Some things can never be known

### Discovery Pacing
- Revelations must be earned
- Major truths revealed gradually
- Some mysteries remain unsolved
- Reader should be able to piece together clues

### Character Knowledge
- Characters only know what they could know
- Information asymmetry drives conflict
- Secrets between characters create tension
- Protagonist doesn't have all answers

---

## Flexibility Notes

While these constraints are non-negotiable, they allow for:

- Creative interpretation of vampire civilization
- Variety in how collapse occurred
- Different theories about what vampires wanted
- Complex relationships between races
- Varied geography and cultures
- Multiple valid interpretations of history

The constraints define the **boundaries**. Everything within those boundaries is open for creative development.
