# Era: The Recent Past (Year 1100 to 1200)

The century leading to the present day. A time of rising tensions, major discoveries, and forces aligning toward a crisis that will arrive with the protagonist's awakening.

---

## Era Overview

**Duration**: 100 years
**Character**: Tension, discovery, convergence
**Dominant Powers**: Four empires in uneasy balance
**Key Development**: Deep ruin discoveries; secret society activity intensifies
**End State**: Present day—storm clouds gathering

---

## The Current World (Year 1200)

### The Four Great Empires

**The Valdrian Empire**
- Population: ~4 million (humans, some dwarves/elves)
- Government: Hereditary monarchy with noble council
- Character: Traditional, proud, militaristic
- Artifacts: Largest royal collection
- Current Ruler: Emperor Cassius VIII
- Capital: Valdris Prime
- Recent Issues: Succession uncertainty; Northern rivalry; internal reform faction

**The Northern Dominion**
- Population: ~3 million (humans, significant dwarf minority)
- Government: Elective military monarchy
- Character: Martial, pragmatic, ambitious
- Artifacts: Best integrated into military
- Current Ruler: Warlord-King Aldric XII
- Capital: Ironhold
- Recent Issues: Expansionist faction; veteran unrest; ruin access disputes

**The Sanctified States**
- Population: ~5 million (humans, very few non-humans)
- Government: Theocratic confederation
- Character: Religious, conservative, suspicious of magic
- Artifacts: Officially restricted; secretly coveted
- Current Ruler: High Theocrat Solarius VII
- Capital: Solarium
- Recent Issues: Fundamentalist revival; reform resistance; non-human persecution

**The Maritime Republic**
- Population: ~3 million (most cosmopolitan)
- Government: Merchant oligarchy
- Character: Commercial, pragmatic, cosmopolitan
- Artifacts: Best artifact market and study
- Current Ruler: First Merchant Corus IX
- Capital: Port Sovereign
- Recent Issues: Class tensions; piracy resurgence; Valdrian trade wars

### Secondary Powers

**Ironpeak Confederation**
- Dwarven industrial power
- Weapons and banking
- Neutral in empire conflicts
- Growing isolationist faction

**Silverwood Domain**
- Elven mysterious isolate
- No diplomatic contact since Year 1050
- Rumored internal crisis
- Preserves deepest vampire knowledge

**Broken Lands Confederation**
- Goblin marginal territories
- Increasing raids suggest desperation
- New leadership more aggressive
- Dark magic rumors

## Recent History (Year 1100-1200)

### Decade by Decade

**Year 1100-1110: The Scholar's Decade**
- Peak of learning
- Major translations completed
- Confidence in understanding vampire history
- Hubris before discoveries

**Year 1110-1120: The Deep Discoveries**
- Year 1112: Vel'Thoras deep expedition reaches new levels
- Year 1115: Northern tomb complex opened
- Year 1118: Twilight border anomalies detected
- Year 1119: "Living artifact" recovered (sealed immediately)

**Year 1120-1130: The Reassessment**
- Year 1121: Scholar purge by Veil Keepers
- Year 1124: Inheritors go underground after exposure
- Year 1127: Order of Dawn expands rapidly
- Year 1129: First hints vampire knowledge more advanced than believed

**Year 1130-1140: Rising Tensions**
- Year 1131: Valdrian-Northern border incident
- Year 1135: Sanctified fundamentalism peaks
- Year 1138: Trade war between empires
- Year 1140: Assassination attempt on Northern king

**Year 1140-1150: The Shadow Conflict**
- Secret society war intensifies
- Artifact thefts multiply
- Scholars disappear
- Ruins sealed by various parties

**Year 1150-1160: The Cool Peace**
- Year 1152: Congress of Silver (empire summit)
- Year 1155: Non-aggression treaties
- Year 1158: Trade normalization
- Surface peace; underground tensions

**Year 1160-1170: The Preparation**
- All major powers rearming
- Secret societies repositioning
- Key artifacts changing hands
- Diplomatic marriages and alliances

**Year 1170-1180: The Discoveries**
- Year 1172: Vel'Thoras inner sanctum located (not breached)
- Year 1175: Twilight sanctuary entrance found (not opened)
- Year 1178: Dimensional readings in multiple locations
- Year 1180: "Awakening protocols" texts discovered

**Year 1180-1190: The Waiting**
- Everyone knows something is coming
- No one knows what
- Expeditions planned and cancelled
- Secret society conflicts go quiet (preparing?)

**Year 1190-1200: The Present**
- Year 1192: New Northern king (Aldric XII) more aggressive
- Year 1195: Sanctified persecution of magic users
- Year 1197: Valdrian succession crisis begins
- Year 1199: Maritime Republic internal coup
- Year 1200: Story begins

## Key Recent Events

### Year 1112: The Vel'Thoras Expedition

**What Happened**:
- Joint Valdrian-Maritime scholarly expedition
- Reached deeper levels of research complex
- Found evidence of stasis chambers
- Expedition lost 30% to traps and guardians
- Sealed and declared too dangerous

**Significance**:
- Protagonist's chamber possibly detected but not reached
- Suggested vampires might have survived
- Created fear of what else might be down there
- Multiple factions planning return missions

### Year 1118: The Living Artifact

**What Happened**:
- Northern expedition recovered artifact
- Artifact exhibited signs of awareness
- Began communicating (demanding blood?)
- Sealed in Veil Keeper vault
- All witnesses sworn to secrecy

**Significance**:
- First proof vampire creations could "live"
- Raised questions about all artifacts
- Intensified secret society conflict
- Northern king wants it back

### Year 1127: Order of Dawn Expansion

**What Happened**:
- Order gains massive recruitment after "Living Artifact" rumors
- New fortress-monasteries built
- Training intensifies
- Demands more political power
- Receives Sanctified States patronage

**Significance**:
- Military force preparing for vampire return
- Potentially the most dangerous faction if protagonist found
- Growing influence in multiple empires
- Knights actively searching ruins

### Year 1175: The Twilight Door

**What Happened**:
- Inheritors located probable sanctuary entrance
- Automated defenses activated
- Expedition wiped out
- Entrance self-sealed
- Proved sanctuaries exist and are defended

**Significance**:
- Vampire (or their systems) still functional
- Major Inheritor setback
- Suggests Twilight preparations worked
- What's inside? Who's inside?

### Year 1178: The Dimensional Readings

**What Happened**:
- Multiple locations showed dimensional instability
- Similar to pre-collapse patterns
- Centered on The Scar
- Growing stronger annually
- No explanation found

**Significance**:
- Echoes of Ascension Experiment?
- Something stirring?
- The breaches reopening?
- Drives current urgency

### Year 1180: Awakening Protocols

**What Happened**:
- Translated text describing how to wake stasis subjects
- Specific to Sanguine Vessels program
- Incomplete—missing key steps
- Currently held by Inheritors

**Significance**:
- Someone could wake the protagonist
- Or wake other stasis vampires
- Or be woken by automatic systems
- Inheritors seeking completion

## The Story Begins (Year 1200)

### Current Political Situation

**Open Tensions**:
- Valdrian-Northern border militarized
- Sanctified persecution increasing
- Maritime trade wars
- Goblin raids escalating

**Hidden Tensions**:
- Secret society cold war
- Artifact arms race
- Ruin expedition competition
- Dimensional instability

### Current Players

**Imperial Leaders**:
- Emperor Cassius VIII (Valdrian): Old, no clear heir, reform vs tradition
- Warlord-King Aldric XII (Northern): Young, aggressive, wants glory
- High Theocrat Solarius VII (Sanctified): Fundamentalist, aging, fears change
- First Merchant Corus IX (Maritime): New, uncertain, facing opposition

**Secret Society Leaders**:
- Veil Keepers: Prepare to destroy protagonist if found
- Inheritors: Actively seeking to wake protagonist
- Order of Dawn: Ready to kill protagonist on sight

### The Protagonist's Situation

**Location**: Stasis chamber in Vel'Thoras inner sanctum

**Status**: Sustained by automatic systems for 1,200 years

**What Will Wake Him**:
- Could be: Inheritors with incomplete protocol
- Could be: Automatic timer finally reaching zero
- Could be: Dimensional instability triggering systems
- Could be: Expedition breaching chamber

**What He'll Wake To**:
- World that doesn't know he exists
- Empires that fear his kind
- Secret societies that want to use/destroy him
- History that remembers his people as demons
- Partial memories of a civilization 1,200 years dead
- Enhanced abilities in a weakened magical world
- Questions about what he is and why he was made

## Conditions for Story

### Why Now?

**Convergence of Factors**:
1. Dimensional instability suggests old wounds reopening
2. Discovery of awakening protocols enables action
3. Political tensions mean any disruption cascades
4. Secret societies at peak conflict
5. 1,200 years of stasis maintenance approaching limits
6. The truth is buried but not destroyed

### What's at Stake?

**For Protagonist**:
- Survival in hostile world
- Understanding his nature and purpose
- Choices about how to exist
- Connection to dead civilization

**For World**:
- What he knows could change everything
- What he can do could change power balance
- His existence proves vampires can survive
- Others might be woken
- Old powers might return

**For Secret Societies**:
- Veil Keepers: Nightmare scenario
- Inheritors: Opportunity of millennia
- Order of Dawn: Prophecy fulfilled

**For Empires**:
- New power to court or destroy
- Political realignment
- Religious crisis (if divine punishment narrative collapses)
- Arms race for vampire magic
