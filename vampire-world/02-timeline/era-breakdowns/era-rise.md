# Era: The Rise (Year -1000 to -500)

The five centuries when vampires conquered the known world, established their civilization, and made the choices that would define their existence—and their end.

---

## Era Overview

**Duration**: 500 years
**Character**: Conquest, construction, codification
**Dominant Faction**: None (era of unification and division)
**Key Development**: Bloodweb creation; covenant formation; servant race subjugation
**End State**: Unified civilization ready for Golden Age

---

## Era Phases

### Phase 1: The Conquest Wars (Year -1000 to -900)

The hundred years when scattered vampire clans united and conquered the other races.

**Starting Situation (Year -1000)**:
- Vampires exist as dozens of independent clans
- Other races have their own kingdoms and territories
- No race dominates; constant shifting conflicts
- Vampires are powerful individually but uncoordinated

**The Unification**:
- Year -995: First Conclave of Clans; discuss alliance against other races
- Year -990: War Leader Vykros the Conqueror emerges; begins unifying by force and persuasion
- Year -980: Sixty clans united under Vykros; "Eternal Dominion" declared
- Year -970: Remaining independent clans absorbed or destroyed
- Year -960: All vampires nominally unified

**The Conquest Campaigns**:
- Year -958: War against human kingdoms begins
- Year -940: Major human kingdoms fall; guerrilla resistance continues
- Year -925: Elven forests invaded; long bloody campaign
- Year -910: Dwarven mountains besieged; finally fall Year -905
- Year -900: Goblin swamps conquered last; total dominion achieved

**Methods**:
- Superior individual combat power
- Night attacks exploiting vampire strengths
- Terror tactics to break morale
- Divide and conquer between races
- Promises to collaborators (rarely kept)

**Costs**:
- Vampire casualties: ~100,000 (significant for small population)
- Human dead: ~2 million
- Elven dead: ~500,000
- Dwarven dead: ~300,000
- Goblin dead: ~1 million
- Total devastation of pre-vampire civilization

### Phase 2: The Founding Generation (Year -900 to -800)

Establishing the systems that would define vampire civilization.

**Challenges**:
- Conquered populations vastly outnumber vampires (~50:1)
- Vampire population small (~500,000)
- No experience governing
- Resistance constant and deadly

**Solutions Implemented**:

*Servant Race Control*:
- Year -895: "Domestication" programs begin—carrot and stick
- Year -880: Bloodline registration for all subjects
- Year -865: Selective breeding initiated
- Year -850: Magical bindings deployed for key populations
- Year -835: Collaboration rewarded; resistance lineages exterminated
- Year -820: First generation raised entirely under vampire rule

*Vampire Governance*:
- Year -890: Council of Clans established (unwieldy; 60+ members)
- Year -870: Reduced to Council of Twelve (major clans only)
- Year -855: Philosophical divisions emerge; proto-covenants form
- Year -840: Three main factions clear: knowledge-seekers, warriors, spiritualists
- Year -825: Council of Twelve becomes Council of Three
- Year -810: Covenants formally established
- Year -800: Bloodweb project initiated to unify civilization

*Infrastructure*:
- Year -885: First vampire cities founded (above ground initially)
- Year -870: Road network begun
- Year -860: Communication systems developed (magical)
- Year -850: Portal network prototype
- Year -840: Underground expansion begins
- Year -820: Standardization of systems across territories
- Year -800: Bloodweb project will integrate everything

### Phase 3: The Covenant Formation (Year -800 to -700)

The century when three covenants crystallized and the Bloodweb transformed everything.

**The Three Covenants Establish**:

*Covenant of the Eternal Mind (Year -810)*:
- Formed from clans valuing knowledge and magical research
- First leader: Archon Nathara the Enlightened
- Mission: Advance vampire capability through knowledge
- Claimed: Research, education, magical development
- Took responsibility for Bloodweb project

*Covenant of the Crimson Throne (Year -808)*:
- Formed from warrior clans and Vykros's loyalists
- First leader: Warlord-Regent Drakhan the Fierce
- Mission: Maintain order and vampire supremacy through strength
- Claimed: Military, borders, servant race management

*Covenant of the Silver Twilight (Year -805)*:
- Formed from spiritual and traditionalist clans
- First leader: High Seer Vaelwyn the Ancient
- Mission: Preserve vampire essence and spiritual traditions
- Claimed: Religion, culture, death rites

**The Bloodweb**:
- Year -800: Project initiated by Mind Covenant
- Year -785: First prototype connects three cities
- Year -770: Expanded to all major settlements
- Year -755: Communication functions added
- Year -740: Power distribution functions added
- Year -725: Life support functions added (blood potency enhancement)
- Year -710: Full network operational
- Year -700: Mind controls Bloodweb; other covenants dependent

**This Century's Legacy**:
- Covenant system created divisions that would never heal
- Bloodweb began as tool, became crutch, would become noose
- Mind's control of Bloodweb gave them permanent advantage
- Throne and Twilight would resent this for 700 years

### Phase 4: The Stabilization (Year -700 to -600)

Consolidating gains, optimizing systems, beginning the climb to Golden Age.

**Political**:
- Year -695: First Covenant War (Mind vs Throne); brief, inconclusive
- Year -680: Treaty of Boundaries establishes permanent territories
- Year -665: Twilight mediates disputes; gains brief prominence
- Year -650: Power balance established; cold peace begins
- Year -620: Joint projects resume; civilization advancing
- Year -600: "Harmony Decade"—closest covenants ever came to cooperation

**Technological/Magical**:
- Year -690: Bloodweb efficiency reaches 95%
- Year -675: Portal network connects all major cities
- Year -660: Weather control systems in core territories
- Year -645: Life extension techniques perfected
- Year -630: Servant race optimization theories developed
- Year -615: First dimensional observations (accidental)
- Year -600: Blood-forging techniques mature

**Servant Races**:
- Year -690: Second generation raised under vampire rule
- Year -670: Resistance movements crushed (temporarily)
- Year -650: Population growth programs expanded
- Year -630: Optimization experiments begin (limited)
- Year -610: Humans: 3 million; Elves: 2 million; Dwarves: 1.5 million; Goblins: 3 million
- Year -600: All races fully integrated into vampire economy

### Phase 5: The Transition to Golden Age (Year -600 to -500)

Setting the stage for peak civilization—and its eventual fall.

**Political**:
- Year -595: Harmony ends; covenant competition resumes
- Year -580: Mind Covenant formally assumes "first among equals" status
- Year -565: Throne military buildup; arms race begins
- Year -550: Twilight begins withdrawal from central politics
- Year -535: Second Covenant War (brief skirmish; settled diplomatically)
- Year -520: Uneasy balance; each covenant pursuing own agenda
- Year -500: Golden Age begins; surface harmony, underlying division

**Technological/Magical**:
- Year -590: Bloodweb integration complete; all vampires connected
- Year -575: Blood potency enhancement universally deployed
- Year -560: Servant optimization programs systematized
- Year -545: Dimensional research formalized
- Year -530: First "Eternal Cities" underground complexes
- Year -515: Medical arts reach near-peak
- Year -500: Ready for Golden Age expansion

**Servant Races**:
- Year -590: Third generation; resistance cultural rather than military
- Year -575: Populations doubled since conquest
- Year -560: Optimization experiments intensify
- Year -545: Goblin breeding programs industrialized
- Year -530: Human administrator class expanded
- Year -515: Dwarven engineering expertise maximally exploited
- Year -500: All races at production peak; resentment buried but not gone

## Key Figures of the Rise Era

### Vykros the Conqueror (Year -1020? to -890)
- United the clans through force and charisma
- Led the conquest campaigns
- Died before covenant formation
- Legacy: The founder; remembered as hero or tyrant depending on perspective
- Possibly assassinated to prevent his preventing covenant division

### Archon Nathara the Enlightened (Year -850 to -720)
- Founded and led Mind Covenant for 90 years
- Conceived and built the Bloodweb
- Brilliant but arrogant; created dependencies intentionally
- Legacy: Created the tool that would kill them all

### Warlord-Regent Drakhan the Fierce (Year -870 to -695)
- First Throne leader; Vykros's most loyal general
- Built military institutions from conquest bands
- Started First Covenant War; didn't win
- Legacy: Throne's resentment of Mind began with him

### High Seer Vaelwyn the Ancient (Year -900 to -600)
- Oldest vampire at time; claimed pre-history memories
- Founded Twilight to preserve "old ways"
- Increasingly marginalized as "progress" dominated
- Legacy: Twilight's withdrawl and preparation tradition

## The Rise Era in Memory

### How Later Vampires Remembered It
"The time of greatness when we took our rightful place. Our ancestors built everything we have. We only failed when we forgot their strength."

### How Servant Races Remember It
"The time of conquest and chains. They killed our kingdoms, stole our children, bred us like cattle. Never forget, never forgive."

### The Shadow Truth
The Rise created everything that would destroy them:
- Covenant divisions that prevented cooperation
- Bloodweb dependency that became fatal vulnerability
- Servant race trauma that ensured no help in crisis
- Belief in vampire supremacy that prevented adaptation

---

## Physical Evidence from Rise Era

**Conquest-Era Ruins**: Oldest vampire structures; cruder but sturdier than later work. Often used as foundations for later buildings.

**Founding Cities**: Original above-ground cities; some still standing as modern ruins; most buried by later expansion.

**Bloodweb Foundation Nodes**: Oldest parts of network; deepest underground; most critical and most decayed by Year 0.

**Early Records**: Few survive; mostly in sealed vaults; language archaic even to Golden Age vampires.
