# Era: The Immediate Aftermath (Year 0 to 50)

The fifty years of chaos, liberation, and slaughter that followed the vampire collapse. The old world died; a new one struggled to be born.

---

## Era Overview

**Duration**: 50 years
**Character**: Chaos, survival, revenge, first attempts at order
**Dominant Power**: None (power vacuum)
**Key Development**: Former servant races establish independence; vampire survivors hunted
**End State**: Proto-kingdoms emerge; vampire civilization fading from memory

---

## The First Year (Year 0-1)

### Month 1: The Dying

**What Happened**:
- Sundering Wave spread outward from capital
- Vampires died by the millions
- Communication and transportation networks collapsed
- Servant race populations suddenly masterless
- Initial confusion—no one knew what was happening

**Servant Race Response**:
- First days: Confusion, fear, hiding
- First week: Realization that vampires were dying
- Second week: First uprisings and escapes
- Third week: Organized resistance emerges
- Fourth week: Active hunting of vampire survivors begins

### Months 2-6: The Liberation

**Regional Variations**:

*Central Region (The Scar)*:
- Completely devastated
- No survivors of any race near ground zero
- Dimensional instabilities made approach deadly
- Remained uninhabitable

*Northern Territories (Former Throne)*:
- Throne attempted to maintain order
- Failed due to starvation and servant uprisings
- Military discipline held longest here
- Human and dwarf populations rose first

*Eastern Provinces (Research Centers)*:
- Looting began immediately for valuable materials
- Many facilities sealed themselves
- Protagonist in stasis in Vel'Thoras
- Elven and human populations dominant

*Western Reaches (Former Twilight)*:
- Twilight sanctuaries sealed
- Surface populations largely abandoned
- Forests began reclaiming territory
- Mixed race populations scattered

*Southern Expanse (Mining/Agriculture)*:
- Dwarves took over mines immediately
- Goblins seized agricultural lands
- Least vampire resistance
- Fastest transition to independence

### Months 7-12: The First Order

**Emerging Leadership**:
- Warlords rose from former servant administrators
- Religious figures claimed divine mandate
- Military leaders from former auxiliary forces
- Tribal chiefs among goblins

**First Conflicts**:
- Race vs race for territory
- Faction vs faction within races
- Everyone vs remaining vampires
- Looters vs settlers

## Years 1-10: The Scramble

### Political Chaos

**Human Territories**:
- Dozens of petty kingdoms declared
- Former administrators became lords
- Religious movements gained power
- Constant warfare between claimants

**Elven Response**:
- Retreated to forests where possible
- Attempted to preserve knowledge
- Internal conflict: isolationists vs integrationists
- Some allied with human kingdoms

**Dwarven Strategy**:
- Seized and held mines and mountain holds
- Defensive posture
- Began rebuilding engineering knowledge
- Traded weapons and tools for food

**Goblin Expansion**:
- Claimed "undesirable" territories
- Rapid population growth
- Tribal confederation attempts
- Raided all other races

### The Vampire Hunt

**Organized Hunting**:
- Year 2: First "Purifier" bands formed
- Year 3: Bounties offered for vampire remains
- Year 5: Major vampire refuge discovered and destroyed
- Year 8: Hunting becomes profession

**Vampire Survivor Strategies**:
- Deep hiding (underground, remote areas)
- Disguise (some could pass as other races)
- Stasis (waiting for better times)
- Flight (to other dimensions, deep underground)

**The Collaborator Purges**:
- Anyone who served vampires closely: targeted
- Administrators, scholars, servants: killed
- Knowledge destruction accelerated
- "Tainted" bloodlines tracked and eliminated

### Knowledge Destruction

**Deliberate Destruction**:
- Vampire libraries burned
- Records in vampire language destroyed
- Magical texts feared and eliminated
- "Collaborator" scholars executed

**Accidental Loss**:
- Infrastructure decay destroyed records
- No one could read vampire script
- Oral knowledge died with practitioners
- Context for artifacts lost

**What Survived**:
- Some texts hidden by brave scholars
- Dwarven technical records (partial)
- Goblin shamanic oral traditions
- Artifacts (without instructions)

## Years 10-30: First Consolidation

### Emerging Powers

**Human Kingdoms** (by Year 30):
- **Valdris Restored**: Eastern kingdom; claimed descent from pre-vampire human royalty
- **The Sanctified Realm**: Central-south; theocratic state; "divine punishment" narrative
- **Free Cities League**: Coastal; merchant republics; pragmatic
- **Northern Marches**: Former Throne territory; military aristocracy

**Elven Enclaves**:
- **Silverwood Remnant**: Largest forest domain; isolationist
- **Scholar Enclaves**: Urban elves preserving knowledge; scattered
- **Wild Elves**: Rejected civilization; forest tribes

**Dwarven Holds**:
- **Ironpeak Confederation**: Largest; controlled best mines
- **Deep Throne Remnant**: Ancient hold; very isolated
- **Surface Dwarves**: Integrated with human kingdoms; minority

**Goblin Territories**:
- **Great Swamp Dominion**: Largest; shamanic leadership
- **Broken Lands Horde**: Nomadic raiders
- **Urban Goblins**: Underclass in human cities

### Religious Development

**Human Religions**:
- **Church of Divine Light**: Vampires were demons; gods saved the righteous
- **Ancestor Cults**: Honor those who resisted; propitiate the dead
- **Mystery Schools**: Secret traditions preserving pre-vampire knowledge

**Elven Spirituality**:
- Nature worship intensified
- Ancestor veneration
- Some preserved vampire-era magical traditions (hidden)

**Dwarven Beliefs**:
- Ancestor and craft-god worship
- Runes took on religious significance
- Deep holds maintained old traditions

**Goblin Shamanism**:
- Spirit world beliefs strengthened
- Shamans became political powers
- Blood magic traditions preserved (feared by others)

### Economic Foundations

**Trade Networks**:
- Year 15: First post-collapse trade routes established
- Year 20: Dwarven weapons/tools become currency equivalent
- Year 25: Coastal cities become trading hubs
- Year 30: Inter-racial trade normalized (tense but functional)

**Resource Conflicts**:
- Former vampire mines: major conflict zones
- Agricultural land: human-goblin warfare
- Forests: elf-human tension
- Artifacts: everyone vs everyone

## Years 30-50: Stabilization

### Political Map (Year 50)

**Major Powers**:
1. Valdris Kingdom (human, east)
2. Sanctified Realm (human, central-south)
3. Free Cities League (human, coast)
4. Northern Marches (human, north)
5. Ironpeak Confederation (dwarf, mountains)
6. Silverwood Domain (elf, western forests)
7. Great Swamp Dominion (goblin, swamps/wastelands)

**Contested Zones**:
- The Scar (uninhabitable, avoided)
- Border regions between all powers
- Vampire ruin sites
- Former vampire cities

### Cultural Crystallization

**Racial Identities Forming**:
- Each race developing distinct post-vampire identity
- Shared trauma becoming founding mythology
- Inter-racial suspicion institutionalized
- "Never again" mentality toward vampire-like power

**Historical Narratives**:
- First written histories of collapse (very biased)
- Oral traditions crystallizing into "fact"
- Each race's heroic narrative taking shape
- Vampire history becoming demon mythology

### Vampire Situation (Year 50)

**Survivors**:
- Estimated: fewer than 10,000
- Locations: deep underground, sealed facilities, stasis, other dimensions
- Status: hunted, hidden, or hibernating
- Organization: none; individual survival only

**Evidence Remaining**:
- Ruins: dangerous, looted near surface, unexplored deep
- Artifacts: scattered, misunderstood, valuable
- Records: fragments, mostly unreadable
- Corpses: found regularly, source of fear and study

**Memory**:
- Living memory still exists (barely)
- Last people born under vampire rule now elderly
- Children know vampires only as monsters
- Transition from history to legend beginning

## Key Figures of the Aftermath

### Human Leaders

**Lord Valdris III** (Year 5-45)
- Claimed descent from pre-vampire royalty
- United eastern human territories
- Founded Valdris Kingdom
- Pragmatic; preserved some knowledge

**High Priestess Solara** (Year 8-52)
- Founded Church of Divine Light
- Established Sanctified Realm
- Drove "divine punishment" narrative
- Destroyed much vampire knowledge as "demonic"

**Admiral Corus** (Year 15-48)
- United coastal cities
- Founded Free Cities League
- Merchant prince; valued trade over ideology
- Preserved vampire artifacts for profit

### Dwarven Leaders

**High King Thoram Ironpeak** (Year 3-67)
- United dwarven holds
- Established Ironpeak Confederation
- Focus on defensive strength
- Preserved technical knowledge

### Elven Leaders

**Elder Selindra Silverwood** (Year 0-120)
- Led elven retreat to forests
- Founded Silverwood Domain
- Isolationist philosophy
- Preserved some vampire-era knowledge

### Goblin Leaders

**Great Shaman Kretch** (Year 10-35)
- United swamp tribes
- Founded Great Swamp Dominion
- Preserved shamanic traditions
- Expanded goblin territory

## Seeds of Future Eras

### What This Era Established

**Political Patterns**:
- Human dominance in most territories
- Dwarven defensive isolationism
- Elven forest retreats
- Goblin marginalization
- Inter-racial tension as norm

**Cultural Patterns**:
- Vampires as demons/monsters
- "Divine punishment" narrative dominant among humans
- Knowledge destruction justified
- Anti-magic sentiment in some regions

**Economic Patterns**:
- Dwarven industrial importance
- Coastal trade dominance
- Artifact value established
- Resource conflicts between races

**Religious Patterns**:
- Church of Divine Light rising
- Shamanic traditions underground
- Knowledge = suspicion in religious areas
- Magic as potentially dangerous

### Questions Left for Next Era

- Which kingdoms will survive?
- Will races cooperate or war?
- What happens when artifacts are activated?
- When will ruins be systematically explored?
- How long until vampires become pure legend?
