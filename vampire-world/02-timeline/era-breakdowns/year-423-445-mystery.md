# The Silent Years: 423-445

Twenty-two years with no records. Not "few records." None. This is what we know, what we suspect, and why it matters.

---

## The Absence

### What Should Exist

By Year 423, the post-Collapse world had five major powers, three established churches, two dozen universities, and hundreds of monasteries—all keeping records. Tax rolls, birth records, death records, court proceedings, property transfers, religious chronicles, scholarly correspondence.

We have records from Year 422. We have records from Year 446. The years between are empty.

### What Actually Exists

**From Valdris**: Nothing. Imperial archives show the gap. Librarians in Year 500 noted it; historians in Year 800 studied it. The records weren't lost—they were never filed.

**From the Northern Kingdoms**: Nothing. Multiple kingdoms, all missing the same years. Some have pages torn from bound chronicles. Some have whole volumes removed.

**From the Church**: Nothing official. The gap in Church records is actually larger—Year 420 to 448. The Church claims "administrative transition." Twenty-eight years of transition?

**From Silverwood**: Elves don't share records. But Thandril Shadowleaf once noted that the Silverwood Council has a sealed archive from this period, accessible only to three elders. They won't say why.

**From the Dwarves**: Dwarves record everything. They have tax records from Year 423. They have mine production from Year 440. They have... nothing else. The Ironpeak archivist who noticed this in Year 890 was found dead three days later. Accident, they said.

## The Fragments

Not everything was erased. Pieces survive—in private collections, buried archives, oral traditions. They raise more questions than they answer.

### Fragment 1: The Merchant's Letter

**Source**: Brennan Trading Company family papers, discovered Year 1156
**Date**: Year 424

> "The roads are closed again. Third time this month. Commander says it's plague but there are no sick people, only soldiers. They're not keeping disease out—they're keeping people in. Something is happening in the interior. Messengers from Valdris stopped coming. The count hasn't collected taxes. It's been four months."

**Questions**:
- What required military cordons?
- Why did official communication stop?
- Why wasn't this plague in any health records?

### Fragment 2: The Monk's Confession

**Source**: Sealed confession, Church archive breach Year 1089
**Date**: Year 443 (written); Year 446 (sealed)

> "I helped burn them. God forgive me. We burned every document, every letter, every chronicle from the affected years. Father Aldric said we were protecting the faithful from dangerous knowledge. I asked what knowledge. He said, 'The knowledge that we were wrong.' Then he had me seal this confession and made me swear to forget. I cannot forget. The screaming in those pages. The names of the dead. All gone now."

**Questions**:
- What was in the documents?
- What was the Church "wrong" about?
- Who were "the dead" whose names were burned?

### Fragment 3: The Scholar's Note

**Source**: Margin of unrelated text, Valdrian Academy Library
**Date**: Approximately Year 460

> "They say the Second Collapse was worse than the first because we did it to ourselves."

**Questions**:
- What was the Second Collapse?
- Who is "we"?
- What did they do?

### Fragment 4: The Dwarven Record

**Source**: Sealed Ironpeak archive, contents leaked Year 1178
**Date**: Year 441

> "Surface delegation arrived. Unusual composition—humans, elves, even goblins working together. They requested access to the Forge. Denied. They requested information about the Forge. Denied. They requested... gods below, they requested we help them build another one. What are they trying to make?"

**Questions**:
- Why were traditional enemies cooperating?
- What did they want to create?
- Did they succeed?

### Fragment 5: The Goblin Memory

**Source**: Oral tradition, transcribed by Inheritor agent Year 1140
**Date**: Remembers Year 430s

> "We almost remembered. The shamans found the old ways. They began to undo what was done to us. Then the humans came with fire, and the elves came with magic, and they broke us again. They broke us worse. They made us forget we were forgetting."

**Questions**:
- What were goblins trying to remember?
- Why did humans and elves cooperate to stop them?
- What was done to goblins the second time?

## The Theories

### Theory 1: The Vampire Rising

**Proponents**: Inheritors of Blood (internal debate)
**Summary**: Vampire survivors emerged from stasis, attempted to rebuild, were destroyed

**The Case**:
- Coordinated military response across hostile kingdoms suggests shared threat
- Record destruction indicates something shameful happened
- Twenty-two years is enough time for vampires to organize and be eliminated

**Evidence For**:
- The Dwarven record mentions "unusual composition" working together
- Multiple powers cooperating is extremely rare—threat must have been existential
- The Monk's confession mentions "screaming in those pages"

**Evidence Against**:
- No vampire remains from this period have ever been found
- Sel'Naroth discovery in Year 812 was treated as unprecedented
- If vampires emerged, why destroy all records instead of claiming victory?

**The Problem**: If this theory is true, the world's powers found and killed vampire survivors, then erased all evidence. Why hide a victory?

---

### Theory 2: The Artifact Catastrophe

**Proponents**: Valdrian Academy (suppressed research)
**Summary**: Someone used a major vampire artifact with catastrophic results

**The Case**:
- The Crown of Seeing was "discovered" in Year 312—plenty of time to learn its use
- The Forge of Souls was found in Year 358—someone may have activated it
- Fragment 4 shows someone wanted to build something like the Forge

**Evidence For**:
- Crown of Seeing disappeared for 300 years after discovery—possibly in use during this period
- Goblin Memory mentions "humans with fire, elves with magic"—coordinated magical attack
- Scholar's Note says "we did it to ourselves"

**Evidence Against**:
- Artifacts recovered after Year 446 show no signs of recent activation
- No physical evidence of catastrophe comparable to the Collapse
- Why would using an artifact require erasing twenty-two years?

**The Problem**: If an artifact disaster occurred, it was either contained or its effects were hidden. But why hide them?

---

### Theory 3: The Goblin Reversal

**Proponents**: Secret society researchers (multiple)
**Summary**: Goblins nearly reversed their modification, threatening the entire post-Collapse order

**The Case**:
- Goblins were modified by vampires to be servile and controllable
- If they reversed this, they'd remember what they were—an intelligent, magical civilization
- This would delegitimize every kingdom using goblin labor or claiming goblin territory

**Evidence For**:
- Goblin Memory explicitly describes almost remembering, then being "broken worse"
- The Goblin Wars (Year 420-520) bracket the Silent Years perfectly
- Fragment 4 shows even elves cooperating—they'd only do that for existential threat

**Evidence Against**:
- Goblins today show no signs of being closer to reversal than before
- The Goblin Wars are documented (barely)—why erase the middle?
- What would require twenty-two years to prevent?

**The Problem**: This theory explains the cooperation and the violence but not the duration. What took twenty-two years?

---

### Theory 4: The Knowledge Plague

**Proponents**: Church of Divine Light (official position when asked, which is rarely)
**Summary**: Dangerous knowledge spread that had to be contained

**The Case**:
- The Church burned documents that contained "dangerous knowledge"
- The Scholar's Note mentions something "we did to ourselves"
- Multiple powers might cooperate to contain information, not just physical threat

**Evidence For**:
- Record destruction is the defining feature of this period
- The Church gap is larger (28 years)—they had more to hide
- Elves sealed their archive—they're protecting knowledge, not hiding failure

**Evidence Against**:
- What knowledge requires military cordons?
- Why would elves and dwarves follow Church doctrine about dangerous knowledge?
- If it's just information, why twenty-two years instead of one purge?

**The Problem**: Knowledge can spread faster than it can be contained. If they tried to suppress something for twenty-two years, they failed.

---

### Theory 5: The Civil War That Never Was

**Proponents**: Northern Kingdom historians (suppressed)
**Summary**: The powers fought each other over something, then agreed to pretend it never happened

**The Case**:
- Military cordons would make sense for war, not plague
- Twenty-two years is a reasonable war duration
- Powers might agree to erase a costly, shameful conflict

**Evidence For**:
- Merchant's letter describes internal military action, not external threat
- Record destruction could be mutual agreement to reset relations
- Explains why all powers have the gap—they're all complicit

**Evidence Against**:
- No fortifications from this period, no mass graves, no war damage
- Adjacent years show normal trade and diplomacy
- Powers that just fought a war don't immediately return to normal

**The Problem**: Wars leave evidence. This left none.

---

### Theory 6: All of the Above

**Proponents**: This analysis
**Summary**: Multiple crises occurred simultaneously, interacting catastrophically

**The Case**:
- Different fragments point to different events
- Twenty-two years is too long for one crisis but appropriate for cascading disasters
- The coordinated response and erasure makes sense if everyone was guilty of something different

**Possible Sequence**:
1. **Year 423**: Goblins begin reversal attempt; word spreads
2. **Year 424-425**: Powers mobilize; military cordons established
3. **Year 426-430**: During the chaos, vampires emerge from hiding
4. **Year 430-435**: Alliance forms to fight both threats; artifacts deployed
5. **Year 435-440**: Artifact use causes unintended catastrophe
6. **Year 440-443**: Powers fight each other over blame and spoils
7. **Year 443-445**: Agreement reached to erase everything; burnings begin

**Evidence For**:
- Explains all fragments
- Explains twenty-two-year duration
- Explains why everyone is complicit in erasure

**Evidence Against**:
- Complicated; may be too convenient
- Still doesn't explain why The Scar wasn't affected (or was it?)
- Requires assuming all five fragments are accurate

## The Physical Evidence

### What's Missing

**Census Records**: Population counts exist for Year 420 and Year 450. The Year 450 count is 11% lower. Where did those people go?

**Construction Projects**: No buildings dated to this period. None. People were building in Year 420 and Year 446, but not between.

**Trade Goods**: Archaeological sites from this period show decreased variety. Long-distance trade nearly stopped.

**Children**: Birth records from Year 446-450 are sparse. Either few children were born in Year 423-445, or many died young.

### What's Present

**The Mass Graves**: Six sites across the continent containing remains dated to this period. Official explanation: plague. Analysis shows many died violently. The graves are unmarked and were clearly concealed.

**The Sealed Ruins**: Four vampire sites sealed during this period—more than any comparable span. Were they sealing them for safety or to hide evidence?

**The Goblin Walls**: The Broken Lands' barrier wasn't built after the Goblin Wars. Parts of it date to Year 430-435. The goblins were contained during the Silent Years, not after.

## Who Knows What

### The Powers

**Valdrian Empire**: Imperial family has access to sealed records. Emperor Marcus IV (Year 1120-1167) reportedly read them and never discussed what he learned. Current Emperor Valdric VII has shown no interest.

**Northern Dominion**: The North's records were most thoroughly destroyed. They may genuinely not know. Or they may be better at pretending.

**Church of Divine Light**: They know. The confession proves they burned records deliberately. What they know—that's the question.

**Silverwood Elves**: They sealed rather than destroyed. Whatever happened, they want the option to reveal it someday.

**Ironpeak Dwarves**: They recorded only facts, not context. They know what was requested (Forge access) but maybe not why.

### The Secret Societies

**Inheritors of Blood**: They've researched this period extensively. Their conclusion is classified even within the organization. They're afraid of what they found.

**Order of Dawn**: They believe vampires emerged during this period. They've used this belief to justify their entire existence. What if they're wrong?

**Veil Keepers**: They want to expose everything. They've tried to access elven and dwarven archives repeatedly. They've failed. Someone doesn't want this exposed.

## Connections to Year 1200

### The Goblin Stirring

Gritha Bloodsinger is preaching about goblins "remembering what they were." This is the same language from the Year 430s fragment. Is she trying again? Will the response be the same?

### The Church Persecution

High Confessor Brennan is escalating persecution of heresy. The Silent Years began with the Church burning documents to protect the faithful from "dangerous knowledge." Is he afraid of something being discovered?

### The Northern Expansion

King Aldric XII's expansion plans include routes toward the old sealed vampire sites. Is he looking for something from this period?

### The Scar's Growth

The Scar has been growing since Year 1178. What if something that happened in Year 423-445 started that growth—and only now is it becoming visible?
