# The Goblin Wars: True History

The official story is containment. The real story is extermination—and it almost failed.

---

## The Official History

### What Children Learn

The Goblin Wars (Year 420-520) were a necessary response to goblin aggression. Following the Collapse, goblins—freed from vampire control but still bearing their modifications—became violent raiders. They attacked human settlements, stole resources, killed innocents. The civilized races united to contain the threat, pushing goblins into the Broken Lands where they could be monitored and controlled.

The war was long because goblins were numerous and savage. It was costly because they fought without honor. It was necessary because coexistence was impossible.

This is a lie.

---

## The Real History

### What Goblins Were

Before vampire modification, goblins were the **Geth'kari**—an ancient civilization of craftsmen, scholars, and magic-users. They built underground cities. They developed metallurgy techniques that dwarves still can't replicate. They had a written language, mathematical systems, astronomical knowledge.

They were conquered by vampires around Year -900. The vampires didn't just enslave them—they modified them. Blood magic altered their minds, their bodies, their children. Within three generations, the Geth'kari were gone. Goblins remained.

The modification removed their intelligence. No—that's wrong. It removed their *access* to their intelligence. The knowledge was still there, locked away. The potential was still there, suppressed. Goblins were made stupid the way you might make someone mute—not by removing their tongue but by making them forget how to speak.

### What Goblins Remembered

The Collapse freed goblins from direct vampire control. But unlike humans, dwarves, and elves, goblins weren't freed—they were **orphaned**. The Bloodweb that suppressed them was gone. But the modification remained.

For the first generation, nothing changed. Goblins were what vampires made them.

Then the shamans began to dream.

### The Shamans

By Year 400, some goblins were experiencing... memories. Not their own memories—ancestral memories, locked away by the modification but leaking through. They saw cities. They saw libraries. They saw themselves as they had been.

These dreamers became shamans. They gathered followers. They developed rituals—half-remembered, half-improvised—to strengthen the connection to what goblins had been.

By Year 420, they were succeeding.

---

## The Reversal Attempt

### What They Tried

Goblin shamans discovered that the modification could be partially reversed through specific magical rituals. The process was slow—it took years per individual—but it worked. Goblins who underwent the reversal regained:

- Complex language (not pidgin, but grammar, nuance, abstraction)
- Technical skills (metalwork, construction, magic)
- Historical memory (who they were, what was done to them)
- Rage

The reversed goblins weren't savage. They were **educated, angry, and organized**. And they wanted to reverse every goblin in the world.

### The Threat

If goblin reversal succeeded fully, the post-Collapse world would face:

- A civilization of millions with legitimate grievances
- Claims to territories currently held by humans
- Claims to artifacts created by goblins, now in other hands
- Magic users with techniques unknown to other races
- Witnesses to vampire atrocities who remembered everything

Every kingdom used goblin labor—modified goblins who were technically free but functionally enslaved. If those goblins were reversed, they wouldn't keep working. They'd demand payment for 900 years of stolen labor.

More importantly: if goblins could be restored, the post-Collapse order was based on injustice. Everyone benefiting from goblin modification—which was everyone—was complicit.

The powers couldn't allow that.

---

## The Response

### Year 420: The Discovery

A Valdrian border patrol encountered a goblin settlement that wasn't behaving normally. The goblins spoke in complete sentences. They had metal tools they'd made themselves. They asked the patrol to leave their territory.

The patrol reported this as evidence of goblin organization and recommended surveillance.

The report reached the Imperial Court. It reached the Church. It reached the elves and dwarves through intelligence networks.

Within three months, there was a secret summit.

### The Thornwald Accord (Year 421)

Representatives from Valdris, the Northern Kingdoms, the Church, Silverwood, and Ironpeak met in the fortress of Thornwald. They agreed:

1. Goblin reversal was an existential threat to the current order
2. It must be stopped by any means necessary
3. No record of the true reason would be kept
4. The official story would be goblin aggression

This was the only time in post-Collapse history that all major powers agreed on anything. That tells you how frightened they were.

### The Strategy

The war wasn't about containing goblins. It was about three objectives:

**Kill the shamans**: Every shaman was a reversal catalyst. They had to die.

**Destroy the rituals**: All knowledge of the reversal process had to be eliminated.

**Re-modify the survivors**: The remaining goblins had to be modified again, more thoroughly than before, so reversal could never be attempted again.

---

## The War

### Year 421-425: The Quiet Campaign

Before the "war" officially began, assassin teams hunted shamans. Elven rangers. Northern scouts. Church inquisitors. Dwarven sappers. They worked together—unprecedented cooperation.

Estimated shamans killed: 400-600.
Official records: zero.

The shamans who survived went underground—literally, into caves and tunnels where they could hide.

### Year 425-430: Open War

With shamans disrupted, conventional forces moved in. The official story began here: "Goblins attacked human settlements, and we responded."

In reality, human forces attacked goblin settlements first. The "raids" were goblin defensive actions—or revenge for villages already destroyed.

**The Clearings**: Human forces would surround a goblin settlement, kill all adults, and take children for "re-education" (re-modification). Settlements weren't captured—they were erased.

**The Hunts**: Any goblin showing signs of reversal was tracked and killed. Speaking in complex sentences was a death sentence. Making metal tools was a death sentence. Remembering was a death sentence.

**The Burnings**: Goblin libraries—rediscovered caches of Geth'kari records—were burned. Every book, every scroll, every stone tablet that might help goblins remember what they were.

### Year 430-435: The Silent Years

The war intensified during the Silent Years—and the records were destroyed along with everything else from that period.

What we know from fragments:

- Mass graves date to this period
- The Goblin Walls were constructed
- Scar dimensional readings spiked
- The "Second Collapse" may have occurred

Something happened that made the powers decide to erase twenty-two years. Was it the scale of the killing? Did something go wrong with the re-modification? Did goblins fight back in a way that shamed their attackers?

We don't know. That's the point.

### Year 435-520: The Mopping Up

By Year 435, the reversal attempt was broken. The surviving shamans were in hiding, their numbers depleted, their rituals disrupted. The remaining goblins were pushed into what became the Broken Lands.

But the war continued for 85 more years. Why?

**The Re-modification**: It took decades to modify every surviving goblin. The process was slower than the original vampire modification—the powers didn't have vampire expertise. They had to do it the hard way: one goblin at a time, through targeted rituals, imposed by force.

**The Children**: Goblin children born to reversed parents might inherit the reversal. Every generation had to be checked, modified if necessary. This continued until Year 520, when the powers were confident the reversal was extinct.

**The Witnesses**: Humans, elves, and dwarves who had participated in the clearings knew the truth. Some talked. They were silenced—executed, imprisoned, disappeared. By Year 520, everyone who knew was either complicit or dead.

## The Technology

### The Goblin Walls

The barrier around the Broken Lands isn't just physical. It has magical components—wards that specifically target goblin reversal. If a goblin begins to remember, the wards suppress it.

This technology was derived from vampire modification magic. How did the post-Collapse powers learn it? During the Silent Years, they were desperate. They may have entered sealed vampire ruins, found modification records, and adapted them.

The Veil Keepers have documented ward signatures on the Goblin Walls. They match ward signatures in Vel'Thoras. Someone broke into a vampire facility and stole the knowledge.

### The Modification Refinement

The re-modification wasn't a copy of the original. It was **worse**.

Original vampire modification: locked away goblin knowledge and culture.

Re-modification: locked away the memory that anything was locked away.

Modern goblins don't know they were Geth'kari. They don't dream of cities. They don't wonder why they can't speak properly. The re-modification removed the wanting.

Almost.

### The Goblin Physiology

Modern goblins are physically different from the goblins of Year 400. They're smaller, weaker, shorter-lived. The re-modification affected their bodies as well as their minds.

This wasn't necessary for containment. It was punishment.

---

## The Survivors

### The Unreversed

The vast majority of goblins were never reversed. They're still in the Broken Lands, living as their modified selves, not knowing what they lost.

Current population: unknown. Estimates range from 2 million to 5 million. They raid, they survive, they don't remember.

### The Hidden

A small number of reversed goblins survived—estimates suggest 200-500. They went deep underground, beyond the Goblin Walls' ward reach. They've been hiding for 700 years.

Do they still exist? Do they still remember? Are they still trying to reverse others?

### The Revenants

Some reversed goblins allowed themselves to be captured and re-modified—as a disguise. They're inside the Broken Lands, appearing normal, waiting for the right moment.

Waiting for what?

---

## The Legacy

### What Goblins Are Now

Modern goblins are a created people. They were made by vampires, unmade by shamans, and remade by everyone else. They're not what they were born to be—they're what was done to them.

They raid because they were designed to be aggressive.
They don't build because they were designed to forget how.
They're hated because the world needs to hate them to justify what was done.

### What Goblins Could Be

If the reversal succeeded—if even one community of goblins was restored—they would be:

- Intelligent (Geth'kari were scholars)
- Skilled (Geth'kari were craftsmen)
- Magical (Geth'kari were magic-users)
- Righteous (they'd remember 900 years of slavery and 100 years of genocide)

They wouldn't forgive.

### What the World Fears

The Goblin Wars are the world's greatest crime. Everyone is complicit—Valdris, the North, the Church, the elves, the dwarves. If goblins remembered and told their story, every current power would be guilty of genocide.

The persecution of goblins isn't about security. It's about silence.

---

## Gritha Bloodsinger

### Who She Is

Gritha is a goblin shaman. She has the dreams. She has memories that shouldn't exist. She has begun the reversal process on herself.

She's not fully reversed—the process takes years—but she's far enough to know what was done. She knows about the Geth'kari. She knows about the war. She knows about the re-modification.

She's furious.

### What She's Doing

Gritha is traveling the Broken Lands and beyond, contacting goblins, teaching them about the reversal. She's starting what the Year 420 shamans started.

She's also preaching to humans, elves, and dwarves who will listen. She's telling them what their ancestors did. She's collecting allies among those who believe in justice.

She has a following. It's growing.

### The Response

Every power wants her dead.

**Valdris**: She's a threat to the Empire's cheap labor supply.
**The North**: She's moving toward Northern territory.
**The Church**: She's preaching against divine order.
**The Elves**: They participated in the war; they don't want witnesses.
**The Dwarves**: They refused Forge access, but they didn't stop the war. They're guilty too.

Kill teams are hunting her. She keeps moving.

### The Plague

Gritha is dying. She has a wasting disease—possibly from the reversal process, possibly from something else. She has months, maybe a year.

She doesn't care. She's not trying to save herself. She's trying to save her people.

If she succeeds before she dies, the reversal continues without her.
If she fails, the goblins stay as they are.

She's racing against her own death to undo 900 years of injustice.

---

## The Present Day

### The Raids

Goblin raids have increased 40% since Year 1195. The official explanation is that goblins are becoming more aggressive.

The real explanation is that Gritha's teaching is working. Some goblins are beginning to remember. They're not raiding for food—they're raiding for revenge.

### The Weapons

Goblins are using better equipment—iron weapons, quality armor, even enchantments. Where are they getting it?

Two possibilities:
1. Reversed goblins are making it (Geth'kari were smiths)
2. Someone is arming them (who benefits from goblin war?)

### The Walls

The Goblin Walls' wards are weakening. They weren't designed to last forever. The vampire technology is degrading.

If the wards fail, the suppression effect ends. Every goblin in the Broken Lands might start dreaming.

### The Prophecy

Goblin oral tradition includes a prophecy—recorded by an Inheritor agent:

> "When the sleeper wakes, the Geth'kari rise. When the scar opens, the walls fall. When the blood remembers, the stolen return."

The sleeper: Vessel Seven?
The scar: The Scar?
The blood remembers: goblin reversal?

If Seven's awakening triggers Scar activity that damages the Goblin Walls, the entire containment fails. Millions of goblins begin to remember. The war restarts—but this time, goblins know what they're fighting for.

---

## The Question

### What Happens If Goblins Win?

Not just survive. Win. Reverse fully, remember everything, reclaim their heritage.

Do they demand territory? Do they demand reparations? Do they demand tribunals for genocide?

Do they forgive?

Nothing in Geth'kari tradition or goblin memory suggests forgiveness. They remember being transformed into slaves. They remember the clearings. They remember the burnings.

They remember everything.

If goblins win, the post-Collapse world order ends. Not through war—though there would be war—but through justice. Every kingdom would have to answer for what it did.

### What Happens If They Lose Again?

If Gritha is killed and the reversal is stopped, goblins remain as they are. Modified. Contained. Forgotten.

Until the next shaman dreams.
Until the next attempt.

The Geth'kari won't stay buried forever. The modification is eroding—each generation, a little more leaks through. Eventually, the reversal will succeed.

It's not a question of if. It's a question of when—and whether the world is ready.
