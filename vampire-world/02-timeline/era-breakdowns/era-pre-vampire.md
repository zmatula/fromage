# Era: The Pre-Vampire Age (Year -1200 to -1000)

The two centuries before vampire dominion. A time of multiple powers, fragile balances, and the slow rise of the vampires from scattered clans to existential threat.

---

## Era Overview

**Duration**: 200 years
**Character**: Multi-polar world; constant low-level conflict; vampire emergence
**Dominant Power**: None (roughly balanced)
**Key Development**: Vampire population growth and organization
**End State**: Vampires poised for conquest

---

## The World Before Vampire Dominion

### Other Races at Their Peak

**Human Kingdoms (Year -1200)**:
- Multiple kingdoms across temperate regions
- Population: ~5 million
- Technology: Iron Age equivalent
- Magic: Limited hedge wizardry; no systematic study
- Government: Feudal monarchies and city-states
- Military: Standing armies; castle fortifications
- Major Powers: Kingdom of Valdris (east), Merchant Principalities (coast), Holy Confederation (central)

**Elven Domains (Year -1200)**:
- Forest kingdoms and hidden cities
- Population: ~2 million
- Technology: Wood and living materials; beautiful but not advanced
- Magic: Nature magic; some foreseeing
- Government: Ancient councils and hereditary rulers
- Military: Archers; forest guerrillas; small numbers
- Major Powers: Silverwood Throne, Moonspire Kingdom, Deeproot Enclaves

**Dwarven Holds (Year -1200)**:
- Mountain kingdoms and underground complexes
- Population: ~1 million
- Technology: Advanced metallurgy and engineering
- Magic: Rune magic; item enchantment
- Government: Clan councils and elected kings
- Military: Heavy infantry; siege engineers; defensive experts
- Major Powers: Ironpeak Confederation, Deep Throne, Coal March Alliance

**Goblin Tribes (Year -1200)**:
- Swamp and wasteland territories
- Population: ~3 million (high birth rate, high death rate)
- Technology: Scavenged and adapted
- Magic: Shamanic traditions; spirit bargaining
- Government: Tribal chiefs and shamanic councils
- Military: Swarm tactics; guerrilla warfare; traps
- Major Powers: Great Swamp Confederation, Broken Tooth Horde, Night Marsh Alliance

### Inter-Race Relations (Year -1200)

**Humans and Elves**: Uneasy neighbors. Trade but also border conflicts. Cultural snobbery both directions.

**Humans and Dwarves**: Strong trade partnerships. Mutual respect. Occasional territorial disputes over mountains.

**Humans and Goblins**: Hostility. Goblins raid human settlements. Humans hunt goblins.

**Elves and Dwarves**: Historic rivalry. Conflicts over forest vs. mining. Trade in specific goods only.

**Elves and Goblins**: Deep hatred. Goblins despoil forests. Elves exterminate goblin settlements.

**Dwarves and Goblins**: Competition for underground territory. Constant low-level warfare.

### The Vampires at Year -1200

**Status**: Scattered clans; nocturnal predators; not unified

**Population**: ~50,000 (approximately)

**Territory**: Marginal lands; caves; ruins; places others avoided

**Organization**: Individual clans of 100-500; no central authority

**Power**: Individual vampires very strong but vulnerable in daylight and to organized resistance

**Reputation**: Monsters; night terrors; boogeymen; not considered a serious political force

**Relationship with Other Races**: Prey upon all; hated and feared by all; hunted when possible

## The Vampire Rise (Year -1200 to -1000)

### Phase 1: The Hunted (Year -1200 to -1150)

**Situation**: Vampires are scattered predators. Other races actively hunt them.

**Key Developments**:
- Year -1190: Human Holy Confederation launches "Purification Crusade" against vampire clans
- Year -1180: Elven Moonspire Kingdom begins systematic vampire hunts
- Year -1170: Dwarven Deep Throne seals mountain passes against vampire access
- Year -1160: Vampire population drops to ~30,000; extinction seems possible
- Year -1155: First Conclave of Survival; clan leaders meet to discuss existential threat

**Turning Point**: Year -1155 Conclave. For first time, vampires consider unification for survival.

### Phase 2: The First Unification Attempts (Year -1150 to -1100)

**Situation**: Vampires try to organize but can't overcome clan rivalries.

**Key Developments**:
- Year -1148: Warlord Karath attempts to unite clans by force; partial success
- Year -1140: Karath's coalition defeats Human Crusade army; major vampire victory
- Year -1135: Karath assassinated by rival clan; coalition collapses
- Year -1125: Second unification attempt under Elder Vexa; fails due to disagreement on strategy
- Year -1110: Third attempt by alliance of smaller clans; crushed by large clans
- Year -1105: Vampire population at ~40,000; recovering but still vulnerable

**Pattern**: Each attempt fails because no leader can overcome clan selfishness.

### Phase 3: The Hidden Growth (Year -1100 to -1050)

**Situation**: Vampires stop trying to unite politically; focus on growing stronger.

**Key Developments**:
- Year -1095: Clans begin expanding territories more aggressively
- Year -1085: Vampire population reaches ~60,000
- Year -1075: First vampire "cities"—hidden compounds with hundreds of residents
- Year -1065: Blood preservation techniques developed; reduces need for constant hunting
- Year -1055: Population reaches ~80,000; doubling in 50 years
- Year -1050: Other races notice vampire resurgence; concern grows

**Strategic Development**: Instead of unifying, vampires simply grow too numerous to destroy.

### Phase 4: The Tipping Point (Year -1050 to -1000)

**Situation**: Vampire numbers reach critical mass; other races can't contain them.

**Key Developments**:
- Year -1045: Human kingdoms attempt coordinated anti-vampire campaign; undermined by human politics
- Year -1040: Elven forest purges kill many vampires but can't finish them
- Year -1035: Dwarven holds sealed; vampires simply avoid dwarves for now
- Year -1025: Vampire population reaches ~120,000
- Year -1015: First significant vampire raids on major cities; other races realize the threat
- Year -1010: Urgent inter-race conference to discuss vampire problem; can't agree on strategy
- Year -1005: Vykros emerges; begins final unification
- Year -1000: Vykros completes unification; 60 clans; ~150,000 vampires; conquest begins

**The Critical Failure**: Other races could have destroyed the vampires if they'd cooperated. They couldn't. Humans blamed elves for not helping. Elves blamed humans for starting crusade that hardened vampires. Dwarves sealed themselves in and ignored the problem. Goblins saw opportunity in chaos.

## Why Vampires Won

### Their Advantages

**Individual Power**: Single vampire worth dozens of mortals in combat

**Immortality**: Elders accumulate centuries of skill and knowledge

**Night Dominance**: Absolute advantage in darkness

**Regeneration**: Survived wounds that killed others

**Blood Bond**: Could create loyal servants from enemies

**No Need to Hold Territory**: Could raid and retreat; other races had to defend static positions

### Other Races' Disadvantages

**Division**: Could not cooperate against common threat

**Day Thinking**: Planned for daylight warfare; vampires attacked at night

**Static Defense**: Had cities and farms to protect

**Mortality**: Best warriors died; vampires kept theirs for centuries

**Fear**: Vampire reputation caused panic and poor decisions

### The Key Factors

1. **Time**: Vampires could wait for opportunities; other races always urgent
2. **Unity**: When vampires finally unified, other races still couldn't
3. **Will**: Vampires were fighting for survival; others for advantage
4. **Adaptation**: Vampires learned from each failure; others repeated mistakes

## What Was Lost

### When Vampires Conquered

**Human Kingdoms**:
- Libraries burned or seized
- Religious traditions suppressed
- Governing institutions destroyed
- Nobility killed or converted to administrators
- Historical records confiscated
- 1,000+ years of human history lost

**Elven Domains**:
- Forest kingdoms destroyed or hidden
- Ancient artifacts seized
- Living architecture killed
- Oral traditions interrupted
- Elders killed; millennia of memory lost
- Nature bonds severed in occupied territories

**Dwarven Holds**:
- Surface communities conquered
- Deep holds eventually breached
- Rune secrets seized
- Engineering knowledge co-opted
- Clan records taken
- Independence destroyed

**Goblin Tribes**:
- Tribal structures destroyed
- Shaman traditions suppressed
- Historical memory deliberately erased
- Population captured as slaves
- Identity crushed
- Reduced to "breeding stock"

### What This Means for Present Day

The other races in Year 1200 are culturally descended from servant populations, not pre-vampire civilizations. Much of what they "remember" is reconstruction or invention. The true pre-vampire heritage is largely lost.

## Evidence That Remains

### Physical

**Pre-Vampire Human Ruins**: Rare; mostly buried under vampire construction. Some in remote areas never conquered, now legends.

**Elven Hidden Places**: Some forest pockets survived vampire era undiscovered. May contain pre-vampire artifacts.

**Dwarven Deep Archives**: Deepest holds may have preserved records during vampire era. Possibly still sealed.

**Goblin Memory Sites**: Secret locations where shamans preserved traditions. Locations passed down orally.

### Documentary

**Almost None**: Vampires systematically destroyed or seized other races' records. What exists is fragments.

**Vampire Records of Conquest**: Describe pre-vampire civilization from conqueror's perspective. Biased but informative.

**Oral Traditions**: Degraded and mythologized over 1,200+ years but contain kernels.

## Mysteries from This Era

### The Origins Question

Where did vampires come from? Were they:
- Created by failed magical experiment?
- Cursed humans transformed?
- Separate race from before recorded history?
- Something from another dimension?
- Natural evolution?

**No definitive answer survives.**

### The Other Races Question

Were other races always here, or did vampires create/modify them?
- Pre-vampire evidence suggests races existed before vampires became dominant
- But vampires may have modified populations extensively during Rise
- The truth may be mixture: original populations plus vampire modification
- This connects to Core Mystery 4

### The Pre-Pre-Vampire Question

What was the world like before even the early vampires (Year -1200 and earlier)?
- Some ruins suggest older civilizations
- Some artifacts can't be attributed to any known race
- Were vampires the first great power, or just the first recorded one?
- Permanent mystery—intentionally left open
