# Era: The Pre-Vampire Age (Year -1200 to -1000)

The two centuries before vampire dominion. A time of multiple powers, fragile balances, and the slow rise of the vampires from scattered clans to existential threat.

---

## Era Overview

**Duration**: 200 years
**Character**: Multi-polar world; constant low-level conflict; vampire emergence
**Dominant Power**: None (roughly balanced)
**Key Development**: Vampire population growth and organization
**End State**: Vampires poised for conquest

---

## The World Before Vampire Dominion

### The Human Kingdoms

**Kingdom of Valdris (Eastern Plains)**

The largest and most powerful human nation, ruled by House Aurelian for seven generations.

*The Aurelian Dynasty*:
- **Aldric I "the Founder"** (Year -1280 to -1245): United eastern tribes; built Valdris Prime
- **Cassian I** (Year -1245 to -1210): Expanded south; built the Great Temple
- **Aldric II "the Lawgiver"** (Year -1210 to -1175): Created the Aurelian Code; standardized currency
- **Marius I** (Year -1175 to -1140): Lost War of the Silver River to Principalities
- **Cassian II "the Weak"** (Year -1140 to -1115): Civil war; nearly lost throne
- **Aldric III "the Wise"** (Year -1115 to -1080): Restored order; first to negotiate with vampires
- **Marius II "the Last"** (Year -1080 to -1000): Conquered by Vykros; executed publicly

*Major Wars*:
- **War of the Silver River** (Year -1150): Lost river trade rights to Merchant Principalities; Marius I's humiliation led to decades of resentment
- **Coastal Conquest** (Year -1080): Failed attempt to take Principality ports; drained treasury

*Cultural Achievements*:
- The Aurelian Code of Law: First written legal system; fragments survive in Valdrian archives
- Great Temple of Valdris Prime: Largest human structure; vampires converted it to blood collection facility
- The Histories of Aldric: Chronicle destroyed by vampires; quotes survive in other texts

*Internal Conflicts*:
- Civil War of Year -1130: Duke Varen challenged Cassian II; five years of bloodshed
- The Merchant Riots of Year -1100: Taxation protests in major cities
- The Succession Crisis of Year -1085: Three claimants before Marius II secured throne

---

**The Merchant Principalities (Coastal Cities)**

A loose federation of trading cities, each ruled by a Prince-Merchant.

*The Great Princes*:
- **Castellan of Portmere**: Controlled grain trade; wealthiest single individual
- **Doge of Seahaven**: Commanded largest fleet; 200+ ships
- **Lord of the Narrow Strait**: Controlled all east-west sea traffic; collected tolls
- **Prince of Goldwater**: Banking center; financed wars for all sides

*Trade Leagues*:
- The Grain Compact: Controlled food prices across human lands
- The Shipbuilders' Guild: Monopoly on large vessel construction
- The Banking Houses: Portmere-Goldwater axis dominated lending

*Major Conflicts*:
- **The Coin Wars** (Year -1100): Goldwater vs. Portmere over banking rights; nearly destroyed both cities
- **The Pirate Suppression** (Year -1070): United fleet action; established naval dominance
- **Valdris Trade War** (Year -1050): Embargo nearly collapsed Valdrian economy

*How Vampires Conquered*: Not military force—debt manipulation. By Year -1010, major Princes owed vampires heavily. Vampires called in debts, demanded payment in "service." Cities fell without battles.

---

**The Holy Confederation (Central Lands)**

A theocratic alliance of human territories united by worship of the Divine Light.

*Religious Structure*:
- The Council of Seven Flames: Ruling body; one Flame from each major temple
- High Priest: Elected from Flames; served for life
- Temple Hierarchy: Priests, Acolytes, Lay Brothers

*Named High Priests*:
- **Solarius the Founder** (Year -1300 to -1250): Established the faith; wrote the Sacred Texts
- **Mara Lightbringer** (Year -1210 to -1180): Expanded missionary work; built first great cathedrals
- **Vorenus the Purifier** (Year -1150 to -1120): Launched Purification Crusade against vampires
- **Aldric Dawnward** (Year -1090 to -1050): Last independent High Priest; murdered by vampire infiltrators

*Theological Disputes*:
- **The Dual Nature Heresy** (Year -1120): Dispute over whether Divine Light was one entity or two forces (light and mercy). Heretics burned.
- **The Succession Schism** (Year -1080): Two Councils claimed legitimacy after Vorenus's death. Three-year split.
- **The Question of Goblins** (Year -1050): Were goblins capable of salvation? Doctrine said no.

*How Vampires Conquered*: Religious conversion of key priests. Vampires infiltrated temples, converted influential clergy who then preached that vampire rule was "divine will." The faithful followed their priests into subjugation.

---

### The Elven Domains

**Silverwood Throne**

The greatest elven power, ancient beyond reckoning.

*Ruling Structure*:
- **The Starlight Conclave**: Seven elders who ruled collectively
- **Queen/King Consort**: Ceremonial leader chosen by Conclave
- **The Twelve Houses**: Noble families with territorial authority

*Named Rulers and Figures*:
- **Queen Selindra Silvermoon** (Year -1400 to present): Still alive in Year 1200; founder of current Silverwood
- **Lord Calindor Thornweald** (Year -1200 to -990): "The Servant King"; collaborated with vampires
- **Lady Aelindra Starweaver** (Year -1150 to -1080): Led resistance to collaboration; assassinated
- **Elder Thalion Deeproot** (Year -1100 to -950): Argued for elven withdrawal; ignored

*The Thornweald Betrayal* (Year -1050):
House Thornweald had long rivaled House Silvermoon for leadership. Lord Calindor Thornweald secretly contacted vampire clans, offering elven service in exchange for vampire magic that would destroy House Silvermoon. The vampires agreed—then conquered everyone. Calindor became "Servant King" under vampire rule, House Silvermoon was diminished, and elves learned the cost of internal betrayal.

*Why Elves Didn't Resist Harder*: By Year -1020, Calindor's faction controlled the Conclave. Resistance was "treason." Some elves fled to become "Wild Elves" rather than submit. Silverwood's shame over this period explains their current secrecy.

---

**Moonspire Kingdom (Northern Forests)**

More militant than Silverwood; fell harder to vampires.

*Ruling Structure*:
- Warrior-King chosen by combat
- Council of War Leaders
- Strict martial hierarchy

*The Fall of Moonspire*:
- Year -1100: King Vaelin began aggressive anti-vampire campaign
- Year -1050: Vampires responded with overwhelming force
- Year -1020: Moonspire's last fortress breached; King Taelin executed
- Year -1000: Kingdom erased; forests burned; population enslaved

*Legacy*: Moonspire's destruction showed vampires would not tolerate resistance. Silverwood's later collaboration was partly a response to Moonspire's fate.

---

**The Elf-Dwarf Wars**

Three major conflicts that weakened both races before vampire conquest.

**First War: The Roothold Dispute** (Year -1400 to -1350):
- Cause: Dwarven mining collapsed elven sacred groves
- Outcome: Dwarves withdrew from disputed territory; resentment lingered
- Legacy: Mutual distrust established

**Second War: The Crystal Caverns** (Year -1200 to -1180):
- Cause: Both races claimed cavern system with magical crystals
- Outcome: Dwarves won militarily; elves sabotaged mines for centuries after
- Legacy: "Crystal treachery" became dwarven slur for elves

**Third War: The Sundering** (Year -1100 to -1080):
- Cause: Accumulated grievances; pretext was border incident
- Outcome: Stalemate; both sides exhausted
- Legacy: Neither would ally with the other against vampires

*What This Cost*: When vampires rose, elves and dwarves refused to coordinate. Each thought the other would be conquered first and weaken vampires. Both were conquered.

---

### The Dwarven Holds

**Ironpeak Confederation (Western Mountains)**

The greatest dwarven power, famous for metalwork.

*Ruling Structure*:
- High King elected by Clan Council
- Seven Major Clans with voting rights
- Succession Wars common when High King died

*Named High Kings*:
- **Borin Ironhand** (Year -1250 to -1190): United the Confederation; built the Great Forge
- **Thrain Deepdelver** (Year -1190 to -1140): Expanded deep mines; found ancient pre-dwarven ruins
- **Durin Goldbeard** (Year -1140 to -1090): Richest High King; funded arts and learning
- **Krag Stonebreaker** (Year -1090 to -1030): Lost Third Elf-Dwarf War; humiliated
- **Borin II** (Year -1030 to -990): Last independent High King; sealed deep holds against vampires

*The Succession Crisis of Year -1090*:
When Durin Goldbeard died, three Clans claimed the throne: Ironhand, Deepdelver, and Stonefist. Civil war lasted eight years. Krag Stonebreaker (Stonefist) won but was weakened. This is why dwarves couldn't resist vampires effectively.

*Why Dwarves Accepted Vampire Rule*:
Vampires offered to recognize Borin II as "legitimate" High King if dwarves submitted. After decades of civil war and the elf-dwarf conflict, dwarves accepted "peace with honor." It was neither.

---

**Deep Throne and Coal March Alliance**

Other major dwarven powers with their own cultures and conflicts.

*Deep Throne*: Known for rune magic; most secretive; sealed completely during vampire era; what's inside remains unknown.

*Coal March Alliance*: Industrial dwarves; less traditional; collaborated with vampires more readily; supplied materials for vampire construction.

---

### The Goblin Tribes

**The Great Swamp Confederation**

Largest goblin political entity, constantly fragmenting and reforming.

*Tribal Structure*:
- Shaman-King ruled through spiritual authority
- War Chiefs commanded military
- Clan Mothers controlled resources and breeding

*Named Shaman-Kings*:
- **Grist Blood-Eye** (Year -1200 to -1150): United the swamp tribes; first Confederation
- **Vrok Tooth-Keeper** (Year -1100 to -1050): Expanded into human borderlands; sparked conflicts
- **Skit Bone-Caller** (Year -1050 to -1000): Last independent Shaman-King; enslaved by vampires

*Shamanic Traditions*:
- **Blood Speech**: Communication through shared blood; basis for later blood magic
- **Spirit Binding**: Negotiation with swamp spirits for favor
- **Ancestor Memory**: Shamans could access previous shamans' knowledge
- **The Calling**: Ritual to detect distant blood; later corrupted by vampires

---

**The Great Gathering** (Year -1050)

The last free goblin council before vampire conquest.

*What Happened*:
All major tribes sent representatives to discuss the vampire threat. For the first time, goblins considered uniting with other races. They sent envoys to humans and elves.

*The Response*:
Humans executed the goblin envoys as "monsters." Elves refused to meet them. Dwarves ignored them.

*Consequence*:
When vampires came, goblins fought—but alone. They were conquered tribe by tribe. The memory of the rejected alliance fuels goblin rage in Year 1200.

---

### Inter-Race Relations at Year -1200

**Humans and Elves**:
- Trade: Elven craft goods for human grain
- Conflicts: The Border Woods disputes (Year -1180, -1150, -1120)
- Cultural tension: Elves saw humans as "mayflies"; humans saw elves as "arrogant immortals"
- Key incident: The Thornvale Massacre (Year -1130)—human lord burned elven village; never punished

**Humans and Dwarves**:
- Trade: Dwarven weapons for human gold; strongest commercial partnership
- Conflicts: Mountain boundary disputes settled by treaties
- Mutual respect: Dwarves admired human ambition; humans admired dwarven craft
- Key alliance: The Demon's Gate Defense (Year -1100)—joint victory against monster incursion

**Elves and Dwarves**:
- Three major wars (detailed above)
- Grudges: "Crystal treachery," "Roothold destroyers"
- Limited trade: Only through neutral human intermediaries
- Key failure: Refusal to ally against vampires

**All Races and Goblins**:
- Humans: Vermin to be exterminated; goblin hunts were sport
- Elves: Despoilers of nature; killed on sight
- Dwarves: Tunnel competitors; constant warfare underground
- Key moment: Rejection at Great Gathering sealed goblins' fate

### Inter-Race Relations (Year -1200)

**Humans and Elves**: Uneasy neighbors. Trade but also border conflicts. Cultural snobbery both directions.

**Humans and Dwarves**: Strong trade partnerships. Mutual respect. Occasional territorial disputes over mountains.

**Humans and Goblins**: Hostility. Goblins raid human settlements. Humans hunt goblins.

**Elves and Dwarves**: Historic rivalry. Conflicts over forest vs. mining. Trade in specific goods only.

**Elves and Goblins**: Deep hatred. Goblins despoil forests. Elves exterminate goblin settlements.

**Dwarves and Goblins**: Competition for underground territory. Constant low-level warfare.

### The Vampires at Year -1200

**Status**: Scattered clans; nocturnal predators; not unified

**Population**: ~50,000 (approximately)

**Territory**: Marginal lands; caves; ruins; places others avoided

**Organization**: Individual clans of 100-500; no central authority

**Power**: Individual vampires very strong but vulnerable in daylight and to organized resistance

**Reputation**: Monsters; night terrors; boogeymen; not considered a serious political force

**Relationship with Other Races**: Prey upon all; hated and feared by all; hunted when possible

## The Vampire Rise (Year -1200 to -1000)

### Phase 1: The Hunted (Year -1200 to -1150)

**Situation**: Vampires are scattered predators. Other races actively hunt them.

**Key Developments**:
- Year -1190: Human Holy Confederation launches "Purification Crusade" against vampire clans
- Year -1180: Elven Moonspire Kingdom begins systematic vampire hunts
- Year -1170: Dwarven Deep Throne seals mountain passes against vampire access
- Year -1160: Vampire population drops to ~30,000; extinction seems possible
- Year -1155: First Conclave of Survival; clan leaders meet to discuss existential threat

**Turning Point**: Year -1155 Conclave. For first time, vampires consider unification for survival.

### Phase 2: The First Unification Attempts (Year -1150 to -1100)

**Situation**: Vampires try to organize but can't overcome clan rivalries.

**Key Developments**:
- Year -1148: Warlord Karath attempts to unite clans by force; partial success
- Year -1140: Karath's coalition defeats Human Crusade army; major vampire victory
- Year -1135: Karath assassinated by rival clan; coalition collapses
- Year -1125: Second unification attempt under Elder Vexa; fails due to disagreement on strategy
- Year -1110: Third attempt by alliance of smaller clans; crushed by large clans
- Year -1105: Vampire population at ~40,000; recovering but still vulnerable

**Pattern**: Each attempt fails because no leader can overcome clan selfishness.

### Phase 3: The Hidden Growth (Year -1100 to -1050)

**Situation**: Vampires stop trying to unite politically; focus on growing stronger.

**Key Developments**:
- Year -1095: Clans begin expanding territories more aggressively
- Year -1085: Vampire population reaches ~60,000
- Year -1075: First vampire "cities"—hidden compounds with hundreds of residents
- Year -1065: Blood preservation techniques developed; reduces need for constant hunting
- Year -1055: Population reaches ~80,000; doubling in 50 years
- Year -1050: Other races notice vampire resurgence; concern grows

**Strategic Development**: Instead of unifying, vampires simply grow too numerous to destroy.

### Phase 4: The Tipping Point (Year -1050 to -1000)

**Situation**: Vampire numbers reach critical mass; other races can't contain them.

**Key Developments**:
- Year -1045: Human kingdoms attempt coordinated anti-vampire campaign; undermined by human politics
- Year -1040: Elven forest purges kill many vampires but can't finish them
- Year -1035: Dwarven holds sealed; vampires simply avoid dwarves for now
- Year -1025: Vampire population reaches ~120,000
- Year -1015: First significant vampire raids on major cities; other races realize the threat
- Year -1010: Urgent inter-race conference to discuss vampire problem; can't agree on strategy
- Year -1005: Vykros emerges; begins final unification
- Year -1000: Vykros completes unification; 60 clans; ~150,000 vampires; conquest begins

**The Critical Failure**: Other races could have destroyed the vampires if they'd cooperated. They couldn't. Humans blamed elves for not helping. Elves blamed humans for starting crusade that hardened vampires. Dwarves sealed themselves in and ignored the problem. Goblins saw opportunity in chaos.

## Why Vampires Won

### Their Advantages

**Individual Power**: Single vampire worth dozens of mortals in combat

**Immortality**: Elders accumulate centuries of skill and knowledge

**Night Dominance**: Absolute advantage in darkness

**Regeneration**: Survived wounds that killed others

**Blood Bond**: Could create loyal servants from enemies

**No Need to Hold Territory**: Could raid and retreat; other races had to defend static positions

### Other Races' Disadvantages

**Division**: Could not cooperate against common threat

**Day Thinking**: Planned for daylight warfare; vampires attacked at night

**Static Defense**: Had cities and farms to protect

**Mortality**: Best warriors died; vampires kept theirs for centuries

**Fear**: Vampire reputation caused panic and poor decisions

### The Key Factors

1. **Time**: Vampires could wait for opportunities; other races always urgent
2. **Unity**: When vampires finally unified, other races still couldn't
3. **Will**: Vampires were fighting for survival; others for advantage
4. **Adaptation**: Vampires learned from each failure; others repeated mistakes

## What Was Lost

### When Vampires Conquered

**Human Kingdoms**:
- Libraries burned or seized
- Religious traditions suppressed
- Governing institutions destroyed
- Nobility killed or converted to administrators
- Historical records confiscated
- 1,000+ years of human history lost

**Elven Domains**:
- Forest kingdoms destroyed or hidden
- Ancient artifacts seized
- Living architecture killed
- Oral traditions interrupted
- Elders killed; millennia of memory lost
- Nature bonds severed in occupied territories

**Dwarven Holds**:
- Surface communities conquered
- Deep holds eventually breached
- Rune secrets seized
- Engineering knowledge co-opted
- Clan records taken
- Independence destroyed

**Goblin Tribes**:
- Tribal structures destroyed
- Shaman traditions suppressed
- Historical memory deliberately erased
- Population captured as slaves
- Identity crushed
- Reduced to "breeding stock"

### What This Means for Present Day

The other races in Year 1200 are culturally descended from servant populations, not pre-vampire civilizations. Much of what they "remember" is reconstruction or invention. The true pre-vampire heritage is largely lost.

## Evidence That Remains

### Physical

**Pre-Vampire Human Ruins**: Rare; mostly buried under vampire construction. Some in remote areas never conquered, now legends.

**Elven Hidden Places**: Some forest pockets survived vampire era undiscovered. May contain pre-vampire artifacts.

**Dwarven Deep Archives**: Deepest holds may have preserved records during vampire era. Possibly still sealed.

**Goblin Memory Sites**: Secret locations where shamans preserved traditions. Locations passed down orally.

### Documentary

**Almost None**: Vampires systematically destroyed or seized other races' records. What exists is fragments.

**Vampire Records of Conquest**: Describe pre-vampire civilization from conqueror's perspective. Biased but informative.

**Oral Traditions**: Degraded and mythologized over 1,200+ years but contain kernels.

## Mysteries from This Era

### The Origins Question

Where did vampires come from? Were they:
- Created by failed magical experiment?
- Cursed humans transformed?
- Separate race from before recorded history?
- Something from another dimension?
- Natural evolution?

**No definitive answer survives.**

### The Other Races Question

Were other races always here, or did vampires create/modify them?
- Pre-vampire evidence suggests races existed before vampires became dominant
- But vampires may have modified populations extensively during Rise
- The truth may be mixture: original populations plus vampire modification
- This connects to Core Mystery 4

### The Pre-Pre-Vampire Question

What was the world like before even the early vampires (Year -1200 and earlier)?
- Some ruins suggest older civilizations
- Some artifacts can't be attributed to any known race
- Were vampires the first great power, or just the first recorded one?
- Permanent mystery—intentionally left open
