# The Lost Centuries: Year 300-700

Why 400 years of history are barely documented, and what might be hidden in the gaps.

---

## The Problem

Between the Dark Ages (Year 50-300) and the Consolidation (Year 700-1100), there's a period historians call the Warring Kingdoms era. But calling it that hides a truth:

**We don't know what happened.**

Records exist. Events are listed. But the period is suspiciously thin. Four hundred years should fill libraries. Instead, it fills shelves.

---

## The Gaps

### Gap 1: Year 423-445

**Twenty-two years with no records at all.**

Not "few records." No records. Every archive, every kingdom, every library—nothing. It's as if these years didn't happen.

**Theories**:
- Massive destruction event (plague, war, disaster)
- Deliberate erasure (coordinated across kingdoms)
- Records exist but are hidden
- Calendrical error (years miscounted)

**The Problem**: How do five rival kingdoms all lose the same twenty-two years?

---

### Gap 2: The Second Collapse

**Mentioned once in a Year 1100 chronicle:**

> "...and thus ended the Second Collapse, after which the kingdoms rebuilt as they had before..."

**Questions**:
- What was the Second Collapse?
- When did it happen?
- Why isn't it in the main timeline?
- What collapsed?

**Theories**:
- Vampire survivors emerged and were destroyed
- Artifact disaster on massive scale
- Dimensional breach reopened
- The term is metaphorical (political collapse)

No other source mentions a "Second Collapse." This single reference is either a scribal error or evidence of something huge that everyone agreed to forget.

---

### Gap 3: The Goblin Modification

The Goblin Wars (420-520) supposedly ended the "goblin threat" and resulted in their containment. But there's evidence the wars were something else.

**Scattered References**:
- "The modification was reversed" (Year 480 medical text)
- "They remember what they were" (Year 512 military report)
- "The shamans must not be allowed to complete the ritual" (Year 515 Church order)

**The Question**: Were the Goblin Wars really about containing goblins—or preventing them from undoing what vampires did to them?

If goblins were trying to restore themselves and the other races stopped them, the "Goblin Wars" were a second genocide. And no one wants to write that down.

---

### Gap 4: The Secret Societies

The Inheritors, Order of Dawn, and Veil Keepers all claim founding dates after Year 700. But internal documents suggest earlier origins.

**Evidence**:
- Inheritor rituals reference "the Four Centuries of Silence"
- Order of Dawn has pre-700 artifacts in their vaults
- Veil Keepers' oldest text is dated Year 389

**The Theory**: The societies formed during the Lost Centuries to preserve knowledge that was being erased. Their official founding dates are when they went public, not when they began.

What were they protecting? And from whom?

---

## The Suspicions

### Someone Erased History

The gaps are too convenient. Too coordinated. Five kingdoms don't lose the same records by accident.

**Who Could Do This?**

1. **The Church**: Most extensive archive network; strongest motive (control of narrative)
2. **The Kingdoms Together**: Coordinated action on a secret they all share
3. **A Third Party**: Something powerful enough to reach all archives

### Something Important Happened

You don't erase history unless history contains something dangerous.

**Possibilities**:
- Vampire survivors were active and numerous
- Someone achieved Bloodweb or dimensional knowledge
- An artifact was used catastrophically
- The races committed an atrocity they agreed to hide

### The Knowledge Exists Somewhere

Total erasure is nearly impossible. Copies survive. Oral traditions preserve fragments. Someone knows what happened during the Lost Centuries.

**Where?**

- Sealed Church archives (highest probability)
- Dwarven deep vaults (they record everything)
- Elven memory keepers (they don't forget)
- Inheritor hidden libraries (their purpose)
- The Broken Lands (goblins remember)

## Specific Mysteries

### The Year 405 Expedition

**Official Record**: "Vel'Thoras outer complex explored."

**One Line?** This was the first expedition to Vel'Thoras since the collapse. It should be extensively documented.

**Questions**:
- Who led the expedition?
- What did they find?
- Why only "outer complex"?
- Did they reach the stasis chambers?

**The Fear**: They found something—maybe Seven, maybe Eleven, maybe something worse—and covered it up. That would mean someone knows about the Vessels 400 years before Year 812.

---

### The Crown's Journey

The Crown of Seeing was "discovered" in Year 312. Discovered where? By whom? The artifact's history goes blank for the next 300 years until it appears with the Veil Keepers.

**What happened to the Crown for three centuries?**

- Who had it?
- What did they see with it?
- What did seeing cost them?
- Why did they give it up?

---

### The Forge Sealing

The Forge of Souls was discovered by dwarves in Year 358. They immediately sealed it.

**Why?**

- What did they find?
- What did they make before sealing it?
- What are they preventing?

Dwarves don't waste resources. Guarding a sealed vault for 850 years is expensive. Whatever's in the Forge—or whatever the Forge can make—justifies eternal vigilance.

---

## What We Can Reconstruct

### The Pattern

Something happened around Year 400-450 that terrified everyone. Vampires, artifacts, dimensional knowledge—something. The response was coordinated erasure.

After Year 450, the kingdoms stopped fighting each other (briefly) and focused on:
- Containing goblins (420-520)
- Sealing artifacts (358-400)
- Establishing doctrinal control (Church consolidation)
- Preventing... something

### The Possibility

The Lost Centuries aren't lost. They're hidden. The records exist in secured archives, passed down through secret societies, preserved by long-lived races.

When Seven awakens, he may be the key that unlocks them—or the catalyst that ensures they stay buried forever.

## Implications for Present Day

### Year 1200 Connections

- **Northern Expansion**: Aldric XII seeks routes to old ruins. Does he know something about the Lost Centuries?
- **Church Persecution**: High Confessor Brennan is particularly aggressive about vampire knowledge. Is he protecting a secret?
- **Inheritor Urgency**: Why are they so desperate to find Seven first? Do they know what the Lost Centuries really contain?
- **Goblin Unrest**: The Broken Lands are stirring. Are the goblins trying again?

### Seven's Role

Seven has no memories of the Lost Centuries—he was in stasis. But he may trigger revelations:
- His awakening forces factions to respond
- His nature connects to erased events
- His survival proves something people wanted disproved
- His existence answers questions that were supposed to stay unasked

---

## The Reader's Question

**What are you not being told?**

History has holes. This history has canyons. The GRRM approach means those holes aren't accidents—they're plot. Something happened during the Lost Centuries that shaped everything that came after.

Seven will walk into a world built on buried secrets. The Lost Centuries are the deepest burial.
