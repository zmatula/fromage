# Era: The Consolidation (Year 700 to 1100)

Four centuries of empire-building, institutionalization, and the establishment of the world order that exists in the present day.

---

## Era Overview

**Duration**: 400 years
**Character**: Peace-building, empire formation, institutional development
**Dominant Powers**: Multiple stable empires emerge
**Key Development**: Modern political system established; scholarship flourishes
**End State**: Current empire system in place; golden age of learning

---

## The Great Peace

### Why Wars Stopped (Mostly)

**Exhaustion**: After 400 years of warfare, every major power was depleted

**Balance of Power**: No single power could dominate; mutual deterrence

**Artifact Stalemate**: All major powers had significant artifact arsenals

**Economic Interests**: Trade more profitable than conquest

**Demographic Recovery**: Populations needed to rebuild

### The Peace System

**Treaties**:
- Year 702: Congress of Iron Rivers establishes framework
- Year 725: Mutual defense pacts
- Year 760: Trade standardization
- Year 800: Border arbitration system

**Institutions**:
- Diplomatic corps in all kingdoms
- Neutral meeting places
- Merchant guilds spanning borders
- Inter-kingdom marriages

**Remaining Conflicts**:
- Border skirmishes (frequent but limited)
- Proxy wars in marginal territories
- Trade disputes
- Religious tensions (contained)
- Goblin raids (perpetual)

---

## Empire Formation

### The Four Great Empires (by Year 900)

**1. The Valdrian Empire** (East)
- Evolved from Valdris Kingdom
- Dominant land power
- Conservative, traditional
- Artifact-wealthy
- Capital: Valdris Prime

**2. The Northern Dominion** (North)
- Evolved from Northern Kingdom
- Military tradition
- Ruin-rich territories
- Aggressive but contained
- Capital: Ironhold

**3. The Sanctified States** (Central-South)
- Reunified Sanctified Realm successor states
- Theocratic confederation
- Largest population
- Internal tensions
- Capital: Solarium

**4. The Maritime Republic** (Coast/Islands)
- Evolved from Free Cities League
- Naval and commercial power
- Most cosmopolitan
- Artifact trade center
- Capital: Port Sovereign

### Secondary Powers

**Ironpeak Confederation** (Mountains)
- Dwarven industrial power
- Weapons supplier to all
- Mediator and banker
- Fiercely independent

**Silverwood Domain** (Western Forests)
- Elven isolation continues
- Mysterious and feared
- Occasional trade
- Preserves old knowledge

**Broken Lands** (Wastelands)
- Goblin territories
- Marginalized but surviving
- Perpetual low-level conflict
- Underground traditions preserved

## Institutional Development

### Government

**Bureaucracy**:
- Professional administrators
- Written law codes
- Tax systems
- Census records

**Military**:
- Standing armies
- Officer corps
- Standardized training
- Artifact integration

**Diplomacy**:
- Permanent embassies
- Diplomatic protocols
- Neutral territories
- Mediator institutions

### Education

**Universities** (by Year 1000):
- Valdrian Imperial Academy
- College of Port Sovereign
- Northern War College
- Various smaller institutions

**Curriculum**:
- History (biased but systematic)
- Natural philosophy
- Magic (controlled)
- Ancient studies (artifact-focused)

**Literacy**:
- Year 700: ~5% of humans literate
- Year 1100: ~15% of humans literate
- Higher among elves, dwarves
- Schools in major cities

### Religion

**Church of Divine Light**:
- Reunified after schism
- More moderate
- Accommodates artifacts
- Still dominant among humans

**Other Traditions**:
- Mystery schools legitimized
- Dwarven ancestor worship respected
- Elven nature worship tolerated
- Goblin shamanism suppressed but surviving

## Scholarship and Discovery

### The Golden Age of Learning (Year 850-1050)

**Why It Happened**:
- Peace allowed sustained study
- Accumulated artifacts to examine
- Translation breakthroughs
- Patronage from empires
- Previous scholarship foundation

**Major Achievements**:

*Translation*:
- Year 815: First complete vampire text translated
- Year 860: Grammar of vampire language reconstructed
- Year 920: Major historical texts deciphered
- Year 980: Technical manuals partially understood

*History*:
- Year 845: "True History of the Collapse" (first scholarly attempt)
- Year 890: Chronology of vampire civilization established
- Year 940: Cultural studies of vampire society
- Year 1000: Multivolume "Encyclopaedia Vampirica"

*Magic*:
- Year 830: Magical theory systematized
- Year 875: Safe artifact activation procedures
- Year 925: Recovery of several vampire techniques
- Year 975: New magical traditions fully established

*Technology*:
- Year 810: Dwarven engineering revival accelerates
- Year 870: Water-powered machinery
- Year 910: Improved metallurgy
- Year 960: Printing technology improved

### Major Ruin Explorations

**Year 745: Deep Vel'Krath Expedition**
- Former Throne military complex
- Recovered weapons cache
- Established Northern Dominion artifact supremacy

**Year 812: Southern Catacombs**
- Massive underground complex
- Vampire preserved in stasis (destroyed on discovery)
- First confirmed post-collapse vampire sighting
- Caused panic, then covered up

**Year 878: Vel'Thoras Outer Complex**
- Research facility outskirts
- Scientific instruments
- Core facility (protagonist's location) not reached
- Sealed and marked dangerous

**Year 934: The Twilight Border**
- Edge of former Twilight territory
- Sanctuary entrance suspected
- Expedition turned back by defenses
- Confirmed something still functional

**Year 1002: The Deep Library**
- Underground archive
- Thousands of texts
- Many damaged but some readable
- Revolutionized scholarship

## Secret Society Evolution

### The Veil Keepers

**Year 700-1100 Development**:
- Became more sophisticated
- Infiltrated governments
- Monitored all major ruin expeditions
- Destroyed or hid most dangerous artifacts
- Believe vampire return increasingly possible

**Key Actions**:
- Year 812: Destroyed stasis vampire before it woke
- Year 880: Assassinated scholar who discovered "too much"
- Year 934: Turned back Twilight Border expedition
- Year 990: Purged member who wanted to go public

### The Inheritors

**Year 700-1100 Development**:
- Grew wealthy and influential
- Collected artifacts systematically
- Funded scholarship
- Established school network
- Believe vampire knowledge can elevate races

**Key Actions**:
- Year 760: Established first artifact vault
- Year 850: Founded three universities secretly
- Year 940: Recovered "blood codex" with transformation rituals
- Year 1020: Attempted (failed) artifact reactivation project

### The Order of the Dawn

**Year 700-1100 Development**:
- Became military order
- Gained legitimacy as vampire hunters
- Trained elite warriors
- Believe vampire return is prophecied

**Key Actions**:
- Year 780: Formal charter from Sanctified States
- Year 850: Established fortress-monasteries
- Year 920: Exterminated "vampire cult" (may have been Inheritors)
- Year 1000: Peak membership and influence

### Conflicts Between Societies

- Veil Keepers vs. Inheritors: Destroy vs. preserve knowledge
- Order of Dawn vs. both: Religious vs. secular approach
- All three: Mutual infiltration, assassination, theft
- Year 950-1000: "Shadow Wars" kill dozens of operatives

## Cultural Developments

### Arts and Literature

**Year 750-850**: Classical revival; imitation of "ancient" styles
**Year 850-950**: Golden age; original works flourish
**Year 950-1050**: Romantic period; vampire mythology in art
**Year 1050-1100**: Realist turn; contemporary subjects

### Philosophy

**Major Schools**:
- Natural philosophy (proto-science)
- Moral philosophy (ethics without religion)
- Political philosophy (rights of races)
- Metaphysics (nature of magic)

**Key Debates**:
- Were vampires evil or just different?
- Should vampire knowledge be recovered?
- What are natural rights of races?
- Is magic divine, natural, or dangerous?

### Social Change

**Year 700-1100**:
- Urbanization increased
- Merchant class powerful
- Literacy spreading
- Women's roles slowly expanding
- Goblin marginalization institutionalized
- Elven isolation romanticized

## End of Era

### The Present Day Emerges

**By Year 1100**:
- Four-empire system stable
- Balance of power established
- Secret societies at peak
- Scholarship comprehensive
- Magic systematized
- Vampires pure legend

### Setting Stage for Recent Past

**Tensions Building**:
- Valdrian-Northern rivalry
- Maritime economic dominance resented
- Sanctified religious fundamentalism rising
- Goblin desperation
- Elven isolation increasingly strange

**Opportunities**:
- Unexplored ruins remain
- Deep artifacts not recovered
- Protagonist still in stasis
- Twilight sanctuaries unexplored
- Dimensional research possible again

## Vampire Status (Year 1100)

**Estimated Survivors**: ~200-500

**Locations**:
- Deep stasis (including protagonist)
- Twilight sanctuaries (unknown)
- Changed forms
- Other dimensions

**Public Knowledge**:
- Ancient history/mythology
- Demonic origin story dominant
- No one believes survivors exist
- Artifacts are "ancient magic" not vampire technology

**Scholarly Knowledge**:
- Sophisticated understanding
- Can read some vampire texts
- Recognize them as civilization not demons
- Still many gaps and errors

**Secret Society Knowledge**:
- Know survivors possible
- Have seen evidence
- Actively searching or protecting
- Most complete picture but still partial
