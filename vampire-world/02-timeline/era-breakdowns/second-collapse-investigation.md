# The Second Collapse Investigation

One reference. One sentence. A thousand questions.

---

## The Source

### The Chronicle of Brennan Ashworth

**Date Written**: Year 1100
**Author**: Chronicler Brennan Ashworth, Imperial Historian
**Context**: A summary history of the Valdrian Empire's formation

**The Passage**:

> "The consolidation of Valdrian territories proceeded swiftly after Year 700, as regional lords recognized the necessity of unity. The devastation of the previous century—the Goblin Wars, the great famines, and of course the Second Collapse—had convinced all but the most stubborn that division meant death. Thus Emperor Aldric I found willing subjects rather than conquered peoples, and the Empire rose not through conquest but through the shared memory of what disunity had cost."

### The Problem

This is the only reference to a "Second Collapse" in any surviving document.

Ashworth wrote it as common knowledge. "Of course the Second Collapse." As if everyone knew what he meant. But no one does. No other chronicle, history, record, or account mentions it. The term appears nowhere else.

### The Investigation

**Year 1103**: Scholar Vera Thenn noticed the reference, searched for corroboration, found none. She wrote to Ashworth's estate requesting his source materials. The estate replied that all his notes had been "donated to the Church." The Church had no record of receiving them.

**Year 1156**: Inheritor researchers began systematic search for the term. They checked 3,000 documents. No matches.

**Year 1189**: Veil Keeper investigation discovered that Ashworth's original manuscript had been edited. The word "Second" was added in different ink, by a different hand. Someone changed "the Collapse" to "the Second Collapse." Why?

**Year 1195**: Current status: unknown. Either Ashworth knew something and recorded it, or someone altered his text to insert a reference. Both options suggest the Second Collapse was real—and hidden.

## Interpretations

### Interpretation 1: Literal Second Collapse

**Meaning**: Another Collapse-scale catastrophe occurred
**When**: Sometime between Year 0 and 700
**Evidence**: The reference treats it as comparable to the original Collapse

**Problems**:
- No physical evidence of Collapse-scale destruction
- No mass vampire deaths (they were already dead)
- No dimensional breach evidence outside The Scar

**Possibility**: If it happened, it was either smaller than the original or its evidence was deliberately destroyed.

---

### Interpretation 2: The Goblin "Collapse"

**Meaning**: The destruction of goblin civilization's reversal attempt
**When**: Year 420-520 (Goblin Wars)
**Evidence**: The passage mentions Goblin Wars in the same sentence

**The Case**:
- Goblins attempting to restore themselves would be "re-collapsing" the post-vampire order
- Stopping them would require similar coordination to stopping vampires
- The powers might call it a "collapse" of their new world order

**Problems**:
- Seems too small to compare to the original Collapse
- Why would Ashworth call it that and no one else?
- The Goblin Wars are documented (barely)—why hide this term?

---

### Interpretation 3: The Silent Years Event

**Meaning**: Something happened during Year 423-445 that deserved the name
**When**: The Silent Years specifically
**Evidence**: The Silent Years are conspicuously erased from history

**The Case**:
- Twenty-two years of records were destroyed—suggests something worth hiding
- The coordination required matches the original Collapse's cooperative response
- The Chronicle mentions "devastation of the previous century"—fits

**Problems**:
- What could happen in the interior that matches a dimensional breach?
- Why call it a "Collapse" if it wasn't dimensional?
- How did it affect The Scar?

---

### Interpretation 4: The Societal Collapse

**Meaning**: Not a physical event but a breakdown of civilization
**When**: The broader Lost Centuries (Year 300-700)
**Evidence**: This period was chaotic, violent, poorly documented

**The Case**:
- Civilization nearly failed during this period—governance, trade, learning collapsed
- Calling it a "Second Collapse" captures the magnitude
- Ashworth was writing about imperial consolidation—societal context makes sense

**Problems**:
- Gradual decline isn't usually called a "collapse"
- Why would this term be suppressed?
- Other chronicles describe the period's troubles without using this term

---

### Interpretation 5: The Dimensional Echo

**Meaning**: The Scar pulsed or expanded catastrophically
**When**: Unknown—possibly Year 423-445
**Evidence**: The Scar is the only active remnant of the original Collapse

**The Case**:
- The Scar has dimensional properties that could theoretically "collapse" again
- This would explain why it's growing now—not the first time
- An echo event would justify the term "Second Collapse"

**Problems**:
- No expedition records mention major Scar changes during this period
- The Scar's observation began in Year 89—changes would be noted
- Why would this require erasing twenty-two years of records?

---

### Interpretation 6: The Deliberate Addition

**Meaning**: Someone wanted this term to exist in the historical record
**When**: The edit was made sometime between Year 1100 and 1189
**Evidence**: The Veil Keeper discovery of altered ink

**The Case**:
- If the Second Collapse was erased, someone might be trying to preserve evidence of it
- Adding the term to an obscure chronicle would plant a seed for future researchers
- This explains why it's the only reference—it's a deliberate message

**Problems**:
- Who added it and why?
- Why not add more detail?
- Is the message accurate or deception?

## Physical Evidence

### The Scar Readings

Inheritor instruments show three periods of significantly increased dimensional activity at The Scar:
- Year 1-50 (aftermath of original Collapse)
- Year 430-440 (during Silent Years)
- Year 1178-present (current growth)

The Year 430-440 spike is rarely discussed. It's in the data. It correlates with the Silent Years. Something happened.

### The Mass Graves

Six mass graves dated to the Silent Years. Combined dead: estimated 200,000-400,000. If this was a "collapse," it killed hundreds of thousands.

For comparison: the original Collapse killed 40 million former slaves in the aftermath. A few hundred thousand is smaller but significant—more than any recorded war of the period.

### The Sealed Sites

Four vampire ruins were sealed during Year 430-435—more than any comparable period. Were they sealing them against a threat? Or sealing something in?

One site—Vel'Thoras—has dimensional readings similar to The Scar. Was there a connection during the Second Collapse? Did the Collapse affect the ruins?

### The Goblin Walls

The Broken Lands barrier was built during Year 430-435. It's not just a wall—it has magical components, wards similar to vampire security systems. Someone used vampire technology to contain the goblins.

During a "collapse," where did they get this technology? Did they learn from the crisis how to use it?

## The Theories

### Theory 1: Vampire Technology Activation

**Premise**: During the Silent Years, someone activated vampire technology that caused a localized collapse event.

**Evidence**:
- Fragment 4 (Dwarven record) shows someone requested Forge access
- The Goblin Walls used vampire-derived magic
- The sealed ruins may have been active during this period

**Implication**: The Second Collapse was artificial—someone tried to recreate vampire power and failed catastrophically.

---

### Theory 2: Scar Expansion Event

**Premise**: The Scar expanded dramatically during Year 430-440, then contracted or was contained.

**Evidence**:
- Dimensional readings show spike during this period
- The Scar is expanding now—may have happened before
- Mass graves could be from the expansion zone

**Implication**: The Scar breathes. It expanded once, was pushed back, and is expanding again. The Second Collapse was its first breath.

---

### Theory 3: Goblin Magical Catastrophe

**Premise**: Goblins attempting to reverse their modification released magical energy comparable to the Collapse.

**Evidence**:
- Goblin Memory describes shamans "finding the old ways"
- The response was coordinated like the original Collapse response
- Goblin Walls required to contain them afterward

**Implication**: Goblins were—are—more powerful than anyone admits. Their modification locked away something dangerous. If Gritha Bloodsinger succeeds, it could happen again.

---

### Theory 4: Intentional Cover Story

**Premise**: The "Second Collapse" was invented to justify atrocities committed during the Silent Years.

**Evidence**:
- The edited text suggests manipulation
- Calling something a "collapse" removes blame—it's a natural disaster, not a choice
- The powers may have needed cover for what they did to the goblins

**Implication**: There was no Second Collapse. There was a war crime, and someone invented a catastrophe to justify it.

## Who Knows What

### The Church

The Church burned Silent Years records. They know what they burned. Do they know if any of it mentioned a Second Collapse? Did they burn that term specifically?

High Confessor Brennan comes from a family with access to sealed Church archives. His escalation of persecution may be connected—is he afraid of something being discovered?

### The Inheritors

They've investigated intensively. They have the dimensional readings. They know the Scar spiked during the Silent Years.

Vera Nighthollow has led Vel'Thoras operations for decades. Does she know if the facility was affected? Does she know what the Second Collapse meant for the Vessels?

### The Elves

Silverwood sealed rather than destroyed their Silent Years archives. They remember everything. They remember a Second Collapse—or they remember that there wasn't one. Either way, they know the truth.

### The Dwarves

They recorded a request for Forge access and a request to "build another one." Did they record what happened when they refused? Do they know if their refusal prevented—or caused—the Second Collapse?

### Someone

Someone edited Ashworth's text. They added "Second" to "the Collapse." They wanted this term to exist in the record. They're either preserving truth or planting deception.

They may still be alive. They may know everything.

## Implications for Year 1200

### If the Second Collapse Was Real

The world has survived two collapses. Both were hidden. Both were followed by suppression. If a third is coming—The Scar expanding, Seven awakening, factions mobilizing—the powers will try to hide it again. They'll sacrifice whatever they have to.

### If It Was Fabricated

Someone invented a catastrophe to justify something else. They may be doing it again. Current events—the persecution, the expansion, the expeditions—may be creating the conditions for another "collapse" that's actually coordinated action.

### Either Way

History has holes. The holes are deliberate. Understanding Year 423-445 may be the key to understanding what's happening now—and what's about to happen.
