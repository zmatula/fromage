# Era: The Warring Kingdoms (Year 300 to 700)

Four centuries of constant warfare, territorial expansion, artifact-powered conflicts, and the forging of modern nations in blood.

---

## Era Overview

**Duration**: 400 years
**Character**: Constant warfare, nation-building, artifact arms race
**Dominant Power**: Shifting; multiple hegemons rise and fall
**Key Development**: Modern empires begin to form; artifacts change warfare
**End State**: Major powers exhausted; ready for consolidation

---

## What Drove the Wars

### Competition for Vampire Ruins

**By Year 300**:
- Systematic ruin exploration began
- Major ruins = major wealth
- Artifacts = military advantage
- Control of ruins = control of future

**The Ruin Rush (Year 300-400)**:
- All powers scrambling for access
- Expeditions became military operations
- Border wars over ruin-rich territories
- New ruins discovered accelerated conflict

### Artifact Arms Race

**Game-Changing Artifacts**:
- Weapons that never dulled
- Armor that turned blades
- Communication devices
- Healing artifacts
- Siege equipment
- Navigation tools

**Political Effects**:
- Small powers could challenge large with right artifact
- Artifact theft became major crime/espionage focus
- Kingdoms rose and fell on artifact finds
- Created new merchant/collector class with political power

### Religious Conflicts

**Church of Divine Light Schism** (Year 350):
- Orthodox faction: artifacts are demonic, destroy them
- Pragmatic faction: artifacts are tools, use them
- Created civil wars within Sanctified Realm
- Spread to other kingdoms

**Holy Wars**:
- Crusades against "heretical" kingdoms
- Wars of religion masked territorial grabs
- Religious justification for conquest
- Persecution of non-human races in some regions

### Racial Tensions

**Human Expansionism**:
- Humans most numerous
- Sought to dominate continent
- Pushed other races to margins
- Justified by religious/cultural supremacy

**Resistance**:
- Elven border wars
- Dwarven defensive actions
- Goblin guerrilla campaigns
- Alliances of convenience

## Major Wars

### The Valdris Expansion Wars (Year 315-380)

**Combatants**: Valdris Kingdom vs. eastern neighbors

**Cause**: Valdris sought to unite eastern territories

**Key Events**:
- Year 315: Valdris invades three smaller kingdoms
- Year 328: Formation of Eastern Alliance against Valdris
- Year 342: Valdris artifact breakthrough (siege weapons)
- Year 355: Eastern Alliance collapses
- Year 368: Valdris reaches maximum extent
- Year 380: Overextension leads to rebellions

**Result**: Valdris dominant in east but weakened; created pattern of empire-building

### The Sanctified Crusades (Year 350-450)

**Combatants**: Sanctified Realm vs. "heretical" kingdoms

**Cause**: Religious schism; territorial ambition disguised as faith

**Key Events**:
- Year 350: Great Schism in Church
- Year 362: First Crusade against heretical duchy
- Year 378: Counter-Crusade; religious civil war
- Year 395: Crusade against elven "demon-worshippers"
- Year 412: Crusade against artifact-using kingdoms
- Year 440: Exhaustion; Peace of the Silver Accord

**Result**: Sanctified Realm fragmented; religious authority diminished; secular powers rose

### The Northern Unification (Year 400-480)

**Combatants**: Northern Marches duchies; external powers

**Cause**: Ruin wealth in northern territories; dreams of Throne legacy

**Key Events**:
- Year 400: Duke Aldric IV declares unification intent
- Year 415: War of Seven Duchies
- Year 432: First Northern Kingdom proclaimed
- Year 448: Valdris intervention
- Year 465: Northern-Valdris War
- Year 480: Treaty of Iron; Northern Kingdom independent

**Result**: Northern Kingdom formed; rich in ruins; military culture; vampire Throne territory successor

### The Goblin Wars (Year 420-520)

**Combatants**: Goblin Confederation vs. human kingdoms

**Cause**: Goblin expansion from marginal territories; human expansion into goblin lands

**Key Events**:
- Year 420: Goblin Confederation unified under Great Chief Razak
- Year 435: Goblin invasion of southern human territories
- Year 450: Human counter-offensive
- Year 475: Goblin shamanic magic surprises humans
- Year 490: Stalemate and atrocities on both sides
- Year 520: Treaty of the Broken Hills; goblins contained but not destroyed

**Result**: Goblins permanently marginalized; deep human-goblin hatred; shamanic tradition preserved in war

### The Merchant Wars (Year 480-560)

**Combatants**: Free Cities League vs. rival ports; pirates

**Cause**: Control of sea trade; artifact smuggling

**Key Events**:
- Year 480: Free Cities at economic peak
- Year 495: Trade war with Valdris
- Year 510: Pirate kingdoms emerge
- Year 525: Free Cities navy expansion
- Year 540: War with Southern Ports
- Year 560: Free Cities dominant at sea

**Result**: Free Cities control maritime trade; wealthiest power; artifact market centers

### The Twilight War (Year 550-590)

**Combatants**: Human kingdoms vs. Silverwood Domain

**Cause**: Human logging in elven-claimed forests; territorial expansion

**Key Events**:
- Year 550: Logging operations in Silverwood borders
- Year 558: Elven warning ignored
- Year 565: Elven attacks on logging camps
- Year 572: Human punitive expedition destroyed
- Year 580: Coalition against Silverwood
- Year 585: Elven guerrilla warfare devastates coalition
- Year 590: Treaty of Shadows; elven borders recognized

**Result**: Elven independence confirmed; human coalition humiliated; elves feared and respected

### The Great Northern War (Year 620-680)

**Combatants**: Northern Kingdom vs. Valdris; allies on both sides

**Cause**: Control of richest ruin territories; regional hegemony

**Key Events**:
- Year 620: Dispute over border ruins
- Year 628: Northern invasion of Valdris territory
- Year 635: Valdris counter-offensive
- Year 648: Battle of Crimson Fields (200,000 casualties)
- Year 655: Both sides exhausted
- Year 668: Dwarven mediation
- Year 680: Peace of the Iron Mountains

**Result**: Both powers devastated; neither hegemonic; set stage for consolidation era

## Artifact Discoveries

### Major Finds

**Year 312: The Crown of Seeing**
- Found in eastern ruin
- Allowed scrying over distances
- Gave Valdris intelligence advantage
- Sparked artifact arms race

**Year 358: The Forge of Souls**
- Dwarven expedition in deep mountains
- Could create limited vampire-style weapons
- Made Ironpeak indispensable ally
- Still functional in Year 1200

**Year 405: The Library of Vel'Thoras (partial)**
- Research complex discovered
- Most sealed or destroyed
- Some texts recovered
- Protagonist's stasis chamber not found

**Year 478: The Healer's Stone**
- Found by Free Cities merchant
- Could cure most diseases
- Made Free Cities center for medicine
- Created healing cult

**Year 534: The Gate Fragment**
- Piece of vampire portal system
- Couldn't make it work
- Suggested transport was possible
- Holy grail of artifact seekers

**Year 612: The Blood Archives**
- Vampire records in readable condition
- Mostly administrative
- First real understanding of vampire society
- Changed scholarly view of collapse

**Year 667: The Mind Codex**
- Magical training manual
- Partially decipherable
- Revolutionized magical education
- Created new magical traditions

## Power Evolution

### Human Kingdoms

**Valdris**: Rose to dominance, overextended, declined to major power
**Sanctified Realm**: Fragmented by schism, multiple successor states
**Free Cities**: Grew wealthy, avoided major land wars, dominant at sea
**Northern Kingdom**: Formed, grew strong, bloodied but independent

### Non-Human Powers

**Dwarves**: Remained isolationist, grew wealthy as weapons suppliers, emerged as mediators
**Elves**: Maintained independence, feared after Twilight War, increasingly mysterious
**Goblins**: Contained, marginalized, but not destroyed; simmering resentment

### New Players

**Successor States**: Sanctified Realm fragments became independent
**Pirate Kingdoms**: Rose and fell in southern seas
**Mercenary Companies**: Became political players
**Artifact Guilds**: Gained formal recognition

---

## Cultural Developments

### Scholarship

- Year 325: Second university founded (Valdris)
- Year 380: Historical society becomes royal institution
- Year 440: First attempt at vampire script translation
- Year 500: Scientific method emerging
- Year 580: Printing technology (limited)
- Year 650: Comprehensive histories written

### Religion

- Great Schism changed everything
- Pragmatic faction eventually dominated
- Artifacts accepted as tools, not demons
- Religious wars discredited crusading
- New syncretic faiths in border regions

### Magic

- Artifact study improved techniques
- New schools founded
- Blood magic remained forbidden but studied
- Healing magic most socially accepted
- Combat magic drove warfare changes

---

## End of Era (Year 700)

### The Exhaustion

**Military**:
- Generations of warfare
- Populations depleted
- Treasuries empty
- Veteran armies destroyed

**Political**:
- Old dynasties extinct or weakened
- New powers emerging
- Alliance systems unstable
- Need for consolidation obvious

**Cultural**:
- War-weariness
- Desire for stability
- Scholarly progress despite wars
- New generation wants peace

### What Would Change

- Era of great wars ending
- Era of consolidation beginning
- Major powers would stabilize
- Modern empire system would emerge
- Artifacts would become established power
- Vampires would be pure legend

---

## Vampire Status (Year 700)

**Estimated Survivors**: ~500-1,000

**Locations**:
- Deep stasis
- Isolated hiding
- Twilight sanctuaries (unknown status)
- Changed forms in deep ruins

**Public Knowledge**:
- Pure mythology
- No living memory even among elves
- "Vampire" = monster/curse
- Historical vampires seen as demons or ancient race

**Scholarly Knowledge**:
- Some understanding of civilization
- Translation work ongoing
- Artifacts studied intensively
- Shadow truths beginning to emerge

**Secret Society Knowledge**:
- Veil Keepers believe survivors exist
- Inheritors seek their knowledge
- Order of Dawn prepares for return
- All have partial truths
