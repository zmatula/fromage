# The Year 812 Discovery: Conflicting Accounts

What was really found at Sel'Naroth, and why the official story doesn't add up.

---

## The Official Account

**Date**: Year 812, Month 6
**Location**: Sel'Naroth ruins, Northern Kingdom territory
**Discovery**: First vampire in stasis since the collapse
**Outcome**: Subject destroyed by expedition team

This is what the records say. It's not what happened.

---

## The Contradictions

### Problem 1: The Timing

**Official Timeline**:
- Year -50 to 0: Sanguine Vessels created
- Year 0: Collapse
- Year 812: "First" stasis vampire discovered

**The Issue**: If the Vessels were the only vampires in stasis, and they were all at Vel'Thoras, why was one found at Sel'Naroth—a Throne military installation?

**Possible Explanations**:
1. Vessels were moved before the collapse (no evidence)
2. Other stasis projects existed (no records)
3. The "discovery" was something else entirely

---

### Problem 2: The Destruction

**Official Story**: The expedition team destroyed the vampire "for safety."

**The Questions**:
- Why destroy the first vampire found in 812 years?
- Who ordered the destruction?
- Where are the remains?
- Why was no study conducted?

**Northern Kingdom Records**: "Subject neutralized per Protocol Seven."

**What is Protocol Seven?**: No other reference to this protocol exists in Northern Kingdom military records before or after Year 812.

---

### Problem 3: The Expedition Team

**Official List**:
- Commander Aldric Thorne (Northern Kingdom military)
- Scholar Mira Vess (Valdrian Academy)
- Brother Kell (Church of Divine Light)
- Four unnamed soldiers

**The Problem**: Scholar Mira Vess doesn't exist in Valdrian Academy records. Brother Kell was reassigned to a remote monastery immediately after the expedition and died there within a year. Commander Thorne was promoted to general and never spoke of the discovery.

**What Happened to Them?**

---

### Problem 4: The Site

**Sel'Naroth Background**:
- Crimson Throne military installation
- Known for weapons development
- Not known for any stasis or research facilities

**After Year 812**:
- Completely sealed
- Declared "structurally unstable"
- Access forbidden even to scholars
- Northern Kingdom military patrols the perimeter

**Why seal a ruin after finding one vampire?**

---

## Alternative Theories

### Theory 1: The Wrong Vessel

**Proponent**: Scholar Kess Orin (Year 1180, executed for heresy)

The vampire found wasn't a Vessel. It was something older—a vampire from before the Sanguine Initiative. The expedition found evidence of earlier stasis experiments, predating Mind's official project.

**Implications**: The Vessels weren't original. Someone else did it first.

**Why Suppress**: If the Vessels were copies, not innovations, it undermines Velanna's reputation and Mind's legacy.

---

### Theory 2: The Wrong Number

**Proponent**: Anonymous Inheritor document (Year 1150)

The expedition found multiple vampires, not one. They destroyed most and kept one—or more. The Northern Kingdom has living vampires in secret custody, being studied for military advantage.

**Implications**: Someone controls vampire knowledge and isn't sharing.

**Why Suppress**: Obvious strategic advantage. Also, violation of Church doctrine.

---

### Theory 3: The Right Vessel

**Proponent**: Veil Keeper internal memo (Year 1190)

The vampire was a Vessel—specifically, Vessel Eleven. It escaped. The expedition didn't destroy it; they lost it. The sealing of Sel'Naroth isn't to keep people out. It's because something got out.

**Implications**: Vessel Eleven has been awake for 400 years.

**Why Suppress**: If Eleven is awake and active, they've had centuries to plan. That's terrifying.

---

### Theory 4: Not a Vampire

**Proponent**: Scholar Brennan Cole (Year 1200, current)

What they found wasn't a vampire at all. It was something that came through the dimensional breach during the collapse—something that looked like a vampire but wasn't. The destruction was necessary because it was dangerous in ways vampires never were.

**Implications**: Something else survived the collapse.

**Why Suppress**: No one wants to know there are things worse than vampires.

---

## The Physical Evidence

### What Exists

**Sel'Naroth Perimeter**: Northern Kingdom guards have maintained continuous patrol for 388 years. Cost: enormous. Why?

**Expedition Equipment**: Scholar Vess's journals were impounded by the Church and never returned. Brother Kell's personal effects were "lost in transit." Commander Thorne's expedition reports are classified.

**The Body**: No remains exist. No samples. No artifacts. Complete destruction—or complete removal.

### What's Missing

**Stasis Chamber**: Where is it? Was it destroyed? Moved?

**Research Notes**: Every vampire stasis project left extensive documentation. Sel'Naroth has none.

**Other Expeditions**: Sel'Naroth was explored before Year 812. Why did earlier expeditions miss this?

---

## The Interested Parties

### Northern Kingdom

**Official Position**: "A minor discovery, properly handled."
**Actual Behavior**: 388 years of military guard, classified records, sealed site.
**What They Know**: Everything they're not telling.

### Church of Divine Light

**Official Position**: "The vampire was destroyed as divine law requires."
**Actual Behavior**: Impounded Scholar Vess's journals, reassigned Brother Kell, executed Scholar Orin for asking questions.
**What They Know**: Enough to kill people over.

### Inheritors of Blood

**Official Position**: "A tragic loss of historical knowledge."
**Actual Behavior**: Multiple attempts to access Sel'Naroth, including three failed infiltrations.
**What They Know**: Not enough—or they wouldn't keep trying.

### Valdrian Academy

**Official Position**: "We have no scholar named Mira Vess on record."
**Actual Behavior**: Quietly removed Vess from all archives in Year 813.
**What They Know**: Who Mira Vess really was.

## Connections to Present Day

### The Seven Awakening

When Vessel Seven awakens in Year 1200, he will be the "second" stasis vampire found. But if the theories are correct:
- He may be the third or fourth
- Someone already knows about Vessels
- Someone has been preparing for him

### The Northern Expansion

King Aldric XII's expansion into contested territory includes routes that approach Sel'Naroth. Coincidence? Or is he looking for something?

### The Living Artifact

One of the five great artifacts is the "Living Artifact"—a thinking, active relic that may be a person. If Vessel Eleven escaped in Year 812, could they be the Living Artifact?

## The Truth (Unknown)

**What Really Happened at Sel'Naroth?**

The expedition found something. It wasn't what the records say. Multiple powerful factions agreed to cover it up—Northern Kingdom, Church, and Academy together. That's remarkable. They don't agree on anything.

Whatever they found was important enough to unite enemies in silence for 388 years.

When Seven awakens, he may find out what it was. Or he may find out that some secrets are kept because knowing them is dangerous.
