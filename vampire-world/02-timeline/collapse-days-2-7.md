# The Collapse: Days 2-7

The first day killed millions. The next six killed hope.

---

## Day 2: The Realization

### Hour 1-6 (Dawn)

**At Neth'Korath**:
Nothing. The capital was gone. Two million vampires, the Apex Spire, the Archive Eternal, the Council chambers—all ash and dimensional breach. The Sundering Wave continued outward at 100 miles per day.

**At Vel'Krath (Throne Capital, 300 miles north)**:
- 04:00 - First reports of "incident" at Neth'Korath
- 05:30 - Reports become "catastrophe"
- 06:00 - Warlord-Regent Cassius summons War Council

**Cassius's First Orders**:
"Find out what happened. Prepare for Mind treachery. This could be a weapon."

He thought Mind had attacked them. He couldn't conceive that Mind had destroyed themselves.

**At Sel'Naroth (Twilight Capital, 400 miles west)**:
- 05:00 - Seers report "dimensional disturbance"
- 05:30 - High Seer Selene convenes emergency Conclave
- 06:00 - Decision: Begin sanctuary protocols

**Selene's First Orders**:
"Seal the Fourth Sanctuary. Begin evacuation of designated personnel. This is not a drill."

She knew. Twilight had prepared for exactly this.

### Hour 6-12 (Morning)

**At Vel'Krath**:
- 08:00 - Scout reports arrive: Neth'Korath "doesn't exist anymore"
- 09:00 - Cassius orders mobilization
- 10:00 - General Kira (no relation to POV character) argues for rescue mission
- 11:00 - Cassius overrules: "Rescue what? There's nothing there."

**General Kira**: "There may be survivors. The outer districts—"

**Cassius**: "Are between us and a dimensional breach that ate two million vampires. We're not sending our forces into that."

**General Kira**: "Then what do we do?"

**Cassius**: "We survive. Mind is gone. Twilight is hiding. We're what's left."

**At Sel'Naroth**:
- 08:00 - First evacuation groups depart for sanctuaries
- 09:00 - Remaining Twilight citizens learn of the collapse
- 10:00 - Panic. Thousands rush sanctuary entrances.
- 11:00 - Selene orders: "Only designated personnel. Turn back the rest."

**Councillor Lyris** (who had warned the Council years ago): "High Seer, they're begging—"

**Selene**: "If we let them in, we'll have 50,000 people in spaces for 25,000. No one survives that. We follow the protocols."

**Lyris**: "The protocols are monstrous."

**Selene**: "The alternative is extinction. Choose."

Lyris chose. She turned back families she'd known for centuries.

### Hour 12-18 (Afternoon)

**The Sundering Wave Expands**:
By noon, it had traveled 150 miles from Neth'Korath. Cities at the edge began experiencing Bloodweb disruptions.

**At Vel'Thoras (Eastern Research Hub, 400 miles)**:
- 12:00 - Director Nalas orders facility lockdown
- 13:00 - Emergency stasis protocols initiated
- 14:00 - Sanguine Vessels secured
- 15:00 - Staff debate: stay or flee?

**Director Nalas**: "The Wave is coming. We don't know its range. This facility has the best shielding outside the sanctuaries."

**Researcher Keth**: "If we stay and the shielding fails—"

**Nalas**: "Then we die here. If we leave and can't outrun the Wave, we die out there. I prefer to die near my work."

Nalas died three days later when the Wave reached Vel'Thoras. The shielding held in certain sections. That's why Seven survived.

### Hour 18-24 (Night)

**At Vel'Krath**:
- 19:00 - Cassius learns of Bloodweb disruptions spreading
- 20:00 - First Throne troops experience weakness
- 21:00 - Cassius orders all forces to blood-forged independence protocols
- 23:00 - Only 40% of forces can operate independently

**General Kael** (former Council attendee): "We have 200,000 soldiers. If the Bloodweb fails completely, we'll have 80,000."

**Cassius**: "Then we use what we have."

**Kael**: "For what?"

**Cassius**: "I don't know yet. But I won't command corpses."

**At the Sanctuaries**:
Through the night, designated Twilight members entered the refuges. The doors began to close. Those outside could hear them sealing.

## Day 3: The Decisions

### The Throne Military Council

**Location**: Vel'Krath War Room
**Attending**: Cassius, General Kael, General Kira, General Vorn, Admiral Thess

**The Situation**:
- Neth'Korath destroyed
- Sundering Wave expanding at 100 miles/day
- Bloodweb failing progressively
- No communication with Mind remnants
- No communication with Twilight
- Servant races beginning to stir

**General Vorn**: "We should march on what's left of Mind. Seize the Bloodweb control nodes. Maybe we can stop this."

**General Kael**: "Seize them how? The nodes are in the Wave path. Anyone who goes there dies."

**Admiral Thess**: "We could evacuate by sea. My fleet can take 50,000 to the outer islands."

**General Kira**: "And then what? We wait until the Wave reaches the coast?"

**Cassius**: "We don't know the Wave's range. It might stop. It might not."

**Kael**: "We need information. We need to know what we're facing."

**Cassius**: "Fine. Kira, send scouts into the Wave's path. Expendable ones. I want to know what happens to them."

**Kira**: "You want me to send soldiers to die."

**Cassius**: "I want to know how they die. That knowledge might save the rest."

### The Scout Mission

General Kira sent twelve vampires toward the Wave. They were volunteers. They knew they wouldn't return.

**Commander Veth** led them. Last transmission:

"Day 3, Hour 16. We're fifty miles from the edge. Bloodweb connection weakening but holding. Will proceed to—"

Transmission ended. They never reached the edge. The Wave was moving faster than estimated.

### Selene's Calculation

At Sel'Naroth, the evacuation was nearly complete. High Seer Selene faced a final decision.

**The Problem**:
- 23,000 designated evacuees had reached sanctuaries
- 2,000 more were still en route
- The Wave would reach Sel'Naroth in approximately four days
- Should sanctuaries seal now or wait?

**Seer Orath**: "The patterns say seal. Every hour increases risk."

**Councillor Lyris**: "There are still families on the road. Children."

**Selene**: "How many sanctuaries are at capacity?"

**Warden Thessaly**: "Two of four. The third is at 90%. The fourth could take another 3,000."

**Selene**: "Then we wait one more day. After that—"

**Orath**: "After that we seal regardless?"

**Selene**: "Yes. I'm sorry, Lyris. I'm not killing 23,000 to save 2,000."

They waited. Some of the 2,000 made it. Most didn't.

## Day 4: The Breakdown

### The First Vampire-on-Vampire Violence

**Location**: Provincial city of Korrath (Throne territory)

**What Happened**:
Blood supplies were failing. The Bloodweb disruption meant normal blood no longer sustained vampires—they needed ten times the usual amount. There wasn't enough.

**Commander Drex** controlled the city garrison. He had 500 soldiers and 2,000 civilians. He had blood for maybe 200.

**His Decision**: "Soldiers first. We'll need them for what's coming."

He seized all blood stores. Civilians who resisted were killed. Civilians who didn't... starved.

By the end of Day 4, Commander Drex's soldiers had killed 300 civilians. The rest fled or hid.

This pattern repeated across Throne territories. Military discipline held—the strong took from the weak.

### The First Servant Uprisings

**Location**: Mining complex at Irondeep (Throne territory)

**What Happened**:
The dwarven slaves had heard rumors. Vampires were dying. The masters were weak.

**Foreman Borin** (descended from the dwarven saboteurs of Year -20) gathered the slaves.

**Borin**: "Grandfather told me stories. He said the day would come when the blood-drinkers fell. That day is now."

They killed the vampire overseers. They took the weapons. They sealed the mine.

By Day 7, Irondeep was free. It would become the nucleus of what later became the Ironpeak Confederation.

### The Mind Remnants

**Location**: Research station Vel'Kiran (150 miles from Neth'Korath)

**Survivors**: Approximately 2,000 Mind vampires who had been outside the capital when the Wave hit.

**Leader**: Senior Researcher Naleth (not Archon—the Archon was dead)

**The Debate**:
Should they try to reach the Bloodweb control nodes and stop the collapse?

**Naleth**: "The experiment triggered a cascade failure. If we can access the central nodes—"

**Researcher Korth**: "The central nodes are in the capital. The capital is gone."

**Naleth**: "There are secondary nodes. If we can synchronize them—"

**Researcher Selene** (not High Seer, different Selene): "You don't know that will work. You're guessing."

**Naleth**: "Yes. I'm guessing. What's your alternative?"

**Selene**: "We run. West, to Twilight. Maybe they'll take us in."

**Naleth**: "Twilight sealed their sanctuaries. They won't open them for us."

**Korth**: "Then we die."

**Naleth**: "Maybe. But I'd rather die trying to fix this than die running from it."

Naleth led 500 vampires toward the secondary nodes. None returned. The rest fled in various directions. A few may have survived.

## Day 5: The Scattering

### Throne Evacuation Begins

**Cassius's Order**: "We cannot hold Vel'Krath. The Wave will reach us in five days. Evacuate to the northern mountains."

**General Kael**: "The mountains won't stop dimensional energy."

**Cassius**: "No. But they have deep caves. Defensible positions. If we can't survive, we can at least die fighting."

**Kael**: "Fighting what?"

**Cassius**: "Whatever comes after us. The servants. Other vampires. I don't know. But we'll fight it."

### The Evacuation

60,000 Throne military began moving north. Behind them came perhaps 100,000 civilians—whoever could keep up.

They left:
- The sick
- The old
- The ones who refused to leave
- The ones who believed it would stop

It didn't stop.

### The Fourth Sanctuary Seals

**Location**: Fourth Sanctuary entrance, Twilight territory

**Time**: Day 5, Hour 14

**Inside**: 9,800 vampires
**Outside**: Approximately 200 trying to enter

The 200 were latecomers. Families who had traveled too slowly. Individuals who had hesitated. They reached the entrance as the doors began to close.

**Warden Thessaly**: "I'm sorry. We're at capacity."

**Civilian Varek**: "My children are inside! You took my children but not me!"

**Thessaly**: "Your children were on the list. You weren't."

**Varek**: "Then let me die with them!"

**Thessaly**: "If I open these doors, everyone inside dies. Including your children."

The doors sealed. Varek and the others remained outside. They were dead within a week.

### The Beginning of the Scar

By Day 5, the area around Neth'Korath had begun to... stabilize. Not heal. Stabilize.

The dimensional breaches weren't closing. They were becoming permanent. The land itself was changing—fused, glassy, wrong.

What would become the Scar was taking shape.

## Day 6: The Desperation

### Throne Column: Day One of March

**Numbers**: 60,000 military, ~100,000 civilians
**Speed**: 30 miles per day
**Distance to mountains**: 200 miles
**Days to arrival**: 6-7
**Days until Wave reaches Vel'Krath**: 4

**General Kira**: "Cassius, the math doesn't work. Even if we make the mountains, the Wave will catch us."

**Cassius**: "The Wave has to cross open ground. The mountains might slow it. Or stop it."

**Kira**: "That's hope, not strategy."

**Cassius**: "It's all I have."

### The Blood Crisis

By Day 6, blood starvation was killing more vampires than the Wave.

**The Problem**: Bloodweb-enhanced blood potency was gone. Vampires needed vastly more blood than they could acquire. The servant races were fleeing, hiding, or fighting back.

**Cassius's Solution**: "Blood rationing. Military personnel get full rations. Civilians get half."

**General Vorn**: "Half isn't enough. They'll die."

**Cassius**: "They'll die slower. That's the best I can do."

Some commanders distributed their rations to civilians. They were dead by Day 7.

### The Mind Remnants: Final Attempt

Naleth's group reached the secondary Bloodweb node at Vel'Kiran. It was still functioning—barely.

**Naleth's Plan**: Activate the node's emergency protocols. Create a localized barrier against the Wave.

**What Happened**: The node activated. The barrier formed. It lasted six hours.

Then the Wave reached Vel'Kiran, overwhelmed the barrier, and killed everyone inside.

But those six hours were recorded. The node's final transmissions would later be recovered by scholars. They're one of the few direct accounts of what the Wave actually was.

**Naleth's last transmission**: "The barrier is holding but losing coherence. The Wave isn't energy—it's absence. It's taking everything we are and pulling it somewhere else. We're being unmade. If anyone receives this, don't try to stop it. Run. Get as far as you can and pray it stops on its own. There is no fixing this. There is only survi—"

## Day 7: The Silence

### Morning: Last Transmissions

By Day 7, most communication systems had failed. The Bloodweb was gone. Speaking Stones were unreliable. Magical sendings were blocked by dimensional interference.

The last confirmed transmission from Throne territory:

**Cassius to anyone listening**: "Covenant of the Crimson Throne, Day 7. We're 120 miles from the northern mountains. The Wave is behind us. We'll reach shelter in four days if we maintain pace. If anyone survives, find us there. Cassius out."

He made the mountains. He died there three weeks later. But some of his soldiers survived in the deep caves. A few may still be there.

### Afternoon: The Wave's Extent

By Day 7 afternoon, the Sundering Wave had traveled approximately 700 miles from Neth'Korath. It had passed through:
- The entire Mind central territory
- The southern Throne provinces
- The eastern edge of Twilight territory

**Death toll by this point**: Approximately 10 million vampires.

### Evening: The Silence Falls

By Day 7 evening, there were no more transmissions from the core territories. No more reports. No more calls for help.

The vampire civilization that had ruled for a thousand years was functionally gone.

What remained:
- The Twilight sanctuaries (sealed)
- The Throne northern remnants (fleeing)
- Scattered survivors everywhere (hiding)
- The protagonist (in stasis at Vel'Thoras)

### The Servant Races

While vampires died, the servant races acted.

**Humans**: Began organizing. The first warlords emerged within days. They would fight each other for decades before anything like order formed.

**Dwarves**: Sealed their deep holds. Emerged later to reclaim their mines. Borin's rebellion at Irondeep became legendary.

**Elves**: Retreated into forests. Began planning their isolation. Thornweald would emerge to lead them in Year 18.

**Goblins**: Fled to the swamps. They would be hunted for centuries. But they remembered. They always remembered.

## Aftermath: The First Month

### The Wave Stops

Approximately Day 30, the Sundering Wave stopped expanding. No one knows why. Perhaps it reached its natural limit. Perhaps it ran out of energy. Perhaps the dimensional breaches finally collapsed.

**Final extent**: Approximately 1,500 miles from Neth'Korath. This became the boundary of the Scar.

### The Death Toll

**Vampires**: ~14 million dead (of 15 million)
**Remaining**: Perhaps 500,000 scattered survivors, plus whoever was in Twilight sanctuaries

Of those 500,000, most would die within six months from starvation, hunting, or suicide.

By Year 1, fewer than 100,000 vampires remained in the world.

### What Survived

**Twilight Sanctuaries**: Sealed and silent. Unknown how many inside. Would not open for... possibly ever.

**Throne Remnants**: A few thousand in northern mountain caves. Slowly starving. Some entered stasis.

**Scattered Individuals**: Hidden in remote locations. Some would survive in isolation. Some would emerge and be killed.

**The Stasis Chambers**: Including the protagonist. Waiting.
