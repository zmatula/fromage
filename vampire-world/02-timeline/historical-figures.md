# Historical Figures of the Post-Collapse Era

Named individuals who shaped the 1,200 years between collapse and present. These are the people who are still debated, celebrated, and despised.

---

## Immediate Aftermath (Year 0-50)

### Cassandra Vael, "The Burner" (Year 8-35)

**Role**: Leader of the Valdrian Purges
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year -15 to Year 35

**Who She Was**
- Daughter of a collaborator family
- Her father managed vampire estates
- She grew up in privilege, attended by servants
- When the collapse came, she saw her father torn apart by a mob
- She was 15

**What She Did**
- Joined the Purifier bands at 18
- Rose to command by 23
- Led systematic hunt for collaborators in Valdrian territories
- By Year 30, she was burning people on accusation alone
- Executed over 40,000 people in seven years

**Her Justification**
- "The corruption must be burned out at the root"
- "One missed collaborator can poison a generation"
- "Better a hundred innocents die than one traitor live"

**Her Fall**
- Her own brother Tobias condemned to burning
- Evidence was thin—a neighbor's accusation
- Cassandra approved the execution
- Three days later, real collaborator caught confessed Tobias was innocent
- Cassandra hanged herself, Year 35

**Legacy**
- Hero to some: "She did what was necessary"
- Monster to others: "She was worse than the vampires"
- Girls still named Cassandra in Valdrian territories
- Her grave is both pilgrimage site and latrine

**Still Debated**
- Was the purge necessary to heal?
- Did she save the region or traumatize it?
- Would you have done differently?

### Roderick of the Ash (Year 3-45)

**Role**: First Warlord-King of the Valdrian Basin
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year -22 to Year 45

**Who He Was**
- Sergeant in vampire auxiliary forces
- When collapse came, he had a sword and knew how to use it
- Gathered survivors, protected villages
- Initially just trying to survive

**What He Did**
- Year 3: First warlord to control a region
- Year 12: Unified five territories into Valdris
- Year 25: Crowned himself king
- Built roads, established courts, created coin
- Killed anyone who threatened his rule

**His Methods**
- Brutal but fair (by the standards of the time)
- Executed bandits publicly
- Fed his soldiers before his family
- Married three times for alliance

**His Death**
- Killed by his own son, Year 45
- The son thought he deserved to rule
- The son was killed by his own generals six months later

**Legacy**
- Every Valdrian king claims descent from him
- "Roderick's Law" still on the books
- His actual descendants died out in Year 200
- Current royal family has no blood connection

**Still Debated**
- Was he a savior or a tyrant?
- Did he create order or just another kind of oppression?

### Sister Mercy (Year 1-48)

**Role**: Founder of the Church of Divine Light
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Unknown - Year 48

**Who She Was**
- No one knows her original name
- First appeared in Year 1, preaching at the Scar
- Said she heard the divine voice during the collapse
- Gathered followers through genuine compassion

**What She Did**
- Preached that the collapse was divine punishment for serving vampires
- Established first temples
- Created doctrine of "Divine Light" opposing vampire darkness
- Fed the hungry, healed the sick, buried the dead

**Her Beliefs**
- Vampires were demons, not creatures
- The collapse was salvation, not tragedy
- All vampire artifacts are cursed
- Knowledge from vampire era is tainted

**Her Death**
- Died Year 48, exhausted and ill
- Thousands attended her funeral
- Body never found—followers say she ascended

**Legacy**
- Church still follows her basic doctrine
- The Order of Dawn claims spiritual descent
- Her compassion is forgotten; her intolerance remains
- Some scholars think she never existed—a composite figure

**Still Debated**
- Was she a saint or a fanatic?
- Did she save souls or doom minds?
- Did she even exist?

### Grimjaw (Year 5-52)

**Role**: First Ironpeak Confederation Thane
**Species**: Dwarf
**Lived**: Year -180 to Year 52

**Who He Was**
- Ancient even by dwarven standards
- Remembered vampire conquest firsthand
- Had waited 180 years for revenge
- Finally got it

**What He Did**
- Year 5: Led dwarves out of the mountains
- Destroyed every vampire structure he found
- Established Ironpeak Confederation
- Made pact with humans against vampire remnants

**His Philosophy**
- "Break it down. Melt it down. Forget it existed."
- Destroyed more vampire knowledge than anyone except Cassandra
- Believed dwarves should look forward, not back

**His Death**
- Died in his sleep, Year 52
- Buried in deepest vault
- Location secret

**Legacy**
- Ironpeak still follows his basic policies
- Dwarves most resistant to vampire artifact trade
- His name means "stubborn determination" in modern Dwarven

---

### Thornweald the Sealer (Year 18-95)

**Role**: First Voice of Silverwood
**Species**: Elf
**Lived**: Year -400 to Year 95

**Who She Was**
- One of the few elves who remembered pre-vampire life
- Had lived through conquest, occupation, liberation
- Wanted one thing: to be left alone

**What She Did**
- Year 18: Declared Silverwood Domain independent
- Closed borders to all outsiders
- Sealed vampire ruins within the forest
- Began elven isolation that continues today

**Her Philosophy**
- "We endured them. We will not endure you."
- "Let the other races destroy themselves with vampire trinkets"
- "The forest remembers. The forest protects."

**Her Death**
- Faded in Year 95—elves don't quite die the same way
- Current Voice claims to commune with her spirit

**Legacy**
- Elven isolation is her direct legacy
- Silverwood remains closed 1,200 years later
- Other races resent elven "hoarding"
- Elves say they're protecting everyone, including themselves

**Still Debated**
- Are elves protecting or hiding?
- What's in the sealed ruins?
- Will Silverwood ever open?

## Dark Ages (Year 50-300)

### King Aldric I "the Builder" (Year 180-245)

**Role**: Second founder of Valdris
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 125 to Year 245

**Who He Was**
- Great-great-grandson of a Roderick general
- Inherited a fragment of Valdris in civil war
- Had three brothers who also claimed the throne
- Killed all three

**What He Did**
- Year 180: United the fragments
- Rebuilt roads Roderick had started
- Established law codes still used today
- Created the Valdrian administrative system
- Founded the University of Brightholme

**His Methods**
- Brilliant administrator
- Ruthless with rivals
- Married four times (two wives died suspiciously)
- Children were tools for alliance

**His Great Compromise**
- Made peace with the Church
- Declared vampire artifacts "dangerous but not demonic"
- Created the permit system for relic study
- Balanced scholars and fanatics

**His Death**
- Died in bed, Year 245
- Probably poisoned by his heir
- His laws lived; his bloodline didn't

**Legacy**
- Every Valdrian reform is compared to his
- "What would Aldric do?" is serious question
- Current dynasty claims spiritual inheritance
- Actually no blood relationship

### The Three Scholars of Ironmount (Year 278)

**Role**: First successful vampire text translators
**Species**: Human (all three)

#### Varek Stoneson
- Dwarf-raised human, understood vampire engineering
- Did the technical translation
- Murdered in Year 280 by religious fanatics
- Never finished his dictionary

#### Sister Helena
- Church scholar, believed understanding evil helped fight it
- Provided cultural context
- Recanted under pressure from the Church, Year 282
- Spent remaining years in a penitent cell
- Her original notes survived in secret

#### Magister Orin
- Free Cities academic, funded the project
- Disappeared in Year 279
- Some say killed by the Church
- Some say fled with the complete translation
- A complete copy allegedly exists somewhere

**What They Achieved**
- First readable vampire text: maintenance manual for Bloodweb node
- Proved vampire script was language, not magic
- Established translation methodology
- Everything since builds on their work

**Legacy**
- Academics call them founders
- Church calls them heretics
- Their work survived despite suppression
- The "disappeared" copy is legendary—many have sought it

### Prophet Vaelen (Year 150-195)

**Role**: Leader of the Redemption Cult
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 105 to Year 195

**Who He Was**
- Claimed to be reincarnated vampire seeker
- Said he remembered the "true purpose" of vampire civilization
- Gathered thousands of followers
- Built a city in the Broken Lands

**What He Preached**
- Vampires were trying to transcend, not dominate
- The collapse was a failed apotheosis
- Humans could complete what vampires started
- His followers could become gods

**What Happened**
- Year 195: mass ritual at the "Ascending Spire" (vampire ruin)
- 3,000 followers participated
- Unknown ritual, unknown artifact
- Everyone died
- Spire still stands, still avoided

**Legacy**
- Redemption Cult is "how not to do it"
- Area still called the Prophet's Folly
- Some say he actually achieved something
- Some say he's still there, neither dead nor alive

**Still Debated**
- Was he insane or onto something?
- What actually happened in that ritual?
- Why won't anyone investigate the Spire?

### Queen Margret the Pious (Year 225-275)

**Role**: Founder of the Sanctified Realm
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 198 to Year 275

**Who She Was**
- Daughter of a petty king
- Took vows, became Sister Margret
- Called to rule when brothers died in plague

**What She Did**
- United seven kingdoms under Church authority
- Year 225: Founded Sanctified Realm
- Made heresy punishable by death
- Launched first Sanctified Crusade against "vampire taint"

**Her Beliefs**
- Absolute faith in Divine Light
- Vampires were literally demons
- Any knowledge from vampire era was corruption
- Better ignorant and pure than learned and damned

**Her Methods**
- Burned libraries
- Executed scholars
- Destroyed artifacts
- Expelled elves and dwarves

**Her Death**
- Died in prayer, Year 275
- Declared saint within a year
- Tomb is pilgrimage site

**Legacy**
- Sanctified States still follow her doctrines
- Most vampire knowledge lost in her purges
- Academics despise her
- The faithful worship her
- She destroyed more knowledge than she knew existed

## Warring Kingdoms (Year 300-700)

### General Theron Blackwood (Year 420-520)

**Role**: Commander of the Goblin Wars
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 380 to Year 520

**Who He Was**
- Professional soldier, brilliant tactician
- Rose through ranks on merit
- Believed in duty above glory
- Had a dwarf for a best friend (unusual then)

**What He Did**
- Year 420-520: Led human forces in Goblin Wars
- Perfected containment warfare
- Pushed goblins into the Broken Lands
- Won every major battle

**His Strategy**
- Don't exterminate—contain
- Goblins could be useful if controlled
- Save human lives, spend goblin ones
- War is economics

**His Private Doubts**
- Personal journals show guilt
- Questioned if goblins were really the enemy
- Wondered if they were fellow victims
- Never said this publicly

**His Death**
- Died in final battle, Year 520
- Killed by goblin poison
- Buried with honors

**Legacy**
- Military academy named for him
- His tactics still taught
- His doubts were published 200 years later
- Caused controversy—some burned the journals

**Still Debated**
- Was containment merciful or cruel?
- Was he a hero or a butcher?
- Were the Goblin Wars even necessary?

### Admiral Sera Blackwater (Year 560-612)

**Role**: Founder of Maritime Republic dominance
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 515 to Year 612

**Who She Was**
- Daughter of a merchant captain
- First woman to command a fleet
- Ruthless, brilliant, charismatic
- Never lost a naval battle

**What She Did**
- Year 560: Broke Valdrian naval monopoly
- Established Maritime Republic control of sea trade
- Created the Banking Houses system
- Made trade more powerful than armies

**Her Philosophy**
- "Gold flows like water. Control the water."
- "Every port is a potential ally. Every ally has a price."
- "Wars are expensive. Trade is profitable."

**Her Methods**
- Bribed when she could
- Fought when she had to
- Made rivals into partners
- Made partners into debtors

**Her Death**
- Died wealthy and old, Year 612
- Possibly poisoned by banking rivals
- Her family still controls three Banking Houses

**Legacy**
- Maritime Republic still follows her model
- "Blackwater doctrine" = finance as warfare
- Current Republic based on her systems

### The Rebel-General Caden Stormhold (Year 510-512)

**Role**: Failed revolutionary
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 475 to Year 512

**Who He Was**
- General in Valdrian forces during Goblin Wars
- Distinguished record, popular with troops
- Had access to captured goblin texts

**What He Discovered**
- Goblin records of vampire era
- Goblins were not servants—they were experiments
- Blood magic was tested on them
- They were trying to restore what was taken

**What He Did**
- Year 510: Proposed peace negotiations with goblins
- Year 511: Presented evidence to Valdrian court
- Year 512: Arrested for treason
- Executed publicly

**His Last Words**
- "We are killing fellow victims and calling it victory."

**What Happened to His Evidence**
- Officially destroyed
- Copies may exist
- Goblins remember him
- Humans mostly don't

**Legacy**
- Footnote in most histories
- Hero in goblin oral tradition
- His ideas were right—no one cared
- Proof that being right doesn't matter if you're inconvenient

**Still Debated**
- Was his evidence real?
- Would peace have been possible?
- What if someone found his research?

### Prince-Bishop Aldric Thorne (Year 340-380)

**Role**: Leader of the Great Schism
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 298 to Year 380

**Who He Was**
- Church leader during theological crisis
- Moderate who became radical
- Split the Church of Divine Light

**The Crisis**
- Question: Are vampire artifacts evil or just dangerous?
- Moderates: "Dangerous—can be studied carefully"
- Purists: "Evil—must be destroyed"
- Aldric started moderate

**What Happened**
- Year 340: His daughter used a healing artifact
- Year 345: She showed signs of "corruption" (probably unrelated illness)
- Year 348: She died
- Year 350: Aldric declared all artifacts demonic

**The Schism**
- Church split between Purists (Aldric) and Pragmatists
- Purists became dominant in Sanctified territories
- Pragmatists survived in Free Cities
- Never healed—still two churches

**Legacy**
- Sanctified extremism comes from his grief
- Pragmatist church still exists but marginalized
- What if his daughter had lived?
- Personal tragedy became continental policy

## Consolidation (Year 700-1100)

### Emperor Valerian III (Year 900-968)

**Role**: Establisher of the Four-Empire System
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 858 to Year 968

**Who He Was**
- Valdrian emperor, diplomat, pragmatist
- Tired of endless wars
- Wanted to build, not fight
- Had no sons, three daughters

**What He Did**
- Year 900: Congress of Iron Rivers
- Negotiated the Four-Empire balance
- Created diplomatic systems still used
- Married daughters to Northern, Maritime, and Sanctified heirs

**The System**
- Four great powers, balanced
- Buffer states, trade agreements, marriage alliances
- Not peace—managed competition
- Has lasted 300 years

**His Philosophy**
- "We cannot eliminate conflict. We can make it expensive."
- "My grandchildren will rule everywhere or nowhere."
- "Better a rich peace than a glorious war."

**His Death**
- Died in bed, Year 968
- Granddaughter on Valdrian throne
- Grandson on Northern throne
- The balance held

**Legacy**
- "Valerian's Peace" still referenced
- Current system is his design
- Breaking it would mean continental war

**Role**: Founder of the Inheritors
**Species**: Unknown (probably human)
**Lived**: Unknown

**Who They Were**
- Identity never confirmed
- Founded the Inheritors in Year 850
- Wrote the original charter
- Disappeared after establishing the organization

**What They Did**
- Created secret society to preserve vampire knowledge
- Established vaults, codes, recruitment methods

- Vampire survivor in disguise
- Collective identity (not one person)
- Time-displaced being

**Their Philosophy**
- Knowledge is power—even dangerous knowledge
- Suppression is not safety
- Someone must be ready
- The cycle may repeat

**Legacy**

- Identity is their greatest mystery
- Some say they're still alive

### High Inquisitor Mordecai Stern (Year 920-975)

**Role**: Order of Dawn leader during the "vampire cult" extermination
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 885 to Year 975

**Who He Was**
- Rose through Order ranks
- Absolutely convinced vampires still existed
- Believed he had a divine mission
- May have been right

**What He Did**
- Year 920: Discovered "vampire cult" in Northern territories
- Evidence: rituals, blood drinking, worship of vampire texts
- Exterminated over 200 people
- Destroyed their library and artifacts

**The Debate**
- Were they really vampire worshippers?
- Were they Inheritors?
- Were they just scholars?
- Did he kill vampires or just people who were curious?

**His Certainty**
- Never doubted
- Wrote detailed accounts of the "evil" he found
- His records are the only evidence
- He burned everything else

**His Death**
- Died hunting another "cult"
- Killed in ambush
- By who? Unknown
- Inheritors? Cult survivors? Random bandits?

**Legacy**
- Order of Dawn considers him hero
- Scholars consider him monster
- The truth died with his victims

### Queen Seraphina the Peacemaker (Year 1050-1120)

**Role**: Last monarch to attempt continental unity
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 1015 to Year 1120

**Who She Was**
- Valdrian queen, idealist
- Believed the empires could unite
- Spent her reign trying
- Failed

**What She Did**
- Year 1050-1100: Series of summits
- Proposed unified council
- Wanted shared army, shared law, shared purpose
- Each empire refused for different reasons

**Why It Failed**
- Valdrian nobles: "We would lose sovereignty"
- Northern kingdom: "We would lose independence"
- Maritime Republic: "We would lose profits"
- Sanctified States: "We would compromise faith"

**Her Response**
- "Then we will die separately."
- She was probably right

**Her Death**
- Died in Year 1120, exhausted and disappointed
- Her reforms mostly reversed by successor
- She had no children

**Legacy**
- "Seraphina's Dream" = idealistic but impossible
- Some say she was naive
- Some say she was ahead of her time
- Current crisis proves she was right

## Recent Past (Year 1100-1200)

### Director Maren Thorne (Year 1124-1180)

**Role**: Inheritor leader during the exposure crisis
**Species**: Human
**Lived**: Year 1088 to Year 1180

**Who She Was**
- Fourth-generation Inheritor
- Took leadership in Year 1124
- The year they were exposed

**The Crisis**
- Year 1124: Inheritor safe house raided
- Documents captured
- Organization revealed
- Mass arrests across continent

**What She Did**
- Organized emergency protocols
- Destroyed compromised vaults
- Evacuated members
- Took Inheritors underground
- Saved the core organization

**Her Methods**
- Ruthless about security
- Killed three members who knew too much
- Made alliances with other secret societies
- Survived

**Her Death**
- Died in hiding, Year 1180
- Location secret
- Inheritors still operating thanks to her

**Legacy**
- Current Inheritor structure is her design
- More secretive, more paranoid, more effective
- They learned from exposure

## How to Use These Figures

### In Dialogue
- Characters debate them: "Cassandra was a monster." "Cassandra saved us."
- Reference them: "What would Aldric the Builder do?"
- Claim descent: "My family served Blackwood."

### In Plot
- Their actions have consequences that reach present
- Their writings, artifacts, descendants matter
- Discovering truth about them changes understanding

### In Theme
- Each represents a response to crisis
- Each was "right" from their perspective
- None were purely good or evil
- Their debates are our debates
