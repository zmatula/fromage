# Year 812: The Stasis Vampire Discovery

*The only confirmed contact with a vampire since the Collapse*
*Hidden for 400 years*

---

## Overview

In Year 812, miners in the Northern Dominion discovered a vampire in stasis—alive. He woke, was questioned, revealed crucial information, and was destroyed. Then the records were sealed. The Church decided the truth was too dangerous to know.

---

## Scene 1: The Discovery

**Year 812, 3rd of Highsun | Northern Dominion, Iron Ridge Mine**
**POV: Foreman Brock Ironhand**

---

Brock had been mining for thirty-two years and had never seen anything like it.

"It just... opened up," Davan said. The young miner's voice was shaking. "We were following the vein and the wall gave way. There's a chamber behind it."

"What kind of chamber?"

"Don't know, sir. Nobody wanted to go in. There's a light."

A light. In a sealed chamber a mile underground. Brock felt a chill that had nothing to do with the mine's temperature.

"Show me."

---

The breach was rough-edged—their pickaxes had punched through whatever seal had held it closed. Beyond, Brock could see a space that wasn't natural. Carved walls. A floor that wasn't stone.

And the light—soft, blue, pulsing gently in the darkness.

"Get the overseer," Brock said. "And the guards. Nobody goes in until—"

"Sir." Davan was pointing. "There's something in there. In the light."

Brock looked. The light was coming from a structure in the center of the chamber—a kind of pod or capsule, covered in symbols he didn't recognize.

Inside the pod, barely visible through a translucent surface, was a figure.

*Not a figure*, Brock realized. *A person.*

"Gods preserve us," he whispered.

---

The overseer came. Then the guards. Then the local priest, and finally Lord Commander Aldric of the Church garrison.

"It's vampire construction," Lord Commander Aldric said. His voice was grim. "I've seen records. This is a stasis chamber. They used them for long-term preservation."

"Can you tell if it's occupied?" the overseer asked.

"It's occupied." Aldric's hand rested on his sword. "The question is whether it's alive."

They argued. Some wanted to seal the chamber and leave it. Some wanted to destroy the pod without opening it. Aldric overruled them all.

"If there's a vampire in there—alive—we need to know what it knows. We need to question it." He looked around. "The Church has protocols for this. We'll take it to the garrison. Proper containment."

Brock watched them remove the pod. Watched the figure inside shift slightly as they moved it—definitely alive, definitely aware on some level.

Eight hundred years since the Collapse. And here was a vampire, sleeping through all of it.

*What does it know?* Brock wondered. *What does it remember?*

He would never find out. An hour later, Lord Commander Aldric's men came back and ordered everyone who'd seen the chamber to report to the garrison.

"For debriefing," the captain said.

Brock went. He never came home.

---

## Scene 2: The Awakening

**Year 812, 5th of Highsun | Church Garrison, Containment Chamber**
**POV: The Vampire (name unknown)**

---

Waking was wrong.

He'd gone into stasis expecting to wake to Velanna's face, to the Bloodweb's hum, to the continuation of the life he'd known. Instead—

Silence. Where the Bloodweb should have been, emptiness. The connection that had sustained him for three centuries was simply gone.

And the faces around him weren't vampires.

Humans. Dressed strangely, holding weapons, staring at him with fear and hostility. He was restrained—silver chains, he could feel them burning—in a room that smelled of stone and incense.

"It's awake," one of them said.

He tried to speak. His voice came out as a croak. He tried again.

"Where—where am I?"

"You're in the Northern Dominion. In a Church containment facility." The speaker was an older human with authority in his stance. "You were found in a stasis chamber beneath Iron Ridge Mine."

"How long?"

"What?"

"How long was I in stasis?"

The human hesitated. "Eight hundred and twelve years."

Eight hundred—

He struggled against the chains. Eight hundred years. The Bloodweb silent. No vampires in sight. Which meant—

"They're all dead," he whispered. "Aren't they. The others."

"Yes. Your kind is extinct."

He closed his eyes. Velanna. Mordecai. Everyone he'd known. Eight centuries dead while he slept.

"How?"

"The Collapse. Your people's weapons destroyed them." The human leaned forward. "We have questions. Answer them, and we'll make this as easy as we can."

Easy. Nothing would be easy again.

"Ask," he said.

---

They questioned him for six days.

He answered as best he could—he'd been a minor researcher, no secrets, no special knowledge. But he knew things they didn't. He knew about the Bloodweb, the covenants, the Ascension Project. He knew what Velanna had been working on when she put him in stasis.

"Project Vessel," the Inquisitor said. "What was that?"

"I don't know details. A human subject modified with vampire physiology. Something Velanna considered important enough to preserve, even as everything fell apart." He thought about it. "She called him her insurance policy. In case the Ascension failed."

"Failed how?"

"I don't know. I was in stasis before it happened."

The Inquisitor wrote notes. The vampire watched him and understood that he was being recorded, catalogued, extracted. Every piece of information he had would be taken from him.

"The Twilight sanctuaries," the Inquisitor said. "You mentioned survivors."

"There were sanctuaries prepared. Protected from the Bloodweb. If the Collapse was what you describe, some would have survived."

"Where?"

"The western mountains. Maybe a thousand vampires. Maybe more."

The Inquisitor's face went pale. "There are surviving vampires."

"I don't know. After eight hundred years—maybe they're dead too. Maybe they're still there. I don't know."

The Inquisitor stood. "Wait here."

He left. The vampire waited in his silver chains and thought about Twilight sanctuaries hidden in the mountains, full of vampires who'd prepared for the end.

*If they're still alive*, he thought, *they've been waiting a very long time.*

---

On the sixth day, the Inquisitor returned with a new question.

"The Scar. The dimensional breach in the southern lands. What do you know about it?"

The vampire felt cold.

"The Ascension experiment was conducted at a dimensional thin point. If the experiment failed catastrophically—" He stopped. "The Scar is where Neth'Korath was. Isn't it."

"Yes."

"Then the dimensional barriers are thin there. Possibly breached. Things could come through."

"What things?"

"I don't know. We were studying dimensions we didn't understand. Entities on the other side that we couldn't communicate with. If the barriers are open—" He looked at the Inquisitor. "Have things come through? In eight hundred years?"

"We don't discuss that with prisoners."

"You should. Because if you don't know what you're dealing with—"

"The questioning is concluded," the Inquisitor said. "You've been helpful."

"What happens now?"

The Inquisitor didn't answer. But the vampire could see it in his face.

"You're going to kill me."

"The Church has determined that your continued existence poses an unacceptable risk."

"I've told you everything I know. I'm no threat to—"

"You're a vampire. You're a threat by existence." The Inquisitor turned to leave. "It will be quick."

The vampire laughed. It was not a humorous sound.

"Eight hundred years. I slept through everything. And I wake up to be executed because of what I am."

"Your people enslaved the world."

"I know. I was there." He looked at the Inquisitor. "You don't know what we were, do you? You've had eight centuries and you still think we were all monsters. You have no idea."

"We know enough."

"You know nothing. And after you kill me, you'll know less." He closed his eyes. "Do what you're going to do. Just—remember what I told you about the Scar. About the sanctuaries. Remember that there are things you don't understand."

The Inquisitor left.

They executed him that night.

---

## Scene 3: The Questioning

**Year 812, 9th of Highsun | Church Garrison, Inquisitor's Office**
**POV: Inquisitor Aldric Sennett**

---

Aldric read his notes for the third time and felt a growing unease.

The vampire was dead. That part was done—a threat eliminated, proper protocols followed. But the information he'd gathered...

*Twilight sanctuaries. Surviving vampires in the western mountains. Project Vessel—a human-vampire hybrid preserved through the Collapse. The Scar as active dimensional breach. Entities on the other side.*

This was not intelligence he could keep to himself. He had to report to the High Council.

And they had to decide what to do with it.

---

**Report on Stasis Vampire Interrogation**
**Submitted by Inquisitor Aldric Sennett**
**Year 812, 11th of Highsun**

SUMMARY: The stasis vampire provided significant intelligence regarding the pre-Collapse period and potential ongoing threats. Key points:

1. **Twilight Sanctuaries**: The vampire confirmed that some vampires prepared for the Collapse and may have survived in protected locations in the western mountains. Current population unknown. Recommend investigation.

2. **Project Vessel**: A human subject modified with vampire physiology was preserved through the Collapse. Location: former site of Neth'Korath (now the Scar). May still be viable. Purpose unclear. Recommend investigation.

3. **Dimensional Breach**: The Scar may be an active thin point in dimensional barriers. Entities from other dimensions may be capable of crossing. Vampire indicated their researchers did not understand these entities. Recommend investigation.

4. **"Children of the Vessels"**: Vampire used this phrase without explanation. May refer to Project Vessel or other programs. Unknown significance.

5. **General Knowledge**: Vampire provided extensive background on vampire civilization, covenants, and culture. This information has been archived for scholarly review.

ASSESSMENT: The intelligence gathered suggests several active threats requiring Church attention. The vampire was cooperative and appeared to be providing accurate information. His destruction was completed per protocol.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Investigate western mountains for Twilight sanctuaries
- Investigate the Scar for Project Vessel
- Establish monitoring of the Scar for dimensional incursion
- Restrict access to this report to High Council only

---

Aldric sent the report. He waited for response.

The response came three days later, by sealed courier from the capital.

It was not what he expected.

---

## Scene 4: The Cover-Up

**Year 812, 17th of Highsun | Cathedral of Sacred Light, Capital**
**POV: High Councilor Marius Dawnward**

---

High Councilor Dawnward read Inquisitor Sennett's report twice.

Then he summoned the Inner Council.

"This cannot become known," he said.

Bishop Aldric—the most senior member—frowned. "The intelligence is significant. If there are surviving vampires—"

"If there are surviving vampires, what do we do? Send an army into the western mountains to hunt for a sanctuary that may or may not exist? And if we find it, then what? A war with vampires we're not prepared to fight?"

"We should prepare, then."

"And how do we explain that to the faithful? Eight centuries of 'the vampires are extinct' and suddenly we're mobilizing for vampire war?" Dawnward shook his head. "The panic alone would destabilize four kingdoms."

"And Project Vessel?" Bishop Aldric pressed. "A human-vampire hybrid in the Scar?"

"The Scar is impassable. No one has entered it and returned. If this 'Vessel' exists, it's buried under ruins we can't reach."

"The dimensional breach—"

"Is a known danger we already monitor. We don't need vampire testimony to tell us the Scar is dangerous."

Bishop Aldric was silent for a moment. "You want to suppress this."

"I want to protect the faithful from panic and protect the Church from instability. This information serves neither purpose." Dawnward looked around the table. "Imagine the questions. 'Are vampires still alive?' 'Did the Church lie to us?' 'Are we safe?' We have no good answers. So we don't answer."

"And the investigation?"

"Quiet. No reports, no records. We send trusted people to the mountains to look for sanctuaries. If they find something, we decide what to do then. If they don't, we've lost nothing."

"And if the Inquisitor talks?"

Dawnward was silent.

Bishop Aldric understood. "He's a good man."

"He's a man who knows too much. We can reassign him somewhere remote. Somewhere he can't cause problems."

"And the miners who found the chamber?"

"Already dealt with."

Bishop Aldric looked sick. But he nodded.

"The records will be sealed," Dawnward said. "Personal vault, Inner Council access only. The official version: the stasis chamber contained a corpse. Destroyed immediately as a precaution. No intelligence gathered. No ongoing threats identified."

"That's a lie."

"It's a necessary lie." Dawnward stood. "We serve the faith. The faith requires stability. That's what we're protecting."

He left. The orders were given. The records were sealed.

Inquisitor Sennett was reassigned to a monastery in the frozen north. He died there five years later—natural causes, officially. The miners who'd found the chamber were never seen again.

The truth was buried for four hundred years.

## Scene 5: The Consequences

**Year 812-1200 | The Secrets That Survived**

---

### The Inquisitor's Nephew

Inquisitor Sennett wrote a letter before his reassignment. He sent it to his nephew—a minor priest named Aldric Vance.

*The Church has buried something. Vampire in stasis, alive, questioned for six days. Information about survivors, about something in the Scar, about threats they won't acknowledge. I don't know why. But I know they're afraid.*

*Don't trust them. Don't forget.*

Aldric Vance didn't forget. He told his son, who told his daughter, who told her children. The story passed down through generations, mutating slightly with each telling but keeping the core: the Church had found a living vampire and hidden the truth.

In Year 1190, a young man named Marcus Vance—direct descendant—heard the story and became a Veil Keeper.

---

### The Miners' Son

One miner—a man named Brock Ironhand—had a son named Daven. Daven was twelve when his father disappeared.

Daven grew up knowing something was wrong. His father had been a loyal man, a hardworking man. He didn't just vanish. He'd been taken.

Daven spent his life trying to find out why. He never did. But he passed the obsession to his children, and they to theirs.

In Year 1050, a descendant named Kira Ironhand discovered Church records referencing the Iron Ridge incident. The records were sealed. She became convinced the Church was hiding something catastrophic.

She became the founder of the Valdrian Veil Keeper chapter.

---

### The Inheritors

The Inheritors of Blood heard rumors of the Year 812 discovery within a decade. Their agents in the Church reported whispers of a sealed report, of an Inquisitor reassigned under suspicious circumstances.

They spent a century trying to access the records. They failed.

But they learned something else: the Inner Council was afraid. Whatever that vampire had said, it had frightened the highest levels of the Church badly enough that they'd killed their own people to keep it secret.

The Inheritors concluded that the vampire had known something about vampire survival. About techniques that might still work. About power that might still be claimed.

They redirected their research toward the Scar. Toward stasis technology. Toward whatever "Project Vessel" might mean.

In Year 1200, Vera Nighthollow—senior Inheritor—finally accesses a fragment of the original report. She learns about the Vessel. She begins searching for it.

---

### The Church

High Councilor Dawnward died in Year 843 without ever speaking of the incident. His successors knew only that there was a sealed vault, and sealed vaults were not to be opened.

For four hundred years, the Year 812 discovery sat in the Inner Council archives. Successive High Councilors glanced at it, found it difficult, and decided it was a problem for the next generation.

In Year 1198, High Councilor Aldric III—facing threats from multiple directions—finally opens the vault and reads the full report.

He understands why his predecessors buried it. He understands why they should have acted on it.

He understands that four hundred years of inaction may have made everything worse.

But he doesn't know what to do either. So he closes the vault, and the secret stays buried.

---

## Historical Note

What the Year 812 vampire knew:

- **Twilight sanctuaries exist.** He was right. They're still there.
- **Project Vessel survived.** He was right. The protagonist sleeps in the Scar.
- **The Scar is an active breach.** He was right. Things have come through.
- **The Church doesn't understand what they're facing.** He was right about that too.

Four hundred years of secrets. And when the protagonist finally wakes in Year 1200, he'll enter a world that could have been prepared for him—but chose not to be.
