# Pre-Vampire Era: Personal Dramas

The betrayals, friendships, and choices that shaped the world before vampire dominion.

---

## The Thornweald Betrayal

### The Players

**Lord Calindor Thornweald**
- Elder of House Thornweald
- Age: 890 years
- Ambition: Destroy House Silvermoon and rule Silverwood

**Lady Aelindra Starweaver**
- House Silvermoon loyalist
- Age: 750 years
- Position: Voice of the Conclave (leader)

**Sethik the Whisperer**
- Vampire clan leader
- Calindor's contact
- Ambition: Conquer elves with minimal losses

### The First Contact (Year -1080)

**How It Happened**:
Calindor was hunting in the forest edge when he encountered a vampire patrol. Instead of fighting, he talked. He shouldn't have survived the conversation. But he offered something more valuable than blood.

**His Proposal**:
"I can give you Silverwood without a war. The Conclave, the defenses, the sacred sites—all of it. In exchange, I want House Silvermoon destroyed and my house in their place."

**Sethik's Response**:
"Why should I trust an elf?"

**Calindor's Answer**:
"Because I hate them more than you do."

### The Secret Correspondence (Year -1080 to -1050)

For thirty years, Calindor fed information to the vampires:
- Conclave meeting schedules
- Defense positions and weaknesses
- Which elders opposed resistance
- Sacred site locations
- Ritual calendars

**His Method**:
Dead drops in the forest. Coded messages. Never the same location twice. Never face-to-face after the first meeting.

**What He Got**:
Promises. Sethik promised vampire magic that would destroy Silvermoon. He promised Calindor would rule. He promised everything—and meant none of it.

### The Discovery (Year -1052)

**Who Found Out**:
Lady Aelindra's network of forest watchers noticed patterns. Messages appearing and disappearing. Animal behavior disrupted. Someone was meeting with something.

**The Investigation**:
Aelindra assigned her most trusted agent, Warden Kael Thornweald—Calindor's own nephew—to investigate.

**Kael's Discovery**:
He found a coded message. He recognized his uncle's cipher. He could have kept it secret. He didn't.

**His Choice**:
Kael brought the message to Aelindra. He condemned his own family. He said, "House before blood. But blood never forgets."

He was right.

### The Confrontation (Year -1051)

**The Evidence**:
Aelindra presented the correspondence to the Conclave. Thirty years of treason, documented.

**Calindor's Defense**:
"Silvermoon has led us to ruin. We are dying under their guidance. I sought alternatives."

**Aelindra's Response**:
"You sought to enslave your own people to destroy your rivals. That's not alternative. That's damnation."

**The Sentence**:
Death. House Thornweald stripped of status. All members exiled from Silverwood.

**The Execution**:
Calindor was given the choice: starlight or steel. He chose steel. His nephew Kael wielded it.

### The Aftermath

**For House Thornweald**:
Most members fled to human lands. Some changed their names. A few killed themselves rather than live with the shame. The house effectively ended.

**For Kael**:
He became "Kael the Kinslayer"—honored and pitied. He never married. He never spoke of his uncle. He walked into the deep forest in Year -1020 and never returned.

**For Silverwood**:
The betrayal revealed how close they'd come to destruction. When vampires finally came, the elves didn't fight effectively—the civil war over Thornweald had weakened them. Some say the betrayal accomplished its goal anyway.

**The Truth No One Mentions**:
Calindor was right that Silvermoon's leadership was failing. He was right that the elves were losing. His solution was monstrous, but his analysis was accurate. That's why his name is so cursed—he was a monster who told the truth.

## The Great Gathering Rejection

### The Goblin Delegation (Year -1050)

**Who Came**:

**Shaman-King Skit Bone-Caller**
- Supreme leader of the Great Swamp Confederation
- First Shaman-King to leave swamp territory in 200 years
- Believed other races might be allies

**War Chief Thresh**
- Military leader; argued against the mission
- Came to protect Skit, not to negotiate
- Was proven right

**Speaker Mira**
- Diplomat trained in human languages
- Hoped to bridge cultural gaps
- Died trying

### The Petition

**What Goblins Asked For**:
"Joint defense against the vampire clans. They hunt all of us. Together we survive. Apart we die."

**What They Offered**:
- Swamp territory as refuge (vampires hated water)
- Blood magic knowledge (goblins understood it best)
- Warrior support (goblins were numerous)

**What They Wanted**:
- Recognition as people, not vermin
- Territorial guarantee
- End to goblin hunts

### The Human Response

**King Marius II of Valdris**:
Present when goblin delegation arrived. His response:

"They send animals to negotiate. Next they'll ask us to treat with wolves and ravens."

He ordered the delegation executed. Speaker Mira was killed on the throne room steps. Shaman-King Skit and War Chief Thresh fought their way out.

### The Single Voice

**Lord Cassian of Valdris** (the king's cousin):
He argued against execution. "They came in peace. They offered alliance. Even if we refuse, we should not murder envoys."

**The King's Response**:
"Cousin, if you love goblins so much, go live in the swamp with them."

Cassian never forgave Marius. When vampires conquered Valdris, Cassian didn't resist. He said, "We deserve what's coming." He was executed anyway.

### The Elven Response

**Silverwood Conclave**:
Refused to meet the goblins at all. The delegation never entered elven territory.

**The Official Reason**:
"Goblins despoil forests. We cannot treat with them."

**The Real Reason**:
The Thornweald scandal was still fresh. The elves were looking inward, not outward. They couldn't process another outside problem.

**The One Who Tried**:

**Warden Thessan** (House Silvermoon) asked to at least hear the goblins:
"If they're willing to cross the forest to speak with us, perhaps we should listen."

**Elder Aelindra's Response**:
"After what Thornweald did, we cannot risk another infiltration. No outside contact."

Thessan obeyed. He later wrote: "We rejected them out of fear, not reason. We called it wisdom. It wasn't."

### The Dwarven Response

**The Ironpeak Confederation**:
Simply ignored the request. The goblin messenger never reached High King Krag Stonebreaker.

**What Happened**:
Dwarven border guards killed the messenger and burned the petition. They didn't even report it. To them, a goblin request wasn't worth mentioning.

### The Consequence

**For Goblins**:
When vampires came, goblins fought alone. They were conquered tribe by tribe. They were experimented on, bred, modified. They suffered more than any other race under vampire rule.

**For Everyone Else**:
They lost potential allies. They lost swamp territory that might have sheltered refugees. They lost blood magic knowledge that might have helped them resist.

**For the Future**:
Goblin rage at the rejection fuels their actions 1,200 years later. Gritha Bloodsinger's hatred for other races isn't random—it's inherited. The Great Gathering's rejection is taught to every goblin child. They will never forget.

## The Elf-Dwarf Friendships

### Thessan and Borin (Year -1200 to -1180)

**Who They Were**:

**Thessan Starweaver** (elf)
- Silverwood hunter and explorer
- Curious about other races
- Broke elven taboos regularly

**Borin Ironfoot** (dwarf)
- Ironpeak miner and craftsman
- Interested in surface world
- Willing to trade

**How They Met**:
Thessan was exploring near dwarven territory when he fell into an old mine shaft. Borin found him. Instead of killing him (as custom demanded), Borin helped him out.

**Their Friendship**:
For twenty years, they met secretly at the forest-mountain boundary. They traded: elven herbs for dwarven tools. Stories for songs. Knowledge for knowledge.

**What They Learned**:
Their peoples weren't so different. Dwarves and elves both valued craft, tradition, honor. The hatred was about territory and resources, not fundamental incompatibility.

**The Proposal**:
When the Second Elf-Dwarf War began (Year -1200), Thessan and Borin proposed a peace conference. They'd built trust. They could bridge the gap.

**What Happened**:
Thessan was executed for "race treason." Borin was exiled to the deepest mines. The war continued for 20 years.

**The Lesson**:
Individual friendship cannot overcome institutional hatred. Thessan and Borin proved peace was possible. Their peoples killed them for it.

### The Last Letter

Thessan wrote to Borin before his execution:

"My friend. They're killing me for knowing you. I don't regret it. I only regret that we couldn't make them see what we saw. Maybe someone will, someday. Maybe this letter will survive and someone will know that once, an elf and a dwarf were friends. That it was possible. That should mean something."

The letter survived. Borin kept it his whole life. His descendants have it still—in the deepest Ironhand vault, hidden from everyone. Why? Because even now, 1,300 years later, cross-race friendship is dangerous to admit.

## The Cross-Race Unions (Pre-Vampire)

### Kira Valdris and Lord Thandril (Year -1150)

**Who They Were**:

**Kira Valdris** (human)
- Princess of Valdris
- Sent to Silverwood as diplomatic envoy
- Fell in love with the wrong person

**Lord Thandril Moonweave** (elf)
- Minor Silverwood noble
- Assigned as Kira's escort
- Made a fatal mistake

**What Happened**:
They had an affair. It should have been impossible to hide—elves and humans have different... mechanics. But they managed for three months.

**The Discovery**:
Kira became pregnant. The child would be half-elven. This had happened before (rarely), but always with severe consequences.

**The Responses**:

**King Marius I of Valdris**:
"My daughter is carrying an animal's spawn. She is no longer my daughter."

**The Silverwood Conclave**:
"Lord Thandril has polluted his bloodline. He is no longer Silverwood."

**The Choices**:
Kira was offered "cleansing" (abortion) and return to court. She refused.
Thandril was offered exile. He accepted, if Kira came with him.

**What They Did**:
They fled to the borderlands. They had the child. They named her Sera.

**What Happened to Them**:
Bandits killed Thandril in Year -1145. Kira raised Sera alone. Sera grew up half-elven in a world that wanted neither half.

**Sera's Legacy**:
She became a mercenary captain. She was killed in Year -1100 fighting vampires. But she had children first. The line continued.

**The Modern Connection**:
The half-elves of Thornhaven (the trading post at Silverwood's border) may descend from Sera. They don't know. They don't want to know. The story is too painful.

## The Goblin Reformer

### Speaker Kess (Year -1100 to -1050)

**Who She Was**:
Goblin diplomat who believed in reform. Learned human and elven languages. Advocated for goblin integration into wider society.

**Her Vision**:
"We are not what they think we are. We have culture, history, wisdom. If we could show them—"

**Her Methods**:
She traveled openly in human lands. She spoke at markets and courts. She was attacked regularly but never attacked back.

**Her Reception**:

**Humans**: Fascinated and horrified. Some listened. Most threw things.
**Elves**: Refused to see her.
**Dwarves**: Killed her escorts, let her go. "Message delivered."

**The Great Gathering**:
Kess helped organize it. She wrote the petition. She trained Speaker Mira (who died delivering it).

**Her End**:
When the Gathering failed, Kess walked into human territory alone. She said she would "try once more."

She was killed within a day. Her body was displayed as a warning: "This is what happens to goblin spies."

**Her Legacy**:
Goblins remember her as the one who tried everything and got nothing. Her failure is cited as proof that peaceful integration is impossible. "Kess tried words. Words died with her."

## What These Stories Mean

### The Pattern

Every attempt at cross-race cooperation failed. Not because cooperation was impossible—but because it threatened those in power.

- **Thornweald's betrayal** succeeded because elves were divided
- **The Great Gathering** failed because humans preferred hate to alliance
- **Thessan and Borin** died because peace threatened war-profiteers
- **Kira and Thandril** were exiled because their love embarrassed their families
- **Speaker Kess** was killed because her message threatened human superiority

### The Alternative History

What if:
- Silverwood hadn't been torn by Thornweald?
- Humans had allied with goblins?
- Elves and dwarves had maintained their friendships?
- Cross-race families had been accepted?

Would vampires have conquered? Maybe. Probably. But not as easily. Not as completely. The races might have built something together—instead of being conquered separately.

### The Inheritance

In Year 1200, the races still don't cooperate:
- Elves hide in Silverwood
- Dwarves seal their mountains
- Goblins are confined to Broken Lands
- Humans rule but don't lead

And whenever cooperation is proposed, someone remembers these stories. "Remember Thornweald. Remember the Gathering. Remember what happens when we trust outsiders."

The pre-vampire failures echo forever. They're used to justify present hatreds. And they'll continue until someone breaks the cycle—or until the cycle breaks everyone.
