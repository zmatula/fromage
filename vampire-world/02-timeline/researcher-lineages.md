# Researcher Lineages

Who trained whom, what knowledge survived, and where those chains lead in Year 1200.

---

## The Mind Covenant Academic Network

Vampire research wasn't individual genius—it was accumulated tradition. Knowledge passed from mentor to student across centuries, each generation building on the last.

When the Collapse killed 99% of vampires, it shattered these chains. But fragments survived through:
- Documents in ruins
- Escaped students
- Implanted memories
- Deliberate preservation

---

## The Primary Lineages

### The Mordecai Line (Dimensional Research)

**Founder**: Archon Mordecai the Ambitious
**Focus**: Dimensional theory, consciousness transfer, transcendence

**First Generation Students**:
- Velanna val'Norath (joined Year -200)
- Theron val'Krath (joined Year -180)
- Sera val'Morn (joined Year -170)

**Second Generation**:
- Velanna's students: Dorian val'Kess, Lyra val'Thorne
- Theron's students: Kael val'Dorn, Marcus val'Ashwood
- Sera's students: None (killed in laboratory accident Year -145)

**Survival**:
- Mordecai: Consciousness encoded in Mind Codex
- Velanna: Died in Collapse, but her notes and implanted memories survive in Seven
- Dorian: Status unknown—possibly in Vel'Thoras stasis
- Others: Presumed dead

**Year 1200 Legacy**:
- Mind Codex holds Mordecai's complete knowledge (if accessed)
- Seven has fragmented Velanna knowledge (unreliable but genuine)
- Inheritors have reconstructed some Velanna research from recovered notes
- Valdrian Academy has fragments of Theron's theoretical work

### The Vaelith Line (Bloodweb Engineering)

**Founder**: Archon Vaelith the Seeker
**Focus**: Bloodweb architecture, network optimization, dependency management

**First Generation Students**:
- Aldric val'Koss (joined Year -160)
- Brennan val'Thorne (joined Year -155)
- Lyris val'Norak (joined Year -150)

**Second Generation**:
- Aldric's students: Twelve named in records
- Brennan's students: Network engineers, mostly unnamed
- Lyris's students: Transferred to Twilight, records incomplete

**Survival**:
- Vaelith: Died in Collapse
- Aldric: Died in Collapse
- Lyris: Evacuated to Twilight, status unknown

**Year 1200 Legacy**:
- Dwarves have Aldric's technical manuals (partial, in dwarvish translation)
- Inheritors have Vaelith's theoretical frameworks
- Lyris's warning about dependency is remembered—she was right about everything
- No one can rebuild the Bloodweb because no one has complete knowledge

### The Cassius Line (Applied Blood Magic)

**Founder**: Warlord-Regent Cassius the Patient (Throne covenant, but research-active)
**Focus**: Combat magic, blood enhancement, military applications

**First Generation Students**:
- Various Throne military researchers
- Cross-trained with Mind on enhancement techniques
- Deliberately kept separate from Mordecai's work

**Survival**:
- Cassius: Died fighting during Collapse
- Students: Mostly died in combat or withdrawal

**Year 1200 Legacy**:
- Northern Kingdom military has fragments of combat technique
- Some weapons were made using these methods—the techniques survive in artifacts
- Order of Dawn studied Throne magic to learn how to counter it

## The Velanna Subnet

Velanna is the most important researcher for Seven's story. Her academic connections:

### Her Mentor

**Mordecai**: Trained her in dimensional theory, consciousness research
- Relationship: Professional, competitive, eventually distrustful
- She suspected he was hiding the Bloodweb dependency crisis
- He suspected she was building something he couldn't control

### Her Students

**Dorian val'Kess**: Primary assistant on Vessel project
- Relationship: Professional, loyal
- He handled most technical procedures
- His fate is unknown—possibly in stasis at Vel'Thoras

**Lyra val'Thorne**: Secondary researcher
- Relationship: Tense—Lyra questioned ethical choices
- She died in failed Vessel conversion (possibly not accidental)
- Her notes survived and show disagreement with recruitment methods

### Her Subjects

**Vessel Seven**: Not a student, but carries her implanted knowledge
- Relationship: Complex—creator and creation
- She showed more concern for him than other Vessels
- She put him in stasis instead of terminating him
- Her motivations remain unclear

## Knowledge Chains to Year 1200

### The Inheritor Connection

The Inheritors claim descent from "those who valued vampire knowledge." Tracing their academic lineage:

**Founding Era (Year 812-900)**:
- First members were scholars who found Vel'Thoras documents
- They may have had guidance from sanctuary survivors
- Early leaders included people with vampire-era education

**Middle Period (Year 900-1100)**:
- Knowledge passed through secret apprenticeship
- Each generation selected promising students
- Training included recovered vampire techniques

**Current Leadership**:
- Vera Nighthollow trained under Master Aldric Shadowmend
- Shadowmend trained under unknown predecessor
- The chain goes back to the founding—possibly to vampire survivors

### The Academy Connection

Valdrian Academy of Natural Philosophy preserved some research chains:

**Founding Era (Year 200-400)**:
- Founded by scholars with vampire-era training fragments
- Early curricula included modified vampire techniques
- Gradually secularized and separated from origins

**Current State**:
- Most professors don't know their fields originated in vampire research
- Some techniques are recognizably derivative
- A few secret scholars maintain the old knowledge

### The Dwarven Connection

Dwarves preserved technical knowledge through different methods:

**The Engineers**:
- Copied Bloodweb technical manuals before the Collapse
- Translated them into dwarvish
- Maintained them as "ancient engineering texts"
- Don't acknowledge the vampire origin

**The Forge Keepers**:
- Guard the Forge of Souls
- Have operational knowledge (how to prevent accidents)
- Don't have creative knowledge (how to build new things)

## Lost Knowledge

### What Died with Mordecai

- Complete dimensional theory
- Consciousness transfer protocols
- True nature of the Ascension Experiment
- Whatever he was really trying to achieve

The Mind Codex may contain this, but no one's accessed it in 1,200 years.

### What Died with Velanna

- Full Vessel creation process
- Why Seven was different
- What Eleven was for
- Her real plans for the project

Seven has fragments, but he doesn't know what's real memory and what's implanted.

### What Died with the Bloodweb

- Network architecture (how to build it)
- Dependency management (how to control it)
- Inversion physics (how it killed everyone)
- Full operational manual

Fragments exist, but no one can rebuild it without the complete picture.

### What Was Deliberately Destroyed

- Throne military secrets (burned to prevent capture)
- Twilight sanctuary locations (sealed even from members)
- Coerced research (subjects erased their own work)
- "Dangerous" knowledge (destroyed by multiple parties)

Some knowledge was killed intentionally. The destroyers thought it was too dangerous to exist.

## Implications for Seven

### His Knowledge

Seven has implanted memories from Velanna. This includes:
- Research methodology
- Theoretical frameworks
- Specific technical knowledge
- Her perspective on events

But it's fragmented. He has pieces without context. He knows things without knowing why he knows them.

### His Position

Seven is a living repository of a destroyed academic tradition. He can:
- Recognize vampire-derived techniques when he sees them
- Understand artifacts others find mysterious
- Connect research threads that have been separated for 1,200 years
- Potentially reconstruct lost knowledge from his fragments

This makes him valuable to everyone and trusted by no one.

### His Limitations

Seven's knowledge is:
- Incomplete (Velanna didn't know everything)
- Fragmented (memory integration wasn't perfect)
- Biased (he sees things from her perspective)
- Outdated (1,200 years of development he missed)

He knows more than anyone alive about vampire research. But he doesn't know enough to change anything alone.

## Research Questions

### Who Still Has Knowledge?

- Mordecai (in the Mind Codex)
- Sanctuary survivors (if any exist)
- Elven archives (sealed)
- Dwarven deep vaults (incomplete)
- Seven (fragmented)

### Who's Trying to Reconstruct?

- Inheritors (primary effort)
- Valdrian Academy (some scholars)
- Northern Kingdom (military applications)
- Individual treasure hunters (profit motive)

### What Could Be Rebuilt?

- With current knowledge: almost nothing
- With Mordecai's Codex: dimensional research
- With sanctuary tech: Bloodweb fragments
- With all sources combined: significant reconstruction

The knowledge to rebuild vampire power exists. It's just scattered across sources that won't cooperate.

*Knowledge doesn't die with people—it dies when the chain breaks. Vampire research was a web of mentor-student relationships stretching back centuries. The Collapse shattered most chains. But fragments survived in documents, artifacts, and one very confused Vessel waking up with someone else's memories. Seven carries pieces of Velanna's knowledge without understanding what they mean. The Inheritors have been trying to reconstruct for 400 years. The Mind Codex holds Mordecai's mind. Somewhere in these fragments is the key to understanding what vampires really achieved—and why they really fell.*
