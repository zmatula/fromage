# Vessel Seven's Conscious Years (Year -7 to Year 0)

Specific memories, relationships, and experiences during the seven years before stasis.

---

## Pre-Conversion Fragments

### What Survives

These are not full memories—they are fragments that emerge unbidden, especially under stress:

**A Face**
- A woman with dark hair and kind eyes
- Feels like warmth, safety
- He doesn't know who she is (mother? sister? lover?)
- When he sees women who resemble her, something aches

**A Place**
- Stone walls, narrow streets, the smell of baking bread
- A market with voices calling
- Could be anywhere in the Valdrian region
- He knows it was home but can't place it

**A Name**
- "Kael" - he doesn't know if it was his name or someone else's
- It surfaces in dreams
- Velanna's records might confirm or deny

**A Sensation**
- The feel of rain on his face
- Standing somewhere high, looking at mountains
- Freedom—a feeling he can't quite remember having as a vampire

### Why These Survive

The conversion process was supposed to erase everything. But the mind protects what matters most. These fragments survived because they were tied to deep emotion—love, home, identity, freedom.

## Year -7: Creation and Early Days

### The Conversion (First Week of Year -7)

**Before**
- Brought to Vel'Thoras in a wagon with others
- Doesn't remember the journey (sedated)
- Woke in a stone room, restrained
- A researcher explained nothing

**The Process**
- Seven days of agony
- Blood replaced entirely—felt like burning from inside
- Organs transforming—could feel them changing
- Moments of clarity between delirium
- A face checking on him: Velanna

**First Waking**
- Hunger—overwhelming, impossible hunger
- Velanna there with blood in a vessel
- First feeding—shame and relief
- She told him: "You survived. That's rare."

### First Month

**Physical Recovery**
- Relearning to walk (body different now)
- Senses overwhelming—could hear heartbeats through walls
- Sunlight: first accidental exposure left burns for days
- Learning to control strength (broke three doors)

**Mental State**
- Confusion about what he was
- Fragments of before mixing with new instincts
- Velanna patient but clinical
- Designated "Vessel Seven" - the name felt like erasure

**The Other Vessels**
- Learned Vessels 1-3 were dead
- Met Vessels 4-6 briefly
- Vessel Six was struggling—shaking, bleeding from eyes
- Seven asked what was wrong; no one answered

## Year -7 to -6: Training Period

### Daily Routine

**Morning (Night)**
- Wake at sundown
- Blood ration delivered (measured precisely)
- Physical training: combat, movement, control
- Meals tracked, output tracked, everything tracked

**Midday (Midnight)**
- Education: vampire history (sanitized version)
- Language: proper vampire tongue
- Culture: how to behave, speak, defer
- Magic basics: what he could do, what was forbidden

**Evening (Pre-Dawn)**
- Testing: physical limits, healing rate, blood efficiency
- Sometimes painful tests
- Always recorded
- Return to quarters before sunrise

### Key Relationships

#### High Researcher Velanna

**His View of Her**
- Creator, authority figure, closest thing to family
- She was brilliant and driven
- Moments of warmth when experiments succeeded
- But he was always a project first

**What He Didn't See**
- Her exhaustion from years of failures
- Her genuine investment in his success
- Her fear about what was coming
- Her private notes showed she saw him as more than data

**Specific Memories**
- She smiled when he mastered his first controlled feeding
- She stayed late once to explain something he didn't understand
- When Vessel Six died, she didn't come to the lab for two days
- He never asked why

#### Assistant Researcher Dorian

**Role**: Junior researcher, often supervised Seven's training

**Personality**
- Young for a vampire (turned 200 years prior)
- Sardonic sense of humor
- Took shortcuts Velanna wouldn't approve
- Actually talked to Seven like a person

**Specific Memories**
- Taught Seven card games during downtime
- Complained about Velanna being "obsessed with perfection"
- Told Seven about Covenant politics (what Seven understood of it)
- Said once: "If this all goes wrong, you're the backup plan. Lucky you."

**Fate**: Unknown—probably died in collapse

#### Security Chief Krath

**Role**: Head of Vel'Thoras security, oversaw containment

**Personality**
- Old vampire, scarred, humorless
- Saw Vessels as weapons, not people
- Efficient and brutal

**Specific Memories**
- Tested Seven's combat abilities personally
- Broke Seven's arm to test healing rate
- Said: "You're slow. Regular vampires would kill you."
- Seven respected and hated him

**Fate**: Unknown

#### The Others in the Facility

**Researchers Seven Recognized**
- Dr. Malachar: blood specialist, always cold
- Archivist Sera: records keeper, never spoke to Seven
- Technician Voss: maintained equipment, sometimes nodded to Seven

**Servants**
- An elf who cleaned Seven's quarters—never learned her name
- She flinched whenever he moved suddenly
- He tried to be still around her
- He never asked why she was afraid

## Year -6: The Death of Vessel Six

### Vessel Six

**Who They Were**
- Created six months before Seven
- Showed early promise
- Seven met them three times in passing
- Thought they looked scared

**The Decline**
- Month three: Vessel Six started showing instability
- Blood processing failing
- Mental degradation—couldn't remember training
- Confined to medical wing

**The Death**

*Seven wasn't supposed to see it. He was in the wrong corridor.*

- Vessel Six convulsing on a table
- Bleeding from eyes, ears, mouth
- Screaming something that might have been a name
- Velanna working frantically
- It took twenty minutes

**Aftermath**
- Seven was escorted away
- No one mentioned it
- Training resumed next night
- He had nightmares for months

**What It Meant**
- He could have been Six
- His success was luck as much as design
- Velanna never explained why he lived and Six died
- The question haunts him

## Year -5 to -4: Expanded Training

### Combat Mastery

**Teachers**
- Krath for basics
- A specialist named Vexis for advanced
- Vexis was the first person to praise him

**Skills Developed**
- Speed fighting (compensate for lesser strength)
- Defensive tactics (survival over dominance)
- Weapon use: blades preferred over vampire magic
- Stealth and evasion

**Assessment**
- "Adequate against servants, vulnerable against true vampires"
- This bothered him more than it should

### Magical Training

**Blood Magic Basics**
- Enhancing his own abilities
- Reading blood (what it told about a creature)
- NOT offensive blood magic (he lacked the power)

**Mental Shielding**
- Protecting his thoughts
- Resisting compulsion
- This was emphasized heavily
- He was told it was for "colonial independence"

**Limitations**
- Couldn't use Bloodweb-dependent magic
- Power ceiling much lower than natural vampires
- Trade-off for independence

### Education: The Lies He Was Told

**About Vampire Society**
- "Benevolent guidance of lesser races"
- "Servants are content in their natural roles"
- "The Dominion brought peace and prosperity"
- He believed it—he had no counter-evidence

**About His Purpose**
- "Colonial expansion beyond Bloodweb range"
- "You'll help establish new territories"
- "A great honor to be selected"
- The truth: insurance against catastrophe they knew was possible

**About the Covenants**
- "Three Covenants in harmonious balance"
- "Minor philosophical differences"
- "All working for vampire prosperity"
- The truth: cold war, imminent conflict

## Year -3: Things Change

### The Mood Shifts

**Signs Seven Noticed**
- Velanna working longer hours
- More researchers in the facility
- New security protocols
- Hushed conversations that stopped when he approached

**Signs He Didn't Understand**
- Stockpiling of supplies
- Testing of independent systems
- Urgency in his training schedule
- Dorian's jokes getting darker

### Vessels 8, 9, and 10

**Year -5: Vessels 8-10 Created**
- Seven was told they were "refinements on his success"
- He was curious—wanted to meet them
- Not permitted: "Different research track"

**What He Learned Later**
- They were partially connected to Bloodweb
- For monitoring purposes
- This killed them in the collapse
- Seven was the only fully independent model

### New Training: Survival

**Emphasis Shifted**
- Less combat, more survival
- Long-term blood management
- Operating without support
- Independent decision-making

**What This Meant**
- They were preparing him to be alone
- No one told him why
- He thought it was for "colonial duties"

## Year -1: The Final Year

### Atmosphere

**Everyone Was Afraid**
- Seven could smell it—fear has a scent
- Velanna's hands shook sometimes
- Dorian stopped making jokes
- Even Krath seemed uncertain

**What Seven Was Told**
- "Transitional period"
- "Some technical difficulties with Bloodweb"
- "Nothing to concern yourself with"

### Vessel 11

**Year -2: Created**
- Different approach than Seven
- In a different facility
- Seven never met them
- Told they were "experimental variant"

**What Seven Knows**
- They existed
- Different design philosophy
- Fate unknown
- Could be alive somewhere

### The Last Months

**Training Intensity**
- Pushed to his limits
- Testing scenarios for isolation
- Emergency protocols
- "What do you do if you wake and no one is there?"

**Final Evaluations**
- Velanna signed off on him as "viable"
- Dorian said: "You'll do fine. You're a survivor."
- Krath said: "You're still too slow. Don't fight if you can run."

## Day 0: The Collapse

### The Last Night (13th Deepwinter)

**Evening**
- Normal training session
- Seven noticed increased activity
- More vampires in corridors than usual
- Energy felt wrong—electric, anxious

**Midnight**
- Alarms began
- Velanna came for him personally
- Her face was pale, controlled
- "Come with me. Now."

### The Emergency

**What Seven Saw**
- Researchers running
- Equipment being destroyed (why?)
- Servants being herded somewhere
- Panic everywhere

**What Velanna Said**
- "There's been an incident at the capital"
- "We're implementing emergency protocols"
- "You need to go into stasis"
- "It's temporary. I'll come for you."

**What She Didn't Say**
- That the experiment had failed
- That the Bloodweb was collapsing
- That she probably wouldn't survive
- That it might be 1,200 years

### The Stasis Chamber

**The Room**
- Level 7, deep in the complex
- Shielded, independent power
- He'd never been there before
- It was cold

**The Process**
- Velanna worked the controls herself
- Blood supply connected
- Monitoring systems activated
- The lid began to close

**Last Words**

Velanna: "You're the success, Seven. Remember that."

Seven: "When will you come back?"

Velanna: "Soon. Sleep now."

*He wanted to ask more. The sedative took him before he could.*

### What He Dreamed

**1,200 Years of Fragments**
- Not true sleep—something in between
- Flashes of memory cycling
- The woman's face, the stone streets, the name Kael
- Velanna's last expression
- Vessel Six screaming
- Dorian's laugh
- Over and over, degrading, fragmenting

**What This Did**
- Memories are damaged, incomplete
- Some things he can't access
- Some things he can't forget
- He woke with 1,200 years of dreams he can't fully remember

## Narrative Use

### Flashback Triggers

These memories surface when:
- He sees something that reminds him (a card game, a woman's face)
- He's under extreme stress
- He's using abilities he learned in training
- He dreams

### Emotional Anchors

- **Velanna** - The closest thing to a parent he can remember
- **Dorian** - The closest thing to a friend
- **Vessel Six** - The reminder of what he could have been
- **The Woman's Face** - The life that was taken from him

### Character Development

**Early Story**
- Memories are fragments, confusing
- He doesn't know what to trust
- He idealizes Velanna

**Middle Story**
- Learns the truth about the program
- Has to reconcile training with reality
- Finds evidence of Velanna's true feelings

**Late Story**
- Decides what these memories mean
- Chooses whether to search for his old identity
- Determines what Velanna was to him
