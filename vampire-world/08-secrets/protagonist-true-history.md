1. Sedation and restraint
2. Complete blood replacement with modified vampire blood
3. Organ transformation over days
4. Mental conditioning during transformation
5. Implanted memories of "better" vampire civilization
6. Designation assignment: Vessel Seven
7. Recovery and basic training

## The Sanguine Vessels Initiative

### Official Purpose

Create vampires capable of surviving Bloodweb disruption for colonial expansion to areas beyond network range.

### True Purpose

Insurance against catastrophic Bloodweb failure. The Covenant of the Eternal Mind knew the Transcendence Program was risky. Sanguine Vessels were backups in case the worst happened.

### Timeline

- **Year -12**: Initiative begins under Mind Covenant
- **Year -10**: Vessels 1-3 created, fail within months
- **Year -8**: Vessels 4-6 created, partial success then failure
- **Year -7**: Vessel 7 created, success—stable and functional
- **Year -5**: Vessels 8-10 created, stable but lesser quality
- **Year -2**: Vessel 11 created, different approach, fate unknown
- **Year -1**: Vessel 12 begun, incomplete at collapse

### Why The Initiative Existed

**Risk Awareness**:
- Mind Covenant knew Transcendence Program could fail
- Bloodweb vulnerability was understood
- Previous experiments had warnings
- Created insurance without admitting risk to others

**Secrecy**:
- Other covenants didn't know purpose
- Would have demanded halt to Transcendence if they knew risks
- Sanguine Vessels were "colonial expansion research"

## Vessel Seven: Creation Details

### The Experiment

**Objective**: Create vampire that could survive independently of Bloodweb

**Modifications**:
- Blood efficiency enhancement (needs less, uses animal blood)
- Independent regeneration (doesn't draw from network)
- Mental shielding (resistant to external control)
- Reduced but stable power (trade-off for independence)

### Why He Was Successful

**Previous Failures**:
- Vessels 1-3: Blood system instability (died of internal hemorrhage)
- Vessels 4-6: Mental breakdown (couldn't handle independence)

**Vessel Seven**:
- Modified blood processing worked correctly
- Mental conditioning held
- Power levels stable
- Physical form maintained

**The Key**:
Unknown specific factor. Velanna's notes suggest combination of original specimen's qualities and procedural refinements. Something about Seven just worked.

### His Training

**Year -7 to Year 0**:
- Basic vampire skills (feeding, senses, combat)
- Knowledge of vampire society (edited version)
- Language and culture
- Magic basics
- Obedience conditioning
- NOT trained on: full vampire history, covenant politics, Transcendence Program

## The Collapse and Survival

### When It Happened

**14th Deepwinter, Year 0**: Ascension Experiment in Neth'Korath

**Vessel Seven's Location**: Stasis chamber, Vel'Thoras Level 7

### Why He Survived

1. **Not Connected to Bloodweb**: Whole point of Sanguine Vessels
2. **In Stasis**: Protected from immediate chaos
3. **Shielded Facility**: Vel'Thoras lower levels survived
4. **Enhanced Regeneration**: Could survive deprivation
5. **Automatic Systems**: His chamber maintained power

### What Happened

**Day of Collapse**:
- Velanna initiated emergency stasis (standard protocol for experiments)
- Intended to retrieve him after crisis passed
- She never returned
- Chamber systems maintained him automatically

**1,200 Years**:
- Stasis not perfect (some awareness in dreams)
- Automatic blood feeding from stored supplies (eventually animal)
- Chamber degrading but functional
- Systems finally failing by Year 1200

### Why Now

**Year 1200 Awakening**:
- Chamber systems critical
- Stasis failing
- External intrusion (expeditions approaching)
- His body reaching point of "wake or die"

## His True Nature

### What He Is

- Experimental creation
- Insurance policy against disaster
- Modified being (not natural vampire)
- Last of his kind (probably)
- Neither fully vampire nor other

### What He's Becoming

**His Choice**:
The experiment created his body and abilities. His memories were implanted. His conditioning shaped his behavior.

But 1,200 years of stasis dreaming, plus learning the truth, plus his own experiences—these will determine who he actually becomes.

**The Arc**:
- **Start**: Product of experiment
- **Middle**: Discovering he's not what he thought
- **End**: Choosing who he will be

### The Key Question

Was anything about him chosen? Or is he entirely created?

**Evidence He's His Own**:
- Fatal flaw (need to belong) may predate conversion
- Personality quirks not in conditioning
- Moral instincts that conflict with implanted values
- Dreams during stasis suggest independent mind

**The Answer**: Unknown—and he must decide for himself
