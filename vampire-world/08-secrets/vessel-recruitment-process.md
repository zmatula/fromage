# Vessel Recruitment Process

How twelve people became experimental subjects—and why they were chosen.

---

## The Selection Criteria

The Sanguine Vessels Initiative needed subjects who could:
- Survive the conversion process (physical resilience)
- Integrate implanted memories (mental flexibility)
- Operate independently (psychological stability)
- Remain controllable (institutional loyalty)

Finding people who met all four criteria was difficult. Finding twelve was nearly impossible.

---

## Recruitment Methods

### Method 1: Voluntary Recruitment (Vessel 1)

**Approach**: "Serve vampire civilization by participating in important research"
**Target**: True believers in Mind covenant ideology
**Incentive**: Honor, advancement, promised benefits to family
**Success Rate**: Poor—belief doesn't predict survival

**Vessel One (Arren Thorne)**
- Background: Third son of failing Valdrian merchant house
- Recruited: Age 24, Year -37
- Motivation: Believed conversion would elevate his family's status
- Family: Promised they would be "rewarded"—they weren't
- Fate: Died in conversion Day 12 (blood clotting cascade)

### Method 2: Captured Subjects (Vessel 2)

**Approach**: Use prisoners and captured enemies
**Target**: Those with no rights or recourse
**Incentive**: None required
**Success Rate**: Poor—resistance affects outcomes

**Vessel Two (Unknown Elf)**
- Background: Captured from Silverwood border raids
- Recruited: Unknown age, Year -34
- Motivation: None—prisoner of war, resisted until sedation
- Family: No connections in vampire records
- Fate: Died Day 6 (elven physiology incompatible with process)

### Method 3: Convenient Access (Vessels 3-4)

**Approach**: Select from servant populations and military
**Target**: Those already within vampire control
**Incentive**: "Elevation to special service" or "enhanced operations"
**Success Rate**: Better—compliance without questions

**Vessel Three (Mira Coldwell)**
- Background: Servant family in Vel'Thoras support village, worked in kitchens since age 12
- Recruited: Age 19, Year -30
- Motivation: Selected from routine blood screenings; family told she was "elevated"
- Family: Her mother was proud
- Fate: Brain hemorrhage Day 21 (mental architecture incompatible)

**Vessel Four (Theron Ashward)**
- Background: Career soldier, Covenant of the Crimson Throne forces, distinguished record
- Recruited: Age 27, Year -15
- Motivation: Told he was being "enhanced for special operations"
- Family: Unknown
- Fate: Survived 3 months, then blood processing failed

### Method 4: No Connections (Vessels 5-6)

**Approach**: Select those who won't be missed
**Target**: Orphans, criminals, those with nothing to lose
**Incentive**: Freedom from current circumstances
**Success Rate**: Better—no expectations, no resentment

**Vessel Five (Sera)**
- Background: Orphan from Neth'Korath servant quarters, worked in textile production
- Recruited: Age 22, Year -12
- Motivation: None—selected during routine processing, no one to notice she was gone
- Family: None; records simply marked "reassigned"
- Fate: Mental breakdown Week 4, terminated Week 6

**Vessel Six (Corvin Marsh)**
- Background: Scholar from Free Cities studying vampire artifacts
- Recruited: Age 31, Year -8
- Motivation: Captured when expedition got too close to Vel'Thoras; begged to be killed instead
- Family: Unknown
- Fate: Survived 6 months, then rapid system deterioration

### Method 5: Purchased/Selected (Vessels 7-10)

**Approach**: "We'll buy your contract" or select for specific profiles
**Target**: Indentured servants, those with optimal genetic markers
**Incentive**: Freedom (in theory); substantial payment to owners
**Success Rate**: Best—refined criteria, no social complications

**Vessel Seven (The Protagonist)**

## The Recruitment Process

### Stage 1: Identification

Potential subjects identified through:
- Medical records (genetic markers, blood compatibility)
- Psychological profiles (flexibility, resilience)
- Social circumstances (isolation, vulnerability)
- Ideological screening (loyalty, suggestibility)

Most candidates were rejected at this stage. Of approximately 500 considered, 50 reached Stage 2.

### Stage 2: Approach

Subjects approached through:
- Direct recruitment (for volunteers)
- Intermediaries (for coerced)
- Purchase contracts (for servants)
- Abduction (rare, but documented)

Approach varied by target—whatever worked. Of 50 approached, 30 agreed or were compelled.

### Stage 3: Preparation

Subjects underwent:
- Medical evaluation (weeks)
- Psychological conditioning (months)
- Memory preparation (specialized training)
- Isolation from former life (permanent)

Of 30 prepared, 12 were selected for conversion.

### Stage 4: Conversion

The actual Vessel creation:
- Blood modification (dangerous, painful)
- Memory implantation (disorienting, sometimes fatal)
- Bloodweb independence development (experimental)
- Recovery and testing (months)

Of 12 converted, 7 survived to testing phase.

### Stage 5: Testing

Survivors underwent:
- Capability assessment
- Stability evaluation
- Independence verification
- Potential optimization (sometimes fatal)

Of 7 tested, 2 remained viable at Collapse (Seven and Eleven).

## Ethical Questions

### Were They Volunteers?

Vessels 1-3 technically consented. But they weren't told:
- The mortality rate (75%+)
- What the process actually involved
- That they'd lose their identities
- That success meant stasis, not glory

Is consent valid without information?

### Were They Slaves?

Vessels 7-9 were purchased. Their "consent" was their owner's consent. They had no choice. But they were also freed from servitude by selection—is that better or worse than dying as servants?

### Were They Victims?

Vessels 4-6 were coerced. Threats against themselves or families. They "agreed" because the alternative was worse.

Vessel Eleven may have been created specifically for this—never had a life before recruitment.

### What Was Seven?

Seven's origins are obscured. Velanna may have:
- Purchased him as a child
- Created him through breeding program
- Found him as an orphan
- Abducted him from somewhere

He has no memory of his pre-conversion life. That may be intentional—he was chosen because he was no one.

## Consequences

### The Families

- Vessel One's family was proud—then confused—then silent
- Vessel Five's family mourned him as executed
- Vessel Ten's family believes he's in a monastery
- Most others had no family or families who stopped asking

These people are still out there in Year 1200—or their descendants are. If Seven is identified as a Vessel, someone might recognize a family resemblance. Someone might ask questions.

### The Knowledge

The recruitment methods are documented in Vel'Thoras. If these records survive:
- The Inheritors would understand how Vessels were made
- The Church would use it as evidence of vampire evil
- The Veil Keepers would want to publish it
- Descendants of subjects would learn the truth

### Seven's Identity

Seven doesn't know who he was before conversion. The records might tell him. But they might also tell him things he doesn't want to know—that he was purchased, or bred, or no one at all.

Is knowing better than not knowing?
