# Twilight Sanctuary Status in Year 1200

What happened to the 25,000 vampires who were supposed to survive.

---

## The Sanctuary Network

Twilight covenant built five sanctuaries in secret between Year -80 and -5. Each was designed to:
- Operate independently of the Bloodweb
- Sustain vampire life for centuries
- Remain hidden from other covenants
- Preserve Twilight culture and knowledge

Total capacity: 25,000 vampires
Total Twilight population: 3 million
Selection rate: Less than 1%

---

## The Five Sanctuaries

### Sanctuary One: Sel'Varis (Primary)

**Location**: Deep beneath the Silverwood mountains, elven territory
**Capacity**: 10,000
**Actual Occupants**: 8,400
**Sealed**: Day 2 of the Collapse

**Design**:
- Largest and most sophisticated
- Self-sustaining blood production (modified animals)
- Extensive libraries and workshops
- Multiple redundant systems

**Status in Year 1200**: Unknown

**Evidence**:
- No seismic disturbances suggesting collapse
- Elves have sealed the mountain approaches
- Elven Council knows something—they won't discuss it
- Inheritor deep-scans show power signatures (inconclusive)

**Probability of Survivors**: 40%

The elves sealed the approaches for a reason. Either they're protecting survivors, or they're containing something that went wrong.

### Sanctuary Two: Vel'Mara (Secondary)

**Location**: Coastal caves, now Maritime Republic territory
**Capacity**: 6,000
**Actual Occupants**: 5,200
**Sealed**: Day 3 of the Collapse

**Design**:
- Underwater access tunnels
- Tidal power generation
- Marine blood sources
- Limited expansion capability

**Status in Year 1200**: Flooded

**Evidence**:
- Maritime divers found flooded tunnels in Year 890
- No bodies (dissolved or escaped?)
- Power systems destroyed by water
- Entry seals broken from inside

**Probability of Survivors**: 5%

Someone opened the sanctuary to the sea. Accident? Sabotage? Escape attempt? The water destroyed the evidence.

### Sanctuary Three: Keth'Vorn (Tertiary)

**Location**: Desert mountains, now Sanctified States territory
**Capacity**: 4,000
**Actual Occupants**: 3,800
**Sealed**: Day 4 of the Collapse

**Design**:
- Geothermal power
- Underground aquifer access
- Defensible narrow approaches
- Spartan but durable

**Status in Year 1200**: Collapsed

**Evidence**:
- Church expedition found ruins in Year 1045
- Structural failure, cause unknown
- Bodies present (estimated 3,000+)
- Signs of internal conflict before collapse

**Probability of Survivors**: 0%

They died in there. Whether from structural failure, internal war, or deliberate destruction, Keth'Vorn is a tomb.

### Sanctuary Four: Mor'Thelas (Quaternary)

**Location**: Northern mountains, now Northern Dominion territory
**Capacity**: 3,000
**Actual Occupants**: 2,600
**Sealed**: Day 5 of the Collapse (last to seal)

**Design**:
- Cold-climate optimized
- Minimal blood requirements
- Hibernation protocols for extended survival
- Emergency surface access

**Status in Year 1200**: Partially opened

**Evidence**:
- Northern scouts found opened exterior seals in Year 812
- Interior access blocked by secondary seals
- Signs of entry/exit over centuries
- Year 812 vampire discovery may connect here (not Sel'Naroth?)

**Probability of Survivors**: 60%

Someone got out. Someone may have gone back in. The Northern Kingdom sealed the approach and won't discuss what they found. This is the sanctuary most likely to contain active survivors.

### Sanctuary Five: Vel'Shadris (Emergency)

**Location**: Unknown (all records sealed)
**Capacity**: 2,000
**Actual Occupants**: Unknown
**Sealed**: Unknown

**Design**: Unknown

**Status in Year 1200**: Unknown

**Evidence**:
- Referenced in Twilight documents as "the last refuge"
- Location deliberately omitted from all records
- Only Selene knew where it was
- May have been designed for Selene herself

**Probability of Survivors**: Unknown

This is the mystery sanctuary. It might be the most important—or it might never have existed.

## The Blood Production Problem

### Why Disconnection Killed

When the Bloodweb collapsed, vampires didn't just lose power—they lost enhanced blood. After centuries of dependency, normal blood couldn't sustain vampire physiology. Disconnected vampires starved even with adequate feeding.

This is why the Collapse killed 99% of vampires over six months, not instantly. They survived the initial inversion but couldn't feed adequately without enhancement.

### How Sanctuaries Solved It

Twilight developed three blood production systems that worked independently of the Bloodweb:

**Modified Animals**:
Sel'Varis and Mor'Thelas used magically modified livestock—cattle and sheep bred to produce pre-enhanced blood. The animals were essentially living Bloodweb nodes. This required:
- Breeding stock (limited genetic diversity)
- Specialized feed
- Magical maintenance
- Regular culling and replacement

**Sustainable for**: 500-800 years before genetic degradation

**Synthesized Blood**:
Vel'Mara attempted artificial blood production using alchemical processes. This worked but was:
- Nutritionally adequate but unsatisfying
- Energy-intensive to produce
- Subject to equipment failure
- Psychologically demoralizing

**Sustainable for**: Indefinitely, if equipment maintained

**Hibernation Protocols**:
Mor'Thelas reduced blood consumption through rotating hibernation. 80% of occupants slept at any time, requiring only 20% of normal blood supply. This worked but meant:
- Loss of community cohesion
- Skill degradation during long sleeps
- Psychological damage from centuries of intermittent consciousness
- Vulnerable to system failure (no one awake to fix problems)

**Sustainable for**: 1,000+ years, but at psychological cost

### Why Systems Failed

**Sel'Varis** (40% survival): Modified animals may have exhausted genetic viability. If the herds died, starvation followed—or the vampires found another solution.

**Vel'Mara** (5% survival): Synthesis equipment requires maintenance. Flooding destroyed the systems. Anyone alive would have starved within months.

**Keth'Vorn** (0% survival): Structural collapse may have killed the herds first. The bodies show signs of starvation before the cave-in.

**Mor'Thelas** (60% survival): Hibernation protocols may still function. The opened seals suggest someone woke up and went looking for better food sources.

### The Inheritor Question

The Inheritors descended from Twilight survivors who had sanctuary access. If they escaped a sanctuary, they would have needed blood production knowledge to survive.

Do they have it? If they know how to produce enhanced blood independently, they've hidden it for 400 years. That would be their most valuable secret—more valuable than any artifact.

## What Went Wrong

### The Selection Problem

25,000 spaces. 3 million Twilight vampires. Selection criteria:
- Essential skills (researchers, craftsmen, historians)
- Ideological purity (true believers in Twilight philosophy)
- Genetic diversity (breeding potential)
- Political reliability (no coup risk)

Those rejected included:
- Military personnel (considered expendable)
- Recent converts (insufficient loyalty)
- The elderly (limited contribution)
- The politically problematic

**Result**: Sanctuaries contained scholars and administrators, not soldiers or leaders. When things went wrong, they lacked people who could handle crisis.

### The Sealing Sequence

The sanctuaries were supposed to seal simultaneously at a predetermined signal. Instead:
- Sel'Varis sealed Day 2 (Selene's order)
- Vel'Mara sealed Day 3 (communication delay)
- Keth'Vorn sealed Day 4 (waited for late arrivals)
- Mor'Thelas sealed Day 5 (last-minute evacuees)

Each delay meant more chaos, more panic, more unselected vampires trying to force entry. Keth'Vorn and Mor'Thelas may have been compromised by unauthorized occupants.

### The Duration Problem

The sanctuaries were designed for decades, maybe a century. They've been sealed for 1,200 years.

- Power systems degrade
- Blood sources exhaust
- Psychology deteriorates
- Equipment fails

Even if the sanctuaries worked perfectly, they weren't designed for this duration. Every system is past its limits.

## Who's Looking

### The Inheritors

**Interest**: Sanctuary survivors would validate their mission and provide vampire knowledge
**Knowledge**: They have partial maps, selection lists, design specifications
**Actions**: Attempted to reach Sel'Varis (blocked by elves), Mor'Thelas (blocked by North)
**Leader**: Vera Nighthollow has made sanctuary discovery a priority

### The Northern Dominion

**Interest**: Control vampire survivors; exploit technology
**Knowledge**: They found Mor'Thelas in Year 812; they know something got out
**Actions**: Sealed approaches, classified records, increased patrols
**Suspicion**: The Year 812 vampire may have come from Mor'Thelas, not a random stasis chamber

### The Church

**Interest**: Destroy any survivors; purify the sites
**Knowledge**: They found Keth'Vorn; they know sanctuaries exist
**Actions**: Burned Keth'Vorn records, hunted for other locations
**Fear**: Living vampires would prove their theology incomplete

### The Elves

**Interest**: Unknown—they won't discuss it
**Knowledge**: They know about Sel'Varis; they may know its status
**Actions**: Sealed the mountains; killed humans who got too close
**Theory**: Either protecting survivors, or containing a threat

## Survival Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Functional Sanctuary

A sanctuary survived intact. Occupants are alive, possibly in hibernation to extend resources. They're waiting for:
- The right time to emerge
- A signal that never came
- Someone to open the door from outside

**Implications**: 2,000-10,000 vampires alive in Year 1200. They'd have 1,200 years of isolation psychology. They'd have preserved knowledge and technology. They'd be extremely dangerous and extremely valuable.

### Scenario 2: Degraded Survivors

A sanctuary partially failed. Some occupants survived through extreme measures:
- Cannibalism of blood
- Hibernation cycles
- Reduced population

**Implications**: Dozens to hundreds of damaged survivors. Psychologically broken, possibly feral. Dangerous but possibly salvageable.

### Scenario 3: Ghost Sanctuary

All occupants died, but the sanctuary preserved their records, artifacts, and blood stores.

**Implications**: Enormous value to researchers and artifact hunters. No direct vampire threat. But blood stores could theoretically create new vampires if the technology existed.

### Scenario 4: Escaped Survivors

Survivors left the sanctuary and have been living hidden among other races for centuries.

**Implications**: Vampires integrated into human/elf/dwarf society. Hidden in plain sight. Possibly influencing events. Possibly responsible for secret society formation.

## Connections to Year 1200

### The Inheritors' Origins

The Inheritors claim to have been founded by "those who valued vampire knowledge." What if their original founders were sanctuary survivors who emerged and created the organization?

This would explain:
- Their detailed knowledge of vampire technology
- Their possession of awakening protocols
- Their certainty that vampires should be preserved, not destroyed
- Vera Nighthollow's obsession with finding more survivors

### The Year 812 Discovery

The official story: vampire found in random stasis chamber.
Alternative: vampire emerged from Mor'Thelas sanctuary.

This would explain:
- Why the North sealed the approach and classified records
- Why the vampire knew about sanctuaries and Vessels
- Why there's a connection to Sel'Naroth records (cover story)
- Why someone edited the Ashworth chronicle to mention "Second Collapse"

### Seven's Potential Allies

If sanctuary survivors exist, they might be:
- The only beings who understand Seven's nature
- Potential allies against common enemies
- Sources of knowledge about his creation
- Rivals for control of vampire legacy

Or they might be:
- Psychologically damaged after 1,200 years
- Hostile to Seven as Mind covenant creation
- Competition for limited blood sources
- More dangerous than helpful

## The Big Question

### Why Haven't They Emerged?

If sanctuary survivors exist, why stay hidden for 1,200 years?

**Theory 1: Waiting for signal**
Selene set conditions for emergence that haven't been met. They're following orders from someone who died in the Collapse.

**Theory 2: Can't emerge**
Something went wrong. They're trapped, or too weak, or too damaged.

**Theory 3: Choosing not to**
They've seen what happened to vampires who emerged (Year 812). They're waiting for better conditions.

**Theory 4: Already out**
They emerged long ago and have been influencing events invisibly. The secret societies, the political manipulations, the "coincidences" of history—vampire hands, hidden in plain sight.
