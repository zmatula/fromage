# The Sanguine Vessels Laboratory

Life inside Vel'Thoras during the Vessels Initiative. The researchers, the subjects, and the bonds that formed in the shadow of extinction.

---

## The Facility

### Location and Layout

**Vel'Thoras Research Complex**, Level 7 - "The Nursery"

A self-contained section for the Sanguine Vessels Initiative. Isolated from the rest of the facility for security and contamination control. Could operate independently for up to one year.

**Sections**:
- Creation Chamber (Vessels made here)
- Maturation Wards (post-creation development)
- Training Arena (ability development)
- Living Quarters (staff and Vessels)
- Archive Room (records, research notes)
- Emergency Stasis Bay (12 chambers)

### Security

**Access**: Blood-keyed doors. Only 14 individuals could enter.

**Monitoring**: Constant. Every room. Even private quarters.

**Communication**: Limited to approved channels. No unsupervised contact with outside.

**Departure**: Forbidden without Velanna's authorization.

The staff joked that they were as much prisoners as subjects. They weren't entirely joking.

## The Research Team

### Director Velanna

**Role**: Project director, chief architect of Vessels
**See also**: [Vampire Figures](../03-cultures/races/vampire-figures.md)

Velanna ran the project with absolute authority. She was brilliant, driven, and increasingly desperate as the timeline compressed. She treated her subjects as data—but her notes reveal unexpected moments of concern.

### Senior Staff

#### Dorian Kesh - Senior Researcher (Year -120 to Year 0)

**Specialty**: Neural architecture and memory implantation
**Personality**: Warm, patient, the "father figure" of the lab
**Relationship with Seven**: Primary trainer and emotional anchor

Dorian was 203 years old—young for a senior researcher but exceptionally talented. He'd worked on several failed projects before the Vessels Initiative. He didn't expect this one to succeed either—but he came to care about the Vessels anyway.

**His Philosophy**: "They're people, not prototypes. If we forget that, we've already failed."

**What He Did for Seven**:
- Taught him to read, speak, think
- Implanted memory engrams with unusual care (less trauma than other Vessels)
- Spent off-hours talking with him about philosophy, history, music
- Smuggled in books Velanna hadn't approved
- Argued repeatedly for Seven's humane treatment

**His Fate**: Died in the collapse. He was in the upper levels when the Wave hit. His body was never found.

**What Seven Remembers**: Dorian's voice. His laugh. The way he said "Seven" like it was a real name, not a designation.

---

#### Krath Velorn - Security Chief (Year -50 to Year 0)

**Specialty**: Combat training and threat assessment
**Personality**: Cold, professional, protective in his own way
**Relationship with Seven**: Trainer, sometimes antagonist, grudging respect

Krath was former Throne military, reassigned to Mind after "disagreement with superiors" (probably refused an immoral order). He trained the Vessels in combat—and he trained them hard.

**His Philosophy**: "I'm not here to make you comfortable. I'm here to make you survive."

**What He Did for Seven**:
- Pushed him past perceived limits
- Taught him that pain was information
- Critiqued him harshly but fairly
- Quietly advocated for keeping Seven away from Bloodweb tests (saved his life)
- Gave him a blade on his "naming day" (Year -5)

**His Fate**: Unknown. He was in the lower levels during the collapse. The emergency stasis may have caught him. If so, he's somewhere in Vel'Thoras—waiting.

**What Seven Remembers**: The blade. The way Krath nodded once—just once—when Seven finally beat him in sparring.

---

#### Mira Vex - Medical Specialist (Year -30 to Year 0)

**Specialty**: Physiological monitoring and blood chemistry
**Personality**: Nervous, meticulous, secretly kind
**Relationship with Seven**: Caretaker, confessor

Mira monitored the Vessels' physical health. She saw them more often than anyone—every day for blood draws, scans, assessments. She was terrified of Velanna but devoted to the Vessels.

**Her Secret**: She kept unofficial notes on each Vessel's personality, preferences, fears. She thought they should be remembered as individuals if they died.

**What She Did for Seven**:
- Adjusted his blood treatments to reduce pain
- Told him which researchers to avoid and why
- Listened when he had nightmares
- Kept his unofficial record with care and detail

**Her Fate**: She entered emergency stasis when the alarms triggered. She's in Vel'Thoras—in the chamber three down from Seven's.

**What Seven Remembers**: Her hands were always cold. She apologized every time she drew blood. She never forgot his birthday.

---

#### Sevik Thorn - Junior Researcher (Year -20 to Year 0)

**Specialty**: Ability development and magical training
**Personality**: Ambitious, competitive, resentful
**Relationship with Seven**: Rival, sometimes bully

Sevik was young (only 150) and desperate to prove himself. He saw the Vessels as his ticket to advancement. He pushed them hard—sometimes too hard—and reported any hint of failure to Velanna.

**His Problem**: He was afraid. Afraid of the collapse, afraid of dying, afraid of being forgotten. He took it out on the Vessels.

**What He Did to Seven**:
- Pushed training beyond safety limits
- Reported minor infractions as major problems
- Competed with Seven for Velanna's attention
- Advocated for "optimizing" Seven through Bloodweb connection (would have killed him)

**His Fate**: Died in Year -3. Accident during an ability test with Vessel Eleven. Official report said equipment failure. Unofficial rumor said Eleven killed him intentionally.

**What Seven Remembers**: Fear. Sevik was the first person Seven hated. Learning that Sevik was afraid too came later—and changed how Seven understood hatred.

---

#### Lira Ashward - Archive Keeper (Year -15 to Year 0)

**Specialty**: Records management and knowledge preservation
**Personality**: Quiet, observant, protective of information
**Relationship with Seven**: Teacher, information source

Lira was the only one who knew everything—where every document was, what every experiment had found, what had been hidden or destroyed. She was supposed to be neutral. She wasn't.

**Her Secret**: She was descended from Lysara, the purged Moderate. She kept copies of her ancestor's warnings hidden in the archives.

**What She Did for Seven**:
- Taught him the archive system
- Showed him records he wasn't supposed to see
- Explained why the project existed (the real reason, not the official one)
- Left him a key to the hidden archive on her last night alive

**Her Fate**: Died in the collapse. She was in the Archive Room when the Wave hit—protecting the records to the end.

**What Seven Remembers**: She smelled like old paper. She told him secrets she'd never told anyone. She trusted him.

## The Vessels

### The Failures (One through Six)

#### Vessel One - Arren Thorne
**Created**: Year -37
**Lived**: 12 days
**Cause of Death**: Internal hemorrhaging from incomplete blood replacement

Arren was a merchant's son who volunteered, believing it would elevate his failing family. The conversion showed promising initial response, but his original blood clotted during transformation. He fought for twelve days before internal hemorrhaging killed him.

**Legacy**: The first. Taught Velanna that enthusiasm wasn't enough—the process needed fundamental revision.

---

#### Vessel Two - The Elven Subject
**Created**: Year -34
**Lived**: 6 days
**Cause of Death**: System shock from magical interference

A captured elf from Silverwood border raids—name never recorded. High magical potential, which researchers thought would help. Instead, the magic interfered with blood transformation. System shock on Day 4, heart stopped Day 6.

**Legacy**: Ruled out elven subjects for the remainder of the initiative. Magical potential was a liability, not an asset.

**What Seven Knows**: Nothing—but this failure is why all later Vessels were human.

---

#### Vessel Three - Mira Coldwell
**Created**: Year -30
**Lived**: 21 days
**Cause of Death**: Mental degradation and brain hemorrhage

Mira was a servant girl from Vel'Thoras support village—selected from routine blood screenings. Her mother was told she'd been "elevated to special service." Physical transformation succeeded, but her mental architecture couldn't handle vampire consciousness. Couldn't form memories by Day 14, brain hemorrhage by Day 21.

**Personality**: Before degradation—hopeful, trusting. During—confused, frightened.

**Last Words**: "Where am I? Who am I? I can't find me anymore."

**What Seven Knows**: Mira's failure is why Velanna adjusted memory implant protocols. Seven has more coherent memories because of what happened to her.

---

#### Vessel Four - Theron Ashward
**Created**: Year -15
**Lived**: 3 months
**Cause of Death**: Gradual system failure despite successful conversion

Theron was a Throne soldier with exceptional physical conditioning—the first real success. Conversion worked, blood system functioned independently for two months. Then slow decline as his systems couldn't maintain without Bloodweb support.

**Personality**: Disciplined, stoic, but struggled with transformation. Missed his unit, his purpose.

**His Final Words**: "Tell the next ones that the body wants to live. Trust that."

**What Seven Knows**: Theron's advice influenced later protocols. His service record is in Vel'Thoras—proof that Vessels were people with histories.

---

#### Vessel Five - Sera
**Created**: Year -12
**Lived**: 6 weeks
**Cause of Death**: Terminated after mental breakdown

Sera was an orphan from Neth'Korath servant quarters—no family connections, wouldn't be missed. Conversion succeeded initially, but she couldn't integrate vampire consciousness with human memories. Violent episodes and self-harm by Week 4, terminated Week 6.

**Her Last Words**: "I can hear myself dying. The girl I was. She's screaming."

**What Seven Knows**: Sera is why memory modification became necessary. Seven has implanted memories because of what happened to her—the human self had to be suppressed.

---

#### Vessel Six - Corvin Marsh
**Created**: Year -8
**Lived**: 6 months
**Cause of Death**: Sudden system failure

Corvin was a Free Cities scholar captured studying vampire artifacts. Brilliant, terrified, never stopped fighting mentally. Conversion succeeded, and he survived five months—longest to date. Then rapid deterioration in Month 6, bleeding from every orifice.

**Personality**: Brilliant, observant, never stopped recording. Kept a hidden journal scratched into his cell wall.

**His Last Word**: A name—"Elise."

**What Seven Knows**: Corvin was the Vessel whose death Seven witnessed accidentally. The face he sees in nightmares. Corvin's hidden journal in Level 6, Cell 14 contains the truth about the initiative.

---

### The Survivors (Seven through Twelve)

#### Vessel Seven - "Seven" (Protagonist)

**Created**: Year -7
**Status**: Survived in stasis

The first complete success. Stable physically, mentally, emotionally. Independent from Bloodweb. Full capabilities. Only flaw: "excessive attachment to subjects of study" (Velanna's assessment) and "emotional instability" (meaning he had feelings).

**His Life in the Lab**: See below.

---

#### Vessel Eight - Brennan Cole
**Created**: Year -5
**Lived**: 5 years (until collapse)
**Cause of Death**: Bloodweb integration test during collapse

Brennan was a Throne soldier—selected for similar profile to Theron. Competent but unimaginative, followed orders precisely. He was connected to partial Bloodweb feed for monitoring, making him dependent.

**Personality**: Professional. Disciplined. Never questioned.

**Last Words**: Unknown—died when Bloodweb failed in the first wave.

**What Seven Knows**: Seven glimpsed Brennan twice, never spoke to him. Brennan's body is likely still somewhere in Vel'Thoras.

---

#### Vessel Nine - Lyssa Thenn
**Created**: Year -5
**Lived**: 5 years (until collapse)
**Cause of Death**: Bloodweb integration test during collapse

Lyssa was a servant from the capital region—earnest, grateful, genuinely believed in vampire civilization. She was happy with her "elevation," which made the researchers uncomfortable.

**Personality**: Trusting. Faithful. Didn't understand why Seven pitied her.

**Last Words**: Unknown—died with Brennan when Bloodweb failed.

**What Seven Knows**: Lyssa represented what believing the lies looked like. Her faith was real, which made her fate worse.

---

#### Vessel Ten - Marcus Vorn
**Created**: Year -5
**Lived**: 5 years (until collapse)
**Cause of Death**: Bloodweb integration test during collapse

Marcus was a convicted murderer from Throne territories—selected from prison. Violent, pragmatic, self-interested. He saw conversion as escape from execution and didn't care about the cost. Actually preferred being a monster.

**Personality**: Dangerous. Restrained more than other Vessels. Grateful for conversion.

**Last Words**: Unknown—died with Brennan and Lyssa when Bloodweb failed.

**What Seven Knows**: Marcus made researchers nervous. His existence proved personality didn't affect physical viability—monsters could survive too.

---

#### Vessel Eleven - "Eleven"
**Created**: Year -3
**Status**: Unknown (possibly survived)

The only other Vessel kept independent from Bloodweb tests. Different from Seven—colder, more calculating. Possible that they killed Sevik. If they survived the collapse, they'd be the only other being like Seven in the world.

**Personality**: Intelligent. Distant. Dangerous?

**Relationship with Seven**: Wary respect. Neither trusted the other completely. Both knew they might be the only survivors.

**What Seven Knows**: Eleven was planning something. They had secrets. They never told Seven what they were.

---

#### Vessel Twelve - "Unborn"
**Created**: Year -1 (incomplete)
**Status**: Never completed

Still in creation chamber when collapse hit. Embryonic stage. Either destroyed in the Wave or still there—frozen in the process of becoming.

**What Seven Knows**: There was supposed to be another one. Someone who never got the chance.

## Seven's Life in the Laboratory

### Year -7 to -6: Creation and Early Development

**The Awakening**:
Seven's first memory: Dorian's face, speaking words he didn't understand yet. Warmth. Light. Sound. Everything at once.

**First Year**:
- Learning language (accelerated by memory implants)
- Basic motor control
- Emotional regulation (mostly failed)
- Introduction to other Vessels (Eight, Nine, Ten created same year)

**First Connection**: Mira. She was there every day. She explained things. She didn't treat him like an experiment.

### Year -6 to -5: Training and Development

**Physical Training** (Krath):
Combat, endurance, pain tolerance. Krath pushed him until he broke, then put him back together stronger. Seven hated him. Seven respected him. Both were true.

**Mental Training** (Dorian):
History, philosophy, strategy. Who vampires were. What they'd built. What was threatening them. Dorian made Seven want to save them.

**Ability Development** (Sevik):
Magical capacity, blood manipulation, enhanced senses. Sevik pushed for more, faster, harder. Accidents happened. Seven learned to distrust ambition.

**The Naming Day** (Year -5):
Not an official event—Krath's idea. He gave Seven a blade and said, "You're not a number. You're a Vessel, and this is yours." First time anyone treated Seven as an individual with possessions, with rights.

### Year -5 to -4: The Testing Begins

**Bloodweb Independence Confirmation**:
Seven was tested repeatedly to ensure he could survive without Bloodweb connection. He could. The tests hurt, but he survived.

**The Decision**:
Vessels Eight, Nine, Ten were scheduled for Bloodweb integration testing—an attempt to make independence reversible. Seven was exempted. Krath argued for it. Velanna agreed. Why?

**Seven's Theory**: They needed at least one guaranteed survivor. He was the insurance's insurance.

**Bright's Theory**: "She likes you. She won't admit it, but you're her favorite."

Seven didn't believe it then. He's not sure now.

### Year -4 to -3: Loss

**The Integration Tests** (Year -4):
Eight, Nine, and Ten died over three days. Seven was forbidden to see them, but he felt them die. The Bloodweb connections briefly linked all Vessels—he experienced their ends.

**Aftermath**:
Seven became withdrawn. Aggressive. Refused to train. Refused to eat. Dorian spent weeks bringing him back.

**Dorian**: "They died for something. You have to live for something. Find out what."

**Seven**: "They died because you made us wrong."

**Dorian**: "Maybe. Then help us make the next ones right."

**Vessel Eleven's Creation** (Year -3):
A new Vessel. Different. Eleven didn't bond with Seven—didn't bond with anyone. They watched. They planned. Seven didn't trust them.

### Year -3 to -1: The Final Years

**Sevik's Death** (Year -3):
Accident during Eleven's training. Equipment failure. Sevik was dead before anyone could help.

**Official Story**: Malfunction.
**Seven's Suspicion**: Eleven killed him.
**Evidence**: None. But Seven saw Eleven's face after. There was nothing there.

**Velanna's Behavior**:
She became more driven. More secretive. Spent hours in the archives. Stopped sleeping. Seven thinks she knew the collapse was coming.

**Dorian's Warning** (Year -2):
"Something's wrong. She's hiding things from us. If anything happens—go to the archive. Lira left something for you."

**The Last Year**:
Tense. Quiet. Everyone waiting for something no one would name.

### Year 0: The End

**Day 0, Hour 3** (Seven's last conscious memory):
Alarms. Red lights. Dorian's voice: "Get to stasis. Now. All Vessels to emergency chambers."

Seven ran. He looked for Bright, Nine, Ten—then remembered. They were gone.

He saw Eleven running toward a different section.

He reached his chamber. The door sealed. The cold came.

Last thought: "I'll find out what happened. I'll find them all. I'll—"

Then nothing for 1,200 years.

## What Seven Carries

### The Names

He knows everyone who died. He says their names sometimes, to remember that they existed.

### The Blade

Krath's gift. Still in stasis with him. The first thing he'll reach for when he wakes.

### The Key

Lira's key to the hidden archive. He needs to find what she left him.

### The Questions

- Why did Velanna save him specifically?
- What was Eleven planning?
- Did anyone else survive?
- What was in the archive?
- Why does he feel like he was meant for something?

### The Debt

Eight, Nine, Ten died so he could live. He owes them something. He doesn't know what yet. But he'll pay it.
