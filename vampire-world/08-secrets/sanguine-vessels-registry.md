# The Sanguine Vessels Registry

Complete record of all twelve Vessels in the Sanguine Vessels Initiative. These were people before they were experiments.

---

## Overview

**Initiative Duration**: Year -12 to Year 0
**Location**: Vel'Thoras Research Complex, Level 5-7
**Lead Researcher**: High Researcher Velanna
**Purpose (Official)**: Create Bloodweb-independent vampires for colonial expansion
**Purpose (Actual)**: Insurance against catastrophic Bloodweb failure

---

## Vessel One

### Pre-Conversion Identity

**Name**: Arren Thorne
**Species**: Human
**Age at Selection**: 24
**Origin**: Valdrian merchant family (minor nobility)
**Selection Reason**: Volunteered for "honor of service"

**Background**
- Third son of a failing merchant house
- Debts mounting, family status declining
- Believed conversion would elevate his family
- Vampires promised his family would be "rewarded"
- They weren't

### The Experiment

**Conversion Date**: Year -37
**Duration**: 12 days
**Outcome**: Death

**What Went Wrong**
- Blood replacement process incomplete
- Original blood clotted during transformation
- Internal hemorrhaging began Day 8
- Dead by Day 12

**Researcher Notes**
- "Subject showed promising initial response"
- "Clotting cascade unstoppable once initiated"
- "Recommend slower blood replacement in future subjects"

### Significance

**To Velanna**: The first failure. Taught her that enthusiasm wasn't enough.

**To the Project**: Established that the conversion process needed fundamental revision.

**Records**: Complete, archived in Vel'Thoras Level 5

## Vessel Four

### Pre-Conversion Identity

**Name**: Theron Ashward
**Species**: Human
**Age at Selection**: 27
**Origin**: Soldier in Covenant of the Crimson Throne forces
**Selection Reason**: Exceptional physical conditioning, psychological resilience

**Background**
- Career soldier, distinguished record
- Selected after showing unusual recovery from wounds
- Told he was being "enhanced for special operations"
- Believed he would serve the Throne in a new capacity
- Didn't know he was transferred to Mind Covenant project

### The Experiment

**Conversion Date**: Year -15
**Duration**: Survived conversion
**Survival Period**: 3 months
**Outcome**: Death

**What Went Wrong**
- Conversion successful, physical form stable
- Blood system functioned independently
- Began showing instability at Month 2
- Blood processing slowly failed
- Internal systems couldn't maintain without Bloodweb
- Declined over three weeks, died Month 3

**Researcher Notes**
- "Longest survival to date"
- "Independence incomplete—system still required external support"
- "Modifications needed to blood processing efficiency"

### Who He Was

**Personality**
- Disciplined, stoic, followed orders
- Struggled with transformation more than expected
- Missed his unit, his purpose
- Asked Velanna: "What am I supposed to be now?"

**His Three Months**
- Trained alongside researchers
- Showed exceptional combat adaptation
- Began to hope he would survive
- Noticed symptoms before researchers did
- Didn't tell them—hoped he was wrong

**His Death**
- Knew it was coming for a week
- Asked to speak to Velanna privately
- Said: "Tell the next ones that the body wants to live. Trust that."
- Died that night

### Significance

**To Velanna**: First Vessel she saw as a person. Changed her approach.

**To the Project**: His advice ("the body wants to live") influenced later protocols.

**To Seven**: Found Theron's service record in Vel'Thoras. Realized Vessels were people with histories.

**Records**: Complete, including personal log (Velanna's private files)

## Vessel Five

### Pre-Conversion Identity

**Name**: Sera (no family name)
**Species**: Human
**Age at Selection**: 22
**Origin**: Orphan from Neth'Korath servant quarters
**Selection Reason**: No family connections, wouldn't be missed

**Background**
- Raised in communal servant housing
- Worked in textile production
- Selected during routine processing
- No one to notice she was gone
- Records simply marked "reassigned"

### The Experiment

**Conversion Date**: Year -12
**Duration**: Survived conversion
**Survival Period**: 6 weeks
**Outcome**: Death

**What Went Wrong**
- Conversion successful
- Blood system initially stable
- Mental breakdown at Week 4
- Couldn't integrate vampire consciousness with human memories
- Violent episodes, self-harm
- Terminated by researchers Week 6

**Researcher Notes**
- "Mental integration failed despite improved protocols"
- "Subject unable to reconcile dual nature"
- "Recommend memory suppression in future subjects"

### Who She Was

**Personality**
- Quiet, observant, compliant
- Never complained during conversion
- Initially adapted well
- Then began to remember her human life with painful clarity

**Her Six Weeks**
- First three weeks: model subject
- Week 4: began talking to someone who wasn't there
- Week 5: refused blood, said it was "poison to who I was"
- Week 6: attacked a researcher, was restrained, terminated

**Her Last Words**
- "I can hear myself dying. The girl I was. She's screaming."

### Significance

**To Velanna**: Decided memory modification was necessary. The human self had to be suppressed.

**To the Project**: Led to implanted memories and identity suppression in future Vessels.

**To Seven**: He has implanted memories because of what happened to Sera.

**Records**: Complete, classified Level 3

## Vessel Six

### Pre-Conversion Identity

**Name**: Corvin Marsh
**Species**: Human
**Age at Selection**: 31
**Origin**: Scholar from Free Cities (captured in raid)
**Selection Reason**: High intelligence, mental adaptability

**Background**
- Academic studying vampire artifacts
- Captured when his expedition got too close to Vel'Thoras
- Initially interrogated for knowledge
- Selected when blood screening showed promise
- Begged to be killed instead

### The Experiment

**Conversion Date**: Year -8
**Duration**: Survived conversion
**Survival Period**: 6 months
**Outcome**: Death

**What Went Wrong**
- Conversion successful
- Blood processing functional
- Physical form stable for 5 months
- Month 6: rapid deterioration
- Blood system failed over 2 weeks
- Died bleeding from every orifice

**Researcher Notes**
- "Longest survival to date"
- "System failure cause unclear"
- "May be inherent limitation of current approach"

### Who He Was

**Personality**
- Brilliant, terrified, never stopped fighting mentally
- Cooperated physically but recorded everything
- Kept a hidden journal scratched into his cell wall
- Believed someone should know what happened here

**His Six Months**
- Learned the facility, the researchers, the project
- Figured out the true purpose (insurance, not colonization)
- Tried to warn Seven once: "They're preparing for the end"
- Seven didn't understand, thought he was delirious

**His Death**
- Seven witnessed it accidentally
- Corvin was seizing, screaming
- His last coherent word was a name: "Elise"

**His Journal**
- Still exists, scratched into stone in his former cell
- Contains his observations about the project
- If Seven finds it, he'll learn the truth about the initiative
- Hidden in Level 6, Cell 14

### Significance

**To Velanna**: So close to success. Pushed her to make final refinements for Seven.

**To Seven**: The Vessel whose death he witnessed. The face he sees in nightmares.

**To the Story**: His journal is a major discoverable secret.

**Records**: Complete, plus hidden journal

## The Pattern

### What Velanna Learned

| Vessel | Lesson |
|--------|--------|
| One | Slow the blood replacement |
| Two | Don't use elves |
| Three | The mind matters |
| Four | The body wants to live |
| Five | Memory must be suppressed |
| Six | So close—refine the process |
| Seven | SUCCESS |

### The Cost

12 people reduced to experimental subjects.

- Some volunteered (believing lies)
- Some were prisoners (no choice)
- Some were servants (treated as property)
- Some were captured (enemies or unlucky)

None of them chose to become what they became.

Seven carries their legacy. Whether he honors it depends on who he becomes.

## Narrative Use

### What Seven Can Learn

**Early**: He was Vessel Seven, there were others, most died

**Four**: The soldier who gave advice that saved Seven

The Vessels were people who became experiments. Seven's journey is about becoming a person again. Their stories show what he came from—and what he's trying to transcend.
