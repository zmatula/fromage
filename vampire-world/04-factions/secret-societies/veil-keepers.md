# The Veil Keepers

A secret society dedicated to preventing vampire return and keeping dangerous knowledge buried. They would destroy the protagonist without hesitation.

---

## Overview

**Full Name**: The Order of the Veil
**Founded**: Year 127
**Headquarters**: None (distributed cells)
**Membership**: ~2,000 active
**Motto**: "Some truths must stay buried"
**Symbol**: A closed eye behind a veil

---

## Origin

### Founding

**Year 127**: Founded by survivors who saw collapse
**Founders**: Scholars, soldiers, clergy who witnessed vampire power
**Purpose**: Ensure vampires never return
**Method**: Destroy dangerous knowledge and artifacts

*Note: This is the official founding story. The true founding occurred in Year 423, during the Silent Years, when the organization was created to DESTROY records of a catastrophic event—not to preserve truth. The name originally meant "Keepers of the Veil" (maintaining the cover-up), not "Keepers behind the Veil" (seeking hidden truth). See [True Origins](true-origins.md).*

### The Original Vision

- Vampires were too powerful to risk return
- Some knowledge is inherently dangerous
- Prevention better than cure
- World better off forgetting

---

## Philosophy

### Core Beliefs

1. **Vampire civilization was genuinely dangerous**
   - Not just demons, but dangerous enough
   - Power without wisdom
   - Would enslave again

2. **Knowledge can be too dangerous**
   - Some things better unknown
   - Curiosity can destroy worlds
   - Ignorance can be safety

3. **Prevention is everything**
   - Once vampires return, too late
   - Must act before, not after
   - Better wrong than dead

### The Debate

**Are They Right?**
- Vampires did nearly destroy world
- Some knowledge IS dangerous
- But also: suppression has costs
- Truth being recovered anyway
- Are they delaying or preventing?

## Organization

### Structure

**The Circle**: 12 senior members, identities secret even to most
**Regional Masters**: ~50, coordinate areas
**Cell Leaders**: ~200, run local operations
**Operatives**: ~1,500, do the work
**Contacts**: Unknown number of assets

### Membership

**Recruitment**:
- Approach promising individuals
- Test loyalty and discretion
- Long vetting process
- Lifetime commitment

**Demographics**:
- Scholars (30%)
- Military/Intelligence (25%)
- Clergy (20%)
- Merchants (15%)
- Other (10%)

### Resources

**Funding**: Ancient endowments, member contributions, state patronage
**Assets**: Safe houses, vaults, weapons
**Influence**: Positions in every major institution
**Knowledge**: Extensive vampire archives (sealed)

### Financial Operations

**Ancient Endowments** (Year 150 foundation):
- Land grants in three empires generate ~100,000 crowns/year
- Managed through front organizations (academies, libraries)
- Principal cannot be touched; income only

**State Patronage**:
- Valdrian Imperial Library: 30,000 crowns (hidden budget line)
- Sanctified Church "historical preservation": 25,000 crowns
- Northern Kingdom intelligence cooperation: 10,000 crowns

**Member Contributions**:
- 2,000 members average 2.5 crowns/year = 5,000 crowns
- Wealthy patrons add ~10,000 crowns in emergencies

**Total Annual Budget**: ~180,000 crowns

**Compared to Rivals**:
- Inheritors: ~250,000 crowns (wealthy patrons, artifact profits)
- Order of Dawn: ~600,000 crowns (state-funded)

**Operational Constraints**:
- Cannot match Inheritor expedition funding
- Must rely on infiltration over force
- Major operations require special fundraising
- Always fighting with limited resources

## Activities

### Primary Mission: Containment

1. **Monitor ruin expeditions**
   - Have operatives in most teams
   - Can redirect, delay, or sabotage
   - Remove dangerous finds

2. **Control dangerous artifacts**
   - Destroy when possible
   - Contain when destruction risky
   - Prevent activation
   - Maintain sealed vaults

3. **Suppress dangerous knowledge**
   - Remove texts from circulation
   - Discredit dangerous theories
   - Assassinate if necessary
   - Maintain "acceptable" history

4. **Watch for vampire activity**
   - Monitor stasis locations
   - Track dimensional readings
   - Investigate survivor rumors
   - Eliminate confirmed threats

### Secondary Mission: Infiltration

- Every major government
- Every major university
- Every major military
- Order of Dawn leadership
- Some Inheritor cells (monitoring)

### Operations

**Year 812**: Destroyed stasis vampire in Southern Catacombs
**Year 880**: Assassinated scholar who discovered transformation methods
**Year 934**: Sabotaged Twilight Border expedition
**Year 990**: Purged member who wanted to go public
**Year 1118**: Seized Living Artifact from Northern Dominion
**Year 1121**: Scholar purge of those close to dangerous truths
