# The Order of the Dawn

A religious military order preparing for vampire return. They would kill the protagonist as a holy duty.

---

## Overview

**Full Name**: The Sacred Order of the Dawn Eternal
**Founded**: Year 234 (monastery), Year 780 (military charter)
**Headquarters**: Dawnhaven, Sanctified States
**Membership**: ~15,000 active knights
**Motto**: "Light eternal against darkness returning"
**Symbol**: Rising sun with sword

---

## Origin

### Founding

**Year 234**: Founded as monastery for those who fought vampires
**Evolution**: Gradually militarized
**Year 780**: Formal military charter from Sanctified States
**Purpose**: Prepare for and prevent vampire return

*Note: This is the official founding story. The true founder, "Aldric the Lightbringer," was not the saint the Order believes. The real origin in Year 631-634 involves patricide, cover-up, and a buried vampire artifact that's still active. See [True Origins](true-origins.md).*

### The Original Vision

- Vampires were demons
- Divine punishment destroyed them
- But evil can return
- Must be prepared to fight again

---

## Philosophy

### Core Beliefs

1. **Vampires were demonic evil**
   - Not just enemies—literally evil
   - Deserved divine punishment
   - Return would be apocalyptic

2. **Divine mission to oppose**
   - Chosen by gods
   - Sacred duty
   - No negotiation with evil
   - Kill without hesitation

3. **Vigilance is eternal**
   - Vampires could return
   - Signs of awakening visible
   - Must always be ready
   - Sacrifice is expected

### Religious Framework

- Church of Divine Light aligned
- Vampires as demons doctrine
- Knights are holy warriors
- Death in service is martyrdom

### The Hypocrisy

**What Leadership Knows**:
- Vampires weren't literally demons
- Divine punishment is myth
- They use vampire artifacts
- They're not entirely honest with faithful

**Why Continue**:
- Threat is still real
- Knights need motivation
- Truth would undermine authority
- Better lies that protect
