# Secret Society Origin Stories

How the Inheritors, Order of Dawn, and Veil Keepers were founded—with their compromises, schisms, and original sins.

---

## The Inheritors of Blood

### The Founding: Year 210

**The Problem**
- Knowledge being destroyed
- Collaborators who understood vampire systems being killed
- Libraries burned as "tainted"
- Truth being replaced by legend

**The Founder**: The Hooded Scholar

**Who They Were**
- Identity never confirmed
- Possibly: escaped scholar from Ironmount
- Possibly: former vampire collaborator in hiding
- Possibly: descendant of Three Scholars (Vessel Six had a theory)
- Wrote the founding charter
- Disappeared after establishing core group

**The Founding Meeting (Year 210)**
- Seven people in a cellar in the Free Cities
- All had knowledge they'd saved
- All were in danger for having it
- They made a pact

**The Original Charter**

> *"Knowledge lost cannot be recovered. Truth suppressed becomes lie. We who know must preserve. We who preserve must hide. We who hide must prepare. The cycle will turn. Someone must be ready."*

---

### The First Century: Survival

**Year 210-310**

**What They Did**
- Gathered artifacts and texts
- Recruited scholars in danger
- Established hidden vaults
- Created coded communication
- Survived

**The Compromises**
- Stole from those who would destroy
- Killed to protect secrets
- Lied constantly
- Recruited people who weren't told everything

**The First Schism: Year 278**

*Three Scholars of Ironmount achieve first translation. Inheritors are divided:*

**Faction A**: Share the knowledge
- "This is what we're FOR"
- "Scholarship serves everyone"
- "Hiding serves no one"

**Faction B**: Protect the knowledge
- "The Church will destroy it"
- "Share and lose control"
- "Not ready yet"

**The Outcome**: Faction B wins. The Scholars' work is preserved but hidden. Varek is murdered. Helena recants. Orin disappears.

*The Inheritors had the knowledge. They let people die to protect it.*

---

### The Middle Centuries: Growth

**Year 310-850**

**What They Became**
- From survival group to organization
- From scholars to power players
- From idealists to pragmatists

**The Network**
- Vaults in every major city
- Agents in every court
- Funding from artifact trade
- Influence through knowledge control

**The Corruption**
- Some leaders used knowledge for personal power
- Some sold secrets to highest bidder
- Some forgot the original purpose
- "Preserve" became "possess"

**The Second Schism: Year 520**

*The Restoration Debate: Should Inheritors try to restore vampire civilization?*

**The Restorationists**
- "Vampire technology could end suffering"
- "We have the knowledge—use it"
- "We could rule better than the kingdoms"

**The Preservationists**
- "Restore what? Slavery?"
- "We preserve—we don't recreate"
- "That way lies becoming what we replaced"

**The Outcome**: Preservationists win. Restorationists expelled. Some become independent—possibly founding groups that would evolve into vampire cults.

*The Inheritors could have changed the world. They chose not to. Were they wise or cowardly?*

---

### The Modern Era: Crisis

**Year 850-1200**

**The Hooded Scholar's Legacy**
- Year 850: New leader takes name "The Hooded Scholar"
- Each leader since uses the title
- Claim spiritual descent from founder
- May be literally descended (blood)

**Year 1124: The Exposure**
- Safe house raided
- Documents captured
- Organization revealed
- Mass arrests

**Director Maren Thorne's Response**
- Emergency protocols
- Destroyed compromised vaults (centuries of work)
- Killed three members who knew too much
- Went deep underground
- Saved the core

*Thorne saved the Inheritors. She also murdered Inheritors. Is that different from what the Church does?*

**The Current State**
- More secretive than ever
- Less idealistic than founders
- More effective than middle period
- Still debating what they're for

---

### The Internal Factions (Current)

**The Academics**
- Preserve and study
- "Knowledge for its own sake"
- Old school, purist, naive

**The Pragmatists**
- Use knowledge for influence
- "Power to protect knowledge"
- Effective, compromised, cynical

**The Restorationists** (secret faction)
- Still want to restore vampire civilization
- Very small, very hidden
- Would use Vessel Seven

**What They Agree On**
- Protect knowledge
- Hide from Church
- Find more artifacts

**What They Fight About**
- What to do with knowledge
- How much to share
- What Vessel Seven means

---

## The Order of Dawn

### The Founding: Year 20

**The Problem**
- Vampire survivors being hunted
- Needed organization, not just mobs
- Purifier bands too chaotic
- Required coordination and doctrine

**The Founder**: Commander Aldric Dawnsbane

**Who He Was**
- Former soldier in vampire auxiliary (under compulsion)
- When collapse freed him, he saw what they'd made him do
- Swore to destroy every vampire
- Meant it

**His Justification**
- "I served them. I know what they were."
- "They were evil. Not complicated—evil."
- "Every vampire alive is a wound in the world."
- "I'll close every wound myself."

**Year 20: The First Hunt**
- 200 survivors gathered
- Aldric led them to a vampire den
- 12 vampires hiding with human collaborators
- Killed them all
- No regrets

---

### The Early Years: The Crusade

**Year 20-87**

**What They Did**
- Systematic vampire hunting
- Killed every vampire they found
- Killed many collaborators
- Killed some who were just suspects

**The Numbers**
- Vampires killed: probably hundreds
- Collaborators killed: probably thousands
- Innocents killed: unknown (substantial)

**The Compromise**
- Had to work with informants (themselves collaborators)
- Had to ignore some to catch others
- Had to make deals with warlords
- Had to become what they hunted

**Aldric's Death (Year 52)**
- Killed hunting a Twilight survivor
- The vampire called him by name
- Said Aldric had served it once
- Aldric froze
- The vampire killed him
- His men killed the vampire

*The founder died because he recognized his former master. He couldn't act. He was still that slave.*

---

### The Transition: From Hunters to Institution

**Year 87-300**

**Year 87: The Last Public Kill**
- Last confirmed vampire execution
- Church declared vampires extinct
- Order needed new purpose

**What They Became**
- From hunters to guardians
- "Protect against supernatural threats"
- "Maintain vigilance"
- "The darkness always returns"

**The Church Integration**
- Year 100: Official Church recognition
- Year 150: Formal doctrine
- Year 200: Hierarchy established
- Lost independence, gained resources

**The First Heresy Hunt (Year 145)**
- No vampires to find
- Started finding "vampire sympathizers"
- The Rationalist scholars
- Burned them instead

*When you can't find enemies, you create them.*

---

### The Middle Period: Expansion

**Year 300-900**

**What They Protected**
- Church interests
- Kingdom borders
- Artifact suppression
- "Public morality"

**What They Became**
- From true believers to bureaucrats
- From hunters to inquisitors
- From protectors to oppressors

**The Internal Debate**
- Some remembered the original mission
- Some preferred the power
- The power won

**Year 780: The Charter**
- Formal royal and Church charter
- Official jurisdiction over "supernatural threats"
- Including: vampires, demons, heretics, cults, scholars
- Very broad definition

**High Inquisitor Mordecai Stern (Year 920)**
- Found "vampire cult" in Northern territories
- Exterminated 200 people
- Were they vampires? Were they scholars? Were they just different?
- He was sure. No one else is.

*The Order killed everyone who might have answered questions about what they were.*

---

### The Modern Era: Power

**Year 900-1200**

**Current Structure**
- Thousands of members
- Every kingdom
- Network, resources, authority
- Parallel to secular power

**Current Leader: High Confessor Brennan**
- Absolute believer
- Convinced vampires are returning
- Preparing for war
- Will not negotiate

**The Reformation Faction (secret)**
- Think Order has lost its way
- Want to return to true mission
- Want to stop hunting scholars
- Very small, very quiet, very endangered

**What They Agree On**
- Vampires are evil
- Vigilance is necessary
- The Order has authority

**What They Fight About**
- What counts as "supernatural threat"
- How much force is justified
- What to do about the Scar
- Whether Brennan has gone too far

---

### The Original Sin

**The Founder's Compromise**
- Aldric knew collaborators. He was one.
- He used them while condemning them
- He killed to prove he wasn't one of them
- The Order was built on self-hatred

**What It Means**
- The most zealous were often the most compromised
- The Order hunts what it fears in itself
- Every inquisitor wonders if they would have served
- That fear makes them merciless

## The Veil Keepers

### The Founding: Year 1050

**The Problem**
- Inheritors had become power players
- Order of Dawn was oppressive
- Both manipulated truth
- Both had lost original purpose

**The Founder**: Archivist Sera Veil

**Who She Was**
- Ironpeak scholar
- Discovered Inheritors were manipulating her research
- Discovered Order had destroyed documents she needed
- Decided both were the problem

**Her Philosophy**
> *"The Inheritors hoard truth. The Order destroys truth. Both serve themselves. Someone must serve the truth itself."*

**Year 1050: The First Circle**
- Nine scholars in Ironpeak
- All betrayed by Inheritors or Order
- All committed to transparency
- All willing to steal for it

---

### The Mission: Transparency

**What They Believe**
- Truth should be free
- Powerful lies are dangerous
- Both secrets and suppression are wrong
- People deserve to know

**What They Do**
- Steal secrets from Inheritors
- Steal suppressed documents from Order
- Publish anonymously
- Destabilize both

**The Methods**
- Infiltration (agents in both organizations)
- Theft (break in, copy, get out)
- Publication (pamphlets, academic papers)
- Sabotage (expose operations, embarrass leaders)

---

### The Early Years: Idealism

**Year 1050-1100**

**The Successes**
- Published several Inheritor documents
- Exposed Order coverups
- Gained reputation for accuracy
- Recruited idealists

**The Problem**
- Publishing truth got people killed
- Exposure led to crackdowns
- Some truths destabilized societies
- "Transparency" had costs

**The First Crisis: Year 1075**

*Veil Keepers published evidence of Valdrian royal family's collaborator past. Result: succession crisis, civil unrest, 200 dead.*

**The Debate**
- Were they responsible?
- "We just told the truth"
- "People died because of what we told"
- "Would silence have been better?"

---

### The Middle Period: Compromise

**Year 1100-1180**

**The Schism: Year 1121**

*The Purge of Year 1121: Veil Keepers published Inheritor vault locations. Order raided them. Inheritors blamed Veil Keepers. Some Veil Keepers thought this was wrong.*

**The Absolutists**
- "All truth must be free"
- "If Inheritors die, that's their fault for hiding"
- "We owe nothing to secret-keepers"

**The Pragmatists**
- "We just helped the Order oppress"
- "Some secrets protect people"
- "Transparency isn't the only value"

**The Outcome**: Absolutists win. Pragmatists expelled or silenced. Veil Keepers become more aggressive.

*They exposed Inheritors to the Order. They became a weapon for the people they claimed to oppose.*

---

### The Modern Era: Radicalization

**Year 1180-1200**

**Current Leader**: The Chronicler (name unknown)
- Absolutist
- Aggressive
- Views both Inheritors and Order as enemies
- Will expose both regardless of consequences

**Current Operations**
- Infiltrated both organizations
- Planning major exposures
- Stockpiling secrets
- Waiting for moment

**The Problem**
- They've become what they opposed
- They decide what truth serves
- They have power without accountability
- Who watches the watchers?

---

### The Original Sin

**Sera Veil's Compromise**
- She stole from the Inheritors using their own methods
- She attacked the Order using their own tactics
- She became a secret society opposing secret societies
- The contradiction was built in

**What It Means**
- "Transparency" from the shadows
- "Truth" selected by the Veil Keepers
- "Freedom" enforced by manipulation
- They're not lying—but they're not what they claim

## The Relationships

### Inheritors vs Order of Dawn

**How They See Each Other**

*Inheritors on Order*: "Thugs burning what they don't understand"

*Order on Inheritors*: "Heretics hoarding demonic knowledge"

**The Conflict**
- Centuries of opposition
- Order wants to destroy what Inheritors preserve
- Inheritors want to survive Order's hunts
- No peace possible

### Inheritors vs Veil Keepers

**How They See Each Other**

*Inheritors on Veil Keepers*: "Reckless idealists who'll get us all killed"

*Veil Keepers on Inheritors*: "Hypocrites hoarding for power"

**The Conflict**
- Veil Keepers expose Inheritor secrets
- Inheritors have killed Veil Keepers for it
- Deep mutual hatred
- Used to be the same people

### Order of Dawn vs Veil Keepers

**How They See Each Other**

*Order on Veil Keepers*: "Dangerous provocateurs"

*Veil Keepers on Order*: "Oppressors who fear truth"

**The Conflict**
- Veil Keepers expose Order operations
- Order hunts Veil Keepers as heretics
- But sometimes Veil Keepers give Order information on Inheritors
- The enemy of my enemy is still my enemy

## What Vessel Seven Means to Each

### Inheritors

**Opportunity**
- Living source of vampire knowledge
- Could answer questions they've had for centuries
- Could validate their purpose
- Could help achieve goals (depending on faction)

**Risk**
- He might destroy them
- He might reveal things they'd rather hide
- He might choose someone else

### Order of Dawn

**Threat**
- Everything they exist to destroy
- Validation of their paranoia
- Chance to prove they're still needed

**Mission**
- Destroy him
- No questions
- No hesitation

### Veil Keepers

**Resource**
- He knows truths everyone hides
- Publishing what he knows would change everything
- Could destroy both Inheritors and Order's narratives

**Risk**
- They might be destroyed for exposing him
- He might have truths that shouldn't be told
- He might not cooperate
