# The Inheritors

A secret society dedicated to recovering and using vampire knowledge. They would capture the protagonist and study him—for better or worse.

---

## Overview

**Full Name**: The Society of Inheritors
**Founded**: Year 189
**Headquarters**: Hidden (believed Maritime Republic)
**Membership**: ~1,500 active
**Motto**: "Knowledge is the true inheritance"
**Symbol**: An open hand receiving light

---

## Origin

### Founding

**Year 189**: Founded by scholars studying vampire ruins
**Founders**: Researchers who saw potential, not just danger
**Purpose**: Recover vampire knowledge for betterment
**Belief**: Races should inherit what vampires left

### The Original Vision

- Vampire knowledge can elevate all races
- Suppression wastes precious legacy
- Understanding prevents repetition
- Progress requires building on past

---

## Philosophy

### Core Beliefs

1. **Knowledge should be recovered**
   - Vampires achieved great things
   - Shouldn't be lost forever
   - Can be used responsibly
   - Suppression is the real danger

2. **Races can transcend vampire limitations**
   - We can use without becoming
   - Ethics can guide application
   - Power with wisdom is possible
   - Legacy without enslavement

3. **The world needs vampire knowledge**
   - To prepare for future threats
   - To advance civilization
   - To understand our history
   - To be complete

### The Debate

**Are They Right?**
- Knowledge IS being lost
- Some could help world
- But also: some knowledge IS dangerous
- Vampires couldn't control it
- Why would others do better?

---

## Activities

### Primary Mission: Recovery

1. **Fund and organize expeditions**
   - To unexplored ruins
   - Better equipped than most
   - Recover everything, not just valuable

2. **Acquire artifacts**
   - Purchase when possible
   - Steal when necessary
   - Compete with all factions
   - Largest private collection

3. **Study and experiment**
   - Translate vampire texts
   - Activate artifacts carefully
   - Understand underlying principles
   - Push boundaries

4. **Preserve and share (internally)**
   - Maintain extensive archives
   - Train new scholars
   - Build on predecessors' work
   - Eventually share with world (plan)

### Secondary Mission: Undermining Suppressors

- Counter Veil Keeper operations
- Rescue scholars targeted for elimination
- Steal from Veil Keeper vaults
- Discredit suppression narratives
- Expose hypocrisy

### Notable Achievements

**Year 760**: Established first artifact vault
**Year 815**: First complete vampire text translation
**Year 850**: Founded three universities (secretly)
**Year 920**: Grammar of vampire language reconstructed
**Year 940**: Recovered blood codex with transformation rituals
**Year 1020**: Attempted artifact reactivation (failed but learned)
**Year 1175**: Twilight sanctuary expedition (disaster)
**Year 1180**: Acquired awakening protocols
