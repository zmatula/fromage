## Secondary Power Relationships

### Ironpeak Confederation

**With All Empires**: Essential neutral partner
- Sells weapons and banking to all
- Cannot afford to take sides
- Has leverage through debts
- Everyone needs them

**With Silverwood**: Ancient respect, possible communication

**With Broken Lands**: Black market trade exists

### Silverwood Domain

**With All Powers**: No contact since Year 1050
- Closed borders
- No explanation
- Preparing for something
- Unknown intentions

**With Ironpeak**: May still communicate

### Broken Lands

**With Human Empires**: Active hostility
- Raids continue
- Goblins marginalized
- Humans want extermination
- War of survival

**With Non-Human Powers**: Unknown, possibly old connections

## Trade Dependencies

### Food

**Producers**: Sanctified States, Valdrian Empire
**Consumers**: Northern Dominion, Maritime Republic, Ironpeak
**Leverage**: Sanctified could starve others

### Weapons

**Producer**: Ironpeak Confederation
**Consumers**: All empires
**Leverage**: Dwarves arm everyone

### Luxury/Artifacts

**Center**: Maritime Republic
**Consumers**: All powers
**Leverage**: Control of information and markets

### Banking

**Centers**: Ironpeak Confederation, Maritime Republic
**Consumers**: All empires
**Leverage**: Debt gives control

## Alliance Blocs

### Traditional Bloc

**Members**: Valdrian Empire, Sanctified States
**Basis**: Religious ties, conservative values
**Against**: Northern aggression, Maritime commerce
**Strength**: Population, legitimacy
**Weakness**: Internal differences on tolerance

### Pragmatic Bloc

**Members**: Northern Dominion, Maritime Republic (loose)
**Basis**: Trade, artifact use
**Against**: Valdrian-Sanctified dominance
**Strength**: Military, commerce
**Weakness**: Not actually allies

### Neutral Powers

**Members**: Ironpeak Confederation
**Position**: Sell to everyone
**Leverage**: Essential to all
**Danger**: Being forced to choose

### Isolated

**Members**: Silverwood Domain
**Position**: Complete isolation
**Question**: What will they do?

### Marginalized

**Members**: Broken Lands
**Position**: Enemy to all human powers
**Future**: Survival war

## Flashpoints

### Immediate (Within 5 Years)

1. **Valdrian-Northern War**
   - Border militarized
   - Aggressive Northern king
   - Valdrian succession crisis
   - War likely within years

2. **Valdrian Succession**
   - Emperor aging without heir
   - Three factions
   - Could trigger civil war
   - Others would intervene

3. **Sanctified Persecution**
   - Increasing refugees
   - Magic users fleeing
   - Non-humans expelled
   - Could trigger responses

### Medium-Term (5-20 Years)

1. **Silverwood Action**
   - 150 years of silence
   - Preparing for something
   - Will act eventually
   - Unknown target

2. **Goblin War**
   - Raids escalating
   - Humans want final solution
   - Goblins preparing
   - Full war possible

3. **Artifact Arms Race**
   - Powers competing
   - Dangerous activations
   - Could trigger disasters

   - Changes everything
   - Alliances shift
   - Religious crisis
   - Power scramble

   - Readings increasing
   - Unknown cause

## Who Would Ally Against Whom

### If Valdrian-Northern War

**Valdrian Side**: Sanctified States (alliance), possibly Maritime (commerce)
**Northern Side**: No formal allies, dwarven weapons
**Neutral**: Ironpeak (sells to both)

### If Protagonist Revealed

**Destroy**: Sanctified States, Order of Dawn, Veil Keepers
**Capture/Study**: Inheritors, Maritime scholars
**Use as Weapon**: Northern Dominion, possibly goblins
**Uncertain**: Valdrian (divided), Ironpeak (calculating)
**Unknown**: Silverwood

### If Elves Act

**Against Elves**: Probably no one
**With Elves**: Unknown
**Outcome**: Elves would win anything they tried

### If Goblins Unite

**Against Goblins**: All human powers
**With Goblins**: No one openly
**Outcome**: Goblin destruction likely

## How Factions Would React to Each Other's Actions

### If One Power Harbors Protagonist

**Other Powers**: Demand surrender or war
**Secret Societies**: Infiltrate and act independently
**Result**: Harbor power becomes target

### If Inheritors Capture Protagonist

**Veil Keepers**: Assault to kill him
**Order of Dawn**: Crusade against Inheritors
**Powers**: Demand handover
**Result**: War over him

### If Order of Dawn Kills Protagonist

**Inheritors**: Devastated, seek revenge
**Veil Keepers**: Satisfied
**Powers**: Debate whether he was real
**Result**: Tensions but stability

### If Protagonist Escapes Everyone

**All Factions**: Hunt continues
**Result**: No peace for him

## The Web of Intrigue

### Everyone Lies

- Valdrians lie about reform intentions
- Northern lies about peaceful goals
- Sanctified lies about tolerance
- Maritime lies about neutrality
- Ironpeak lies about what they know
- All secret societies lie about everything

### Everyone Spies

- Every power has agents in others
- Secret societies infiltrate everyone
- No one trusts anyone
- Information is power

### Everyone Plans

- Valdris plans succession control
- North plans war
- Sanctified plans purification
- Maritime plans profit
- Ironpeak plans survival
- Societies plan for protagonist

### The Catalyst

**Protagonist's awakening will**:
- Disrupt all plans
- Force choices
- Reveal secrets
- Change alliances
- Make some things better
- Make many things worse
