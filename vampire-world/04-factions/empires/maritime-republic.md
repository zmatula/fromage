# The Maritime Republic

A merchant oligarchy ruling the seas. The most cosmopolitan power, center of artifact trade, and home to the best scholars not under religious control.

---

## Overview

**Full Name**: The Free Maritime Republic of the Sovereign Seas
**Founded**: Year 267 (Free Cities League), Year 489 (unified Republic)
**Capital**: Port Sovereign
**Population**: ~3 million
**Dominant Race**: Human (60%), mixed minorities (40%)
**Government**: Merchant oligarchy with elected First Merchant
**Current Ruler**: First Merchant Corus IX (age 48, elected Year 1199)

---

## Territory

**Geography**:
- Coastal cities and island chains
- Mediterranean climate
- Natural harbors
- Limited hinterland
- Controls key straits and sea lanes

**Major Cities**:
- **Port Sovereign** (capital, ~350,000): Greatest port, seat of Trade Council
- **Scholar's Haven** (~120,000): University city, research center
- **Merchant's Rest** (~100,000): Banking center
- **Tidegate** (~80,000): Naval headquarters
- **Island Jewel** (~60,000): Luxury goods, entertainment

**Strategic Resources**:
- Naval supremacy
- Trade route control
- Artifact market dominance
- Intellectual capital (universities)
- Cosmopolitan population

---

## Government Structure

### The First Merchant

**Selection**:
- Elected by Trade Council
- 5-year terms (can be reelected)
- Must be Trade Council member

**Powers**:
- Executes Council decisions
- Foreign policy representative
- Naval commander in emergencies
- Proposes legislation

**Limitations**:
- Council can override any decision
- Cannot declare war alone
- Must maintain Council support
- Can be removed mid-term

### The Trade Council

**Composition**:
- 100 members from richest merchant families
- 50 guild representatives
- 30 ship captains
- 20 scholars

**Powers**:
- All legislative authority
- Control taxation and spending
- Elect and remove First Merchant
- Declare war and peace

### Current Political Factions

**Old Families**:
- Traditional merchant aristocracy
- Conservative, protectionist
- Led by Merchant Prince Caspian Goldwave

**New Money**:
- Aggressive traders, younger generation
- Expansionist, innovative
- Led by Trader Captain Mirena Swiftwind

**Scholars' Bloc**:
- Protect academic freedom
- Push for artifact research
- Led by Dean Aldric Penwright

## Current Ruler: First Merchant Corus IX

### Personal History

**Birth**: Year 1152, son of shipping magnate
**Career**: Built trading empire, expanded Eastern routes
**Election**: Year 1199, after internal coup
**Character**: Pragmatic, calculating, uncertain

### Personality

**Strengths**:
- Business genius
- Pragmatic negotiator
- Understands value
- Broad perspective

**Weaknesses**:
- No military experience
- Distrusted by old families
- Came to power through coup
- Legitimacy questioned

### The Year 1199 Coup

- Previous First Merchant died suddenly (poisoned?)
- Emergency election amid crisis
- Corus won narrow victory
- Old Families claim fraud
- Corus suspects assassination plot against him

### Goals

**Public**: Restore stability, expand trade
**Private**: Consolidate power, survive
**Challenge**: Build legitimacy while watching for knives

## Economy

### Economic Base

**Trade**: Primary activity; controls sea lanes
**Banking**: Major financial center (with dwarves)
**Manufacturing**: Luxury goods, ships
**Artifact Market**: Largest legitimate trade

### Wealth Sources

- Trade tariffs
- Banking fees
- Market taxes
- Ship licensing
- Artifact commissions

### Artifact Trade

- Largest legitimate market
- Experts verify authenticity
- Prices set by auction
- Black market also thrives
- Information flows freely (for price)

### Current Issues

- Trade war with Valdris hurting profits
- Corus's legitimacy affecting confidence
- Piracy resurgence
- Class tensions (wealth gap)

## Banking House Coup: Economic Consequences

### Immediate Costs (Year 1199)
- Coup funding: ~300,000 crowns (military bribes, operations)
- Council treasury seized: 150,000 crowns recovered
- Net cost to Consortium: 150,000 crowns

### Revenue Crisis
- Pre-coup tariff collection: ~200,000 crowns/year
- Post-coup collection: ~120,000 crowns/year (merchants refusing)
- Gap: 80,000 crowns/year lost
- **Problem**: No legitimacy = no compliance

### Merchant Confidence
- Trade volume down 15% (uncertainty)
- Insurance rates up 25% (political risk)
- Capital flight: ~100,000 crowns moved to Valdrian banks
- Foreign investment frozen

### Counter-Coup Funding
- Captain Stormwind raising funds from:
  - Old families: ~150,000 crowns pledged
  - Merchant houses wanting order restored: ~100,000 crowns
  - Free Captains (conditional): ~50,000 crowns
- **Total war chest**: ~300,000 crowns (enough to challenge)

### Consortium Vulnerability
- Current liquid assets: ~500,000 crowns (down from 10 million in total assets)
- Monthly burn rate: ~50,000 crowns (military, administration)
- **Runway**: 10 months before fiscal crisis
- **Desperate Moves**: May need to foreclose debts early, sell artifacts, seek foreign backing

## Culture

### Values

- **Wealth**: Money is power and respect
- **Freedom**: Personal liberty (for those who can afford it)
- **Pragmatism**: Whatever works
- **Knowledge**: Information is valuable
- **Tolerance**: All welcome (if they pay)

### Religion

**Diverse**:
- Church of Divine Light present but not dominant
- All faiths tolerated
- No religious restrictions on trade
- Pragmatic secularism

### View of Vampire History

**Scholarly**: Best research, most nuanced view
**Popular**: Artifacts are trade goods
**Practical**: Don't care about theology; care about value

### Relationship with Ruins

- Fund expeditions for profit share
- Study artifacts for market value
- Publish findings (for subscription)
- No religious restrictions
- Everything has a price

## Foreign Relations

### Valdrian Empire

**Status**: Economic rivalry
**Issues**: Trade wars, tariffs, commercial espionage
**Cooperation**: Cultural exchange, scholarly ties
**Secret**: Many Valdrian nobles invested in Maritime trade

### Northern Dominion

**Status**: Profitable trading partner
**Issues**: Compete for artifact market
**Cooperation**: Naval technology, weapons trade
**Secret**: Sell to both sides in any conflict

### Sanctified States

**Status**: Necessary commerce
**Issues**: Sanctified persecution pushes refugees here
**Cooperation**: Food imports
**Secret**: Shelter Sanctified dissidents

### Ironpeak Confederation

**Status**: Banking partnership
**Issues**: Compete for financial business
**Cooperation**: Joint ventures, information sharing
**Secret**: Dwarves have leverage through debts

### Silverwood Domain

**Status**: Rare trade
**Issues**: Elves barely engage
**Value**: Elven goods extremely valuable when available
**Secret**: Some merchants have elven contacts

### Broken Lands

**Status**: Unofficial trade
**Issues**: Officially embargoed
**Reality**: Black market trade in goblin goods
**Secret**: Some goblin artifacts most valuable
