# Church of Divine Light: Internal Conflicts

The faithful are divided. Moderates against militants, scholars against zealots, and everyone hiding what they found in the sealed archives.

---

## The Public Unity

The Church of Divine Light presents itself as unified—one doctrine, one hierarchy, one purpose. Behind that facade:
- Three major factions fighting for control
- A sealed prophecy that could split the Church
- Secret research that contradicts official doctrine
- Clergy who help vampires while preaching against them

The Church is at war with itself. The congregation doesn't know.

---

## The Three Factions

### The Shepherd Faction (Moderates)

**Philosophy**: "Guide the flock through compassion, not fear."

**Leadership**: Cardinal Thorne (age 67), former missionary, beloved by commoners.

**Beliefs**:
- The Church should serve people, not rule them
- Vampires were a test from God, not pure evil
- Healing and charity matter more than doctrinal purity
- The Inquisition has gone too far

**Membership**: ~40% of clergy, strongest in rural areas and among lower ranks.

**Political Position**:
- Support Prince Marcus for Emperor (he's genuinely faithful)
- Oppose military expansion
- Want reconciliation with elves and dwarves
- Skeptical of Order of Dawn violence

**The Danger They Represent**: If they gain control, Church political power decreases. Kingdoms would lose their religious legitimacy for war.

### The Sword Faction (Militants)

**Philosophy**: "Evil must be cut out before it spreads."

**Leadership**: Cardinal Valorian (age 54), former soldier, brother of Duke Valorian.

**Beliefs**:
- Vampires were absolute evil; any remnant must be destroyed
- The Church must be strong or be destroyed
- Charity weakens; discipline strengthens
- The Inquisition isn't harsh enough

**Membership**: ~35% of clergy, strongest in military orders and Inquisition.

**Political Position**:
- Support whoever supports the Church (currently undecided)
- Favor military expansion to spread the faith
- Want crusade against The Scar
- Fund the Order of Dawn directly

**The Danger They Represent**: If they gain control, religious wars begin. Non-humans become targets. The Scar expedition becomes a death march.

### The Scroll Faction (Scholars)

**Philosophy**: "Truth serves God better than ignorance."

**Leadership**: Cardinal Ashford (age 72), former archivist, knows too many secrets.

**Beliefs**:
- The Church has hidden too much
- Some vampire knowledge could help humanity
- Doctrine should adapt to evidence
- The sealed archives should be opened

**Membership**: ~25% of clergy, strongest in academies and libraries.

**Political Position**:
- No strong imperial candidate preference
- Want alliance with Valdrian Academy
- Quietly sympathetic to Inheritors
- Oppose the Order of Dawn's destruction of artifacts

**The Danger They Represent**: If they gain control, Church secrets emerge. Doctrine becomes debatable. The Church's authority over truth collapses.

---

## The Current Balance

**The Supreme Pontiff** is dying—the same illness that's killing Emperor Aldric IV. He's been Shepherd faction his whole career, but he's too weak to control the Church.

**Power distribution**:
- Sword controls the Inquisition and military orders
- Shepherd controls rural parishes and charity
- Scroll controls archives and education

**The coming election**:
When the Pontiff dies, the three Cardinals will compete. Each has enough support to block the others but not enough to win.

**The compromise**: Whoever can offer the most to the other two wins. Cardinal Thorne could offer moral authority. Cardinal Valorian could offer military protection. Cardinal Ashford could offer... secrets.

And he has them.

## The Sealed Prophecy

### What It Is

In Year 89, the same year the Inheritors found Sel'Naroth, a Church visionary named Sister Helena had a revelation. She spoke for three days without pause, and scribes recorded every word.

Then the Church sealed the prophecy and forbade discussion.

### What It Says (Fragments)

The full text is in the sealed archives. Fragments have leaked:

> "The blood will wake when the star falls. Seven times seven, and Seven remains."

> "The wound that will not heal will speak, and its words will split the faithful."

> "When the sleeper opens eyes, the light will divide. Brother against brother, shepherd against sword."

> "The old covenant returns. Those who served will be served. Those who ruled will kneel."

> "In the Year of the Opening, choose: burn what rises, or rise with what burns."

### Interpretations

**Shepherd interpretation**: Seven is a test of compassion. How we treat him shows what we truly believe. The prophecy warns against violence.

**Sword interpretation**: Seven is the threat. "Burn what rises" means destroy him before he destroys us. The prophecy commands action.

**Scroll interpretation**: The prophecy isn't about Seven—it's about the Church. "Brother against brother" means schism. We need to understand before we act.

### Why It's Sealed

The prophecy predicts Church division. If it's widely known:
- Factions will use it as justification
- People will see schism as inevitable
- Self-fulfilling prophecy becomes likely

The Pontiff sealed it to prevent what it predicted. But the Cardinals all know it. They're already positioning for the split.

### The Year of the Opening

The prophecy references "the Year of the Opening." Scholars debate:
- Year 812 (first stasis vampire found)?
- Year 1200 (Seven's awakening)?
- Some future date?

If Year 1200 is the Opening, the prophecy becomes immediately relevant. The choice—"burn what rises, or rise with what burns"—must be made now.

## The Secret Research

### The Archive Discovery

In Year 1178, Scroll faction archivists cataloging the sealed archives found something that shouldn't exist: **vampire theological texts**.

Not texts about vampires—texts by vampires. About their gods, their creation myths, their understanding of divinity.

### What the Texts Show

**Vampire Creation Myth**:
Vampires believed they were created by a god called "The First Blood"—a being that predates human gods. They weren't damned humans; they were a separate creation.

**Vampire Theology**:
Vampires had their own priests, their own rituals, their own understanding of the afterlife. They didn't worship the Divine Light—but they did worship *something*.

**The Implication**:
If vampires had a legitimate religion, the Church's doctrine that vampires were "soulless abominations" is wrong. They had souls. They had gods. They had faith.

They weren't evil by nature. They were people.

### Faction Responses

**Shepherds** (those who know): This proves vampires were people. We need to reconsider our doctrine.

**Swords** (those who know): This is corruption. The texts are lies designed to deceive. Destroy them.

**Scrolls** (those who know): This is truth. We need to study it, understand it, integrate it.

### Who Knows

- Cardinal Ashford (Scroll leader): Found it, studied it, kept it secret
- Cardinal Thorne (Shepherd leader): Was told, is troubled, prays for guidance
- Cardinal Valorian (Sword leader): Suspects something, doesn't know what
- Three senior archivists: Sworn to secrecy

If this becomes public:
- Church doctrine on vampires collapses
- Order of Dawn loses justification
- Seven becomes a person, not a monster

Cardinal Ashford is using this knowledge as leverage. He hasn't decided how yet.

## The Clergy Who Help

### The Phenomenon

Despite official doctrine, some clergy quietly assist vampire research, protect vampire artifacts, and would likely help a vampire directly.

Why?

### Brother Marcus Lightward

**Position**: Rural priest, Eastern Valdris
**Secret**: He has vampire blood. His grandmother was turned, then cured through a ritual that wasn't supposed to work. The blood persists, diluted.
**What he does**: Protects vampire artifacts that come through his parish. Sends them to scholars instead of Inquisition.
**What he wants**: To understand what he is. To know if he's damned.

### Sister Cordelia Ashford

**Position**: Archivist, Cathedral of Light (Cardinal Ashford's niece)
**Secret**: She's been copying sealed texts and sending them to the Inheritors.
**What she does**: Intelligence pipeline between Church scholars and Inheritors.
**What she wants**: Truth preserved, whatever it is. She doesn't trust the Church to keep it.

*Note: Not to be confused with Helena Ashford, the Veil Keeper archivist descended from Scholar Merik Ashford. The Ashford name is common in scholarly circles.*

### Father Aldric Valorian

**Position**: Inquisitor (Cardinal Valorian's son—yes, the militant has a traitor son)
**Secret**: He's seen what the Inquisition does. He's horrified.
**What he does**: Warns targets before raids. Has saved dozens.
**What he wants**: To be the priest his father never was. To protect instead of destroy.

### The Network

These aren't isolated individuals. There's a quiet network—maybe fifty clergy—who share information, protect targets, and wait.

They call themselves the **Shepherds of Shadow**. The name is ironic: they use Shadow (Twilight covenant term) because they protect from darkness by walking in it.

If Seven awakens and needs Church help, they'll be there.

## The Inquisition Problem

### What It's Become

The Inquisition was founded to identify vampire corruption. Now it:
- Tortures suspects
- Executes without trial
- Seizes property for the Church
- Terrorizes anyone who questions doctrine

It's become the Sword faction's private army.

### The Excesses

**The Ashford Purge** (Year 1187): Entire family executed for "vampire sympathy." Evidence: a great-grandmother had bought vampire art. The family had no idea. Property seized.

**The Academy Trials** (Year 1190): Twelve scholars tortured for "forbidden research." Research: historical study of vampire agriculture. Three died. The rest recanted.

**The Nightly Raids** (ongoing): Random searches of homes, businesses, churches. Looking for vampire artifacts. Finding excuses to seize wealth.

### Shepherd Opposition

Cardinal Thorne has been trying to restrict the Inquisition for twenty years. Cardinal Valorian has blocked every attempt.

**The standoff**: Thorne can't disband the Inquisition without Valorian's faction rebelling. Valorian can't expand the Inquisition without Thorne's faction rebelling.

The Pontiff has maintained this balance. When he dies, it breaks.

## The Coming Schism

### How It Happens

The Pontiff dies → Election deadlocks → Factions refuse compromise → One faction breaks away.

**If Swords break**: Military orders declare independent authority. Crusade against The Scar proceeds without Church approval. Religious civil war.

**If Shepherds break**: Rural parishes stop paying Church tithes. Local faith continues without central authority. Church fragments.

**If Scrolls break**: Archives are opened or hidden. Truth either floods out or disappears forever. Church loses control of history.

### Seven's Role

Seven's awakening forces the choice the prophecy predicted.

**If Seven is peaceful**: Shepherds vindicated. Swords discredited. Scrolls get to study him.

**If Seven is violent**: Swords vindicated. Shepherds discredited. Scrolls blamed for not destroying texts.

**If Seven is complex**: Everyone claims their interpretation was right. Schism guaranteed.

The worst case: Seven meets different factions separately. Shepherds see compassion. Swords see evil. Scrolls see knowledge. Each faction believes they know the truth. None do. All act on their belief.

## The Election Timeline

### Current Status

Pontiff has months to live. Cardinals are already campaigning.

**Cardinal Thorne** (Shepherd): Promising reform, charity expansion, Inquisition restrictions. Popular with commoners, distrusted by nobles.

**Cardinal Valorian** (Sword): Promising strength, purity, crusade. Popular with military, distrusted by scholars.

**Cardinal Ashford** (Scroll): Promising wisdom, truth, careful study. Popular with academics, distrusted by everyone else.

### The Kingmaker

None can win alone. Someone must compromise.

**Most likely**: Ashford supports either Thorne or Valorian in exchange for concessions. He wants archive access and research freedom. Both might give it.

**Dark horse**: An outside candidate—a younger Cardinal, a famous priest—emerges as compromise. But who?

**Wildcard**: Seven awakens during the election. Everything changes.

### The Timing

If Seven awakens before the election:
- His existence becomes the central issue
- Whoever handles him correctly wins
- Handling him "correctly" means different things to each faction

If Seven awakens after:
- New Pontiff sets policy
- Church response is unified (even if wrong)
- Less chaos, more coordinated action

The Inheritors know this. They've been trying to time Seven's awakening. They want him awake during the election.

## Implications for the Story

### Seven's Church Encounters

When Seven meets Church representatives, he might encounter:
- A Shepherd who wants to help
- A Sword who wants to kill
- A Scroll who wants to study
- A Shepherd of Shadow who's been waiting for him

Different encounters, different impressions. The Church isn't one thing—it's three things fighting.

### The Prophecy Becomes Public

If the sealed prophecy leaks:
- Factions stop cooperating entirely
- People take sides based on interpretation
- "Burn what rises" becomes a rallying cry

Seven should hope it stays sealed. But Cardinal Ashford might leak it as a weapon.

### The Research Goes Public

If the vampire theological texts are revealed:
- Church doctrine on vampires collapses
- Seven becomes sympathetic figure (he had faith, once)
- Order of Dawn loses mandate

Seven should hope it goes public. But Cardinal Valorian will do anything to prevent it.
