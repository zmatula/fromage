# The Broken Lands Confederation

The goblin territories. Marginalized, desperate, and hiding secrets about vampire blood magic that could change everything.

---

## Overview

**Full Name**: The Confederation of the Broken Lands
**Founded**: Year 687 (after Goblin Wars)
**Capital**: None (mobile leadership)
**Population**: ~800,000
**Dominant Race**: Goblin (100%)
**Government**: Tribal confederation with rotating leadership
**Current Ruler**: War Chief Skarva Bloodtooth (age 41, since Year 1195)

---

## Territory

**Geography**:
- Marginal wastelands
- Badlands, swamps, deep caves
- Resource-poor surface
- Rich underground
- Borders patrolled by enemies

**Major Settlements**:
- **Scattered tribal camps** (surface)
- **Deep warrens** (underground, hidden)
- **Sacred places** (shaman sites)
- **Raid staging points** (border)

**Strategic Resources**:
- Underground networks
- Blood magic tradition
- Knowledge of vampire ruins
- Desperation (nothing to lose)

---

## Government Structure

### The War Chief

**Selection**:
- Elected by tribal council
- Usually strongest/most successful leader
- Can be challenged at any time

**Powers**:
- Commands raids
- Negotiates for confederation
- Coordinates tribal actions
- Mediates disputes

**Limitations**:
- Cannot command individual tribes
- Can be replaced by challenge
- Must maintain success to keep support
- No hereditary succession

### The Tribal Council

**Composition**:
- Chiefs of ~40 major tribes
- Each speaks for their people
- Votes by acclamation

**Powers**:
- Elect War Chief
- Decide war and peace
- Major resource allocation
- Interpret traditions

## The Goblin Wars

### What Happened

**Year 412-687**: On-and-off warfare
**Year 650-687**: Final campaigns
**Result**: Goblins pushed to marginal lands
**Reality**: Ethnic cleansing/genocide

### Human Version

"Goblins invaded; we defended ourselves"

### Goblin Version

"Humans invaded; we fought for survival"

### Truth

Both expanding, mutual atrocities, goblins lost because outnumbered

### Impact

## Border Culture

### The Marcher Humans

Humans who live on the goblin frontier have absorbed goblin influences whether they admit it or not:

**Language**:
- Know goblin warning calls
- Use goblin terrain terms
- Curse in goblin (considered low-class)

**Practices**:
- Check for goblin signs before travel
- Leave offerings at certain sites
- Know which goblins can be traded with (quietly)

**Food**:
- Eat certain goblin-style preparations
- Know which swamp plants are edible
- Brew similar fermented drinks

**Combat**:
- Adopted goblin tunnel tactics
- Use goblin-style ambushes
- Recognize raid patterns by tribe

### Familiar Enemies

After 500 years, goblins and Marcher humans know each other:
- Same raiding families for generations
- Personal vendettas and strange respects
- Unspoken rules about certain targets
- Occasional warnings about common threats (monsters, weather)

### What the Cities Don't Understand

Urban humans see goblins as monsters. Marcher humans see them as enemies—but enemies they understand. This creates tension between border folk and their own governments.
