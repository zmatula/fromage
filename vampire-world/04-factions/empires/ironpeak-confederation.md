# The Ironpeak Confederation

The dwarven industrial power. Neutral in empire conflicts, essential to all, and keepers of technical secrets that rival vampire knowledge.

---

## Overview

**Full Name**: The Confederation of the Ironpeak Holds
**Founded**: Year 89 (Alliance), Year 445 (Confederation)
**Capital**: Ironpeak (administrative); holds are autonomous
**Population**: ~1.5 million
**Dominant Race**: Dwarven (95%)
**Government**: Confederation of autonomous holds
**Current Ruler**: High Thane Borin Stonehammer (age 312, elected Year 1089)

---

## Territory

**Geography**:
- Mountain ranges central to continent
- Underground holds and surface cities
- Controls key mountain passes
- Rich mineral deposits

**Major Holds**:
- **Ironpeak** (~150,000): Administrative center, foreign diplomacy
- **Deepforge** (~120,000): Industrial heart, weapons production
- **Goldvault** (~100,000): Banking center
- **Oldhold** (~80,000): Ancient seat, traditional practices
- **Highgate** (~70,000): Trade gateway

**Strategic Resources**:
- All major metal deposits
- Best weapons manufacturing
- Banking network
- Technical expertise
- Mountain fortifications

---

## Government Structure

### The High Thane

**Selection**:
- Elected by Council of Thanes
- Usually elder with proven service
- Lifetime appointment

**Powers**:
- Represents Confederation externally
- Mediates between holds
- Commands Confederate Guard
- Foreign treaty negotiator

**Limitations**:
- Cannot command individual holds
- Cannot override hold autonomy
- Council can override his decisions
- Cannot raise taxes

### The Council of Thanes

**Composition**:
- 12 Thanes (one per major hold)
- Each Thane hereditary within clan
- Equal votes regardless of hold size

**Powers**:
- Confederation policy
- War and peace
- Trade agreements
- Mediate disputes

### Individual Holds

**Autonomy**:
- Own governments and laws
- Own militaries
- Own economic policies
- Only bound by Confederation treaties

## Current Ruler: High Thane Borin Stonehammer

### Personal History

**Birth**: Year 888, son of Thane of Deepforge
**Career**: Led Confederation through last 111 years
**Character**: Patient, cautious, deeply traditional

### Personality

**Strengths**:
- Immensely patient (dwarven lifespan)
- Master negotiator
- Commands universal respect
- Remembers history personally

**Weaknesses**:
- Very conservative
- Slow to decide
- Dislikes change
- May be losing touch

### Goals

**Public**: Maintain neutrality and prosperity
**Private**: Preserve dwarven independence
**Fear**: Being drawn into human wars

### Economic Base

**Mining**: All major metal sources
**Manufacturing**: Best weapons and tools
**Banking**: Pan-continental network
**Trade**: Essential goods to all powers

### Financial Network

- Major banks in all human capitals
- Hold debt on all empires
- Control metal supply
- Can crash any economy

## Culture

### Values

- **Craft**: Excellence in creation
- **Tradition**: Old ways are proven
- **Loyalty**: Clan and hold first
- **Patience**: Think in centuries
- **Privacy**: Keep your business

### Religion

**Ancestor Worship**:
- Venerate clan founders
- Maintain genealogies for millennia
- Consult ancestor wisdom
- Shrines in every hold

### View of Vampire History

**Pragmatic**: They're gone; their stuff works
**Technical**: More interested in how than why
**Cautious**: Don't wake what's sleeping
**Secret**: Know more than they say

### Relationship with Ruins

- Technical expeditions when profitable
- Recover useful items
- Seal dangerous areas
- Don't share everything found
- Some deep holds have sealed chambers

## Succession Concerns

### High Thane Borin Stonehammer
- Age: 312 years (elderly for dwarf)
- Health: Strong but declining
- Policy: Strict neutrality, banking leverage, patience
- Problem: His neutrality is personal conviction, not institutional

### Potential Successors

**Elder Traditionalist: Ancestor Speaker Grom**
- Age: 289
- Position: Withdraw further from surface affairs
- Policy: Seal holds, reduce trade, wait out human conflicts
- Consequence: Dwarven retreat would collapse human economies

**Young Pragmatist: Forge Master Durin**
- Age: 156
- Position: Choose winning side and profit
- Policy: Alliance with Northern (they'll win, share in victory)
- Consequence: Would end neutrality, trigger continental realignment

**Merchant Advocate: Vault Keeper Thora**
- Age: 203
- Position: Exploit conflict for maximum profit
- Policy: Lend to all sides, foreclose on losers
- Consequence: Would make dwarves hated by all, risk coordinated attack

**Deep Hold Conservative: Hold Lord Balin**
- Age: 267
- Position: Abandon surface entirely
- Policy: Seal all holds, become self-sufficient
- Consequence: Would remove dwarven economy from continent entirely

### Current Succession Politics
- Borin favors no one openly (maintains authority)
- Thane Council divided (no majority for any position)
- Young dwarves increasingly support Durin (tired of "neutral" inaction)
- Crisis will come when Borin dies or becomes incapacitated

### Timeline
- Borin has perhaps 20-50 years (dwarven lifespan uncertain at this age)
- Succession fight could begin at any sign of weakness
- If war breaks out, pressure to choose sides intensifies
- Protagonist's awakening may force dwarven choice earlier

## Secret Knowledge

### What Dwarves Know

**Technical**:
- How much vampire tech works
- How to maintain artifacts
- What systems are connected
- What shouldn't be activated

**Historical**:
- Pre-collapse dwarven civilization
- True nature of vampire-dwarf relations
- Where certain things are buried
- What happened to certain vampires

### What They Hide

**Forge of Souls**: In dwarven possession since Year 358
- Can create artifact-like items
- Never used (publicly)
- Considered too dangerous
- Or is it being used secretly?

**Deep Hold Chambers**:
- Some sealed since collapse
- Contents unknown
- Rumors of stasis subjects
- Rumors of active systems

**Vampire Blood**:
- Some dwarves have it
- From collaboration era
- Kept absolutely secret
- Grants certain abilities
