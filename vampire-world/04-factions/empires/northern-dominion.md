# The Northern Dominion

A martial nation forged in war, ruled by elected warrior-kings. Pragmatic, ambitious, and home to the richest vampire ruins.

---

## Overview

**Full Name**: The Northern Dominion of the Free Warriors
**Founded**: Year 156 (Kingdom), Year 523 (Dominion)
**Capital**: Ironhold
**Population**: ~3 million
**Dominant Race**: Human (75%), dwarven minority (15%), others (10%)
**Government**: Elective military monarchy with War Council
**Current Ruler**: Warlord-King Aldric XII (age 34, reign began Year 1192)

---

## Territory

**Geography**:
- Northern highlands and mountain valleys
- Harsh winters, short growing seasons
- Rich mineral deposits
- Dense forests for timber
- Mountain border with Valdrian Empire

**Major Cities**:
- **Ironhold** (capital, ~200,000): Fortress-city, seat of War Council
- **Northgate** (~120,000): Trade hub with Ironpeak dwarves
- **Vel'Krath Reach** (~80,000): Built atop Throne covenant ruins
- **Frost Harbor** (~70,000): Northern port, fishing
- **Eagle's Peak** (~50,000): Military academy, border fortress

**Strategic Resources**:
- Iron and precious metal mines
- Timber for ships and construction
- Hardy population accustomed to hardship
- Access to largest vampire military complex (Vel'Krath)

---

## Government Structure

### The Warlord-King

**Selection**:
- Elected by War Council upon previous king's death
- Must be proven warrior
- Can be from any noble family
- Lifetime position unless deposed

**Powers**:
- Supreme military commander
- Foreign policy authority
- Appoints military commanders
- Can propose laws to Council

**Limitations**:
- War Council can override with 2/3 vote
- Cannot change succession rules
- Major campaigns require Council approval
- Can be deposed for cowardice or incompetence

### The War Council

**Composition**:
- 50 Warlords (hereditary military nobles)
- 25 Merit Lords (commoners raised for service)
- 15 Forge Masters (dwarven representatives)
- 10 Trade Captains (merchant representatives)

**Powers**:
- Elect and depose king
- Approve wars and treaties
- Control taxation
- Confirm military appointments

### Current Political Factions

**Expansionists** (growing):
- Want war with Valdris
- Push for aggressive ruin expeditions
- Led by King Aldric XII himself

**Veterans** (declining):
- Cautious, war-weary
- Prefer consolidation
- Led by old Warlord Theron Greystone

**Dwarven Bloc**:
- Protect dwarven interests
- Want neutrality
- Led by Forge Master Duran Ironhand

## Current Ruler: Warlord-King Aldric XII

### Personal History

**Birth**: Year 1166, son of Warlord Karn Blackwood
**Military Career**: Decorated commander in border skirmishes
**Election**: Year 1192, youngest king in 200 years
**Marriage**: Princess Helena of Valdris (Year 1190)—political marriage

### Personality

**Strengths**:
- Brilliant tactician
- Charismatic leader
- Physically imposing
- Popular with soldiers

**Weaknesses**:
- Impulsive
- Craves glory
- Underestimates opponents
- Restless in peacetime

### Goals

**Public**: Secure Northern borders, protect Northern interests
**Private**: War with Valdris, conquer ruin-rich territories
**Secret**: Recover the "Living Artifact" from Veil Keepers

### The Valdrian Connection

- Married to Valdrian princess
- Potential claim through her if succession fails
- Some suspect he's positioning for Valdrian throne
- Wife's loyalties unclear

## Military

### The Dominion Host

**Standing Forces**: ~80,000 professional warriors
**Reserves**: ~150,000 able-bodied fighters
**Character**: Aggressive, adaptable, artifact-integrated

**Organization**:
- 16 War Hosts (5,000 each)
- Combined arms with cavalry, infantry, archers
- Artifact weapons widely distributed
- Elite berserker units

### Northern Fleet

**Ships**: ~150 warships
**Character**: Raiding and coastal defense
**Specialty**: Cold-water warfare, ice navigation

### Artifact Military

**Known Holdings**:
- Best military integration of vampire artifacts
- Vel'Krath weapons cache (Year 745 discovery)
- Multiple enhanced units
- Artifact-augmented siege weapons

**Doctrine**: Artifacts as standard military equipment, not special reserves

### The Living Artifact

- Recovered Year 1118
- Exhibited awareness and communication
- Demanded blood
- Seized by Veil Keepers
- King Aldric wants it back

---

## Economy

### Economic Base

**Mining**: Iron, silver, gold, gems
**Timber**: Largest exporter
**Mercenaries**: Sell military services
**Ruin Trade**: Artifacts from Vel'Krath region

### Wealth Sources

- Mineral extraction taxes
- Mercenary contracts
- Artifact sales
- Trade tariffs

### Current Issues

- Agriculture insufficient, imports food
- Depends on dwarven technical expertise
- Valdrian tariffs hurt exports
- Warriors need employment (war)

---

## Culture

### Values

- **Strength**: Respect earned through prowess
- **Honor**: Word is bond
- **Merit**: Advancement through deed
- **Brotherhood**: Loyalty to comrades
- **Glory**: Fame through great acts

### Religion

**Mixed traditions**:
- Church of Divine Light (human majority)
- Dwarven ancestor worship (significant)
- Warrior cult traditions
- Pragmatic, less devout than Sanctified

### View of Vampire History

**Official**: Vampires were powerful enemies, honorably defeated
**Popular**: Less demonic than Valdrian view; more respect for strength
**Practical**: Vampire stuff works—use it

### Relationship with Ruins

- Active state exploitation
- Many successful expeditions
- Warriors explore regularly
- Less superstitious fear
- Vel'Krath considered Northern heritage

---

## Vampire Artifacts

### Vel'Krath Cache

**Discovery**: Year 745
**Contents**: Military equipment from Throne covenant
- Vampire weapons adapted for human use
- Defensive artifacts
- Communication devices
- Training manuals partially translated

**Impact**: Made Northern military dominant for centuries

### Current Holdings

- Most extensive military artifact collection
- Distributed to units, not hoarded
- Some items not fully understood
- Ongoing research and experimentation

### The Living Artifact

**What It Is**: Unknown—possibly autonomous system, trapped intelligence, or creation like protagonist
**Location**: Currently in Veil Keeper custody
**Importance**: King Aldric sees it as Northern property
**Danger**: Could reveal truths about vampire survivors

- Must escape Northern custody
- Connection to Living Artifact

**If Protagonist Revealed**:
- King would demand he serve Dominion
- Military would want to study/replicate
- Dwarves would be very interested
- Some would respect his survival
- Others would fear vampire return
- War faction would accelerate plans
