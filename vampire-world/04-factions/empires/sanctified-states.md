# The Sanctified States

A theocratic confederation united by faith. The most populous human power, most hostile to magic users, and most threatened by the truth of vampire history.

---

## Overview

**Full Name**: The Holy Confederation of Sanctified States
**Founded**: Year 234 (Sanctified Realm), Year 512 (Confederation after schism)
**Capital**: Solarium
**Population**: ~5 million
**Dominant Race**: Human (95%), minimal non-humans
**Government**: Theocratic confederation with High Theocrat
**Current Ruler**: High Theocrat Solarius VII (age 71, elected Year 1172)

---

## Territory

**Geography**:
- Central-southern plains and river valleys
- Mild climate, good farmland
- Rolling hills, few mountains
- No direct border with non-human powers

**Major Cities**:
- **Solarium** (capital, ~400,000): Seat of Church, holiest city
- **Dawnhaven** (~180,000): Order of Dawn headquarters
- **Pilgrims' Rest** (~120,000): Religious tourism, shrines
- **Grainfield** (~100,000): Agricultural center
- **Southwatch** (~90,000): Border fortress

**Strategic Resources**:
- Largest agricultural production
- Largest population
- Religious authority and soft power
- Order of Dawn military order

---

## Government Structure

### The High Theocrat

**Selection**:
- Elected by Council of Cardinals upon predecessor's death
- Must be Cardinal (senior clergy)
- Lifetime appointment

**Powers**:
- Supreme religious authority
- Head of state
- Interprets divine will
- Commands Order of Dawn

**Limitations**:
- Council of Cardinals can override
- Must maintain appearance of piety
- Temporal power limited by state autonomy
- Cannot directly command state armies

### The Council of Cardinals

**Composition**:
- 12 Cardinals (regional religious leaders)
- Elected by clergy of their regions
- Lifetime appointments

**Powers**:
- Elect High Theocrat
- Interpret religious doctrine
- Control Church finances
- Approve major policies

### The States

**Structure**:
- 8 semi-autonomous states
- Each governed by Blessed Lord (secular + religious authority)
- Own militaries and laws (within Church framework)
- Pay tithes to central Church

### Current Political Factions

**Fundamentalists** (majority):
- Strict doctrinal interpretation
- Anti-magic, anti-non-human
- Led by Cardinal Mordecai Blackfire

**Moderates** (declining):
- Pragmatic tolerance
- Work with other races
- Led by Cardinal Helena Brightwater

**Militant** (Order of Dawn):
- Military preparation for vampire return
- Active hunting of "corruption"
- Led by Grand Master Aurelius Dawnbringer

## Current Ruler: High Theocrat Solarius VII

### Personal History

**Birth**: Year 1129, son of minor nobles
**Career**: Rose through Church hierarchy
**Election**: Year 1172, compromise candidate
**Character**: Doctrinaire, fearful, aging

### Personality

**Strengths**:
- Genuinely devout
- Skilled administrator
- Commands loyalty
- Experienced politician

**Weaknesses**:
- Fears change
- Increasingly fundamentalist
- Health failing
- Paranoid about heresy

### Goals

**Public**: Protect the faithful, maintain purity
**Private**: Crush threats to Church authority
**Fear**: The truth about divine punishment being exposed

### The Growing Crisis

- Scholarly evidence threatens doctrine
- Magic users harder to suppress
- Non-human races gaining power
- Young clergy questioning traditions
- Solarius responds with persecution

## Military

### State Militias

**Combined Forces**: ~100,000 across all states
**Character**: Variable quality, religious motivation
**Weakness**: No unified command

### Order of Dawn

**Forces**: ~15,000 elite warriors
**Character**: Fanatical, well-trained, artifact-armed
**Purpose**: Hunt vampire remnants, protect faithful

**Organization**:
- 30 fortress-monasteries
- Each with 500 knight-brothers
- Support staff and auxiliaries
- Answer only to High Theocrat

**Capabilities**:
- Anti-vampire training
- Artifact identification
- Holy magic (or so they believe)
- Intelligence network

### Artifact Policy

**Official**: Vampire artifacts are demonic corruption
**Reality**: Order of Dawn uses "purified" artifacts
**Hypocrisy**: Leadership knows, faithful don't

## Economy

### Economic Base

**Agriculture**: Breadbasket of human lands
**Pilgrimage**: Religious tourism
**Tithes**: Church taxation
**Indulgences**: Payments for spiritual favors

### Wealth Sources

- Church tithes (10% of all production)
- Land ownership (Church owns 30% of land)
- Pilgrimage industry
- State contributions

### Current Issues

- Agricultural success masks economic simplicity
- Little industry or innovation
- Depends on trade with others
- Rising costs of Order of Dawn

## Culture

### Values

- **Faith**: Devotion to Divine Light
- **Purity**: Rejection of corruption
- **Obedience**: Respect for authority
- **Sacrifice**: Service to Church
- **Humanity**: Human supremacy

### Religion

**Church of Divine Light**: Absolutely dominant
- Mandatory worship
- Clergy have immense power
- Other faiths suppressed
- Magic viewed as suspicious at best

### View of Vampire History

**Doctrine**: Divine punishment of demon oppressors
- Vampires were literally demons
- Gods struck them down for humans
- Artifacts are demonic corruption
- Using them risks divine punishment

**Threat Level**: Highest of any power
- Protagonist proves doctrine false
- Church authority collapses
- Sanctified identity destroyed

### Relationship with Ruins

- Ruins are cursed, dangerous
- Entry forbidden without Church permission
- Order of Dawn controls access
- Artifacts "purified" or destroyed
- Knowledge suppressed

## Current Propaganda Campaigns

### "The Corruption Grows" (Year 1198-present)

**Purpose**: Justify expanded persecution
**Message**: Magic users increasing, non-humans infiltrating, danger rising
**Method**: Sermons, pamphlets, market-square preachers, traveling shows

**Who Creates It**:
- Cardinal Blackfire's office controls messaging
- Order of Dawn contributes "evidence"
- Local clergy adapt to regional concerns

**Who Believes It**:
- Faithful peasantry: Completely
- Urban merchants: Skeptically (hurts trade)
- Educated clergy: Selectively
- Other nations: See it as excuse for aggression

### "Northern Threat" (Year 1195-present)

**Purpose**: Prepare population for potential conflict
**Message**: Northern Kingdom worships vampire artifacts, plans invasion
**Method**: Military displays, border incident reports (exaggerated)

**The Irony**: The Sanctified States have more vampire artifacts than the Northern Kingdom. But the faithful don't know that.

### Effects
- Refugee families torn apart
- Innocent scholars executed
- Trade with other nations suffering
- But Church authority strengthened
