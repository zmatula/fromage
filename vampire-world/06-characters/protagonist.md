# The Protagonist - Vessel Seven

An experimental vampire creation, designed to survive outside the Bloodweb. He wakes 1,200 years after the collapse into a world that fears his kind.

---

## Identity

**Designation**: Vessel Seven (Sanguine Vessels Initiative)
**True Name**: Unknown (vampires used designations; may have had a name before conversion)
**Created**: Year -7
**Age at Stasis**: Approximately 7 years post-creation
**Current State**: Waking in Year 1200 after 1,200 years of stasis

---

## True History

### Origin

**Pre-Conversion**: Unknown—likely a human or elf selected for specific qualities
**Conversion**: Year -7 in Vel'Thoras research complex
**Creator**: High Researcher Velanna (Mind Covenant)
**Project**: Sanguine Vessels Initiative

### Purpose

**Official**: Create vampires who could survive Bloodweb disruption
**Real Goal**: Insurance against catastrophic failure (they knew risks existed)
**Method**: Modified blood magic allowing independent sustenance
**Result**: Enhanced but limited—trades raw power for independence

### The Experiment

**Vessels 1-6**: Failed (various complications)
**Vessel 7**: Success—stable, functional, meets parameters
**Vessels 8-10**: Partial success (died in collapse)
**Vessel 11**: Unknown (possibly escaped, possibly in another facility)
**Vessel 12**: Incomplete at collapse

### Why He Survived

1. **Not connected to Bloodweb**: Immune to inversion
2. **In stasis**: Protected from initial chaos
3. **Shielded facility**: Vel'Thoras lower levels survived
4. **Enhanced regeneration**: Could survive deprivation
5. **Automatic systems**: Maintained his chamber

## What He Remembers

### Accurate Memories

- Basic vampire culture and society
- His creation and early training
- Some of High Researcher Velanna
- General knowledge of the world
- His abilities and how to use them

### False/Implanted Memories

- Believes vampires were more benevolent than reality
- Remembers "happy" servant races
- Doesn't recall full extent of vampire cruelty
- Has idealized memories of his creator
- May believe he was volunteer (he wasn't)

### Gaps

- Doesn't know why he was created (insurance)
- Doesn't know collapse was imminent
- Missing memories of other Vessels
- Unclear on covenant politics
- Doesn't know what happened to Velanna

### What He'll Learn

- Vampires were more ruthless than he knew
- He was an experiment, not a person
- His memories were manipulated
- The collapse was preventable
- There may be others like him

## Abilities

### Core Vampiric Traits

**Strength**: 3-4x human
**Speed**: 2-3x human
**Senses**: Enhanced hearing, smell, night vision
**Healing**: Rapid regeneration (not instant)
**Lifespan**: Indefinite (can be killed)

### Sanguine Vessel Enhancements

**Blood Independence**:
- Can sustain on less blood than normal vampires
- Can go weeks without feeding (weakened)
- Can use animal blood (less effective)
- Not dependent on Bloodweb

**Enhanced Regeneration**:
- Faster healing than standard vampires
- Can regrow minor parts
- Requires blood to fuel

**Mental Resilience**:
- Resistant to magical mental influence
- Can shield thoughts
- Harder to control

### Limitations

**Not Peak Vampire**:
- Weaker than fully Bloodweb-connected vampires were
- Less raw magical power
- Cannot use Bloodweb-dependent magic
- Cannot command as vampires could

**Blood Requirements**:
- Still needs blood to function
- Weakens significantly without it
- Healing consumes blood rapidly
- Cannot go indefinitely

**Sunlight Sensitivity**:
- Not instantly fatal but very harmful
- Weakened and pained in direct sun
- Can operate in shade/overcast
- Must avoid prolonged exposure

**Physical Vulnerabilities**:
- Can be killed by sufficient damage
- Fire is especially dangerous
- Decapitation is fatal
- Silver causes extra pain (not fatal)
- Holy symbols: psychological, not magical

### Core Traits

**Curious**: Wants to understand what happened and why
**Cautious**: Survival instinct is strong
**Conflicted**: Between vampire identity and evidence of their cruelty
**Pragmatic**: Does what's necessary but has limits
**Isolated**: Doesn't know how to connect after 1,200 years alone

### Shaped By

**Vampire Creation**:
- Trained to value knowledge
- Taught vampire superiority
- Conditioned for obedience
- Given false memories

**1,200 Years Alone**:
- Dreams in stasis
- Fragmented consciousness
- Existential questions
- No social contact

### How He Presents

- Initially disoriented and wary
- Struggles with modern languages (vampire tongue)
- Doesn't understand social cues
- Can seem cold (vampire training)
- Moments of unexpected empathy

## Fatal Flaw

### The Flaw: Need to Belong

**Manifestation**: Will make bad decisions to find connection
**Origin**: Created as experiment, never truly belonged
**Danger**: Can be manipulated by those who offer acceptance
**Examples**:
- Trusting the wrong faction because they seem welcoming
- Revealing too much to gain trust
- Staying when he should flee
- Defending those who don't deserve it

### How It Grows

- Early story: Makes him vulnerable
- Middle story: Causes major setback
- Late story: Must overcome or succumb
