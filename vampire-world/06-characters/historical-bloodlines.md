# Historical Bloodlines

Family lines, ideological succession, and connections across 1,200 years.

---

## Major House Lineages

### House Aurelian → House Varen (Valdris)

**Pre-Vampire Era**:
- House Aurelian ruled the Kingdom of Valdris from Year -1280 to -1000
- Last King: Marius II "the Last," executed by Vykros

**During Vampire Era**:
- Aurelian survivors served as administrators
- Some became collaborators; others resisted
- The name became associated with "former glory"

**Post-Collapse**:
- Year 12: Roderick claims distant Aurelian ancestry (probably false)
- Year 200: House Varen emerges, claiming Aurelian descent (possibly true)
- Year 900: Varen becomes dominant noble house
- Year 1200: Current succession crisis involves Varen claimants

**Current Representatives**:
- **Duke Aldric Varen**: Claims direct descent; strongest succession claimant
- **The Bastard (Markus)**: Allegedly illegitimate Varen; actually may have better claim than Aldric

**The Truth**:
House Varen's Aurelian connection is possible but unproven. Records were destroyed. They may be descended from Aurelian servants, not kings. But in politics, the claim matters more than the truth.

---

### House Blackwater (Maritime Republic)

**Origins**:
- Founded by Admiral Sera Blackwater, Year 560-612
- Built on naval dominance and trade control

**Secret Origin**:
- Sera's grandmother was Vera Goldveil, the hidden collaborator
- House Blackwater's initial capital came from vampire gold
- They don't talk about this

**Key Generations**:
- Year 612: Sera dies; her daughter Kess takes over
- Year 780: House splits into naval and banking branches
- Year 950: Banking branch (House Blackwater-Coin) dominates
- Year 1200: Master Erasmus Blackwater leads coup against Maritime Republic

**Current Representatives**:
- **Master Erasmus Blackwater**: Banking house leader; drove current Republic coup
- **Captain Thessa Blackwater**: Naval branch; secretly opposes Erasmus

**Family Secrets**:
- Vera Goldveil's journals in family vault
- Records of vampire-era financial networks
- Knowledge of where more vampire gold is hidden

---

### House Thornweald → House Silvermoon (Elves)

**Pre-Vampire Era**:
- House Thornweald betrayed elves to vampires (Year -1050)
- Lord Calindor Thornweald became "Servant King"
- House Silvermoon was diminished but survived

**During Vampire Era**:
- Thornweald ruled as vampire puppets
- Silvermoon preserved traditions in secret
- Bitter rivalry between houses

**Post-Collapse**:
- Year 18: Thornweald the Sealer (House Silvermoon) takes control
- House Thornweald stripped of status; members exiled or killed
- House Silvermoon claims moral authority through resistance

**Current Representatives**:
- **Aelindra Moonshadow**: Current Voice; House Silvermoon bloodline
- **Thandril Shadowleaf**: (POV character) Minor house; no Thornweald connection

**Hidden Truth**:
Some Thornweald descendants survived. They changed their names. They might be anywhere in Silverwood—or outside it. The name is cursed, but the blood continues.

---

### House Ironhand (Dwarves)

**Pre-Vampire Era**:
- High King Borin Ironhand united the Confederation (Year -1250)
- House Ironhand ruled for most of pre-vampire history

**During Vampire Era**:
- Ironhand kings became puppet rulers
- Some collaborated; some resisted secretly
- The house survived but was diminished

**Post-Collapse**:
- Year 15: Grimjaw (not Ironhand) founds new Confederation
- Ironhand becomes one of seven major clans
- No longer ruling house but still influential

**Current Representatives**:
- **Thane Borin XII**: Claims descent from original Borin (probably true)
- **Master Smith Kira Ironhand**: Most skilled smith; has no interest in politics

**Family Tradition**:
Every generation produces at least one master smith. The original Borin's techniques are passed down. Some say they include vampire-era methods learned during occupation.

## Ideological Succession

### The Lysara Line

**Origin**: Lysara the Dissenter, purged Year -28 for warning about Bloodweb dependency

**Her Teaching**: "We have made ourselves into a species that cannot survive without infrastructure. When it fails, we will discover what we have become."

**Succession**:
- Year -28: Lysara executed; her students scattered
- Year 0: Several students in Twilight sanctuaries
- Year 200: "Lysaran" philosophy emerges among post-collapse scholars
- Year 850: The Hooded Scholar (Inheritor founder) reportedly quotes Lysara
- Year 1200: Inheritors preserve Lysaran texts

**Current Representatives**:
- The Inheritors claim ideological descent
- Some Valdrian Academy scholars follow Lysaran principles
- High Confessor Brennan considers Lysaranism a heresy

**The Teaching Today**:
"Dependency is vulnerability. Self-sufficiency is survival." Applied to artifact reliance, Bloodweb memory, and political alliances.

---

### The Mercy Tradition

**Origin**: Sister Mercy, founder of Church of Divine Light, Year 1-48

**Her Teaching**: "The collapse was divine punishment for serving demons. All vampire knowledge is corruption."

**Succession**:
- Year 48: Sister Mercy dies; Council of Flames takes over
- Year 225: Queen Margret codifies Mercy doctrine
- Year 350: Prince-Bishop Aldric Thorne splits the Church
- Year 780: Order of Dawn formalizes militant interpretation
- Year 1200: High Confessor Brennan leads most extreme version

**Current Representatives**:
- **High Confessor Brennan**: Direct ideological heir; believes persecution is mercy
- **Bishop Helena**: Moderate Mercy tradition; argues for education over execution

**The Teaching Today**:
Two versions: Brennan's "destroy all vampire taint" and Helena's "teach people to resist temptation." Same origin, opposite applications.

---

### The Caden Legacy

**Origin**: Rebel-General Caden Stormhold, executed Year 512 for advocating goblin peace

**His Teaching**: "We are killing fellow victims and calling it victory."

**Succession**:
- Year 512: Caden executed; his research destroyed
- Year 600: Goblin shamans preserve his words in oral tradition
- Year 900: "Cadenites" appear in Free Cities (humanitarians)
- Year 1100: Movement crushed by combined empire action
- Year 1200: Underground network preserves his ideas

**Current Representatives**:
- **Gritha Bloodsinger**: (POV character) Doesn't know the name but follows the principles
- **Scholar Merik Voss**: Secret Cadenite in Valdrian Academy
- No public representatives—the ideology is illegal everywhere

**The Teaching Today**:
"Goblins are victims, not enemies. The Goblin Wars were genocide, not justice." Saying this publicly will get you killed in most places.

## Cross-Era Connections

### The Ashward Name

**Year -30**: Elven crafter strike leaders include an Ashward (executed)
**Year 3**: Ashford children accused of vampire blood (disappeared)
**Year -7**: Vessel Four was Theron Ashward (failed in creation)
**Year 500**: Captain Mira Ashford in Maritime Republic
**Year 1200**: Marcus Ashford (POV character) searches for sister

**Connection?**:
The name appears across races and centuries. Coincidence? A family that keeps changing races through marriage? Something else?

**Marcus's Research**:
He's noticed the pattern. He doesn't know what it means. He's afraid to find out.

---

### The Goldveil-Blackwater Axis

**Year 0-40**: Vera Goldveil builds fortune on hidden vampire gold
**Year 320**: House Goldveil founded as banking house
**Year 560**: Sera Blackwater (Vera's great-granddaughter) founds naval house
**Year 780**: Houses formally separate
**Year 1200**: Both houses in Banking Consortium; run the Republic

**The Secret**:
Both houses descend from a collaborator. Both fortunes rest on vampire wealth. Neither talks about it. Both have family vaults full of vampire-era documents.

**The Risk**:
If this became public, both houses would be destroyed—by mobs, rivals, or the Church. The secret keeps them allied. The secret keeps them vulnerable.

### The Three Scholars' Descendants

**Year 278**: Varek Stoneson, Sister Helena, Magister Orin translate first vampire text

**Their Fates**:
- Varek: Murdered Year 280; no known descendants
- Sister Helena: Recanted; died in penitent cell; no descendants
- Magister Orin: Disappeared Year 279; fate unknown

*Note: This Sister Helena (Year 278) is NOT related to Helena Ashford (Year 1200), who is a Veil Keeper archivist descended from Scholar Merik Ashford (murdered Year 789 in a separate incident). The name is coincidental. These are two different scholar murder events 500 years apart.*

**The Theory**:
Orin escaped with the complete translation. He had children. His descendants have the text.

**Possible Descendants**:
- The Hooded Scholar (Year 850): Some say Orin bloodline
- Director Selene Thorne (Inheritors): Claimed Orin connection
- Scholar Kess Orin (Year 1200): Too obvious to be real?

**The Search**:
For 900 years, people have hunted Orin's descendants, hoping to find the complete translation. If it exists. If he had children. If they kept it.

## Family Secrets

### What Houses Know and Hide

**House Varen**:
- Their Aurelian claim is based on a single document
- That document may be forged
- If proven false, their succession claim collapses

**House Blackwater**:
- Vera Goldveil's journals
- Locations of remaining vampire gold
- Financial records that would expose other houses

**House Silvermoon**:
- What's actually in the sealed elven ruins
- Whether any Thornweald descendants remain
- What the Twilight Prophecy really says

**House Ironhand**:
- Location of the Forge of Souls
- What the Forge actually does
- Why they sealed it instead of destroying it

## Rivalries Across Generations

### Valdrian Succession

**Year 180**: Aldric I takes throne from three brothers (kills all three)
**Year 700**: House Varen defeats House Blackwood for regency
**Year 1100**: Queen Seraphina (Varen) tries to reform; fails
**Year 1197**: Current succession crisis

**The Pattern**:
Every major succession involves violence. Every victor claims legitimate descent. Every loser is erased from history.

### Ironpeak Confederation

**Year -1090**: Three-clan civil war (Ironhand, Deepdelver, Stonefist)
**Year 15**: Grimjaw unites clans against vampire remnants
**Year 400**: Deepdelver rebellion crushed
**Year 800**: Stonefist schism over artifact policy
**Year 1200**: Three clans still watching each other

**The Pattern**:
Dwarves don't forget. Grudges from 1,300 years ago still affect policy. The Stonefist schism leaders' descendants are still excluded from some councils.
