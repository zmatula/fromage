# The Scholar Families Network

Three families, one shared tragedy, and none of them know it yet.

---

## The Connection

In Year 789, three scholars were murdered by the Inheritors' founders at Sel'Naroth:
- Scholar Merik Ashford
- Scholar Denna Thornwood
- Scholar Aldric Vane

Their families were told it was a cave-in. The families never met. They grieved separately and moved on separately.

411 years later, their descendants are alive, active, and unknowingly connected by a shared injustice that could destroy the Inheritors if they discovered it together.

---

## The Ashford Line

### The Victim

**Scholar Merik Ashford** (Year 751-789)
- Specialist in vampire linguistics
- Minor academic from eastern Valdris
- Married with two children when he died

### The Descendant

**Helena Ashford** (Year 1162-present)
- Senior Veil Keeper archivist
- Age 38
- Location: Various (Veil Keeper cells)

### Her Situation

Helena joined the Veil Keepers because she believed in truth. She's spent twenty years documenting what others hide. She's good at it—too good.

She's been copying Inheritor sealed texts for years. She knows they're hiding something. She doesn't know what. She doesn't know it's personal.

### What She's Found

- Inconsistencies in Inheritor founding records
- Sealed documents referencing "founding protocols"
- Unusual interest in her family by Inheritor agents
- The name "Ashford" appearing in contexts it shouldn't

### What She Suspects

Helena suspects the Inheritors are hiding something about their origins. She's been investigating quietly. She hasn't made the personal connection yet—she doesn't know her ancestor was involved.

When she does, she'll have motive (revenge), means (Veil Keeper resources), and opportunity (she's already inside their archives).

### Her Weakness

Helena is a loner. She doesn't trust easily. She investigates alone. If she found the truth, she might act alone—and Vera is watching. Acting alone gets people killed.

## The Thornwood Line

### The Victim

**Scholar Denna Thornwood** (Year 745-789)
- Specialist in magical theory
- Academic from Valdrian Academy family
- Unmarried but with siblings

### The Descendant

**Marcus Thornwood** (Year 1158-present)
- Valdrian Academy historian
- Age 42
- Location: Valdris capital

### His Situation

Marcus comes from an academic family. His ancestor Denna is remembered as a talented scholar who died tragically. The family has always believed the cave-in story—but they've also always believed there was more to discover at Sel'Naroth.

Marcus has spent twenty years petitioning for access to the site. The Inheritors keep blocking him. He thinks they're protecting dangerous knowledge. He's half right—they're protecting dangerous truth.

### What He's Found

- Repeated Inheritor opposition to his access requests
- Inconsistencies in the official cave-in report
- Records showing Denna's work was more complete than claimed
- Hints that she found something important before she died

### What He Suspects

Marcus suspects the Inheritors took Denna's work and suppressed it. He thinks they're sitting on knowledge that should belong to the Academy—to the world. He doesn't suspect murder.

When he discovers the truth, he'll have motive (family honor), means (Academy resources and credibility), and opportunity (he's positioned as a legitimate historian).

### His Weakness

Marcus plays by the rules. He petitions, he requests, he follows procedure. He's been doing this for twenty years with no results. If he found the truth, he might go public through proper channels—and give Vera time to suppress it.

## The Vane Line

### The Victim

**Scholar Aldric Vane** (Year 753-789)
- Specialist in pre-Collapse history
- Minor noble family, Maritime Federation
- Married with one daughter when he died

### The Descendant

**Lady Cassandra Vane** (Year 1165-present)
- Minor noble
- Age 35
- Location: Maritime Federation capital

### Her Situation

Cassandra's family fell when Aldric died. They were suspected of involvement in illegal excavation—grave robbing, artifact theft. The charges were never proven but never cleared. The family lost status, wealth, and reputation.

Cassandra has spent her adult life trying to restore her family name. She believes proving Aldric was a legitimate scholar—not a criminal—will clear them. She's traced every record, interviewed every descendant of expedition members, rebuilt the timeline piece by piece.

She's the closest to the truth.

### What She's Found

- The cave-in story doesn't match geological records
- Other expedition members disappeared or were silenced
- The Inheritor founders were present but not officially listed
- Someone falsified records after Aldric's death

### What She Suspects

Cassandra suspects her ancestor was framed. She believes someone set up the expedition to fail, killed the scholars, and blamed Aldric to cover up theft. She doesn't know who—but she knows it was deliberate.

When she discovers the full truth, she'll have motive (generations of injustice), means (noble resources and legal standing), and opportunity (she's already built the case).

### Her Weakness

Cassandra is focused on family reputation. She wants to clear the Vane name—but she also wants to be a Vane. Noble status, noble connections, noble rules. She might hesitate to expose truth if it costs her everything she's worked for.

## The Convergence

### What They Don't Know

None of the three families know the others exist. They've never met, never corresponded, never compared notes. They're investigating the same crime from three different angles without knowing they're connected.

**Helena** has internal Inheritor documents.
**Marcus** has academic records and historical analysis.
**Cassandra** has legal evidence and expedition records.

Together, they have everything. Apart, they each have pieces that don't quite fit.

### How They Might Connect

**Scenario 1: Academic Conference**
Marcus presents his Sel'Naroth research. Cassandra attends because of family connection. Helena is there copying documents. They meet, compare notes, realize the pattern.

**Scenario 2: Document Discovery**
Helena finds a list of expedition members. Ashford, Thornwood, Vane. She recognizes her own name. She reaches out to the others.

**Scenario 3: Vera's Slip**
Vera's surveillance becomes obvious. One of them notices, investigates, discovers the other two are also being watched. Why would the Inheritors watch three unrelated people?

**Scenario 4: Seven Triggers It**
Seven's awakening forces the Inheritors to act. They make a move against one of the families. The others notice and investigate.

### What Happens When They Meet

If the three families share their evidence:
- Helena's documents prove internal knowledge
- Marcus's analysis proves academic fraud
- Cassandra's legal records prove falsification

The picture becomes clear: the Inheritors murdered their ancestors, covered it up, and built their organization on the theft.

They have enough to destroy the Inheritors. They have enough to expose Vera. They have enough to change everything.

## Vera's Counter-Moves

### Current Surveillance

Vera has agents watching all three families:
- Helena: Inheritor mole in Veil Keeper cells
- Marcus: Academic colleague reporting on his research
- Cassandra: Maritime contact tracking her legal movements

She knows what they're finding. She knows how close they're getting.

### Her Options

**Option 1: Discredit**
Plant false evidence that discredits each investigator. Make Helena look like a fraud, Marcus like an obsessive, Cassandra like a disgruntled noble.

**Option 2: Distract**
Feed them false leads that take them away from the truth. Let them chase shadows while the real evidence is hidden.

**Option 3: Silence**
Her ancestors killed to protect the secret. She could do the same. Three accidents, three illnesses, three disappearances.

**Option 4: Confess**
Come forward, admit everything, and ask for mercy. The Inheritors would be destroyed, but maybe something better could be built.

She hasn't chosen yet. She's watching and waiting. Seven's awakening will force the decision.

### The Crown's Advice

The whispers from Mordecai's Crown tell Vera what to do: silence them. Protect knowledge by any means. Her ancestor was right; she should be too.

Vera isn't sure if that's wisdom or corruption. She isn't sure there's a difference anymore.

## Plot Implications

### Early Story

The three families operate separately. Helena copies documents. Marcus petitions. Cassandra investigates. Seven awakens and they're all still apart.

### Mid Story

Something forces convergence. Maybe one of them gets too close and Vera acts. Maybe Seven's existence creates urgency. Maybe they just meet by chance.

They compare notes. They see the pattern. They realize.

### Late Story Options

**Alliance**: The three families unite with Seven against the Inheritors. The truth comes out. The Inheritors fall or reform.

**Division**: One family is co-opted (money, threats, blackmail). The alliance fractures. The truth is suppressed.

**Tragedy**: Vera chooses her ancestors' path. Some or all of them die. The secret is buried again.

**Redemption**: Vera chooses confession. The truth comes out from inside. The Inheritors are destroyed, but something genuine rises from the wreckage.

### For Seven

These three families are potential allies—people who have reason to hate the Inheritors, reason to believe Seven, reason to fight.

But they're also potential tools. Seven could use them, manipulate their anger, point them at his enemies. That would make him no better than Vera.

How he treats them reveals who he's becoming.
