# Commander Kira Frost

A Northern military intelligence officer torn between duty and conscience—and secretly a Veil Keeper.

---

## Overview

**Name**: Kira Frost
**Age**: 31
**Race**: Human
**Position**: Commander, Northern Military Intelligence
**Location**: Ironhold / Field operations
**Affiliation**: Northern Dominion (also secret Veil Keeper)

---

## Background

**Birth**: Year 1169, military family
**Training**: War College, intelligence specialty
**Career**: Rapid rise through skill
**Secret**: Recruited by Veil Keepers at 25

### Key Events

- Year 1187: Commissioned officer
- Year 1194: Recruited by Veil Keepers
- Year 1196: Intelligence command
- Year 1198: Learned about Living Artifact
- Year 1200: Assigned to locate protagonist

---

## Personality

### Strengths
- Brilliant analyst
- Loyal to what's right (not just orders)
- Skilled operative
- Genuinely caring

### Weaknesses
- Torn loyalties destroying her
- Can't commit fully to either side
- Secrets isolating her
- Analysis paralysis

---

## Fatal Flaw

**The Flaw**: Cannot choose a side

Paralyzed between Northern duty and Veil Keeper mission. Both are betrayals.

---

## Goals

**Public**: Serve Northern Dominion
**Private**: Protect world from vampire return
**Secret**: Find third option that doesn't require betrayal
**With Protagonist**: Uncertain—orders conflict

---

## Relationship to Protagonist

### Northern Orders
- Find and capture for military use
- King wants him as weapon

### Veil Keeper Orders
- Find and eliminate
- Too dangerous to exist

### Her Dilemma
- Neither feels right
- He might not be threat
- But might not be safe
- What should she do?

---

## Story Role

**POV Purpose**: Shows Northern perspective, Veil Keeper operations, loyalty conflicts

**Arc**: Must choose what she truly believes and act on it

---

## Key Relationships

- **King Aldric**: Commander, doesn't trust fully
- **Veil Keeper handlers**: Secret superiors
- **Protagonist**: Target/puzzle
- **Her soldiers**: Loyal to her

*Kira represents the impossible position—when every choice is betrayal, what do you do?*
