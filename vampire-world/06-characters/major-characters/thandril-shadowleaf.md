# Thandril Shadowleaf

An elven agent operating outside Silverwood, watching and waiting—with orders he doesn't fully understand.

---

## Overview

**Name**: Thandril Shadowleaf
**Age**: 340
**Race**: Elf
**Position**: External agent, Silverwood Domain
**Location**: Outside Silverwood (mobile, covert)
**Affiliation**: Silverwood Domain

---

## Background

**Birth**: Year 860
**Training**: Centuries of service
**Assignment**: External observation since Year 1050
**Status**: One of few elves outside Silverwood

### Key Events

- Year 1050: Assigned outside when borders closed
- Year 1050-1200: Watching, waiting, reporting
- Year 1199: Received new orders regarding "awakening"
- Year 1200: Moving toward Vel'Thoras

---

## Personality

### Strengths
- Patient (centuries of experience)
- Highly skilled
- Loyal to Silverwood
- Curious about the world

### Weaknesses
- Follows orders without full understanding
- Isolated for 150 years
- Conflicted about mission
- Lonely

---

## Fatal Flaw

**The Flaw**: Obedience without understanding

Has followed orders for 150 years without knowing why. What if they're wrong?

---

## Goals

**Public**: Observe and report
**Private**: Understand why he's out here
**Secret**: Return home
**With Protagonist**: Orders are unclear—contact? capture? kill?

---

## Relationship to Protagonist

### His Orders
- Observe the awakening
- Make contact under certain conditions
- Uncertain what conditions are
- Report but don't act (yet)

- Orders are vague
- Has his own judgment
- 150 years of waiting

### The Mystery
- What do elves want with protagonist?
