# Elder Shaman Gritha Bloodsinger

An ancient goblin keeper of blood magic who sees in the protagonist both threat and opportunity for her dying people.

---

## Overview

**Name**: Gritha Bloodsinger
**Age**: 89 (old for goblin)
**Race**: Goblin
**Position**: Elder Shaman, keeper of old knowledge
**Location**: Broken Lands (mobile)
**Affiliation**: Broken Lands Confederation

---

## Background

**Birth**: Year 1111, traditional shaman lineage
**Training**: Learned blood magic from grandmother
**Role**: Keeper of vampire-era knowledge
**Status**: Most respected shaman alive

### Key Events

- Year 1130: Completed shaman training
- Year 1160: Became elder shaman
- Year 1195: Advised War Chief Skarva
- Year 1199: Sensed "awakening" through blood magic
- Year 1200: Seeks protagonist

---

## Personality

### Strengths
- Ancient wisdom
- Patient and cunning
- Genuinely loves her people
- Understands blood magic deeply

### Weaknesses
- Would sacrifice anyone for goblins
- Bitter hatred of humans
- Manipulative
- Dying and desperate

---

## Fatal Flaw

**The Flaw**: Rage has consumed wisdom

Justified anger at genocide has become all-consuming. Can't see past revenge.

---

## Goals

**Public**: Protect goblin knowledge
**Private**: Goblin revenge on humans
**Secret**: Use protagonist to destroy human powers
**With Protagonist**: Capture and use as weapon

---

## Relationship to Protagonist

### What She Knows
- Recognizes what he is
- Understands blood magic connection
- Knows his potential

### What She Wants
- Weapon against humans
- Blood magic enhancement
- Symbol for goblin cause
- Revenge through him

### The Complexity
- Might actually help him
- Understands him better than others
- But will use him
- Shared marginalization

---

## Story Role

**POV Purpose**: Shows goblin perspective, blood magic, justified rage, cycle of violence

**Arc**: Must choose between revenge and survival

---

## Key Relationships

- **War Chief Skarva**: Advises, manipulates
- **Young shamans**: Teaching, worried about
- **Protagonist**: Tool/kinship/threat
- **Human powers**: Enemies

---

*Gritha represents what happens when justified anger becomes everything—wisdom lost to rage.*
