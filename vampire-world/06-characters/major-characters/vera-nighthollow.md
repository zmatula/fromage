## The Founder's Secret

Vera knows the truth about the Inheritors' founding—and her family's role in it.

### Her Lineage

Vera is a direct descendant of **Sera Nighthollow**, one of the three founders who murdered scholars at Sel'Naroth in Year 789. The secret has been passed down through the leadership chain: from Sera to her successor, eventually to Vera.

She knows:
- The Inheritors were founded on murder
- Her ancestor killed three scholars to keep knowledge secret
- The Year 812 "discovery" was staged
- Everything they've built is based on theft and blood

### The Burden

Vera carries this knowledge alone. She can't share it—it would destroy the Inheritors. She can't forget it—it shaped who they are. She rationalized it: the founders did wrong, but the work since then has been good. The ends justify the means.

But she's not sure she believes that anymore.

### The Three Families

Vera knows about the murdered scholars' descendants:
- **Helena Ashford** (Veil Keepers): Suspicious of Inheritors, getting closer
- **Marcus Thornwood** (Academy): Petitioning for Sel'Naroth access
- **Lady Cassandra Vane** (Maritime): Trying to clear her family name

She has people watching all three. If any of them discovers the truth, she'll face her ancestor's choice: silence them, or let everything collapse.

She tells herself she wouldn't kill them. She's not sure that's true.

### The Crown Whispers

Vera has been wearing the Crown of Mordecai during private rituals. The whispers have told her things about Seven she shouldn't know. They've also told her things about herself.

The whispers say her ancestor made the right choice. They say knowledge must be protected by any means. They say she'll do what she must when the time comes.

She's not sure if that's Mordecai speaking, the Crown itself, or her own thoughts finding a voice.

---

*Vera represents the Inheritor tragedy—they genuinely care, but can't stop treating people as means to knowledge. And beneath that tragedy is a darker one: she knows the whole organization is built on murder, and she's chosen to carry that secret rather than let the truth destroy what they've built.*
