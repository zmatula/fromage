## Reader Knowledge Position

### Where Reader Is Relative to Seven

| Story Phase | Reader Position | Effect |
|-------------|-----------------|--------|
| **Weeks 1-2** | WITH Seven | Share confusion, discovery |
| **Weeks 3-4** | AHEAD of Seven (faction POVs) | Dramatic irony, tension |
| **Weeks 5-6** | AHEAD of Seven (secrets revealed) | Anticipation of Seven's discoveries |
| **Weeks 7-8** | WITH Seven (catches up) | Shared understanding |
| **Weeks 9-10** | BEHIND Seven (his plans hidden) | Surprise at Seven's actions |
| **Weeks 11-12** | WITH Seven (revelation) | Shared climax experience |

### Information Reveal Sequence

**Reader learns before Seven:**
- Faction true motivations (via POV chapters)
- Who is hunting him and why
- Political developments affecting his situation
- What allies are planning

**Seven learns before reader:**
- Blood Archive contents (partial withholding)
- His true history details
- His ultimate plan

**Revealed simultaneously:**
- Dimensional threat nature
- Vessel Eleven's fate
- Climax revelations
